# Class Schedule & Program Launch Templates

## How to Use These Templates
Every class launch, challenge, and seasonal campaign needs a multi-channel announcement. These templates give you the copy for every channel — post it once on Instagram, send the email to your list, text your loyalty members, and you're done. Customize the class name, date, and any specific details, then send.

The most effective launches happen in layers: tease it 7 days out, announce it fully, send a reminder, close with urgency. The templates below are organized to support that sequence.
