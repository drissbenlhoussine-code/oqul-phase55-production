# Member Retention & Re-Engagement Scripts

## How to Use This System

Retaining an existing member is significantly more valuable than acquiring a new one — by most estimates, it costs five to seven times more to bring in a new client than to keep one you already have. When a member leaves your studio, you lose not just their monthly revenue but also the referrals they would have made, the community energy they brought to class, and the time and money you already invested in getting them through the door in the first place. The scripts in this system are designed to make retention feel personal, proactive, and effortless rather than desperate or transactional. Use them to build a communication rhythm that makes every member feel seen — because the members who feel most connected to your studio are the ones who stay for years, not months.

To use these scripts effectively, set up automated email sequences inside your email platform (Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, Klaviyo, or whatever you use) triggered by key dates: join date, birthday, and last class attendance. Personalize every message with the member's first name at minimum, and where possible reference the specific class format they attend most often. For at-risk member tracking, create a simple spreadsheet or use your studio management software (Mindbody, Pike13, Glofox) to flag any member who has not checked in within the past 10 days — that is your early-warning window. Catching disengagement early, before a member mentally checks out, is the difference between a quick win-back and a months-long reactivation campaign. These scripts are your system for doing that consistently and at scale.

And yet here you are, one month in and still showing up. We do not take that lightly, and we hope you do not either. The research on habit formation is clear: the first month is the hardest, and you have already made it through. From here, the practice starts to feel more natural, more automatic, and — we think you will find — more rewarding with every week that passes. We have loved having you in the studio this month and we are so glad you chose us to be part of your routine. With gratitude,

### "We Noticed You Haven't Been In" — After 21 Days (Email)

**Subject Line:** It has been a few weeks — can we help you find your way back?

Hi [First Name],

It has been about three weeks since we have seen you in the studio, and we want to reach out with some genuine honesty: we miss you, and we are a little worried we might have lost you. If life just got busy and you are planning to come back soon, please disregard this and we will see you on the mat. But if something got in the way — if momentum stalled, if returning feels harder than it should, if you have been meaning to come back but cannot quite make it happen — we want you to know that we have been there too, and we have helped a lot of members navigate exactly this moment. One of the most common things we hear is that after a few weeks away, coming back feels awkward or intimidating — like you have lost your progress and do not want to face that. We want to say clearly: that feeling is completely normal and also not as true as it feels. Your body remembers more than you think, and your community here will meet you right where you are without a second thought. Another thing that gets in the way is simply not having a plan — no class booked, no accountability, and then the week slips by again. We can fix that right now. We would love to invite you to a beginner-friendly class or a lower-intensity session to ease you back in with zero pressure, and we would love even more to offer you a free one-on-one check-in with one of our instructors to talk through where you are and what would feel good right now. You do not have to earn your way back. You do not have to be where you were. You just have to show up once, and we will handle everything from there. Reply to this email or text us at [Studio Phone Number] and let us help you take that one step. We are rooting for you, [First Name], and we really hope to see you soon.

With care,
[Studio Name] Team
