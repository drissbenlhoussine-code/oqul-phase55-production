# Objection Handling Scripts

## "IT'S TOO EXPENSIVE"
"I hear you — cost is a real concern. Let me ask: what were you thinking was a reasonable monthly investment for this coverage? [Listen] Here's what I want you to consider: the monthly cost of this policy vs. the one-time financial devastation your family would face without it. Most clients tell me that when they frame it that way, it actually feels very affordable. Can we look at a smaller coverage amount that fits your budget?"

## "I ALREADY HAVE COVERAGE THROUGH WORK"
"That's great — employer coverage is a real benefit. Here's something most people don't realize: group coverage typically ends when your employment ends, and the coverage amounts are often 1x–2x your salary — when most families need 10x to replace income long-term. I'm not saying replace it — I'd love to review your current policy and see if you have any gaps we should address. Would 15 minutes be worth it to find out?"

## "I NEED TO THINK ABOUT IT"
"Of course — this is an important decision. Can I ask what specifically you're thinking through? Is it the cost, the coverage amount, or something else? [Listen] Often when I understand what's holding someone back, I can either address it directly or at least make sure you have the right information to decide. What would help you feel confident moving forward?"

## "I'LL DO IT LATER"
"I completely understand. Life is busy. But here's something worth considering: the longer we wait, the more your premium increases — and if your health changes in the meantime, it can affect your eligibility. What if we just got the application submitted today — you have [X] days to review everything and cancel with no obligation. Would that take some pressure off?"

## "I DON'T BELIEVE I NEED IT"
"That's actually the most common thing I hear from people who end up needing it most. Can I ask — what's your plan if you became unable to work tomorrow? [Listen] I'm not trying to scare you. I just want to make sure your plan accounts for that possibility. If you have one, tell me about it and maybe I can confirm it's solid."
