# Insurance Business Systems Guide

## BUILDING A REFERRAL-DRIVEN INSURANCE BUSINESS

### Weekly Non-Negotiables
- 10 outreach contacts (past clients, sphere, partners)
- 3 follow-ups on open quotes
- 2 coffee meetings with referral partners
- 1 piece of educational content published
- All reviews responded to

### Client Lifecycle Management
1. **New inquiry** → contact within 1 hour
2. **Needs analysis** → thorough conversation, not just a quote
3. **Proposal delivery** → explain clearly, answer every question
4. **Follow-up sequence** → days 1, 3, 7, 14
5. **Policy delivery** → congratulate, explain, request review
6. **30-day check-in** → any questions?
7. **Annual review** → locked in on your calendar
8. **Referral ask** → at delivery and at each annual review

### Referral Partner ROI Tracking
Track each referral partner:
- Referrals received (monthly, yearly)
- Conversion rate from their referrals
- Revenue generated
- Effort invested

Double down on your top 3 partners quarterly.

### Revenue KPIs
- New policies written per month
- Premium per policy (target)
- Retention rate (target: 90%+)
- Referral rate (% of new business from referrals)
- Revenue per client over 3 years
