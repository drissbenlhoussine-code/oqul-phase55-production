# Lead & Follow-Up Scripts

## INITIAL INQUIRY RESPONSE
"Hi [Name]! Thanks for reaching out. I'm [Name] with [Company]. Happy to help you find the right coverage. Can I ask — what type of insurance are you looking for, and is this your first time getting coverage or are you looking to replace something you already have?"

## NEEDS ANALYSIS QUESTIONS
1. "Tell me about your current coverage situation — what do you have and what gaps are you concerned about?"
2. "What's your biggest financial concern if something happened to you or your income tomorrow?"
3. "Do you have dependents relying on your income?"
4. "What's your approximate monthly budget for this coverage?"
5. "When would you ideally like to have coverage in place?"

## FOLLOW-UP SEQUENCE

**Day 1:**
"Hi [Name], [Name] here from [Company]. Just checking in after our conversation today. Did you have any questions about the options I presented? I want to make sure you have everything you need to feel confident in your decision."

**Day 3:**
"Hi [Name], following up on your quote. I know it's a big decision — I want to help, not pressure. If you have any questions or want me to walk through the numbers again, just say the word."

**Day 7:**
"Hi [Name], last follow-up from [Company]. Your quote is still valid and I'm happy to adjust it if your needs have changed. Even if the timing isn't right today — keep my number. You'll want someone you trust when the time comes."

**Day 14:**
"Hi [Name], checking in one more time. I don't want to be a nuisance — just want to make sure you found a solution that protects your family. If I can ever be a resource, I'm here."
