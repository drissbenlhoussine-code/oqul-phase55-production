# Referral System Templates

## CLIENT REFERRAL REQUEST

**After policy delivery:**
"[Name], I'm really glad we got this coverage in place for you. I work primarily from referrals — people who trust me with their protection are the best source of new clients. If you have any friends or family members who you know need to review their coverage, I'd be honored to help them the same way I helped you. Would it be okay if I reached out to [name] on your behalf? Or I can give you some cards to pass along."

**Annual review referral ask:**
"[Name], another year of protecting what matters most. I hope you've been well! Quick question — do you know anyone who might benefit from sitting down with me like we did? A lot of people don't have the right coverage and don't even know it. I promise I'll take great care of anyone you send my way."

## REFERRAL PARTNER EMAILS

**Financial Advisor:**
"Hi [Name], I'm an independent insurance agent specializing in [niche]. A lot of my clients are in the wealth-building phase and I imagine some of your clients have insurance gaps they may not even be aware of. I'd love to be a trusted resource you can refer to — I'll treat every client you send with care, and I'll never try to cross-sell financial services outside my lane. Would you be open to a coffee to learn more about each other's work?"

**Realtor:**
"Hi [Name], I work with a lot of homebuyers who need homeowner's insurance as part of the closing process. I'd love to be your go-to referral for coverage — fast quotes, great rates, and I make the process easy so it doesn't slow down your closing. Would you be open to a quick call or coffee?"
