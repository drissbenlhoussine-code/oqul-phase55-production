# Insurance Agent Local Marketing Playbook

## BUILDING YOUR LOCAL PRESENCE

### Google My Business
- Complete profile with all insurance types listed
- Request reviews from every new client at policy delivery
- Post weekly: tips, education, community content
- Respond to every review within 24 hours

### Chamber of Commerce & Networking
- Join your local chamber and attend every event (people buy from people they know)
- Sponsor chamber events (visible, memorable)
- Volunteer for chamber committees
- Follow up within 48 hours of every business card received

### Referral Partner Strategy
Priority partners: Realtors, mortgage brokers, CPAs, financial advisors, auto dealers
- Monthly coffee meetings with top partners
- Send value-add content (rate updates, coverage tips) not just sales pitches
- Provide excellent service to every referral they send

### Community Involvement
- Sponsor local school or sports teams
- Attend community events and be visible
- Host free financial literacy seminars
- Partner with HR departments at local employers (group presentations)

### Direct Outreach
- Personal LinkedIn for professional networking
- Facebook for community engagement and trust-building
- Postcard campaigns to new homeowners (they just became home insurance buyers)
- Welcome to neighborhood card to new residents
