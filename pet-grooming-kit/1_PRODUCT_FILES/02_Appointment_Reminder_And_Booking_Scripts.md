# Appointment Reminder & Booking Scripts

## BOOKING CONFIRMATION TEXT
"Hi [Name]! [Pet Name]'s appointment is confirmed at [Salon Name] on [Day], [Date] at [Time]. We'll see you both then! 🐾 Questions? Text or call [Phone]."

## 48-HOUR REMINDER
"Hi [Name]! Just a reminder that [Pet Name] has a grooming appointment at [Salon Name] on [Day] at [Time]. Please let us know if you need to reschedule — we need 24 hours notice. See you soon! 🐶"

## 24-HOUR REMINDER
"Reminder: [Pet Name] is due for their groom tomorrow at [Time] at [Salon Name]! We can't wait to see them. 🐾 If you're running late, please text us — we'll do our best to accommodate."

## DAY-OF MESSAGE
"Good morning [Name]! [Pet Name]'s appointment is today at [Time]. We're excited to see them! Address: [Address]. See you soon! 🐾"

## OVERDUE PET REMINDER (when it's been 8+ weeks since last groom)
"Hi [Name]! [Pet Name] is due for their grooming appointment — it's been about [timeframe] since their last visit. We want to keep them comfortable and healthy! We have openings this week: [days/times]. Want to grab a slot? 🐶"

## NO-SHOW FOLLOW-UP
"Hi [Name], we missed you and [Pet Name] today! Life gets busy — no worries. We'd love to get them rescheduled. Next availability: [times]. Text or call [Phone] whenever you're ready. 🐾"
