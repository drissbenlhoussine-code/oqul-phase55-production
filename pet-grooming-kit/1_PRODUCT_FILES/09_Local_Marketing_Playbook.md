# Pet Grooming Local Marketing Playbook

## GROWING YOUR PET COMMUNITY

### Google My Business
- Upload adorable before/after photos (with permission)
- Post 3x per week: transformations, tips, new services
- Request reviews after every single groom
- Target: 80+ reviews with 4.8+ average

### Facebook Groups
- Join local pet owner Facebook groups
- Share helpful grooming tips (non-promotional)
- Respond to every grooming question
- Offer periodic group-exclusive discounts

### Vet Clinic Partnerships
- Drop off business cards and referral cards at local vet offices
- Offer vet staff a free or discounted groom (they'll refer patients)
- Ask vets to recommend grooming frequency for specific breeds

### Pet Store Partnerships
- Partner with local independent pet stores for co-promotion
- Leave business cards at checkout
- Offer co-promotions (spend $X at pet store, get $X off groom)

### Local Events
- Pet-friendly market days
- Dog park presence (mobile setup, card distribution)
- Charity grooming events
- Sponsor local animal rescue fundraisers

### Referral Culture
- Thank every person who refers with a handwritten note + reward
- Post shoutouts to referring clients on social
- Create a "VIP Referrer" status for top referrers
