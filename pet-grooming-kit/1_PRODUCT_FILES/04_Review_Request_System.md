# Review Request System

## POST-GROOM TEXT (within 30 min of pickup)
"[Name], [Pet Name] was an absolute delight today! 🐾 If you loved their groom, could you take a minute to leave us a Google review? [Link] It really helps us grow. Thank you! — [Salon Name]"

## EMAIL (Day 2)
Subject: How is [Pet Name] doing? 🐾

Hi [Name],

We hope [Pet Name] is happy and fluffy since their visit!

If you're pleased with how they look and how we treated them, we'd be so grateful for a quick Google review. Even just a sentence or two means everything to a small business like ours.

[Leave a Review on Google →]

If there's anything we could have done better, please reach out directly — we always want to know.

Thank you for trusting us with [Pet Name]!

Lots of love,
[Salon Name] Team 🐾

## IN-PERSON SCRIPT
"[Name], it was so great seeing [Pet Name] today! They were such a good boy/girl. If you're happy with how they look, we'd love it if you shared a Google review — I can send you the link right now. It means so much to us."

## PHOTO SHARING WITH REVIEW REQUEST
Send an after-groom photo (with permission obtained at intake):
"[Pet Name] all done! 😍🐾 We love how they turned out! If you agree, would you share a Google review? [Link] — it helps us help more pets in [City]! Thank you!"
