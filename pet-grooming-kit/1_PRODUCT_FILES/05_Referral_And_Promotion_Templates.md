"[Name], we love having [Pet Name] in our salon! If you have friends with dogs (or cats!) who need a great groomer, we'd love the referral. Our program: when your friend books and comes in, you get $[X] off your next groom — and they get [offer]. Just have them mention your name! 🐾"

**Referral Card (to print):**
