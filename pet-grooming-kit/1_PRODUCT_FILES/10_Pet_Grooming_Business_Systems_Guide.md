# Pet Grooming Business Systems Guide

## SCALING YOUR GROOMING BUSINESS

### Daily Operations Checklist
□ Confirm all appointments via text day before
□ Prepare station for first appointment
□ Take before photo (with permission)
□ Document any skin, coat, or health observations
□ Take after photo (with permission)
□ Send photo to owner at pickup
□ Post-groom text within 30 min
□ Request review via text
□ Log rebook due date for each pet

### Client Management System
- Track each pet's breed, service preferences, coat notes, temperament
- Set automatic alerts for overdue pets (8 weeks standard, breed-adjusted)
- Note any product sensitivities or health conditions
- Record owner preferences (length, style, accessories)

### Pricing Strategy
- Full groom by size: Small ($X) · Medium ($X) · Large ($X) · XL ($X)
- Add-ons: teeth brushing, nail grinding, ear cleaning, de-shed, spa treatment
- Packages: 3-groom bundle (save X%), 6-groom bundle (save X%)
- Loyalty card: 10th groom at 50% off

### Revenue Growth
- Add-on upsells at booking: "Would you like to add teeth brushing for $[X]?"
- Retail products: shampoo, brushes, nail tools with your branding
- Gift cards: marketed heavily in November/December
- Gift packages: "new puppy" or "rescue welcome" package

### Financial KPIs
- Revenue per appointment
- Average ticket with add-ons
- Client retention rate (target: 75%+)
- Rebooking rate at time of pickup
- Review count growth month-over-month
