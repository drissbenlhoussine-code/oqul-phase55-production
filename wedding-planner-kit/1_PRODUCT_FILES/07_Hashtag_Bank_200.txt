HOW TO USE THESE SETS

Never use the same set of hashtags on back-to-back posts — rotate through at least two or three different sets before returning to one you have already used, since Instagram's algorithm deprioritizes accounts that repeat identical tag groups. Instagram allows up to 30 hashtags per post, so you can stack a full Rotation Set with an additional 5–10 tags pulled from any Tier that matches your specific post content (for example, add Tier 8 seasonal tags to Set A when posting a fall wedding gallery). Mix one or two high-volume tags from Tier 1 with a Rotation Set on every post so your content gets initial broad exposure while the mid-size and niche tags do the longer-tail targeting work. Save each Rotation Set as a saved reply or notes shortcut on your phone so you can copy and paste at posting time rather than manually typing hashtags in the field. Review your Instagram Insights monthly to see which tags are driving reach and impressions, then swap out underperforming tags from a set for alternatives from the corresponding Tier to keep your strategy fresh.
