# Refund Policy for Digital Products

Thank you for your purchase. We genuinely care about your experience and want you to feel confident buying from our shop. Please take a moment to read through this policy — we've written it to be straightforward and fair, not full of fine print.

---

## Our Policy

**Digital products are non-refundable once downloaded.**

We know that's not what anyone wants to read, but this is a standard industry policy for digital goods — and it's also consistent with Etsy's platform policies for digital downloads. We want to be upfront about this from the start so there are no surprises.

That said, this policy exists alongside a genuine commitment to making sure you can actually use what you purchased. If something isn't working, please reach out to us before assuming there's nothing we can do. There almost always is.

---

## Why Digital Products Are Non-Refundable

Unlike a physical product, a digital file is delivered instantly and cannot be "returned." The moment a download link is accessed, you have a copy of the product — and that copy doesn't disappear if a refund is issued. This makes refunds impractical in the traditional sense, and it's why the digital products industry as a whole operates on a no-refund basis after download.

We invested significant time in creating high-quality, professionally designed templates and tools, and we trust that the value they deliver far outweighs the purchase price. Our goal is always that you finish your purchase feeling like you got more than you paid for.

## What To Do If You Have a Technical Issue

A non-refund policy should never mean you're left stuck with something that doesn't work. Here's how we handle the most common situations:

### Files Won't Open

This is usually a software or file association issue, not a problem with the files themselves. Before reaching out, try these steps:

1. Make sure you've fully extracted the ZIP file (don't try to open files from inside the ZIP).
2. Right-click the file and select **Open With**, then choose the appropriate application (Excel, Google Sheets, a browser for HTML files, etc.).
3. Check our companion guide *How to Open CSV Files* for step-by-step instructions for each application.
4. Try opening the file on a different device or browser.

If you've worked through these steps and still can't get the files open, message us and we'll troubleshoot with you directly.

### Files Appear Corrupted or Incomplete

If a file downloads but appears damaged or is missing content, it is most likely a partial download. Here's what to do:

1. Return to your Etsy **Purchases** page and click **Download Files** again to re-download.
2. Make sure your internet connection is stable before starting the download.
3. Try using a different browser.

If you re-download and the file is still not right, please contact us through Etsy Messages and include a screenshot if possible. We will investigate and provide a working file.

### Accidental Purchase Before Downloading

If you purchased the product by mistake and have **not yet downloaded any files**, please contact Etsy Support as quickly as possible. Etsy may be able to assist with a cancellation in that situation. You can reach Etsy's support team at [help.etsy.com](https://help.etsy.com).

Once a download has been initiated, we are unable to process a refund under our policy — but again, if you have a genuine technical issue with the files themselves, please reach out and we will do our best to help.

## How to Contact Us

For any technical issues or questions about your purchase, please contact us through **Etsy Messages**:

1. Go to [etsy.com](https://www.etsy.com) and log in to your account.
2. Navigate to **Messages** in the top menu.
3. Start a new conversation with our shop.

**When you message us, please include:**
- Your **order number** (found on your Etsy purchase confirmation)
- A **brief description** of the issue you're experiencing
- If relevant, a **screenshot** showing what you're seeing

We aim to respond to all messages within **1–2 business days**. We're a small operation, so we appreciate your patience — but we do read every message and we will get back to you.
