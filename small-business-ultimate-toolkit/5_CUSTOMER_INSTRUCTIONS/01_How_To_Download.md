# How to Download Your Files from Etsy

Welcome, and thank you for your purchase! This guide will walk you through everything you need to know to access, download, and organize your new digital files. If you run into any trouble, don't worry — we've got you covered with troubleshooting tips at the end.

---

## After Purchase — Instant Access

One of the best things about digital downloads is that you get instant access — no waiting for shipping, no tracking numbers. Here's what happens immediately after your purchase:

1. **Etsy processes your payment** and automatically marks your order as complete.
2. **A download link appears** on your order confirmation page within seconds.
3. **A confirmation email is sent** to the email address associated with your Etsy account, containing a link back to your downloads.

### Where to Find Your Download Link

You have two easy ways to access your files:

**Option A — From the Etsy Website:**
1. Log in to your Etsy account at [etsy.com](https://www.etsy.com).
2. Click on your account icon in the top-right corner.
3. Select **Purchases and Reviews** from the dropdown menu.
4. Find your order and click the **Download Files** button next to it.

**Option B — From Your Email:**
1. Check the inbox of the email address linked to your Etsy account.
2. Look for an email from Etsy with the subject line similar to "Your download from [Shop Name] is ready."
3. Click the **Download** link inside the email — it will take you directly to your files.

> **Tip:** If you don't see the email, check your spam or junk folder. Etsy confirmation emails occasionally end up there.

---

## Downloading Your Files

Once you've found the download link, here's how to get the files onto your device:

1. Click the **Download Files** button on the Etsy purchases page.
2. A download will begin automatically, or your browser may ask you where to save the file — choose a location you'll remember, such as your Desktop or Downloads folder.
3. Wait for the download to complete. The time will vary depending on your internet speed and file size, but most downloads finish within a few seconds to a minute.
4. Once complete, you'll find the file(s) in the location you chose (typically your **Downloads** folder by default).

### About ZIP Files

Your purchase may arrive as a **ZIP file** — a single compressed package containing all of your files bundled together. This is completely normal and makes downloading multiple files at once much faster and easier. You'll need to unzip (extract) the file before you can use the contents.

---

## Unzipping Your Files

### On Windows

1. Locate the ZIP file in your Downloads folder (it will have a `.zip` extension and a zipper icon).
2. **Right-click** on the ZIP file.
3. Select **Extract All...** from the context menu.
4. A dialog box will appear — choose where you'd like to extract the files (or leave the default location) and click **Extract**.
5. A new folder will appear with all of your files inside, ready to use.

### On Mac

1. Locate the ZIP file in your Downloads folder.
2. **Double-click** the ZIP file.
3. Mac will automatically unzip it and create a new folder in the same location with all the extracted files inside.
4. You can then delete the original ZIP file if you'd like to keep things tidy.

### What You'll See After Unzipping

After unzipping, you'll find a folder containing all of your purchased files — likely a combination of `.csv` spreadsheet files, a `.html` invoice template, and any PDF or instruction files included with your purchase. Each file is clearly named so you can identify its purpose at a glance.

## Saving and Organizing Your Files

Taking a few minutes to organize your files now will save you a lot of time later.

### Recommended Folder Structure

```
My Business Files/
├── Small Business Toolkit/
│   ├── Originals/          ← Keep untouched backup copies here
│   ├── Working Files/      ← Work from copies in this folder
│   └── Completed/          ← Store filled-in or archived versions here
```

Always keep a copy of the original, unedited files in a separate folder. That way, if you make a mistake, you can always start fresh from the original.

### Cloud Backup Recommendation

We strongly recommend backing up your files to a cloud storage service. This protects your purchase from accidental deletion or device failure:

- **Google Drive** — free 15GB storage, accessible from any device
- **iCloud Drive** — great for Mac and iPhone users
- **Dropbox** — excellent for sharing with team members

Simply drag and drop your extracted folder into your cloud storage app and your files will be safely backed up automatically.

## Troubleshooting

### The Download Button Doesn't Appear
- Refresh the page and try again.
- Make sure you are logged in to the correct Etsy account used for the purchase.
- Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, or Safari).
- Clear your browser's cache and cookies, then reload the page.

### My Files Won't Open
- Make sure you have appropriate software installed (see the companion guide *How to Open CSV Files* for details).
- Ensure the file fully downloaded — a partial download can result in a corrupted file. Try downloading again.
- On Windows, you may need to right-click the file, select **Open With**, and choose the correct application.

### Who to Contact
- **For Etsy account or billing issues:** Visit [Etsy Help Center](https://help.etsy.com) and contact Etsy Support directly.
- **For issues with the files themselves:** Send us a message through Etsy — we're happy to help and typically respond within 1–2 business days.
