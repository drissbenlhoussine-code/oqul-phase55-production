# Small Business Email Templates Pack
## 50 Professional Email Templates for Every Business Situation

---

This pack contains 50 ready-to-use email templates organized into 10 categories covering the most common business communication needs. Each template includes a subject line and a complete email body with [PLACEHOLDERS] for your personalization. Adapt tone, length, and specifics to match your brand voice before sending.
