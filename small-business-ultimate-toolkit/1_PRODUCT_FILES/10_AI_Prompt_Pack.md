# Small Business AI Prompt Pack
## 150 ChatGPT Prompts to Grow Your Business

Artificial intelligence is no longer a luxury reserved for large corporations with deep pockets. Today, tools like ChatGPT give small business owners access to a tireless creative partner, copywriter, strategist, and analyst — available 24/7 at a fraction of the cost of hiring specialists. This prompt pack gives you 150 ready-to-use prompts across every major area of your business, from marketing and social media to pricing strategy, customer service, and brand storytelling. To get the most out of these prompts, simply replace the placeholders like [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE], [YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE], [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE], and [YOUR NICHE] with your specific details before pasting into ChatGPT. The more specific you are, the better the results. Think of these prompts as a starting point — feel free to add follow-up questions, ask for revisions, or tell ChatGPT to try a different tone or angle until you get exactly what you need.

## Section 1: Marketing Prompts (15 Prompts)

Marketing is the engine that drives visibility, leads, and revenue. Whether you are just starting out or looking to scale, these prompts will help you craft compelling campaigns, identify your ideal customers, and create messaging that converts. Use these to build marketing plans, write ad copy, analyze your competitors, and position your business for growth.

1. Write a comprehensive 90-day marketing plan for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] targeting [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE]. Break the plan into three 30-day phases: Phase 1 (Awareness), Phase 2 (Engagement), Phase 3 (Conversion). For each phase, list three specific marketing tactics, the platforms or channels to use, the type of content to produce, and one measurable KPI to track success.

2. Create five different unique selling proposition (USP) statements for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] that would resonate with [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE]. Each USP should be one to two sentences, highlight a specific benefit rather than a feature, and differentiate the business from typical competitors in [YOUR NICHE]. Make each one feel distinct in tone — try bold, emotional, factual, humorous, and aspirational versions.

3. Write a competitor analysis framework for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] in [YOUR NICHE]. Include ten specific questions I should research about each competitor, three ways to identify gaps in their offerings that I could capitalize on, and a simple scoring matrix I can use to benchmark my business against them across five key dimensions: price, quality, customer service, marketing reach, and brand identity.

4. Generate a list of 20 low-cost or no-cost marketing ideas specifically for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] targeting [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE]. For each idea, provide a one-sentence description of what it involves, the expected time investment, and the likely marketing outcome. Prioritize ideas that can drive results within 30 to 60 days without requiring a large advertising budget.

5. Write a Google Ads campaign strategy for [YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE] aimed at [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE]. Include three campaign types to consider, five high-intent keyword phrases to target, two sample ad headlines with descriptions for each, and a recommended daily budget range for a small business just starting with paid advertising. Also include one negative keyword list to reduce wasted spend.

6. Create a referral marketing program outline for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE]. Include the name and concept of the program, the incentive structure for both the referrer and the new customer, the step-by-step process a customer would follow to refer someone, the language I should use to promote the program via email and social media, and how I would track and reward referrals accurately.

7. Write a press release announcing [YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE] for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE]. Use proper press release format including a compelling headline, dateline, opening paragraph that answers who, what, when, where, and why, two supporting body paragraphs with detail, a quote from the business owner, a boilerplate company description, and contact information. Make it newsworthy and suitable for local media outreach.

8. Develop a seasonal marketing calendar for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] covering all 12 months of the year. For each month, identify the most relevant holidays, observances, or seasonal trends that [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE] cares about, suggest one promotional campaign idea, and recommend the best marketing channel to use. Focus on campaigns that are realistic for a small team to execute.

9. Write five different calls-to-action (CTAs) for [YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE] that could be used on a website homepage, landing page, email, social media post, and print flyer respectively. Each CTA should be action-oriented, benefit-focused, and appropriate for the medium it will appear on. Explain briefly why each CTA would be effective for [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE].

10. Create a local marketing strategy for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] that focuses on building visibility and customer loyalty within a specific geographic area. Include five offline tactics such as community events or partnerships, five online local tactics such as Google Business Profile optimization, and a plan for encouraging and responding to local reviews. Tailor all suggestions to appeal to [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE].

11. Write three versions of a marketing message for [YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE] — one for a cold audience who has never heard of the business, one for a warm audience who has engaged with content before, and one for a hot audience ready to buy. Each version should be two to three sentences and clearly reflect where the customer is in the buying journey, using language appropriate for [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE].

12. Develop an affiliate marketing program structure for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] in [YOUR NICHE]. Include the commission structure, the type of affiliates or partners to recruit, the marketing materials I should provide to affiliates, how to onboard and communicate with them, and the tools or platforms I should use to manage the program. Include a sample outreach message I could send to potential affiliates.

13. Create a marketing funnel outline for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] that covers the complete customer journey from first awareness to repeat purchase. For each stage of the funnel — awareness, interest, consideration, intent, purchase, and loyalty — describe the type of content or marketing activity to deploy, the platform or channel to use, and one metric to measure effectiveness at that stage.

14. Write a promotional campaign concept for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] that could be run during a slow season. Include the campaign name, the core offer or incentive, the messaging angle that would motivate [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE] to act during that period, the channels to promote it through, and a week-by-week rollout plan spanning four weeks. Make it exciting and actionable.

15. Generate a brand positioning statement and tagline for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] serving [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE] in [YOUR NICHE]. The positioning statement should follow the classic format: "For [target audience] who [need/want], [brand name] is the [category] that [key benefit] because [reason to believe]." Then write five tagline options — short, punchy, and memorable — that capture the brand's core value in under eight words each.

## Section 2: Social Media Prompts (15 Prompts)

Social media is one of the most powerful tools available to small businesses for building brand awareness, engaging customers, and driving sales without a massive advertising budget. These prompts will help you create content calendars, write platform-specific posts, grow your following organically, and turn engagement into real business results. Use them to work smarter, not harder, on social media.

1. Create a 30-day social media content calendar for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] across Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn. For each day, specify the platform, the type of post (educational, promotional, behind-the-scenes, testimonial, entertaining, or engagement), the topic or theme, and a brief description of the content. Distribute content types evenly throughout the month and ensure at least six promotional posts are spaced throughout so they do not feel intrusive.

2. Write ten unique Instagram Reel concepts for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] that would appeal to [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE]. For each concept, include the hook for the first three seconds that stops the scroll, the main content structure in three to five steps, a strong call to action, and one trending audio or format style that would suit the concept. Make them educational, entertaining, or emotionally resonant rather than purely promotional.

3. Write a LinkedIn article outline and introduction paragraph for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] on the topic of [YOUR NICHE]. The article should position the business owner as a thought leader, provide genuine value to [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE], and be written in a professional but conversational tone. Include a working title, five main section headers with bullet points under each, and a compelling opening paragraph of 100 to 150 words that hooks the reader immediately.

4. Create 15 caption templates for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] that can be reused with minor edits across different Instagram or Facebook posts. Each caption should include a hook sentence, two to three lines of value or story, a call to action, and a line for hashtags. Vary the tone across the 15 templates — include motivational, educational, humorous, behind-the-scenes, and promotional styles.

5. Write a social media bio for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] optimized for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and TikTok respectively. Each bio should be appropriate in length and tone for that platform, clearly communicate what the business does, who it serves, and what the audience should do next. Include a call to action appropriate for each platform such as a link in bio, a DM prompt, or a website visit.

6. Generate a list of 30 hashtag sets for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] in [YOUR NICHE] that I can rotate through on Instagram posts. Organize them into three groups: ten sets of broad hashtags (over 500K uses), ten sets of mid-range hashtags (50K to 500K uses), and ten sets of niche hashtags (under 50K uses). Explain the strategy for combining hashtags from each group to maximize reach and discoverability for [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE].

7. Write five Facebook ad copy variations for [YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE] targeting [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE]. For each variation, write a primary text (two to three sentences), a headline (under ten words), a description (one sentence), and a call-to-action button label. Test different angles: problem-solution, social proof, scarcity, curiosity, and transformation. Keep the language clear, direct, and benefit-driven.

8. Create a Pinterest content strategy for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] targeting [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE]. Include five board ideas with names and descriptions, ten pin topic ideas for each board (50 pin ideas total), guidance on how to write SEO-optimized pin descriptions, best times to pin for maximum reach, and how to use Pinterest analytics to refine the strategy over time. Explain how Pinterest can drive long-term organic traffic to a small business website.

9. Write a social media crisis response plan for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE]. Include a step-by-step process for responding to a negative viral comment or review, three sample response templates for different types of complaints (product issue, service failure, miscommunication), how to take the conversation offline professionally, and how to prevent future incidents. Keep the tone professional, empathetic, and brand-consistent.

10. Generate 20 engagement-boosting question prompts that [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] could post on Instagram Stories or Facebook to increase interaction with [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE]. Make the questions specific to [YOUR NICHE] and vary the format — include this-or-that polls, fill-in-the-blank, opinion questions, trivia, and personal experience questions. Each question should feel fun and relevant, not generic.

11. Write a TikTok content strategy for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] that would help reach [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE] organically. Include five content pillars that align with the brand, ten specific video ideas for the first month, tips for using trending sounds and hashtags effectively, a posting frequency recommendation, and how to engage with the TikTok community to grow faster. Keep the strategy realistic for a solo or small-team business.

12. Create a social proof and testimonial strategy for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] on social media. Include how to request testimonials from happy customers, five templates for how to post a testimonial on Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn in an engaging way, how to use before-and-after content ethically, and how to build a library of user-generated content. Explain how consistent social proof increases conversion rates for [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE].

13. Write a script for a 60-second promotional video for [YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE] that could be used on Instagram Reels, TikTok, or YouTube Shorts. Include the hook in the first three seconds, a clear problem statement, the solution your product or service provides, two to three specific benefits, a brief social proof reference, and a strong call to action. Write it in a conversational and authentic tone that suits [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE].

14. Develop a social media growth strategy for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] that focuses on gaining 500 to 1,000 real followers in the next 90 days without paid advertising. Include five organic growth tactics, a collaboration or cross-promotion strategy, how to use relevant accounts and hashtags for outreach, a content consistency plan, and how to measure progress weekly. Make it specific to [YOUR NICHE] and [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE].

15. Create a complete social media audit checklist for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE]. Include what to review on each platform (profile completeness, content quality, posting frequency, engagement rate, audience demographics), how to analyze which content is performing best and why, questions to ask when evaluating whether a platform is worth continuing, and an action plan template for making improvements based on the audit findings.

## Section 3: Customer Service Prompts (15 Prompts)

Exceptional customer service is what turns one-time buyers into loyal advocates who refer others and return again and again. These prompts will help you write professional and empathetic responses, build systems for handling complaints, create FAQ documents, and develop a customer experience that sets your business apart in [YOUR NICHE]. Use them to save time and ensure consistent, high-quality communication with every customer.

1. Write a complete FAQ document for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] covering the 20 most commonly asked questions that [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE] might have about [YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE]. For each question, write a clear, concise, and friendly answer of two to four sentences. Organize the questions into four categories: General Information, Pricing and Payment, Delivery or Service Process, and Returns or Cancellations.

2. Create a customer service response template library for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] covering 10 common customer situations: a product complaint, a delayed order or service, a request for a refund, a billing error, a question about availability, a compliment, a request for a discount, a question before purchase, a cancellation request, and a request for a recommendation. Each response should be professional, empathetic, and solution-focused.

3. Write a customer onboarding welcome sequence for new clients of [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE]. Include what to say in the first email or message after a purchase, what information to share in the first week to set expectations, how to check in at the midpoint of a service engagement, what to send at the end to encourage a review and rebooking. Keep all communication warm, helpful, and reassuring for [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE].

4. Develop a system for handling negative reviews for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] online. Include a three-step process for responding to any negative review publicly, five sample response templates for different types of complaints, how to follow up privately to resolve the issue, when and how to request a review update, and how to use negative feedback internally to improve the business. Make the responses empathetic without being defensive.

5. Write a customer loyalty program outline for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] designed to reward repeat customers and increase lifetime value. Include the program name, the point or reward structure, how customers earn and redeem rewards, how to communicate the program to [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE], three exclusive benefits for top-tier loyal customers, and how to track participation and measure the program's impact on revenue.

6. Create a customer feedback survey for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] that could be sent after a purchase or service completion. Include 12 questions that cover overall satisfaction, specific aspects of the product or service, ease of the purchasing or booking process, quality of communication, likelihood to recommend, and open-ended suggestions. Make the questions clear and unbiased, and include a brief intro message explaining why the feedback matters.

7. Write a script for a customer service phone call for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] that covers how to greet a caller, how to identify and clarify their issue, how to offer a solution or escalate if needed, and how to close the call professionally. Include specific language to use and avoid, how to handle an upset or emotional caller, and tips for documenting the interaction afterward. Make it practical and easy to follow for a new team member.

8. Develop a proactive customer communication strategy for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] that prevents complaints before they happen. Include what information to communicate upfront at the time of purchase, how to set expectations about timelines and deliverables, what to say if there is a delay or issue, how to send proactive check-in messages, and how this approach builds trust and reduces refund requests from [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE].

9. Write five empathy statements and five problem-solving phrases that customer service representatives at [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] should memorize and use regularly. For each empathy statement, explain the situation it is best suited for. For each problem-solving phrase, explain what it communicates to the customer. Also write five phrases to avoid using because they escalate tension or come across as dismissive, with alternatives for each.

10. Create a customer win-back strategy for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] targeting previous customers who have not purchased or engaged in six months or more. Include a three-part reactivation email sequence with subject lines and body copy, a special offer or incentive to include, how to segment and identify lapsed customers, what language resonates with someone who may have left due to price, experience, or simply forgetting the brand exists.

11. Write a service recovery plan for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] for when something goes significantly wrong — such as a major error, a missed deadline, or a product failure that impacts [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE]. Include the immediate steps to take within the first 24 hours, how to communicate the issue to affected customers, what to offer as compensation, how to prevent the same issue from recurring, and how to restore customer trust after a serious mistake.

12. Develop a set of customer communication guidelines for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] to ensure consistent tone and quality across all team members and channels. Include five brand voice principles, a list of dos and don'ts for written communication, how to adapt the tone for different platforms (email, social media, text, phone), response time standards for each channel, and how to handle situations that fall outside normal policy.

13. Write a script for following up with a potential customer who inquired about [YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE] but has not yet made a purchase. Include a friendly opening that references their inquiry, a brief reminder of the key benefit, a way to address any hesitation or objection they might have, a limited-time incentive or reason to act now, and a clear and low-pressure call to action. Keep it warm and helpful, not pushy.

14. Create a complete customer journey map for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] from the moment a potential customer first discovers the brand to after their third purchase. For each stage of the journey — awareness, consideration, first purchase, post-purchase, and retention — describe the customer's emotions and questions at that stage, the touchpoints they interact with, and what [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] should do to create a positive experience. Identify the top two moments where customers are most likely to drop off and suggest how to prevent that.

15. Write a set of automated text message or chat responses for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] covering ten common customer inquiries: hours of operation, location or service area, pricing overview, booking or ordering process, current wait time or availability, confirmation of appointment or order, delivery or completion status update, how to cancel or reschedule, how to leave a review, and how to contact a human team member. Keep each message under 160 characters where possible and ensure they feel personal, not robotic.

## Section 4: Product Description Prompts (15 Prompts)

A great product description does more than describe what something is — it sells the experience, addresses objections, and motivates the customer to buy now. Whether you sell physical products, digital downloads, or services, these prompts will help you write compelling, SEO-friendly descriptions that convert browsers into buyers. Use the placeholders to customize every prompt for your specific offerings in [YOUR NICHE].

1. Write a compelling product description for [YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE] targeting [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE]. Include a headline that leads with the biggest benefit, two to three sentences that paint a picture of the result or transformation the customer will experience, a bulleted list of five key features with their corresponding benefits, and a confident call to action. Make the language sensory and specific, avoiding generic phrases like "high quality" or "great value."

2. Rewrite the following basic product description in a way that dramatically increases its appeal to [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE]: "[paste your existing description here]." Make it more emotionally engaging, benefit-driven, and specific. Keep the factual details but translate them into language that speaks to what the customer actually cares about. Offer three different versions: one professional, one conversational, and one high-energy.

3. Create product descriptions for five different variations of [YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE] — for example, different sizes, tiers, flavors, styles, or price points. Each description should be unique (no copy-paste with minor edits), highlight what makes that specific variation the right choice for a particular type of buyer, and include a clear differentiator that helps the customer choose the right one for their needs without feeling overwhelmed.

4. Write an SEO-optimized product description for [YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE] that naturally incorporates the following keywords: [list three to five keywords relevant to your niche]. The description should be 150 to 200 words, include the primary keyword in the first sentence, use secondary keywords naturally throughout, and end with a call to action. Make sure it reads naturally for a human reader while still being keyword-rich for search engines.

5. Create a "before and after" style product description for [YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE] that illustrates the transformation a customer experiences. Describe in vivid detail what life looks like before using the product or service (the pain point, the frustration, the problem), and then contrast it with what life looks like after (the result, the relief, the improvement). End with a bridge sentence that connects the transformation directly to [YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE] and a clear call to action.

6. Write a product description for [YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE] that specifically addresses and overcomes the top three objections [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE] commonly has before purchasing. Identify each objection, acknowledge it empathetically, and then counter it with a specific benefit, piece of evidence, or guarantee. Weave this objection-handling naturally into the description so it does not feel defensive, and end with a confidence-building statement and call to action.

7. Create a luxury or premium-positioned product description for [YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE] that justifies a higher price point to [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE]. Use elevated language that conveys craftsmanship, exclusivity, or exceptional quality without being pretentious. Focus on the experience of ownership or use rather than just the product itself, include subtle social proof elements, and write a call to action that feels aspirational rather than pressured.

8. Write a product description for [YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE] that uses storytelling to make an emotional connection with [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE]. Open with a relatable scenario or mini-story that reflects the customer's situation before discovering the product, transition into how the product solves the problem, and close with a vision of the customer's life improved. Keep the total length under 200 words but make every word count.

9. Generate five alternative headlines for the product description of [YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE] that could be A/B tested on a product page or in an email. Each headline should take a different angle: benefit-focused, curiosity-driven, social proof-based, problem-solution, and direct statement. For each headline, write one sentence explaining why it would work for [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE] and what emotion or motivation it taps into.

10. Write a product description bundle for three complementary products or services in [YOUR NICHE] that [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] could sell together as a package deal. Each individual description should be 75 to 100 words, and then write an additional 100-word description for the bundle as a whole that emphasizes the combined value and why buying all three together makes sense for [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE]. Include a suggested bundle name.

11. Create a product description for a digital product or download offered by [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] — such as an ebook, template, course, or toolkit — targeting [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE]. Include what is inside (list format), what the customer will be able to do after using it that they cannot do now, who it is specifically for and who it is not for, the format and how it is delivered, and a call to action that emphasizes the immediate value of a digital purchase.

12. Write a product description for [YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE] specifically designed for use on Amazon or another marketplace where the business is competing with many similar listings. Include a keyword-rich title format, five bullet points that each lead with a capitalized benefit phrase followed by supporting detail, a 200-word description section, and the key specs in a structured format. Make it stand out in a crowded marketplace.

13. Create a seasonal or limited-edition product description for a special version of [YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE] available only for a limited time or in limited quantities. Include language that creates genuine urgency and scarcity without being manipulative, highlights what makes this version special compared to the regular offering, and gives [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE] a clear and compelling reason to buy now rather than wait.

14. Write a product description for [YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE] targeted at a gift buyer rather than the end user. The language should help someone who is shopping for a gift for [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE] understand why this product makes a thoughtful and impressive gift, what kind of person would love receiving it, and how to present or personalize it. Include a gift-focused call to action such as "Give them the gift of [result]."

15. Generate a comparison-style product description that positions [YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE] against the generic or cheaper alternatives available to [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE]. Without naming competitors, highlight the specific shortcomings of typical options in [YOUR NICHE] and explain why [YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE] is the superior choice. Use factual, specific language rather than vague claims, and frame the higher price or premium positioning as an obvious and worthwhile investment.

## Section 5: Email Writing Prompts (15 Prompts)

Email remains one of the highest-return marketing channels available to small businesses, generating an average of $36 for every $1 spent. These prompts will help you write every type of business email — from welcome sequences and promotional campaigns to follow-ups and reactivation emails — in a fraction of the time it would normally take. Use these to build email assets that work for you around the clock.

1. Write a 30-day email marketing sequence for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] targeting [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE]. The sequence should include 12 emails strategically spaced throughout the month. For each email, provide the day it goes out, the subject line, the goal of the email (welcome, educate, nurture, build trust, handle objections, soft sell, hard sell, close), and a 50-word outline of the body content. Ensure the sequence feels like a natural conversation, not a series of sales pitches.

2. Write a five-email abandoned cart or abandoned inquiry email sequence for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] targeting someone who showed interest in [YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE] but did not complete the purchase or booking. For each email, include the timing after abandonment, the subject line, the body text, and the specific psychological trigger used (curiosity, urgency, social proof, objection handling, final offer). Keep each email short and focused on removing one barrier to purchase.

3. Create a reactivation email campaign for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] targeting subscribers or past customers who have not engaged in three to six months. Write five emails in the sequence, each with a subject line, the goal of the email, and the full body copy. Include one email that explicitly asks if they want to stay on the list (the "stay or go" email), one that offers a compelling comeback incentive, and one final goodbye email that creates genuine urgency to re-engage.

4. Write a promotional email for [YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE] announcing a limited-time sale or special offer for [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE]. Include a compelling subject line with urgency, a preheader text, an opening that immediately communicates the offer, the specific terms of the sale (percentage off, dollar amount, bonus included), social proof or a reason why this offer exists, a clear call to action, a deadline, and a PS line that reinforces the urgency of the offer.

5. Create a five-email new product or service launch sequence for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE]. Email one is a teaser, email two is the announcement, email three highlights key benefits and addresses FAQs, email four shares testimonials or early results, and email five is the final call before an introductory offer expires. For each email, write the subject line, preheader, and full body copy. Include a consistent and exciting brand voice throughout the sequence.

6. Write a weekly email newsletter template for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] that could be reused every week with different content. The template should include a warm greeting and brief personal update, one main piece of value (tip, how-to, industry insight, or story relevant to [YOUR NICHE]), one promotional mention that feels natural rather than forced, one engagement question or CTA to reply or share, and a brief sign-off. Keep the total length under 400 words.

7. Create a referral request email for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] asking happy customers to refer their friends or family to [YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE]. Write three versions: one for email, one for text message, and one for a handwritten note card. Each version should thank the customer for their support, explain the referral program or incentive clearly, make it easy for them to share, and feel genuine rather than transactional. Include specific language for [YOUR NICHE] that builds on the relationship.

8. Write a cold outreach email for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] reaching out to a potential B2B client or partnership opportunity in [YOUR NICHE]. Keep the email under 150 words, open with a genuine personalized observation about the recipient's business, clearly state the value you can provide (not what you sell, but the outcome they gain), include a low-commitment call to action such as a brief 15-minute call, and close with a professional but approachable sign-off. Write three subject line options.

9. Create an email that announces a price increase for [YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE] to existing customers of [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE]. Frame the increase positively by leading with what has improved or expanded in value, give customers adequate notice (recommend how much), offer them a way to lock in the current price before the increase, thank them for their loyalty, and make them feel like insiders getting a special advantage. Keep the tone confident and grateful, not apologetic.

10. Write an email sequence for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] designed to upsell or cross-sell to existing customers. Include three emails: the first introduces the complementary or upgraded offering in the context of the customer's previous purchase, the second shares a specific story or case study of someone who benefited from the upgrade, and the third makes a time-sensitive offer. Write full subject lines and body copy for each and include a clear call to action in every email.

11. Create a "behind the scenes" email for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] that gives [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE] an insider look at how [YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE] is made, sourced, or delivered. The email should build trust, create an emotional connection to the brand, highlight the care and craft that goes into the business, and end with a soft promotional element. Write a subject line that creates curiosity and a tone that feels personal and authentic, as if written directly by the business owner.

12. Write an event invitation email for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] inviting [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE] to a webinar, workshop, in-store event, or virtual Q&A related to [YOUR NICHE]. Include all essential event details clearly, create excitement about what attendees will gain or learn, add a social proof element such as who else is attending or what past attendees have said, include a clear and urgent RSVP call to action with a deadline, and write a follow-up reminder email to send 24 hours before the event.

13. Create a post-purchase email sequence for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] that runs for seven days after a customer buys [YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE]. Include the order confirmation email (day 0), a "getting started" or what to expect email (day 1), a check-in email (day 3) that offers support or tips, a social proof amplification email (day 5) encouraging the customer to share their experience, and a cross-sell or review request email (day 7). Write subject lines and body outlines for each.

14. Write five email subject line formulas for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] that consistently generate high open rates with [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE], along with three example subject lines for each formula. Include formulas based on: curiosity gaps, numbered lists, personal questions, bold statements, and urgency with specificity. Explain in one sentence why each formula works psychologically for [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE] in [YOUR NICHE].

15. Create a re-engagement email for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] targeting subscribers who have not opened an email in 90 or more days. Write three versions testing different angles: a "we miss you" emotional approach, a "here is what you have been missing" value-focused approach, and a blunt "should we break up?" subject line approach. For each version, write the complete email including subject line, preheader, body, and call to action. Include A/B testing notes on how to evaluate which version performs best.

## A Final Note on Using These Prompts

These 150 prompts are a starting point, not a ceiling. The real power of AI-assisted business building lies in the conversation — use these prompts to open a dialogue with ChatGPT, then refine, expand, and redirect based on what comes back. Do not accept the first output as final; instead, ask for revisions, request different tones or angles, and always add your own expertise and personality to the generated content before publishing or sending.

The more specific you make each prompt — replacing every placeholder with real details about your business, your customers, and your goals — the better and more tailored the results will be. Think of ChatGPT as a talented junior team member who needs clear direction to do their best work. The quality of your input directly determines the quality of your output.

Revisit this prompt pack regularly. As your business grows and evolves, these prompts will serve different purposes at different stages. What you need in year one will be very different from what you need in year three — and ChatGPT will grow with you every step of the way.
