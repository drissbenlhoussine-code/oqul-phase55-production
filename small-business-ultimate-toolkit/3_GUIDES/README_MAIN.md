## Quick Start Guide

1. **Start with your finances first.** Open files 01 (Expense Tracker) and 02 (Income Tracker) and enter any transactions from the current month. Getting your money organized is the highest-leverage thing you can do when starting with this toolkit.
2. **Set up your Client Tracker (file 04).** Add every current client and active lead to the CRM spreadsheet. Fill in their status, contact details, and any upcoming follow-up dates so nothing slips through the cracks.
3. **Customize your Invoice Template (file 05).** Open the HTML file in a browser or text editor and replace the placeholder fields with your business name, address, payment terms, and branding. Save a master copy so you're ready to invoice at a moment's notice.
4. **Run your Pricing Calculator (file 07).** Before your next sale or client quote, use the pricing calculator to ensure your rates actually cover your costs, time, and desired profit margin.
5. **Plan your content for the month (file 09).** Fill in your Social Media Content Calendar with post ideas, platform assignments, and publishing dates for the next 30 days. Batch-scheduling your content saves hours each week.
6. **Use the AI Prompt Pack (file 10) and Email Templates (file 11) as daily tools.** Whenever you need to write copy, respond to a client, or brainstorm ideas, pull from these resources to work faster and communicate more professionally.
7. **Complete the Monthly Business Review (file 08) on the last day of every month.** This is the single most important habit in the toolkit — regular review helps you catch problems early, celebrate wins, and set smarter goals for the month ahead.
8. **Track your 90-day goals in the Business Goal Planner (file 12).** At the start of each quarter, define your top goals, break them into weekly actions, and use the KPI tracker to monitor your progress every week.

## How to Use with Google Sheets

1. **Open Google Drive** and navigate to the folder where you've saved your toolkit files.
2. **Drag and drop any CSV file** directly into Google Drive, or use the **New > File Upload** option to upload your CSV files one at a time.
3. **Right-click the uploaded CSV file** in Google Drive and select **Open With > Google Sheets**. Google Sheets will automatically import the CSV and format it as a spreadsheet.
4. **Once open in Google Sheets**, go to **File > Save As Google Sheets** to convert the file to native Google Sheets format — this enables you to use formulas, formatting, and sharing features without restriction.
5. **Customize column widths and freeze the header row** (View > Freeze > 1 Row) to make navigation easier as your data grows.
6. **Share your sheet** with a bookkeeper, business partner, or virtual assistant by clicking the **Share** button in the top right and entering their email address.
7. **Bookmark your key spreadsheets** in your browser for fast daily access — consistency is much easier when your tools are one click away.

## Tips for Best Results

- **Update your trackers daily, not weekly.** It takes 2 minutes a day to log expenses and income — but 2 hours at the end of the month if you let it pile up. Small daily habits compound into complete, accurate records.
- **Create a dedicated "Business Toolkit" folder** in Google Drive and organize files into subfolders matching the included folder structure (Financial Tools, Client Management, Marketing, etc.).
- **Color-code your spreadsheet rows** in Google Sheets using conditional formatting — for example, mark unpaid invoices in red and paid invoices in green for instant visual clarity.
- **Never skip the Monthly Business Review.** This is the tool that turns raw data into insight. Schedule it as a recurring calendar event on the last business day of every month — treat it like a meeting you cannot cancel.
- **Use the AI Prompt Pack proactively.** Don't wait until you're stuck — browse the prompts at the start of each week and identify 2–3 that could accelerate your current projects.
- **Protect your master files.** Before customizing any template, make a duplicate and keep the original blank version. This way you can always reset to a clean copy.
- **Combine the Checklist (BONUS 01) with your calendar.** Copy the daily and weekly task items into your preferred task management app (Notion, Todoist, Google Tasks) and set recurring reminders.
- **Review your Etsy Seller Tracker (BONUS 02) every Monday morning.** Look for listings with high views but low conversion rates — those are prime candidates for better photos, stronger titles, or updated keywords.
- **Use the Business Goal Planner quarterly, not just once.** Every 90 days, archive the previous quarter's planner, start a fresh one, and carry forward any incomplete goals with updated timelines.
- **Treat this toolkit as a living system.** Add columns, modify categories, and adjust templates to fit your specific business model. The files are starting points — customize them to serve you.
