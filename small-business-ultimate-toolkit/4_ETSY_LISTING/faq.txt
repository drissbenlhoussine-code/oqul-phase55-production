Q1: What file formats are included?

A complete list of included files and formats is provided in the Quick-Start Guide.

Q2: Do I need special software to use these files?

Q3: Can I use these templates with Google Sheets?

A: Yes. All spreadsheet files work fully with Google Sheets.
