# Small Business Daily, Weekly & Monthly Checklist
## Your Complete Business Operations Checklist

Running a successful small business requires consistent habits and disciplined routines. This checklist is designed to help you stay on top of every aspect of your business — from daily client communication and financial tracking to monthly strategic reviews and growth planning. Use it as your go-to operations guide to ensure nothing falls through the cracks, no matter how busy your schedule gets.

---

## DAILY TASKS

- [ ] Check and respond to all business emails within 24 hours
- [ ] Review and update your task list for the day
- [ ] Post or schedule at least one piece of social media content
- [ ] Follow up on any outstanding client inquiries or messages
- [ ] Record all business expenses incurred today in your expense tracker
- [ ] Record any payments received in your income tracker
- [ ] Review your calendar and confirm upcoming appointments
- [ ] Spend 30 minutes on the most important revenue-generating task
- [ ] Check in on any active client projects or deliverables
- [ ] Back up any important files or project work completed today

---

## WEEKLY TASKS

- [ ] Review your weekly revenue against your monthly income goal
- [ ] Send invoices for any completed work or outstanding balances
- [ ] Follow up on any unpaid invoices that are past due
- [ ] Review and respond to all customer reviews or feedback received this week
- [ ] Plan and batch-create social media content for the upcoming week
- [ ] Audit your to-do list and carry forward or reschedule incomplete tasks
- [ ] Check inventory levels and reorder any supplies running low
- [ ] Dedicate at least 1 hour to a skill-building or business education activity
- [ ] Review weekly website analytics or shop stats (views, clicks, conversions)
- [ ] Connect with at least one potential client, referral partner, or peer in your industry

---

## MONTHLY TASKS

- [ ] Reconcile all income and expenses against your bank and payment processor statements
- [ ] Calculate your total monthly revenue, expenses, and net profit
- [ ] Complete your Monthly Business Review worksheet to reflect on wins and challenges
- [ ] Set 3–5 specific, measurable goals for the coming month
- [ ] Review your pricing on key products or services and adjust if needed
- [ ] Audit your top-performing and lowest-performing offerings
- [ ] Update your client tracker with new leads, status changes, and follow-up notes
- [ ] Review your social media analytics and identify what content performed best
- [ ] Evaluate any tools, subscriptions, or software you're paying for — cancel unused ones
- [ ] Schedule time for a full business review and planning session for the next month

---

## FINANCE & ACCOUNTING TASKS

- [ ] Record every business expense in the expense tracker on the day it occurs
- [ ] Categorize all expenses accurately (marketing, supplies, software, travel, etc.)
- [ ] Note the tax deductibility status of each expense as you log it
- [ ] Issue invoices promptly — within 24 hours of completing work
- [ ] Follow up on overdue invoices at 7 days, 14 days, and 30 days past due
- [ ] Separate all business income from personal funds — keep accounts distinct
- [ ] Set aside your estimated tax percentage from every payment you receive
- [ ] Review your profit margin monthly and identify areas to cut costs or increase revenue
- [ ] Save all receipts digitally (photo or PDF) and link them to your expense entries
- [ ] Prepare a summary of income, expenses, and profit at the end of every quarter

---

## MARKETING TASKS

- [ ] Post consistently on your chosen primary social media platform (daily or per your schedule)
- [ ] Engage with comments, DMs, and mentions on all social platforms within 24 hours
- [ ] Send your email newsletter or marketing email at least once per month (ideally weekly)
- [ ] Update at least one product listing, portfolio piece, or service page each week
- [ ] Research 3–5 new keywords or hashtags relevant to your niche and test them this week
- [ ] Monitor competitor activity — note new offers, pricing changes, or promotions
- [ ] Reach out to at least one past client this month to reconnect or request a referral
- [ ] Create or update one piece of evergreen content (blog post, pin, reel, or guide)
- [ ] Run or review the performance of any active paid ads or promoted listings
- [ ] Track which marketing channels are driving the most traffic and sales this month

---

## CUSTOMER SERVICE TASKS

- [ ] Respond to all customer questions and messages within 24 hours (ideally within 4 hours)
- [ ] Send order confirmations and delivery updates promptly for every transaction
- [ ] Follow up with customers 3–7 days after delivery to confirm satisfaction
- [ ] Address any complaints or issues professionally and resolve them within 48 hours
- [ ] Request reviews or testimonials from happy customers after successful projects
- [ ] Keep a record of all customer feedback — positive and negative — to identify patterns
- [ ] Maintain a FAQ document and update it whenever new questions arise repeatedly
- [ ] Personalize your customer communication — use names and reference their specific order
- [ ] Offer a loyalty incentive or thank-you discount to repeat customers quarterly
- [ ] Review your return, refund, and dispute policy monthly to ensure it remains fair and clear

---

## BUSINESS GROWTH TASKS

- [ ] Identify one new income stream or product idea to research or develop this month
- [ ] Reach out to at least two potential collaboration partners, affiliates, or referral sources
- [ ] Invest in one professional development resource this month (course, book, workshop, or coaching)
- [ ] Review your 90-day business goals and assess your progress against each milestone
- [ ] Identify your single biggest business bottleneck and take one concrete action to resolve it
- [ ] Ask at least one satisfied client for a written testimonial or case study this month
- [ ] Explore one new platform, marketplace, or sales channel relevant to your business
- [ ] Block dedicated time in your calendar each week for strategic thinking — not just execution

---

*Consistency is the foundation of every thriving small business. You don't have to do everything perfectly — you just have to keep showing up, keep tracking, and keep improving. One completed checklist at a time, you are building something remarkable. Celebrate every win, learn from every challenge, and trust the process.*
