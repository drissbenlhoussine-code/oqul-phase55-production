# Review & Referral System

## REVIEW REQUEST

**Same day text:**
"Hi [Name]! Just wanted to make sure you're happy with today's clean. 😊 If so, a quick Google review would mean so much to us — it helps other families in [City] find a service they can trust. [Google Link] Thank you!"

**Email (Day 2):**
Subject: How was your clean, [Name]?

Hi [Name],

We hope you walked into a home you love!

If your experience was positive, we'd be incredibly grateful if you'd take 60 seconds to share a Google review. It's the most powerful way to help us grow and help other families find reliable cleaning.

[Leave a Review →]

And if there's anything that wasn't quite right — please tell us. We'll make it right.

Thank you,
[Company] Team

## REFERRAL PROGRAM

**Client Script:**
"[Name], we love having you as a client. If you know anyone who's looking for a reliable cleaning service, we'd love the referral. Our program: for every friend you send who books with us, you get $[X] off your next clean. They get [offer] too. Everyone wins! 🏠"

**Referral Email:**
Subject: Give your friends a gift (and earn a reward)

Hi [Name],

You've been such an amazing client — thank you for trusting us in your home.

We're growing our schedule and would love to meet more people like you.

Here's the deal: refer a friend who books a clean, and you both get rewarded:
→ You: $[X] credit on your next service
→ Your friend: [X]% off their first clean

Just have them mention your name when they book.

Thank you for being part of the [Company] family! 💚
