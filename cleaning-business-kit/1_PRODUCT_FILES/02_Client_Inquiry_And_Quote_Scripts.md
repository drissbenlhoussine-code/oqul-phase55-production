## OBJECTION HANDLING

"That seems expensive":
"I understand. Let me explain what's included in that price — [detail]. A lot of clients find that when they break it down by the hours saved and the consistency they get, it's actually really good value. Can I offer you a first-time trial at [discount] so you can experience the difference?"

"I've had bad experiences with cleaners before":
"I hear that a lot, and it's exactly why we do things differently. Every cleaner we send is background-checked, trained on our specific systems, and we do quality checks. If you're ever not 100% satisfied, we come back and re-clean that area free of charge."
