# Cleaning Business Systems Guide

## BUILDING A SCALABLE CLEANING BUSINESS

### Operations Checklist Per Job
□ Confirm 24 hours before via text
□ Arrive within agreed time window
□ Complete checklist for that service type
□ Photo documentation (entry, key areas, completed)
□ Final walkthrough
□ Send post-service message within 30 minutes
□ Request review within 2 hours
□ Log for rebooking follow-up

### Staff Training System
Week 1: Products, safety, equipment
Week 2: Standard clean procedure
Week 3: Deep clean procedure
Week 4: Customer interaction, professionalism

Monthly: Product updates, technique refinement, team feedback

### Pricing Structure
- Base rate: [square footage formula or per-room pricing]
- Deep clean premium: 1.5x to 2x standard rate
- Move-in/out: flat rate based on home size
- Airbnb turnover: package rate with guaranteed turnaround time
- Add-ons: inside oven, inside fridge, windows, garage

### KPIs to Track Monthly
- Revenue per cleaner per day
- Client retention rate (target: 80%+)
- Average jobs per client per year
- Referral rate (% of new clients from referrals)
- Review count and average rating
- Rebook rate at time of service
