# Cleaning Business Local Marketing Playbook

## GROWING IN YOUR COMMUNITY

### Google My Business
- Upload before/after photos (with client permission)
- Post 2–3x per week: promotions, tips, team features
- Request reviews after every single clean
- Respond to every review within 24 hours
- Optimize for keywords: "[city] house cleaning," "[city] maid service"

### Nextdoor Strategy
- Respond to every cleaning-related question in your service area
- Offer a "neighborhood discount" — exclusive rate for Nextdoor users
- Post before/after with neighbor permission

### Door Hanger Campaigns
After every job:
"We just cleaned the home at [nearby address]. If you'd like a quote for your home, we're offering [offer] this week. [Phone / Website]"

Target: 30 surrounding homes per job completed

### Local Business Partnerships
- Real estate agents: pre-listing and post-closing cleans
- Airbnb hosts: turnover cleaning contracts
- Property managers: regular maintenance contracts
- Moving companies: bundle move-out cleans
- Interior designers: post-renovation cleans

### Referral Network Among Clients
- Provide referral cards at every clean
- Text referral incentive reminder every 60 days
- Run quarterly "refer a neighbor" campaigns
