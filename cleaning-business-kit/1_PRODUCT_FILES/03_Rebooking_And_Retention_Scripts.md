# Rebooking & Retention Scripts

## POST-SERVICE REBOOKING

**Immediate (at end of clean):**
"[Name], we're all done! Everything has been [details]. We'd love to keep you on a regular schedule — would bi-weekly work for you, or would you prefer monthly? I can lock in your next appointment right now."

**Text (same day):**
"Hi [Name]! Cleaning complete at [Address]. Hope you love it! 😊 If you'd like to schedule your next visit, I can set you up for [frequency] — just reply and I'll lock you in."

## LAPSED CLIENT REACTIVATION

**30 days:**
"Hi [Name], it's been about a month since we last cleaned your home. If you're ready for another visit, we have [availability]. Same great team — just say the word!"

**60 days:**
"Hi [Name], [Name] here from [Company]. We miss cleaning for you! If life got busy and it slipped, no worries — let's get you back on schedule. We have a special returning client offer this month: [offer]. Interested?"

**90+ days:**
Subject: We want to earn your business back, [Name]

Hi [Name],

It's been a while since we last cleaned your home, and I wanted to reach out personally.

If there was something that didn't meet your expectations — I'd genuinely like to know so we can make it right.

And if you're simply ready for a fresh start, we're offering returning clients [offer] this month.

Either way, we'd love to hear from you.

[Your Name], [Company]
