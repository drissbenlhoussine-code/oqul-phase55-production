# Review Request & Referral System

## POST-SERVICE REVIEW REQUEST

**At Checkout (in person):**
"[Name], thank you so much for coming in today! I'm so glad you love it. If you get a moment, could you leave us a quick Google review? It really helps us out and only takes a minute. I can even text you the link right now if you'd like!"

**Text (within 2 hours of appointment):**
"Hi [Name]! So glad you're loving your [service] today! 💛 If you have a moment, a quick Google review would mean the world to us. Here's the link: [Google Review Link] Thank you! — [Stylist/Salon Name]"

**Email (next morning):**
Subject: How do you feel about your new look?

Hi [Name],

It was so lovely having you in yesterday! We hope you're still loving it today.

If you're happy with your experience, we'd be incredibly grateful for a quick Google review. Even just one sentence helps other clients find us.

[Leave a Review →]

Thank you so much for trusting us with your hair!

Love,
[Salon Name] Team

## REFERRAL PROGRAM

**Client Referral Template:**
"[Name], you've been such an amazing client and we love having you here. We're growing and would love to meet more people like you! Our referral program: for every friend you send our way who books an appointment, you get [reward — e.g., $15 off your next service]. Just have them mention your name when they book. 💛"

**Referral Card (print):**
Front: "Your friend [Client Name] thinks you deserve great hair. So do we."
Back: "[Salon Name] — [Service] for new clients · [Phone] · [Address] — Mention this card for [offer]"
