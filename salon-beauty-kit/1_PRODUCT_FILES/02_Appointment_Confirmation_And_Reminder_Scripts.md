# Appointment Confirmation & Reminder Scripts

## BOOKING CONFIRMATION

**Immediate Text After Booking:**
"Hi [Name]! Your appointment is confirmed at [Salon Name] on [Day], [Date] at [Time] with [Stylist]. We can't wait to see you! 💛 Questions? Text or call [Phone]."

**Email Confirmation:**
Subject: Appointment Confirmed — [Salon Name] — [Date] at [Time]

Hi [Name],

Your appointment is confirmed! Here are the details:

📅 Date: [Day], [Date]
⏰ Time: [Time]
💇 Service: [Service]
👩 Stylist: [Stylist Name]
📍 Location: [Address]

What to know:
• Arrive 5 minutes early if this is your first visit
• If you need to cancel or reschedule, please give us 24 hours notice
• Parking is available [details]

Can't wait to see you!
[Salon Name] Team

## APPOINTMENT REMINDERS

**48-Hour Reminder:**
"Hi [Name]! Just a reminder that your appointment at [Salon Name] is [Day] at [Time] with [Stylist]. Reply YES to confirm or call [Phone] if you need to reschedule. See you soon! ✂️"

**24-Hour Reminder:**
"See you tomorrow, [Name]! [Time] at [Salon Name] with [Stylist]. If you have any inspiration photos to share, feel free to text them to this number before your visit. We love to prep! 💇‍♀️"

**Day-Of Reminder:**
"Good morning [Name]! Your appointment is today at [Time]. We're excited to see you! Address: [Address]. Text us if you're running a few minutes behind."

## NO-SHOW RECOVERY

**Same-Day:**
"Hi [Name], we noticed you missed your appointment today at [Time]. We'd love to get you rescheduled — we have [X] openings this week. Want to grab one? Call or text [Phone]."

**Next Day:**
"Hi [Name], [Stylist] wanted to reach out after missing you yesterday. Life gets busy — we completely understand. We'd love to get you in. DM or call whenever you're ready. 💛"
