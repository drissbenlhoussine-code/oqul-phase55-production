# Salon Business Systems Guide

## RUNNING A FULLY BOOKED SALON

### Booking System Essentials
- Online booking enabled 24/7
- Automated confirmation immediately upon booking
- 48-hour reminder
- 24-hour reminder
- Same-day reminder
- Post-service follow-up (within 2 hours)

### Client Retention Tracking
- Flag clients who haven't booked in 60 days → send reactivation text
- Flag clients at 90 days → personal text from stylist
- Flag clients at 120+ days → win-back offer
- Track retention rate monthly: aim for 70%+ returning clients

### Revenue Per Client (RPC) Growth
- Upsell treatments during consultations
- Recommend retail products at checkout
- Introduce package deals (e.g., 3 color services at reduced rate)
- Offer memberships for frequent service clients

### Staff & Culture
- Weekly team huddle (15 min): bookings, goals, wins
- Monthly training: technique OR business skill
- Recognize stylist milestones publicly
- Review retention and revenue by stylist monthly
- Encourage stylists to build their own social following

### Financial KPIs to Track Monthly
- Revenue per stylist per day
- Average ticket size
- Retail sales as % of revenue
- New client acquisition rate
- Client retention rate
- Rebook rate at checkout
