# Client Reactivation Scripts

## 3-MONTH LAPSE REACTIVATION

**Text:**
"Hi [Name]! It's been a little while since we've seen you at [Salon Name]. We miss you! 💛 We have some great availability coming up — would you like to book a slot? Your color/cut is probably due for a refresh. [Link or Phone]"

**Email:**
Subject: We've been thinking about you, [Name] 💛

Hi [Name],

It's been a little while since your last visit and we just wanted to check in!

We know life gets busy, and sometimes [Salon Name] gets pushed down the list. But your hair has been waiting patiently. 😊

We have availability this week and next. Book online [Link] or reply to this email and we'll sort you out.

As a thank you for coming back: [offer — e.g., 10% off your next service / complimentary conditioning treatment].

See you soon!
[Stylist/Salon Name]
