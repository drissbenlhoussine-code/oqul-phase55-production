**February — Valentine's Day**
"Valentine's Day is [X] days away. Whether you're treating yourself or surprising someone special — give the gift of a fresh look. Gift cards available. Book your February appointment now."

**March/April — Spring Reset**
"Spring is here. Time to shake off winter and refresh your look. [Salon Name] spring promotion: [offer]. Available through [Date]. Limited slots — book early."

**Summer — Pre-Summer Glow Up**
"Summer is almost here. Time to book your pre-season appointment: fresh cut, color refresh, or a treatment to protect your hair from sun and salt. June books fast — grab your slot."

**September — Fall Color Season**
"Fall color season is officially open at [Salon Name]. Brunettes, coppers, chocolates, and warm balayage — autumn is your moment. Booking for September now. Limited openings."

**November/December — Holiday Glam**
"Holiday season is the most booked time of year. Don't get left without an appointment. December slots are open now — get your spot before they're gone. ✨"
