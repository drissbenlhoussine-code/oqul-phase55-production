# Salon Local Marketing Playbook

## YOUR LOCAL GROWTH STRATEGY

### Google My Business
- Complete profile with all services listed
- Upload 30+ photos (salon, results, team)
- Post 3x per week (new looks, promotions, tips)
- Request Google reviews after every appointment
- Respond to every review within 24 hours

### Yelp Optimization
- Claim and verify your listing
- Add before/after photos with descriptions
- Enable Yelp Deals for slow periods
- Respond to every review

### Instagram Strategy
- Transformation photos with permission
- Tag location in every post
- Collaborate with local lifestyle accounts
- Use location hashtags consistently
- Stories daily — salon life, tips, polls

### Community Partnerships
- Partner with local bridal boutiques for wedding referrals
- Connect with hotel concierge teams (visitors seeking salons)
- Collaborate with local photographers (styled shoots)
- Network with other non-competing beauty professionals (lash, nail, brow)

### Neighborhood Outreach
- Host a "Neighbors Night" — discount evening for people who live within 1 mile
- Sponsor local school or sports events
- Participate in local market or pop-up events
- Offer corporate package for local offices (on-site styling days)
