# Events, Private Dining & Community Marketing Templates

## How to Use These Templates

Events are one of the most underused revenue levers in the restaurant industry. A well-promoted live music night can turn a slow Tuesday into a full house. A Valentine's prix fixe can generate more revenue in one evening than a typical weekend. A corporate catering relationship, once established, can bring in consistent monthly income with almost no ongoing marketing spend. These templates give you ready-to-use copy for every type of event and community touchpoint — from a casual trivia night announcement to a full private dining sales email — so you can promote more, more consistently, without spending hours writing from scratch.

Use these templates across multiple channels simultaneously for maximum impact. When you announce a wine tasting event, for example, send the email version to your list, post the social caption, and text your most engaged regulars. Stack the channels, keep the message consistent, and give yourself at least two to three weeks of lead time for ticketed or reservation-required events. For community marketing, think long-term relationship over short-term transaction — a thoughtful outreach email to a local business or food blogger is the start of a relationship that can generate guests for years. Customize every bracketed field, adjust the voice to match your restaurant's personality, and use these as your foundation.
