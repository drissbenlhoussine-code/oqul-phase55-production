# Mortgage Broker Local Marketing Playbook

## BUILDING YOUR REFERRAL NETWORK

### Realtor Relationship Strategy (Priority #1)
- Identify top 20 producing agents in your market
- Send personalized introduction emails
- Attend real estate office meetings (bring value — market updates, buyer tips)
- Host quarterly lunch-and-learns: "Mortgage Updates for Agents"
- Provide weekly rate sheets with your branding
- Close on time, every time — this is the ultimate referral generator

### Financial Advisor Network
- Connect with fee-only financial advisors
- Position as a resource for their homebuying/refinance clients
- Host joint webinars: "Homebuying as Part of Your Financial Plan"

### CPA & Accountant Network
- CPA clients often need mortgages for investment properties and self-employed situations
- Offer free self-employed buyer consultations as a referral gift
- Share tax-related homeownership content on social

### Community Presence
- Sponsor first-time homebuyer seminars
- Partner with credit unions and community banks
- Volunteer at financial literacy events

### Digital Strategy
- Weekly rate update email to past clients and sphere
- LinkedIn for professional connections
- Facebook/Instagram for consumer-facing content
- Google My Business with consistent review strategy
