# Mortgage Business Systems Guide

## THE REFERRAL-DRIVEN MORTGAGE BUSINESS

### Daily Habits
- Review pipeline: all active files and next action needed
- Send 3 personal outreach messages (past clients, referral partners, sphere)
- Post on social media (education, rates, market update)
- Follow up on all leads within 24 hours

### Pipeline Management
- Use a CRM to track every lead with next step and follow-up date
- Stage 1: New Lead → contact attempt within 1 hour
- Stage 2: Contacted → schedule pre-approval call
- Stage 3: Application → document collection in progress
- Stage 4: Processing → regular client updates every 3 days
- Stage 5: Closing → coordinate with title, agent, and client
- Stage 6: Closed → post-close follow-up system activated

### Referral System
- Send thank you note + gift within 24 hours of closing for every referral
- Check in with referral partners monthly (email, call, or in-person)
- Share their content and tag them on social media
- Create a "referral tier" system: track who sends most business, prioritize those relationships

### Revenue & KPIs
- Units closed per month
- Average loan size
- Revenue per unit
- Lead-to-close conversion rate
- Days from application to close
- Referral partner count and growth
