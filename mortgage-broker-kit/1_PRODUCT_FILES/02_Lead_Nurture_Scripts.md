# Lead Nurture Scripts

## INITIAL INQUIRY RESPONSE (within 1 hour)
"Hi [Name]! Thanks for reaching out. I'm [Name] with [Company]. I'd love to help you explore your options. A few quick questions to get started: Are you looking to purchase or refinance? Do you have a purchase price or loan amount in mind? And have you been pre-approved before or is this your first time going through the process? Happy to jump on a quick call anytime today. — [Your Name]"

## PRE-APPROVAL CONVERSATION SCRIPT
"Great, so let's talk about the pre-approval process. It's simpler than most people expect. I'll need to pull your credit (a soft pull first), review some basic income and asset documentation, and I can typically have a pre-approval letter ready within 24–48 hours. Does that timeline work for you?"

## 30-DAY NURTURE SEQUENCE (for leads not ready yet)

**Week 1 Email:**
Subject: [Name] — your home buying timeline
Hi [Name], great speaking with you! As promised, here's a simple breakdown of the steps between where you are now and closing day: [attach/embed timeline]. The first step — pre-approval — can happen anytime. Let me know when you're ready to start.

**Week 2 Email:**
Subject: Quick mortgage tip for [Name]
Hi [Name], just a helpful tip this week: paying down revolving credit balances below 30% utilization can boost your credit score meaningfully — and a higher score means a lower rate. Worth a look before we start the process. Let me know if you have questions!

**Week 3 Email:**
Subject: Rates this week — [Name]
Hi [Name], a quick rate check-in: [current rates] for a 30-year fixed. At [loan amount], that's approximately $[monthly payment]/month. Just so you have a number to think about as you plan. Ready to get your official number? I'm here.

**Week 4 Email:**
Subject: Are you ready to start, [Name]?
Hi [Name], it's been about a month since we connected. I want to check in — are you getting closer to being ready? No pressure at all. Whenever the timing is right, I'm here to make the process smooth. Your pre-approval is valid for 90 days and takes less than an hour to start.
