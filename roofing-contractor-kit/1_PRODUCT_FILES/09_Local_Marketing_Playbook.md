# Roofing Local Marketing Playbook

## DOMINATING YOUR SERVICE AREA

### Google My Business (Priority #1)
- Complete 100% of your GMB profile
- Add all services with detailed descriptions
- Upload 20+ high-quality job photos
- Post 2x per week (promotions, completed jobs, tips)
- Respond to every review within 24 hours
- Target: 50+ Google reviews with 4.8+ average

### Nextdoor Strategy
- Create a business profile
- Respond to every roofing question in your service area
- Post before/after photos with homeowner permission
- Run targeted neighborhood promotions after storms

### Direct Mail Campaigns
**Storm Aftermath:** Mail to affected zip codes within 48 hours
**Seasonal:** Spring/Fall postcard campaigns
**Neighborhood Completion:** When you finish a job, mail to 50 surrounding homes

Postcard template:
"Hi neighbor! We just replaced the roof at [Address nearby]. If your roof is due for an inspection, we'd love to give you a free assessment as a courtesy to the neighborhood. [Phone] · [Website]"

### Door Hanger System
After every job completion, door hang 20 homes in each direction:
"We just finished a roof at [X] [Street]. Free inspection for neighbors."

### Referral Partner Network
- Real estate agents (pre-listing inspections)
- Insurance agents (claim referrals)
- Property managers (multi-unit buildings)
- Home inspectors (they find issues they can't fix)
- General contractors (they need roofers)
