# Lead Inquiry & Estimate Follow-Up Scripts

## INITIAL PHONE INQUIRY

**When a lead calls:**
"Thank you for calling [Company Name], this is [Name]. How can I help you today?"

[After they explain:]
"I completely understand. Let me get a few details so I can make sure we send the right person out. Can I get your name and address? And is this storm damage, a leak, or are you looking for an assessment or full replacement?"

[Schedule the appointment:]
"Great. We have [Day] at [Time] or [Day] at [Time] available for a free roof inspection. Which works better for you? And the best number to confirm is this one?"

**Lead Qualification Questions:**
1. "Is this for your primary residence or a rental/investment property?"
2. "Are you working with insurance or out of pocket?"
3. "Have you had any other estimates?"
4. "What's your timeline — is this urgent or are you planning ahead?"

## TEXT LEAD RESPONSE (within 5 minutes)

"Hi [Name]! This is [Your Name] from [Company]. Thanks for reaching out! We'd love to help. Can I ask — is this related to recent storm damage, an active leak, or a general inspection? I want to make sure I send the right team member. 🏠"

## ESTIMATE FOLLOW-UP SEQUENCE

**Follow-Up 1 — Same Day as Estimate (2 hours after)**
"Hi [Name], this is [Name] from [Company]. Just checking in after today's estimate. Do you have any questions about what we covered? I want to make sure you have everything you need to make the best decision."

**Follow-Up 2 — Day 2**
Text: "Hi [Name], following up on your roof estimate. We're scheduling jobs for [timeframe] and wanted to check if you had any questions or needed clarification on anything. Happy to hop on a quick call."

**Follow-Up 3 — Day 5**
"Hi [Name], [Name] here from [Company]. I know roofing decisions take time. I'm happy to answer any questions or concerns — especially about materials, warranty, or timeline. No pressure at all. Just want you to feel confident in your choice."

**Follow-Up 4 — Day 10**
"Hi [Name], last follow-up from [Company]. Your estimate is valid until [date]. If you'd like to proceed or have any final questions, I'm here. If the timing isn't right, totally understand — we're here whenever you need us."

## INSURANCE CLAIM ASSISTANCE SCRIPT

"Mr./Ms. [Name], I want to make sure you get the maximum benefit from your insurance claim. Here's what typically happens: your insurance adjuster will schedule an inspection. I'd recommend we be present for that — we've done this hundreds of times and we know what they look for. Would it be okay if we coordinate with you on that timing?"
