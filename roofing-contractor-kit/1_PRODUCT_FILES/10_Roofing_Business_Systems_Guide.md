# Roofing Business Systems Guide

## OPERATIONS & SCALING

### Job Flow System
1. **Lead In** → CRM entry within 1 hour
2. **Inspection Scheduled** → Confirmation sent, reminder day before
3. **Estimate Delivered** → Same day or next morning
4. **Follow-Up** → Days 1, 3, 7, 14 (see scripts)
5. **Job Booked** → Materials ordered, crew scheduled
6. **Day Before** → Homeowner reminder, material delivery confirmed
7. **Job Day** → Crew check-in, progress photos
8. **Completion** → Walkthrough, cleanup check, invoice
9. **Review Request** → Within 2 hours of completion
10. **30-Day Check-In** → Satisfaction call, referral ask

### Crew Management
- Daily safety briefing (5 minutes)
- Photo documentation at start and end of every job
- Cleanup checklist signed off by crew lead
- Quality control inspection before crew leaves site

### Financial KPIs to Track
- Revenue per job (average)
- Gross margin by job type
- Lead cost per source
- Close rate by salesperson
- Revenue per crew per day
- Material cost as % of revenue

### Scaling Checklist
□ CRM system in place
□ Standard estimate template
□ Crew training documented
□ Insurance and licenses current
□ Safety program written and practiced
□ Subcontractor agreements signed
□ Review generation system active
□ Referral program live
