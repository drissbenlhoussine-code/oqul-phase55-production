# Lead Generation Scripts for Real Estate Agents
## 25 Done-for-You DM, Email, and Follow-Up Scripts

---

### HOW TO USE THESE SCRIPTS

These scripts are starting points — not scripts to read word-for-word. Personalize with the prospect's name, their specific neighborhood, or a detail you know about them. The goal of every script is to get a conversation started, not to close a deal in one message.

**The Golden Rule:** Lead with value. Ask before pitching. Listen more than you talk.

---

## SECTION 1: SOCIAL MEDIA DM SCRIPTS (7 Scripts)

---

### DM SCRIPT 01 — New Follower Welcome (After They Follow You)

**Platform:** Instagram, Facebook  
**When to use:** Within 24 hours of someone new following your account

---

Hey [First Name] — thanks for following! I share [market updates / buying tips / local neighborhood content] a few times a week. Let me know if you ever have questions about the [City] market — I'm always happy to help. 😊

---

**Follow-up if they respond:** "Are you currently thinking about buying, selling, or just keeping an eye on the market?"

---

### DM SCRIPT 02 — Post Engagement Follow-Up (They Liked or Commented)

**Platform:** Instagram, Facebook  
**When to use:** After someone engages with your content

---

Hey [First Name]! Saw you [liked / commented on] my post about [topic] — glad it was helpful! Are you thinking about [buying / selling] in [area] sometime soon, or just exploring options?

---

**Note:** Don't DM everyone who likes — use this for people who seem genuinely interested (multiple engagements, comments with questions, etc.)

---

### DM SCRIPT 03 — Story Poll Responder Follow-Up

**Platform:** Instagram  
**When to use:** After running a "Buying or Renting?" or "How soon are you moving?" poll

---

Hey [First Name]! You voted [response] on my poll — I love that you're thinking about it! Are you in the early research stage or do you have a timeline in mind? Happy to share what I'm seeing in the [City] market right now.

---

### DM SCRIPT 04 — Cold Outreach — Local Community Member

**Platform:** Instagram, Nextdoor  
**When to use:** Reaching out to people in local groups or community accounts

---

Hey [First Name] — I noticed you're also in [neighborhood / local group]. I'm a local real estate agent and I share market updates for [area] on my page. No pitch — just wanted to connect with neighbors! Feel free to reach out if you ever have questions about property values or the market. 🏡

---

### DM SCRIPT 05 — Referral Reconnect (Past Client's Friend/Family)

**Platform:** Text or DM  
**When to use:** When a past client mentions someone who might be moving

---

Hey [First Name] — [Referral Name] mentioned you might be thinking about [buying / selling / relocating]. I helped [him/her/them] with [their home / finding their place] in [year or area] and they thought we'd be a good fit to chat. No pressure at all — happy to answer questions or just give you a sense of the market. Would a quick 10-minute call be helpful?

---

### DM SCRIPT 06 — FSBO (For Sale By Owner) Outreach

**Platform:** Email, DM, or door knock follow-up  
**When to use:** Approaching owners selling without an agent

---

Hi [Name] — I noticed your home at [address] is listed for sale by owner. I specialize in [neighborhood] and wanted to reach out — not to list your home, just to offer a free market analysis so you can make sure you're priced right. Many FSBO sellers are leaving money on the table or dealing with legal exposure they don't know about. I'd love to share what similar homes in your area actually sold for recently. Would a 15-minute conversation be worth it?

---

**Follow-up if no response (5 days later):**

Hi [Name] — just following up on my note from [date]. I sold [X homes / a home on your street] recently and have buyers actively looking in your area. Would it make sense to connect for 10 minutes?

---

### DM SCRIPT 07 — Expired Listing Outreach

**Platform:** Phone, email, or direct mail  
**When to use:** When a listing expires without selling

---

Hi [Name] — I noticed your home at [address] recently came off the market. That can be frustrating, especially after everything that goes into preparing a home for sale. I've helped [X] sellers in [neighborhood] successfully sell after their listing expired, usually by making a few key changes to pricing strategy and marketing. Would you be open to a no-obligation conversation to talk through what might have happened and what options look like now?

## SECTION 3: PHONE SCRIPTS (5 Scripts)

---

### PHONE SCRIPT 01 — Cold Call Opening (General)

---

**Opener:**  
"Hi, is this [Name]? Great — this is [Your Name] with [Brokerage]. I'm a local agent specializing in [neighborhood/area]. I know you weren't expecting my call, so I'll be brief — [reason for calling in one sentence]. Is now an okay time for 2 minutes?"

**If yes:** Go into your value prop or question.  
**If no:** "No problem — when would be a better time to reach you? I'll make it quick." Confirm time and call back exactly then.  
**If they ask how you got their number:** "I work primarily in [area] and do research on homeowners in the market. I apologize if the call feels unexpected — I'll get right to the point."

---

### PHONE SCRIPT 02 — FSBO Cold Call

---

"Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] calling about the home you have listed for sale on [street/address]. I'm not calling to ask you to list with me — I know you've probably already had a dozen agents call with that pitch. I'm actually calling because I have [X] pre-qualified buyers who are specifically looking in [neighborhood] and I wanted to see if there might be a fit. Would it be okay if I asked you a couple of questions about the home?"

---

### PHONE SCRIPT 03 — Expired Listing Call
