# Open House Templates for Real Estate Agents
## Pre-Event, Day-Of, and Follow-Up Systems

---

### HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE

Open houses are lead generation events — not just property showings. Every visitor is a potential buyer, future seller, or referral source. These templates cover everything from pre-event marketing to post-event follow-up so no lead falls through the cracks.

### TEMPLATE 07 — Conversation Starter Checklist (For You, Day Of)

Print this and keep it in your pocket. Use these questions to start genuine conversations with every visitor.

**Opening:**
- [ ] "Welcome! Have you seen this neighborhood before or is this your first time in the area?"
- [ ] "Are you currently working with an agent, or are you still in the early stages?"
- [ ] "What brought you out today — are you actively searching, or more browsing?"

**Discovery:**
- [ ] "What's most important to you in your next home — location, size, condition, or price?"
- [ ] "Have you seen any other homes recently that you liked? What did you like about them?"
- [ ] "What's your ideal timeline for making a move?"
- [ ] "Are you planning to sell a current home first, or would this be in addition to what you have?"

**Qualifying:**
- [ ] "Have you spoken with a lender yet? I can connect you with a great one if not."
- [ ] "Is [neighborhood] your first choice or are you looking in a few different areas?"

**Closing the Conversation:**
- [ ] "Can I follow up with you with comparable sales data and more details on this home?"
- [ ] "If this one isn't quite right, would it be okay if I sent you similar homes that come on the market?"

## SECTION 4: OPEN HOUSE PREPARATION CHECKLIST

---

### PRE-EVENT (1 Week Before)

- [ ] Professional photos taken and edited  
- [ ] Listing entered on MLS with all fields complete  
- [ ] Open house registered on Zillow, Realtor.com, Redfin, and MLS  
- [ ] Social media posts scheduled (4 days of countdown)  
- [ ] Email blast sent to database  
- [ ] Neighbor letters dropped (100–200 homes in a 3-block radius)  
- [ ] Sign placement approved with seller  
- [ ] Printed materials prepared: sign-in sheet, feature sheet, feedback forms  
- [ ] Comparable sales pulled and current  
- [ ] Refreshments purchased (water, light snacks)  

### DAY BEFORE

- [ ] Confirm with seller: home cleaned, decluttered, and staged  
- [ ] Remind seller to secure valuables, pets, and medications  
- [ ] Confirm with seller they'll be OFF the property during open house  
- [ ] Prepare your talking points for the home  
- [ ] Charge phone and tablet  

### DAY OF (2 Hours Before Open)

- [ ] Place directional signs from major intersections  
- [ ] Set up entry table: sign-in sheet, pens, feature sheets  
- [ ] Set up feedback forms at exit  
- [ ] Open all blinds and turn on all lights  
- [ ] Set temperature to comfortable level  
- [ ] Light a candle or diffuse a neutral scent (optional)  
- [ ] Play soft background music  
- [ ] Take a photo of the home for your social media Story  

### DURING THE OPEN HOUSE

- [ ] Greet every visitor at the door  
- [ ] Invite everyone to sign in  
- [ ] Use conversation starters from Template 07  
- [ ] Never follow visitors room-to-room — give them space  
- [ ] Take notes on each visitor's interest level  

### AFTER THE OPEN HOUSE (Within 2 Hours)

- [ ] Remove all directional signs  
- [ ] Secure the property (lock all doors and windows)  
- [ ] Send seller update (Template 14)  
- [ ] Send same-day thank you texts to all sign-ins  
- [ ] Update CRM with visitor notes  

### NEXT DAY

- [ ] Send follow-up emails to all sign-ins  
- [ ] Call anyone who expressed strong interest  
- [ ] Share open house recap on social media ("X people visited — here's what buyers are saying about [neighborhood]")
