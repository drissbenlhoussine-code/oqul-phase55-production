# Listing Announcement Templates for Real Estate Agents
## Just Listed | Under Contract | Just Sold | Price Reduced

---

### HOW TO USE THESE TEMPLATES

Every listing milestone is a marketing opportunity. Use these templates across Instagram, Facebook, email, and text to announce listings, build credibility, and generate new leads from people watching your market activity.

**Replace all [BRACKETS] with your specific details before posting.**

## SECTION 6: QUICK CAPTION VARIATIONS (MIX AND MATCH)

Use these opening lines and closing hooks to mix and match with any of the above templates:

**Opening Lines:**
- "I love this part of the job."
- "Big day for my clients."
- "In [X] years of selling real estate in [area], this one stands out."
- "The market is talking. Here's what it's saying."
- "Three offers. Seven days. Done."
- "Helping people [find their home / move on to their next chapter] never gets old."
- "Swipe to see why buyers are moving fast on this one →"
- "My sellers asked me to keep it quiet. I can't anymore."

**Closing Hooks:**
- "DM me 'BUY' or 'SELL' and let's talk."
- "Thinking about making a move this year? Let's talk."
- "I have [X] buyers actively looking in [area]. Could be your buyer."
- "What would you change about your current home if you could? Start there."
- "Real estate questions? My DMs are always open."
- "Tag someone who's been saying they want to buy this year 👇"
- "Save this if you're thinking about buying/selling in the next 6 months."
- "Follow for weekly [City] market updates."
