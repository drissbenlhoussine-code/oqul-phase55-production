# Client Message Templates for Real Estate Agents
## Buyer, Seller, and Lead Nurture Communications

---

### HOW TO USE THESE TEMPLATES

These templates cover the full client lifecycle — from first contact through closing and beyond. Customize with the client's name, property details, and your personal tone. The more you personalize, the better they perform.
