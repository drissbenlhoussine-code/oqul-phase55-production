# Instagram Stories Templates Guide for Real Estate Agents
## 30 Story Formats + Scripts for Daily Content

---

### WHY STORIES WORK DIFFERENTLY THAN FEED POSTS

Stories are your relationship-building tool. Feed posts build your brand — Stories build trust. Show up in Stories consistently and people feel like they know you. That familiarity is what makes them reach out when they're ready to buy or sell.

Stories disappear in 24 hours, which means:
- You can post 5-10 Stories per day without "spamming" your feed
- The bar for polish is lower — raw and real performs well
- You can ask questions and get direct DM responses easily
- Viewers who engage with Stories regularly are your warmest leads

## SECTION 1: DAILY STORY FRAMEWORKS

---

### FRAMEWORK 01 — "Morning Market Check" (Daily, 2-3 Slides)

**Purpose:** Establish yourself as the local market expert

**Slide 1:** Text on branded background  
*"Good morning [City] 👋 Here's what's new in the real estate market today:"*

**Slide 2:** Specific data or news  
*One of: new listing alert / price reduction / just sold nearby / interest rate note / market stat*

**Slide 3:** Engagement prompt  
*"What are you watching in the [City] market right now? DM me!"*

---

### FRAMEWORK 02 — "What I'm Working On Today" (2-3 Slides)

**Purpose:** Show the reality of your work — builds authenticity

**Slide 1:** Morning setup  
*Photo of your desk, coffee, or MLS screen. Caption: "Another day in [City] real estate. Here's what's on my schedule:"*

**Slide 2:** Activity preview  
*Text: "[X] showings / listing appointment / offer strategy call / open house prep — the full day 🏡"*

**Slide 3:** End-of-day wrap  
*Brief summary of what happened — what you saw at showings, how an appointment went, buyer feedback, etc.*

---

### FRAMEWORK 03 — "Story of the Week" (Weekly, 4-5 Slides)

**Purpose:** Share a client win or lesson in narrative form

**Slide 1:** Hook  
*"Let me tell you about the [home / deal / buyer / seller] situation I had this week."*

**Slide 2:** The setup  
*Context — what was the challenge or goal*

**Slide 3:** The complication  
*What made it difficult — multiple offers, pricing issue, inspection problem, etc.*

**Slide 4:** The resolution  
*How you solved it or what the outcome was*

**Slide 5:** The lesson + CTA  
*"Here's what I want you to take from this: [insight]. DM me if you're in a similar situation."*

---

### FRAMEWORK 04 — "Quick Tip Tuesday" (Weekly, 1-3 Slides)

**Purpose:** Education in bite-size format

**Slide 1:** Headline  
*"QUICK TIP: [Topic in 5 words or less]"*

**Slide 2:** The tip  
*2-3 bullet points or 2-3 short sentences. No more.*

**Slide 3 (optional):** Application  
*"Here's how to apply this: [one action step]. DM me with questions."*

---

### FRAMEWORK 05 — "Behind the Listing" (For New Listings, 4-5 Slides)

**Purpose:** Generate showing interest through storytelling

**Slide 1:** Exterior or front door photo  
*"Behind the listing: [Address] 👀"*

**Slide 2-3:** Best interior shots  
*Caption each with a specific detail — NOT just "beautiful kitchen." Example: "This kitchen was completely renovated in 2023 — new cabinets, quartz countertops, and that unlisted walk-in pantry that made my buyer gasp a little."*

**Slide 4:** One "you have to see it in person" detail  
*Something the photos can't capture — light, space, view, sound, neighborhood feel.*

**Slide 5:** CTA  
*"Showing slots open [Day] — DM me to schedule. Serious inquiries only, please."*

## SECTION 2: ENGAGEMENT STORY TEMPLATES

---

### ENGAGEMENT TEMPLATE 01 — Poll: Rent vs. Buy

**Slide 1:** Poll sticker  
*"Where do you stand?"*  
Option A: Team Buy 🏡  
Option B: Team Rent 🔑

**Slide 2 (next day — reply to poll results):**  
*"X% of you said [result]. Here's my take on why that makes sense in [City] right now:"*  
[2-3 bullet points of honest commentary based on the market]

---

### ENGAGEMENT TEMPLATE 02 — Quiz: Real Estate IQ Test

**Slide 1:** Quiz sticker  
*"Quick real estate quiz! How long does the average home inspection take?"*  
- 30 minutes
- 2-3 hours ✅
- 6+ hours
- Half a day

**Slide 2 (follow-up):**  
*"If you guessed [correct answer] — nice work! Here's what actually happens during a home inspection and why it matters:"*  
[Brief education follow-up]

---

### ENGAGEMENT TEMPLATE 03 — Question Box: "Ask Me Anything"

**Slide 1:**  
*"It's Ask Me Anything day 👇 Real estate questions only — no such thing as a dumb question."*  
[Question box sticker]

**Slide 2-8:** Answer the top questions you receive  
*"Great question from [Name]: [Question]"*  
*[Your honest, direct answer in 2-4 sentences]*

---

### ENGAGEMENT TEMPLATE 04 — Emoji Slider: "How Excited Are You?"

**Slide 1:**  
*"How excited are you about buying a home this year?"*  
[Emoji slider — 😴 to 🔥]

**Slide 2 (next day):**  
*"Average excitement level: [X emoji]. If you're in the 🔥 camp — DM me. Let's make it happen."*

---

### ENGAGEMENT TEMPLATE 05 — Countdown: Open House Hype

**Day 3 before:** Countdown sticker  
*"3 days until the open house at [Address]. Tap to set a reminder."*

**Day 2:** One interior photo  
*"Can you spot what makes this kitchen special? 👀 Open house [Day]."*

**Day 1:** Final reminder  
*"TOMORROW. Open house at [Address] from [Time] to [Time]. No appointment needed. DM me if you have questions before you come."*

**Day of:** Live update  
*"We're live at [Address]! Come see me — I'll be here until [Time]."*

## SECTION 3: EDUCATIONAL STORY FORMATS

---

### EDUCATION TEMPLATE 01 — "Did You Know?" Series (3 Slides)

**Slide 1:**  
*"DID YOU KNOW? 🤔"*  
[Surprising real estate fact — e.g., "In [City], the average home sold for $X more per sq ft in spring than winter over the last 5 years."]

**Slide 2:**  
*"Here's why that matters:"*  
[Brief explanation of the implication]

**Slide 3:**  
*"Question? DM me — I love talking about this stuff."*

---

### EDUCATION TEMPLATE 02 — "Steps" Mini-Tutorial

**Use for:** Explaining the offer process, the listing process, the inspection process, etc.

**Slide 1:** Title  
*"The [buying/selling] process in [City] — simplified. Swipe through 👉"*

**Slides 2-6:** One step per slide  
*Format: Step [#]: [Title]  
[1-2 sentence explanation]*

**Final Slide:**  
*"Questions? Drop them in my DMs. I walk buyers/sellers through this every day."*

---

### EDUCATION TEMPLATE 03 — "Myth vs. Reality"

**Slide 1:** Myth  
*"MYTH: You need 20% down to buy a home."*  
[Red X or FALSE label]

**Slide 2:** Reality  
*"REALITY: Many buyers put down 3.5% (FHA) or 5% (conventional). Some programs go to 0% for qualifying buyers. Down payment is often the smallest barrier."*

**Slide 3:** Follow-up  
*"The real barriers are usually [credit / DTI / market knowledge]. DM me 'DOWN PAYMENT' and I'll share the options in [City]."*

---

### EDUCATION TEMPLATE 04 — Rate/Market Update (Weekly)

**Slide 1:**  
*"Rate check — [Date]"*  
[Today's approximate rate for 30-year fixed]

**Slide 2:**  
*"What that means for a $[X] home with X% down:"*  
Monthly payment estimate

**Slide 3:**  
*"Rates have [moved X% / stayed stable / been volatile] this week. Here's my take on what that means for buyers in [City]:"*  
[2-3 sentence honest commentary]

## SECTION 6: STORY STRATEGY NOTES

---

### POSTING FREQUENCY

| Activity Level | Stories Per Day | Goal |
|---------------|----------------|------|
| Minimum | 1-3 | Stay top of mind |
| Recommended | 3-7 | Build real relationship |
| Power week | 8-12 | Awareness push (open house week, listing launch, market event) |

---

### BEST STORY POSTING TIMES

- **Morning:** 7–9am (commute audience)
- **Lunch:** 12–1pm
- **Evening:** 6–9pm (highest overall engagement)

Avoid posting all at once — space them through the day.

---

### STORY HIGHLIGHTS TO SET UP

| Highlight | What Goes In It |
|----------|----------------|
| 🏡 Listings | Active listings, virtual tours, property details |
| 🎉 Just Sold | Sold announcements, client wins |
| 📊 Market | Market updates, stats, insights |
| 💡 Tips | Buyer and seller tip posts |
| 👋 About Me | Personal brand, why I do this, testimonials |
| ❓ FAQs | Answers to common questions |

---

### STORY CTA ROTATION

Vary your CTA so it doesn't feel repetitive:
- "DM me your question"
- "DM me the word [KEYWORD] and I'll send you [resource]"
- "Tap the link in my bio for more"
- "Comment below" (when using Stories that link to feed posts)
- "Send this to someone who needs to see it"
- "Tag a friend who's thinking about buying/selling"
- "Reply to this Story"
