# Market Update Templates for Real Estate Agents
## Monthly Reports, Social Posts, and Email Newsletters

---

### HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE

Market updates are one of the most powerful content types for real estate agents. They position you as the expert, give people a reason to follow you, and naturally generate "what's my home worth?" conversations. Use these templates every month without starting from scratch.

**Where to find your data:** MLS statistics, your local association's market reports, Redfin, Zillow Research, or your brokerage's monthly data package.

### SOCIAL TEMPLATE 05 — "Is Now a Good Time?" Direct Address

---

"Is now a good time to buy?" — I get this question every week.

Honest answer: it depends on you.

✅ Good time if:
• You plan to stay at least 5+ years
• Your finances are stable (job, income, savings)
• You've found a home that fits your life
• You understand the payments at today's rates

❌ Might want to wait if:
• Your job situation is uncertain
• You're stretching the budget too thin
• You're expecting prices to drop significantly (data doesn't support that in [City])
• You'd need to sell quickly in under 3 years

The market will always feel imperfect. The question is whether it's right for YOU right now.

DM me if you want to talk through your specific situation — no pitch, just conversation.

## SECTION 5: MARKET UPDATE CONTENT CALENDAR

### Suggested Monthly Posting Schedule

| Week | Content | Platform |
|------|---------|---------|
| Week 1 | Full monthly market report | Email + Blog |
| Week 1 | "By the Numbers" stat post | Instagram + Facebook |
| Week 2 | Trend commentary or opinion post | Instagram + Facebook |
| Week 2 | Story — quick verbal update | Instagram Stories |
| Week 3 | Neighborhood spotlight or comparison | Instagram + Facebook |
| Week 3 | "Is now a good time?" or buyer/seller advice | Instagram + Facebook |
| Week 4 | Notable sale highlight or case study | Instagram + Facebook |
| Week 4 | Preview of next month's watch items | Stories or Feed |

**Minimum viable:** 1 social post per week + 1 monthly email. Consistency over frequency.
