## SECTION 1: PERSONALIZING YOUR VOICE

Every template in this kit is a starting point, not a script. The agents who see the most results from content marketing are the ones who add their personality to every post. This guide shows you how.

| Very Casual | Casual | Balanced | Professional | Very Formal |
|------------|--------|---------|-------------|-------------|

---

### STEP 2: ADJUST TEMPLATE LANGUAGE TO MATCH YOUR VOICE

**Example: Switching template tones**

**Original template line (neutral):**  
*"Homes in [City] sold for an average of $[X] this month."*

**Made casual:**  
*"Real talk — homes in [City] are going for around $X right now. Here's what that actually means for you."*

**Made warm:**  
*"I want you to have the real numbers, not just the headlines. [City] median was $X this month, and here's how that breaks down for buyers vs. sellers."*

**Made direct/authoritative:**  
*"[City] market data for [Month]: Median sale price $X. If you're making a decision this quarter, here's what you need to know."*

---

### STEP 3: ADD LOCAL SPECIFICITY

The fastest way to make any template better: make it hyperlocal.

Replace:
- "[City]" → your actual city
- "[neighborhood]" → your actual specialty neighborhood(s)
- "[average days on market]" → your actual current data
- "[comparable homes]" → actual addresses and sale prices (with care for privacy)
- "[nearby amenities]" → specific coffee shops, parks, schools your audience knows

**Example:**

Generic: *"This home is in a great neighborhood."*

Local: *"This home is three blocks from [Coffee Shop Name], walkable to [Elementary School Name], and in the [Neighborhood Name] pocket that's been appreciating 8% year over year — which most buyers don't realize because it doesn't get as much attention as [Adjacent Neighborhood]."*

---

### STEP 4: ADD PERSONAL TOUCHES

Weave in real experiences from your work. People follow you for YOU, not for templates.

**Formulas for personal additions:**

"This reminds me of a situation I had with a client recently..."

"I've been getting this question a lot lately..."

"Honest confession as an agent: [something real and humble]"

"What nobody tells you about [topic]: [your personal observation after X years in the market]"

"I've sold [X homes / worked with X buyers] in [neighborhood] and here's what I consistently see..."

## SECTION 2: CONTENT PILLARS GUIDE

---

### YOUR 5 CONTENT PILLARS

For consistent content, every post should fall into one of these 5 buckets:

| Pillar | % of Posts | Goal |
|--------|-----------|------|
| Educational | 30-40% | Build authority / Trust |
| Promotional | 20-25% | Drive leads / Show listings |
| Engagement | 20-25% | Build community / Replies |
| Behind the Scenes | 10-15% | Build personal connection |
| Testimonial/Social Proof | 10-15% | Build credibility |

**Rule of thumb:** For every promotional post, post 3-4 educational or engagement posts. Too much self-promotion makes people unfollow.

### CONTENT BATCHING WORKFLOW

Don't post in real time — batch your content.

**1x per month (2 hours):**
- Pull that month's market data
- Choose 20 caption ideas from the content calendar
- Draft all 20 captions
- Schedule with Buffer, Later, or Meta Business Suite

**1x per week (30 minutes):**
- Take any listing photos or BTS content you have
- Write any timely or market-reactive posts
- Check DMs and respond

**Daily (10 minutes):**
- Post 3-5 Stories
- Reply to comments and DMs
- Engage with 5-10 accounts in your audience (comment, reply to Stories)

## SECTION 4: CAPTION WRITING FORMULA

---

### THE REAL ESTATE CAPTION FORMULA

Every strong caption has 4 parts:

**1. HOOK (Line 1 — visible before "more")**
The most important line. Must make someone stop scrolling.

Strong hook formulas:
- Question: "What nobody tells first-time buyers about closing costs:"
- Bold statement: "You don't need 20% down."
- Surprising stat: "The average [City] home spent only 9 days on market last month."
- Relatability: "Losing your third offer is a feeling I genuinely hope you never know."
- Controversy: "Hot take: overpricing your home costs more than underpricing it."

**2. BODY (Lines 2-8)**
Deliver the value, story, or information. Short paragraphs. One idea per paragraph. Use line breaks generously.

**3. CTA (Second to last)**
Tell them exactly what to do next. One specific action.
- "DM me 'SELLER' and I'll tell you what your home is worth right now."
- "Save this for when you're ready to start your search."
- "Tag someone who needs to see this."
- "Comment below — what's your biggest question about [topic]?"

**4. HASHTAGS (Last)**
5-10 hashtags. Mixed from your Hashtag Bank sets. See File 07 for the full bank.

---

### HOOKS THAT CONSISTENTLY WORK FOR REAL ESTATE

Educational:
- "5 things every [first-time buyer / seller in [City]] should know:"
- "The real reason [common misconception] isn't true:"
- "Nobody talks about this part of buying a home:"
- "What I wish someone had told me before I became a real estate agent:"

Promotional:
- "Just listed — and I don't think it'll last long."
- "My clients almost gave up. Then this happened."
- "SOLD. In [X] days. Over asking. Here's how."

Engagement:
- "Would you rather: [Option A] or [Option B]? Comment below."
- "What's your biggest fear about [buying / selling] right now?"
- "On a scale of 1-10, how ready are you to make a move this year?"

## SECTION 5: CONTENT REPURPOSING SYSTEM

---

### TURN ONE PIECE OF CONTENT INTO FIVE

Don't create from scratch every time. Repurpose.

**Source: A new listing**
1. Feed post: Listing announcement with top 3 features
2. Stories: Behind the listing (3-5 slides)
3. Reels: 30-second walkthrough video
4. Facebook: Listing post with link to full MLS listing
5. Email: "New listing that might interest you" to relevant buyer leads

**Source: A just sold**
1. Feed post: SOLD announcement with stats
2. Story: Quick personal reflection on the deal
3. Email: Neighborhood value update to adjacent homeowners
4. Testimonial post: Feature client quote (with permission)
5. Market update: Add to monthly data post

**Source: A market update**
1. Email: Full monthly market report
2. Feed post: Data graphic with 5 key stats
3. Stories: 30-second verbal update to camera
4. Carousel: "What this means for buyers vs. sellers"
5. Facebook: Linked post to your website/blog

## SECTION 6: LEAD CONVERSION FROM CONTENT

---

### TURNING FOLLOWERS INTO CLIENTS

Most real estate agents post content and wait for clients to reach out. The agents who convert followers into clients are more proactive.

**DM Strategy:**
- When someone engages meaningfully with your content (comments, replies to stories, saves), send a genuine DM
- Not: "Thanks for the like! I'd love to help you buy or sell a home!"
- Yes: "Hey [Name] — saw you voted [buying] on my poll! Are you early stages or do you have a timeline?"

**Free Resource Strategy:**
- Offer something valuable in exchange for a DM: buyer guide, home value report, neighborhood breakdown
- Every person who DMs you for a resource is a warm lead
- Follow up within 24 hours, then add to your monthly email list

**Story CTA Strategy:**
- End every story sequence with a DM prompt
- Use keywords (DM me "BUY" / DM me "SELL" / DM me "VALUE") to make it easy
- Respond to every DM within a few hours when possible

---

### WHEN SOMEONE ASKS ABOUT BUYING OR SELLING IN DMS

**Don't:** Launch into a pitch immediately.

**Do:**
1. Ask one clarifying question: "Are you looking for yourself or is this more early exploration?"
2. Listen to their answer
3. Provide value first: share a resource, answer a question, give market context
4. Suggest a call only after you've established you can actually help them

## SECTION 7: TRACKING WHAT WORKS

---

### CONTENT METRICS TO TRACK MONTHLY

| Metric | What It Tells You |
|--------|------------------|
| Reach per post | How many people saw it |
| Saves | High-value content — people saved for future reference |
| Shares | Viral potential — resonated enough to share |
| Profile visits from post | Post drove curiosity about you |
| Follows from content | Post attracted new audience |
| DMs from content | Content drove direct outreach |
| Link clicks | Traffic to your website or listing |

**The most important metric for lead generation:** DMs and profile visits. Saves indicate quality content. Reach indicates algorithm favor.

---

### MONTHLY CONTENT REVIEW QUESTIONS

At the end of each month, spend 20 minutes answering:

1. Which post got the most saves? Why? Make more like it.
2. Which post got the most DMs? What was the CTA? Use that CTA again.
3. Which post got the most reach? Was it a Reel, carousel, or single image?
4. Which post flopped despite being high-effort? What would you do differently?
5. Did your follower count grow? By how much?
6. How many DM conversations turned into consultations this month?
7. What content do you want to make more of next month?
