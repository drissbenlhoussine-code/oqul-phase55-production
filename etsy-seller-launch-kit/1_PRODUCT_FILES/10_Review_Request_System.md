# ETSY REVIEW REQUEST SYSTEM
## Etsy Seller Launch Kit — Template 10 of 10

**The complete system for getting more 5-star reviews on Etsy.**

---

## WHY REVIEWS DOMINATE YOUR ETSY SUCCESS

- **Search ranking:** Etsy ranks shops with higher review scores and more reviews higher in search
- **Conversion rate:** Listings with 50+ reviews convert at 3–5× higher rates than listings with 0
- **Star Seller badge:** Requires 95%+ 5-star reviews — unlocks a prominent trust badge
- **Buyer psychology:** 92% of Etsy buyers read reviews before purchasing from a new shop

**The math:**
A listing with 4.9 stars and 100 reviews will outsell an identical listing with 0 reviews by 5–10× in most niches. Reviews are not a "nice to have" — they're your most important growth lever in the first 6 months.

---

## THE REVIEW FUNNEL

Every sale should go through this sequence:

```
Sale Complete
    ↓
[Auto-reply or instant thank you message]
    ↓
[Product delivered perfectly]
    ↓
[Post-delivery review request — Day 1–3]
    ↓
[Follow-up if no review — Day 7–10]
    ↓
Review received → Reply publicly within 24 hours
```

[Your Name]
[Shop Name]

---

### Message 2 — Post-Delivery Review Request (Send 2–4 days after purchase)

---

Hi [Name]!

I hope [product name] has been useful for you.

If you're happy with your purchase, I'd love to ask one small favor: a review on Etsy.

It genuinely makes a difference for independent sellers like me — reviews are how buyers decide whether to trust a new shop, and a few honest words from you could help another [buyer type] make the same decision you did.

It takes about 60 seconds:
Etsy app → "You" → Purchases and Reviews → Write a Review

Thank you so much either way.

[Your Name]

---

### Message 3 — Follow-Up (Send ONCE at Day 7–10 if no review)

---

Hi [Name],

Just a quick note — in case it slipped through the cracks, the option to leave a review for [product name] is still open for the next few days.

If you found it useful, I'd genuinely appreciate 2 minutes of your time. If something wasn't right, please tell me first — I'd love the chance to make it right.

Either way, thank you for your purchase.

[Your Name]

---

**RULE: Send the follow-up ONCE. Never twice.**
Chasing reviews is both ineffective and risks a bad experience.

## PART 3 — HOW TO RESPOND TO REVIEWS (PUBLICLY)

**Why responding to reviews matters:**
Your response is visible to all future buyers. A thoughtful reply to a 5-star review shows personality. A calm, professional reply to a 1-star review shows integrity.

---

### 5-Star Review Response Templates

**Template A — Reference what they said:**

"Thank you so much, [Name]! I'm so glad [specific thing they mentioned — "the templates were easy to customize"] worked out for you. It was a pleasure — I hope to see you back soon!"

**Template B — Warm and brief:**

"This made my day, [Name]! Thank you for the kind words — it means a lot to a small shop like mine. Enjoy every bit of it!"

**Template C — Keyword-rich (subtle SEO value):**

"Thank you, [Name]! So happy our [product type] worked well for you. Reviews like yours help other [buyer type] find exactly what they need. Truly appreciated!"

---

### 3-Star Review Response Templates

**Template A — Acknowledge + Offer:**

"Thank you for your honest feedback, [Name]. I'm glad [positive aspect they mentioned] worked for you. I'm sorry [issue] didn't meet your expectations — if you'd like to message me, I'd love to make it right."

**Template B — No specific complaint given:**

"Thank you for taking the time to leave a review, [Name]. I'd love to know what I could have done better — please message me anytime and I'll be happy to help."

---

### 1-Star Review Response Templates

**Template A — Stay calm, invite private conversation:**

"Thank you for the feedback, [Name]. I'm genuinely sorry this didn't meet your expectations — that's never the experience I want for any buyer. Please message me directly and I'll do everything I can to make it right."

**Template B — When their complaint is factually incorrect:**

"Thank you for the review, [Name]. I'm sorry to hear about your experience. As noted in the listing, [clarification of fact — e.g. 'this is a digital download, not a physical item']. I'd welcome the chance to help resolve this — please message me."

**NEVER:**
- Argue with the reviewer publicly
- Call them dishonest (even if they are)
- Post their private information
- Respond while angry — wait at least 24 hours

## PART 4 — REDUCING NEGATIVE REVIEWS (PREVENTIVE)

Most negative Etsy reviews come from:

| Root Cause | Prevention |
|---|---|
| Misunderstanding what they're buying | Clearer listing photos and description |
| File format confusion | State format and software clearly in description |
| Can't access digital download | Better purchase instructions in auto-reply |
| Didn't like the design | More realistic mockup photos |
| Shipping took too long (physical) | Set realistic processing times — never optimistic |
| Wrong expectations | Read your worst reviews and fix the source |

**Pre-emptive action:** Read every 1–3 star review you receive and ask: "What in my listing caused this misunderstanding?" Then fix the listing.
