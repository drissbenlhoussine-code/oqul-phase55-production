## HOW TO FIND ETSY KEYWORDS (The Right Way)

### Method 1 — Etsy Search Bar (Free, Always Accurate)

The search bar autocomplete shows you what real buyers are typing RIGHT NOW.

**Process:**
1. Open Etsy in a private/incognito browser (prevents your history from influencing results)
2. Type your product type — e.g. "wedding invitation"
3. Note every autocomplete suggestion
4. Then type "wedding invitation t" — note what appears
5. Continue through the alphabet: "wedding invitation a", "b", "c"...
6. Record all suggestions in the tracker below

**Why this works:** Etsy's autocomplete is powered by actual search data. If it autocompletes it, people are searching it.

---

### Method 2 — Competitor Reverse Engineering

1. Search your main keyword on Etsy
2. Click on a top-selling listing (look for high review counts + "Bestseller" badge)
3. Read their title — note all keywords used
4. Read their tags (visible on listing page on desktop)
5. Note which keywords appear across multiple top sellers — those are proven

---

### Method 3 — Erank or Marmalead (Free Tiers Available)

Both tools show:
- Search volume for specific keywords
- Competition level
- Trend direction (rising vs. declining)

**Erank.com** — Free tier gives keyword data and listing grades
**Marmalead.com** — Paid ($19/mo) but excellent for serious sellers

---

### Method 4 — Pinterest & Google

Search your product on Pinterest and Google — note what they autocomplete.
These platforms reveal buyer language that often works on Etsy too.
