## SECTION 3 — SHOP POLICIES AUDIT

Policies directly affect buyer confidence and dispute outcomes.

**Refund Policy:**
- [ ] Clearly states your refund policy for digital products ("Due to the digital nature, no refunds after download")
- [ ] States process for issues ("Contact me within X days and I'll make it right")

**Digital Download Policy:**
- [ ] Explains what format files come in
- [ ] Explains how to access the download
- [ ] States compatibility requirements (software, device)
- [ ] Addresses "I can't open the file" — what to do

**Custom & Personalized Orders:**
- [ ] Policy exists if you offer custom work
- [ ] Turnaround time stated
- [ ] What information is needed

**Privacy Policy:**
- [ ] Present (required in many jurisdictions)
- [ ] Mentions GDPR if you sell to EU customers
