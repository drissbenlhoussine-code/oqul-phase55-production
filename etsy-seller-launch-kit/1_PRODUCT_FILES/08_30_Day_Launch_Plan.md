# 30-DAY ETSY SHOP LAUNCH PLAN
## Etsy Seller Launch Kit — Template 8 of 10

**A day-by-day action plan to go from zero to your first sale in 30 days.**

---

## BEFORE YOU BEGIN: THE LAUNCH MINDSET

The sellers who succeed on Etsy in 30 days don't post 1 listing and wait. They:
1. Launch with a minimum of **10 listings** (shops with more listings rank higher)
2. Update something **every day** for 30 days (activity signals freshness to Etsy)
3. Drive **external traffic** from Pinterest and Instagram (Etsy rewards external traffic)
4. **Obsess over the data** — check Etsy Stats every 2–3 days and adjust

If you only have 1 product: create multiple listings with different titles/angles, or add variations.

---

## WEEK 1 — BUILD THE FOUNDATION

### Day 1 — Shop Setup

- [ ] Create your Etsy seller account
- [ ] Choose your shop name (check availability on Etsy + Instagram)
- [ ] Set your shop location and currency
- [ ] Add payment and billing info
- [ ] Write your shop announcement (use Template 2 or AI Prompt #34)
- [ ] Write your shop policies (use AI Prompt #42)

**Today's goal:** Shop is open, even if empty.

---

### Day 2 — Brand Setup

- [ ] Design shop banner (Canva, 3360×840px)
- [ ] Create shop icon/logo
- [ ] Write About section — minimum 200 words (use AI Prompt #60 for mission statement)
- [ ] Set up shop sections with keyword-rich names

**Today's goal:** Shop looks professional to any visitor.

---

### Day 3 — First 3 Listings

- [ ] Research keywords for Listing 1 (use Template 3 — Keyword Tracker)
- [ ] Write title and 13 tags for Listing 1
- [ ] Write description for Listing 1 (use Template 2)
- [ ] Upload photos for Listing 1 (minimum 5, ideally 10)
- [ ] Publish Listing 1
- [ ] Repeat for Listings 2 and 3

**Today's goal:** 3 listings live.

---

### Day 4 — Listings 4–6

- [ ] Same process as Day 3 for 3 more listings
- [ ] Each listing should target a DIFFERENT keyword cluster
- [ ] Vary your photo styles across listings (don't use identical mockups for all)

**Today's goal:** 6 listings live.

---

### Day 5 — Listings 7–10

- [ ] Publish 4 more listings
- [ ] At least 1 listing should be a bundle (bundles attract higher-AOV buyers)
- [ ] Run SEO checklist (Template 1) on all 10 listings

**Today's goal:** 10 listings live, all SEO-checked.

---

### Day 6 — Pinterest Launch

- [ ] Create a Pinterest business account (free)
- [ ] Create a board named after your primary product keyword
- [ ] Pin all 10 listings with keyword-rich descriptions (use AI Prompt #31)
- [ ] Create 5 additional "inspiration" pins with text overlays linking to your shop

**Why Pinterest:** Pinterest pins are indexed by Google. A single pin can drive traffic for months.

**Today's goal:** 15+ pins live on Pinterest.

---

### Day 7 — Week 1 Review

- [ ] Check Etsy Stats — which listings have the most views?
- [ ] Which listing has the best click-through rate (views → orders)?
- [ ] Note: which photos stopped buyers from clicking? Which ones converted?
- [ ] Update any listings with 0 views — the title may have a keyword issue

**Today's goal:** Understanding which listings are working and why.

---

## WEEK 2 — DRIVE TRAFFIC

### Day 8 — Social Media Setup

- [ ] Create or optimize your Instagram account for the shop
- [ ] Write a keyword-rich bio with your shop link in bio
- [ ] Post your first 3 product photos with captions (use AI Prompt #32)
- [ ] Add link to Etsy shop in bio

---

### Day 9 — SEO Deep Dive

- [ ] Check which keywords are driving views (Shop Manager → Stats → Traffic Sources)
- [ ] For any listing with views but 0 sales: is the conversion problem photos or price?
- [ ] Update the 3 lowest-performing listings with new titles/tags based on data
- [ ] Add 5 more pins to Pinterest

---

### Day 10 — Add More Listings

- [ ] Publish 2–3 new listings
- [ ] Try a different product format (e.g., if you've done single items, try a bundle)
- [ ] Aim to have 12–15 active listings by end of Week 2

---

### Day 11 — Instagram Content Day

- [ ] Post 3 Instagram Stories: one product showcase, one "how to use," one poll/question
- [ ] Engage with 10 accounts in your niche (like, comment authentically)
- [ ] Add 5 more Pinterest pins

---

### Day 12 — Optimize Your Best Listing

- [ ] Identify your #1 viewed listing
- [ ] Update it: add a 10th photo if missing, add more detail to description
- [ ] Research 5 new keyword variations and test 3 new tags

---

### Day 13 — Run Your First Sale/Coupon

- [ ] Create a sale in Etsy (Shop Manager → Marketing → Sales and Coupons)
- [ ] Set 15–20% off your entire shop for 48 hours
- [ ] Announce the sale on Instagram Stories and Pinterest
- [ ] Etsy may also email your favorites list about the sale automatically

---

### Day 14 — Week 2 Review

- [ ] Check revenue, views, orders, and conversion rate vs. Week 1
- [ ] Update your Monthly Sales Tracker (Template 7)
- [ ] Which listing has the most saves? (Favorites are a strong buying signal)
- [ ] Set Week 3 goals based on what the data tells you

---

## WEEK 3 — CONVERT BROWSERS INTO BUYERS

### Day 15 — Social Proof Push

- [ ] If you have any sales/reviews: screenshot and share as an Instagram Story
- [ ] Add a review-quote graphic as your 6th listing photo on your best seller
- [ ] If no reviews yet: message the 2–3 people who've purchased and ask directly

---

### Day 16 — Create a Bundle or Discount Stack

- [ ] Bundle your 2 best-selling products into a discounted bundle listing
- [ ] Set the bundle price at a 15–20% savings vs. buying separately
- [ ] Publish and add to Pinterest immediately

---

### Day 17 — External Traffic Day

- [ ] Join 2–3 Facebook Groups for your buyer niche
- [ ] Provide genuine help in the group (answer questions, share tips)
- [ ] Mention your shop naturally when relevant (most groups allow this weekly)
- [ ] Post on Reddit (r/Etsy or relevant subreddit — use AI Prompt #35)

---

### Day 18 — Listing Refresh Day

- [ ] Pick 3 listings that have views but low conversion
- [ ] Rewrite their first photo text to be bolder/clearer at thumbnail size
- [ ] Rewrite their description hook (first 3 lines)
- [ ] Update their 3 weakest tags with new keyword research

---

### Day 19 — Etsy Ads Test (Optional)

- [ ] If you have any listings with proven organic views: try $1–$3/day Etsy Ads budget
- [ ] Only advertise listings that have already proven they can convert organically
- [ ] Set up the ad and let it run for at least 14 days before judging results
- [ ] Track: ROAS (Revenue ÷ Ad Spend). Target: above 3× (every $1 spent = $3+ revenue)

---

### Day 20 — Content That Drives Traffic

- [ ] Write and post a helpful Instagram carousel: "[X] things buyers get wrong about [your niche]"
- [ ] Each slide addresses a real misconception — last slide mentions your product as the solution
- [ ] Add the carousel to Pinterest as individual pins

---

### Day 21 — Week 3 Review

- [ ] Sales this week vs. Week 2?
- [ ] Conversion rate improving?
- [ ] Which listing now has the most saves?
- [ ] Any reviews? If yes — reply publicly to all of them.

---

## WEEK 4 — OPTIMIZE AND SCALE

### Day 22 — SEO Final Pass

- [ ] Run the full SEO Checklist (Template 1) on your top 5 listings
- [ ] Fix any remaining gaps: fill all 13 tags, optimize description structure
- [ ] Verify all listings have 8–10 photos (fill any gaps)

---

### Day 23 — Email List Starter

- [ ] Add a freebie PDF to your Etsy shop (a valuable free resource in your niche)
- [ ] Include a link to your email signup inside the PDF
- [ ] Suggest buyers download it in a listing update or shop announcement

---

### Day 24 — Testimonial Collection

- [ ] Message every buyer who hasn't left a review (use Template 9 — Review Requests)
- [ ] Send once only — don't repeat
- [ ] Reply publicly to any reviews that have come in

---

### Day 25 — Plan Month 2

- [ ] Identify your top 3 selling products
- [ ] Plan 5 new listings for Month 2 that complement the winners
- [ ] Schedule your seasonal keyword updates for next month

---

### Day 26 — Pinterest Deep Session

- [ ] Create 15 new Pinterest pins for your top 5 listings (3 pins per listing, different angles)
- [ ] Join 3 Pinterest Group Boards in your niche and start pinning to them
- [ ] Verify your Etsy shop with Pinterest (get the verified merchant badge)

---

### Day 27 — Analyse Your Competition Again

- [ ] Go back to your top 3 competitors — what new products have they added?
- [ ] What are their newest reviews saying? (Any unmet need you could fill?)
- [ ] Update your keyword tracker based on any new terms they're ranking for

---

### Day 28 — Run Your Second Sale

- [ ] Run a 48-hour "end of month" flash sale: 20% off
- [ ] Target buyers who have favorited but not purchased (Etsy may notify them automatically)
- [ ] Announce on all channels
