## Etsy Seller Launch Kit — Template 4 of 10

**The definitive guide to every element of a high-converting Etsy listing.**

## ELEMENT 1 — LISTING TITLE

**What it does:** Tells Etsy what to rank you for AND convinces buyers to click.

**The anatomy of a perfect title:**

```
[Primary Keyword] | [Secondary Keyword] | [Style or Feature] | [Use Case or Occasion]
```

**Character limits:**
- Maximum: 140 characters total
- What buyers see in search: ~55–65 characters
- What shows on mobile: ~40 characters
- **Your primary keyword must appear in the first 40 characters**

**Title scoring rubric:**

| Criterion | Points | Your Score |
|---|---|---|
| Primary keyword in first 40 chars | 25 | /25 |
| Uses 120–140 characters (no wasted space) | 15 | /15 |
| Reads naturally (not keyword stuffing) | 20 | /20 |
| Includes occasion or use case | 15 | /15 |
| Includes style or aesthetic | 15 | /15 |
| No all-caps, no special chars | 10 | /10 |
| **Total** | **100** | **/100** |

**Common title mistakes:**
- ❌ Starting with your shop name ("LunaDesigns — Wedding Invitation Template")
- ❌ All lowercase ("watercolor wedding invitation digital download")
- ❌ Keyword stuffing ("Wedding Invitation Template Digital Invite Wedding Digital Wedding Download Wedding Printable")
- ❌ Too short — leaving 80+ characters unused

**Strong title examples:**

*Digital product:*
`Watercolor Wedding Invitation Template | Editable Canva | Dusty Blue Floral | Instant Download`

*Physical product:*
`Personalized Leather Journal | Monogram Notebook | Gift for Writer | A5 Hardcover Diary`

*Canva template:*
`Instagram Feed Template | Aesthetic Social Media Kit | Canva Editable | Influencer Content`

## ELEMENT 2 — PHOTOS

**Why photos are #1:** 90% of purchase decisions on Etsy are made based on photos. A great product with mediocre photos will lose to an average product with great photos.

**Photo slot strategy (use all 10):**

| Photo # | Purpose | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (Hero) | Clean product shot — must work as a thumbnail | White/neutral background. Your product centered. Readable text if any. |
| 2 | Lifestyle / in use | Show the product being used or in a real context |
| 3 | Detail / close-up | Show quality, texture, fine details |
| 4 | Multi-variant or size comparison | Show options available |
| 5 | What's included / contents shot | For bundles, kits, or digital downloads |
| 6 | Social proof / review quote | Screenshot of a real review on a clean background |
| 7 | Process / behind the scenes | Builds trust and humanizes your brand |
| 8 | Styling options | Show the product styled 2–3 different ways |
| 9 | FAQ answered visually | Address the #1 objection or question as a graphic |
| 10 | Call to action | "Add to cart" + key benefit, or shop link |

**Technical specifications:**
- Minimum: 2000px on the shortest side
- Recommended: 2700×2025px (Etsy's ideal ratio)
- Format: JPG or PNG
- Maximum file size: 100MB per photo
- Avoid: Watermarks, borders, collages in the first photo

**Mobile optimization:**
- View every photo at thumbnail size before publishing
- Text in photos must be readable at 150×150px
- High contrast beats subtle design at small sizes

**Common photo mistakes:**
- ❌ Dark or blurry product photos
- ❌ Cluttered background competing with the product
- ❌ Text that disappears at thumbnail size
- ❌ Using only 1–3 photos (every empty slot is lost trust)
- ❌ No lifestyle shot (buyers can't imagine it in their life)

## ELEMENT 3 — PRICING

**Pricing psychology on Etsy:**

| Price Point | Buyer Psychology | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Under $5 | Impulse buy — high volume needed | Clipart, single SVGs, simple printables |
| $7–$15 | Sweet spot for most digital products | Templates, planners, caption banks |
| $15–$35 | "Premium" digital — needs more proof | Kits, bundles, comprehensive guides |
| $35–$75 | Professional tools | Business templates, courses, SOPs |
| $75+ | Requires strong social proof | High-end custom or premium courses |

**Pricing formula for digital products:**

```
Base Price = (Time to create ÷ Expected sales) + Platform fees + Profit margin
```

**Example:**
- Time to create: 10 hours at your $50/hr value = $500
- Expected sales: 500 units over lifetime
- Cost per sale: $500 ÷ 500 = $1.00
- Etsy fees per sale at $15: ~$1.60
- Minimum viable price: $2.60
- But you should price for VALUE, not just cost

**Pricing rules:**
- Price at or slightly above the market average if your product is better quality
- Never be the cheapest — buyers associate low price with low quality on Etsy

- Use $X.97 or $X.99 — psychological pricing still works

## ELEMENT 4 — DESCRIPTION

**The structure that converts:**

**Paragraph 1 (Hook):** Speak to the desire or problem. First 160 characters matter most — this is what Google indexes.

**Section 2 (What's included):** Bullet list. Be specific. Not "5 templates" but "5 editable Canva templates (Instagram post 1080×1080, Story 1080×1920, etc.)"

**Section 3 (Who it's for):** 3–5 buyer types. Helps buyers self-identify.

**Section 4 (How it works):** 4–6 numbered steps. Make it feel easy.

**Section 5 (Technical details):** Format, software, dimensions, compatibility.

**Section 6 (FAQ):** Answer the top 3–5 questions before they have to ask.

**Close:** CTA + "Message the shop with questions."

**Keyword density:**
- Primary keyword: 3–4 times naturally
- Secondary keywords: 1–2 times each
- Never forced — if it reads awkwardly, remove it

## ELEMENT 5 — TAGS

**Etsy uses your tags AND title together.** Don't repeat title words in tags.

**Tag composition formula:**
- 3 tags: core product type (2-word variations)
- 2 tags: style / aesthetic ("minimalist planner," "boho wedding")
- 2 tags: occasion / recipient ("graduation gift," "for her")
- 2 tags: use case ("home office decor," "classroom organization")
- 2 tags: long-tail specific ("2025 weekly planner," "nurse appreciation gift")
- 2 tags: seasonal / trending

**How to test tags:**
After 30 days, check Etsy Stats → Traffic Sources → Etsy Search.
You'll see which keywords drove visits. Keep what works, replace what doesn't.

## ELEMENT 6 — SHOP SECTIONS

Each section name is indexed by Etsy search.
Name sections by keyword, not by product name.

| ❌ Weak Section Name | ✅ Strong Section Name |
|---|---|
| Collection 1 | Wedding Templates |
| My Favorites | Canva Instagram Templates |
| New Items | Printable Wall Art |
| Bundles | Party Printable Bundles |

## ELEMENT 7 — LISTING RENEWAL STRATEGY

Etsy "freshness" matters — new or recently renewed listings get a small ranking boost.

**Renewal schedule:**
- Auto-renew: leave on (Etsy auto-renews every 4 months for $0.20)
- Manual refresh: After editing a listing (changing photos, title, or description), save it to signal Etsy that it's been updated
- Seasonal refresh: Update 1 keyword and 1 photo every season even if nothing else changes

## ELEMENT 8 — CONVERSION RATE OPTIMIZATION

**What a "good" conversion rate looks like:**
- Digital products: 1–5% is good, 5%+ is excellent
- Physical products: 0.5–2% is typical
- A listing under 0.5% needs help

**If people visit but don't buy — diagnosis:**

| Symptom | Likely Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| High views, low clicks | Thumbnail photo is weak | Redesign hero photo |
| High clicks, low adds to cart | Price too high or description unclear | Add value context or lower price |
| High adds to cart, low checkout | Shipping cost surprise or trust issue | Show reviews prominently, clarify shipping |
| Low views overall | SEO / title / tags weak | Full keyword refresh |

## 30-DAY POST-PUBLISH CHECKLIST

After publishing a new or updated listing:
- Day 1: Share on Pinterest (creates a backlink + drives traffic)
- Day 3: Share on Instagram Stories with "link in bio"
- Day 7: Check Etsy Stats — note early view count
- Day 14: Check conversion rate — above 1%? Good. Below? Fix photos first.
- Day 30: Full stats review. Update lowest-performing tag. Reshare on Pinterest.
