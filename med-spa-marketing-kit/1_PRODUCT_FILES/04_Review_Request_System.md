# Review Request System

## IMMEDIATE POST-TREATMENT TEXT (send within 2 hours)

"Hi [Name]! It was so great seeing you today. I hope you're feeling wonderful. As your results settle over the next few days, feel free to reach out with any questions. We're always here. 😊 — [Provider Name], [Spa Name]"

## 48-HOUR FOLLOW-UP

"Hi [Name], checking in! How are you feeling? Results should be starting to show. If you're happy with your experience, we'd be incredibly grateful if you'd share a Google review — it takes less than a minute and helps us reach more clients like you: [Google Review Link] Thank you so much! 🌟"

## 2-WEEK REVIEW REQUEST

Subject: How are your results, [Name]?

Hi [Name],

It's been about two weeks since your visit — the sweet spot where results are fully settled.

We'd love to hear how you're feeling!

If your experience was positive, would you mind leaving us a quick Google review? Even just a sentence or two means the world to us and helps other clients find care they can trust.

→ [Leave a Review: Google Link]

With gratitude,
[Provider Name]
[Spa Name]

## AUTOMATED REVIEW SEQUENCE (for email platforms)

**Email 1 (Day 2):** Checking in on your results
**Email 2 (Day 7):** Quick question for you, [Name]
**Email 3 (Day 14):** Would you share your experience?
**Email 4 (Day 30):** [Name], your results are fully settled — love to hear your thoughts

## HANDLING NEGATIVE FEEDBACK

If a client expresses dissatisfaction:
1. Call them personally within 24 hours
2. "I'm so sorry your experience didn't meet your expectations. Can you tell me more about what happened?"
3. Offer a complimentary follow-up appointment
4. Never argue or get defensive in writing
5. Document everything
