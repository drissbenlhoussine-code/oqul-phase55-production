# SMS & Reactivation Templates

## APPOINTMENT REMINDERS

**48-Hour Reminder:**
"Hi [Name]! Reminder: your appointment at [Spa Name] is [Day] at [Time]. Reply C to confirm or call [Phone] to reschedule. We're excited to see you! 🌟"

**24-Hour Reminder:**
"See you tomorrow, [Name]! [Day] at [Time] at [Spa Name]. Arrive 10 min early for your intake form. Questions? Call or text [Phone]. ✨"

**Morning of Appointment:**
"Good morning [Name]! Your appointment is today at [Time]. We're looking forward to seeing you. Address: [Address]. Text us if you're running late!"

## REACTIVATION CAMPAIGNS

**3-Month Reactivation (Botox clients):**
"Hi [Name]! It's been about 3 months since your last Botox treatment — perfect time for a touch-up to maintain your results. We have openings this week. Would you like to grab your usual slot? — [Spa Name]"

**6-Month Reactivation (Filler/Skin clients):**
"Hi [Name], it's [Provider Name] at [Spa Name]. We haven't seen you in a while and we miss you! Your skin may be ready for its next treatment. I'd love to check in and see how things are going. Are you open to a quick call this week?"
