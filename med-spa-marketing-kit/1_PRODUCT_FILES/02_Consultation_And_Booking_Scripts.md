# Consultation & Booking Scripts

## INITIAL INQUIRY RESPONSE (SMS/DM)

**Template 1 — Quick Response**
Hi [Name]! Thanks for reaching out to [Spa Name]. We'd love to chat about your goals and what we can do for you. We offer complimentary consultations — would you prefer weekdays or weekends? We have [X] openings this week. 😊

**Template 2 — Warm Lead**
Hi [Name], thank you for your message! We saw you were interested in [treatment]. We have a few questions to make sure we recommend the right solution for you. When works best for a quick consultation call — morning or afternoon?

## PHONE CONSULTATION SCRIPT

**Opening:**
"Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] from [Spa Name]. Thank you so much for reaching out! I have about 15 minutes set aside for us. I'd love to learn a bit more about what brought you in today — can you tell me your main concern or goal?"

**Discovery Questions:**
1. "How long have you been thinking about addressing this?"
2. "Have you had any treatments before, or would this be your first time?"
3. "What's your ideal outcome — are you looking for something subtle, or a more noticeable result?"
4. "Do you have a timeline in mind? Any events coming up?"
5. "Do you have any allergies, medical conditions, or medications we should know about?"

**The Recommendation:**
"Based on what you've shared, I'd actually recommend [treatment] because [reason specific to their concern]. What this will do is [explain benefit in plain language]. Most clients see results within [timeframe]."

**Handling Price Concerns:**
"I completely understand. Let me put it this way — [treatment] at our spa is $[X]. If we break that down, it's about $[per month] per month for [duration] of results. A lot of clients find that makes it much more manageable. Would you like to come in so I can assess your specific needs in person?"

**Closing the Booking:**
"I'd love to get you in for an in-person consultation so we can look at your skin directly and give you a personalized plan. We're open [days] from [hours]. I have [date] at [time] or [date] at [time] — which works better for you?"

## IN-PERSON CONSULTATION SCRIPT

**Welcome:**
"Welcome to [Spa Name]! I'm [Name]. I'm really glad you're here. Before we get started, I want you to know — this consultation is completely pressure-free. Our job today is just to understand your goals and see what options make sense for you."

**Assessment:**
"May I take a look at your skin in our natural lighting? I want to see your skin in real life, not just photos."
[Examine skin]
"What I'm noticing is [observations]. This tells me [explanation]. Based on this, I think [recommendation] would give you the best results because [rationale]."

**Treatment Plan:**
"Here's what I'd suggest as a starting point: [Treatment 1] to address [concern], and potentially [Treatment 2] down the line as we see how your skin responds. A lot of clients start with one thing, see great results, and then we build from there."
