# Before/After Content Plan

## ETHICAL GUIDELINES FOR BEFORE/AFTER CONTENT

✓ Always get signed photo consent before and after
✓ Use consistent lighting and angles
✓ Never alter images beyond color correction
✓ Include realistic timeframes (e.g., "2 weeks post-treatment")
✓ Disclose any treatments clearly
✓ Follow your platform's medical content guidelines

## PHOTO PROTOCOL

**Before Photos:**
- No makeup, hair pulled back
- Consistent lighting (natural preferred)
- Front, 45°, and side profiles
- Patient relaxed (no forced expressions)
- Note: treatment type, date, units/amount used

**After Photos:**
- Same conditions as before
- Taken at: 2 weeks, 3 months, 6 months
- Same angle, same lighting
- Document patient satisfaction score (1-10)
