# Med Spa Business Systems Guide

## CHAPTER 1: BUILDING YOUR CLIENT GROWTH ENGINE

### The 5-Stage Client Journey
1. **Discovery** — Potential clients find you through social, search, or referral
2. **Interest** — They engage with your content and learn about your services
3. **Consultation** — They book a free consultation (your conversion point)
4. **Treatment** — First treatment experience defines loyalty
5. **Retention** — Follow-up, results, and rebooking drive lifetime value

### Key Metrics to Track Monthly
- New inquiry volume
- Consultation booking rate (inquiries → consults)
- Consult conversion rate (consults → treatments)
- Average transaction value
- Client retention rate (at 3, 6, 12 months)
- Google review count and average rating
- Revenue per client per year

## CHAPTER 2: SOCIAL MEDIA STRATEGY

### Content Pillars (rotate weekly)
1. **Education** — Demystify treatments, share facts, bust myths
2. **Social Proof** — Reviews, testimonials, before/after (with consent)
3. **Behind the Scenes** — Provider expertise, clinic culture, day-in-life
4. **Service Spotlights** — Deep-dive into individual treatments
5. **Promotions** — Special offers, availability announcements
6. **Engagement** — Polls, questions, community building

### Posting Frequency
- Instagram Feed: 4–5x per week
- Instagram Stories: Daily
- Facebook: 3–4x per week
- TikTok (if applicable): 3–5x per week
- Google My Business: 2x per week

## CHAPTER 3: REFERRAL SYSTEM

### Client Referral Program
"Refer a friend and you both receive [X]% off your next treatment."
- Send referral cards at checkout
- Include referral link in follow-up emails
- Acknowledge every referral personally

### Medical Professional Referral Network
- Partner with dermatologists, plastic surgeons, OBGYNs
- Provide professional referral cards
- Offer reciprocal referrals where appropriate
- Send quarterly updates on new services

## CHAPTER 4: PRICING STRATEGY

### Value-Based Pricing Framework
- Research local market rates
- Price at or slightly above market (premium positioning)
- Bundle packages to increase average transaction value
- Membership/loyalty programs drive retention

### Recommended Package Structure
- Starter Package: [Treatment A + B] at [X% savings]
- Results Package: [Treatment series] at [X% savings]
- Annual Membership: Unlimited [treatment] + perks at [monthly fee]
