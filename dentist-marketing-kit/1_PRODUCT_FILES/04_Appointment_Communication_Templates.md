# Appointment Communication Templates for Dental Practices
## Confirmations, Reminders, Recalls, and Scheduling Scripts

---

### HOW TO USE THIS FILE

These templates cover every phase of the appointment cycle: scheduling, confirming, reminding, recalling, and handling changes. Adapt them to your practice management software or send manually for high-priority patients.

**Goal:** Reduce no-shows, keep your schedule full, and make patients feel cared for between visits.

### CONF-03 — Email Confirmation (With Pre-Appointment Instructions)

**Subject:** Confirmed: [First Name]'s appointment on [Date] — a few things to know

---

Hi [First Name],

Your appointment on [Date] at [Time] is confirmed. Here's what you should know before you come in:

**For this type of appointment ([e.g., crown prep / extraction / whitening / new patient exam]):**

[Choose the relevant pre-appointment instruction block from below]

---

**For Teeth Whitening:**
- Brush and floss before your appointment but avoid whitening toothpastes
- We recommend taking an over-the-counter pain reliever 1 hour before your visit if you tend to have sensitivity
- Wear comfortable clothing — you'll be in the chair for approximately [60–90] minutes
- Avoid coffee, tea, red wine, and dark-colored foods for 24 hours after treatment

---

**For Extractions / Oral Surgery:**
- Do NOT eat or drink for [X hours] before your appointment if you are receiving sedation
- Arrange for someone to drive you home (required for IV sedation; strongly recommended for oral sedation)
- Wear loose, comfortable clothing with short sleeves if IV placement is possible
- Let us know about all current medications, including supplements

---

**For Crown / Filling Appointments:**
- No special preparation needed for a standard filling
- You may experience numbness for [2–4 hours] after — avoid eating on that side during that time
- Have soft foods available at home for after your appointment

---

**For Invisalign Consultation:**
- Come ready with photos of your smile (front-facing and side profile)
- Think about what you'd like to change — we'll review your goals together
- The consultation is complimentary and takes about [30–45] minutes

---

We look forward to seeing you. Questions? Call [Phone].

— [Practice Name]

## SECTION 5: SCHEDULING PHONE SCRIPTS

---

### PHONE-01 — Inbound New Patient Call

---

**You:** "Thank you for calling [Practice Name], this is [Name]. How can I help you today?"

**If they want to book:**  
"Wonderful! I'd love to get you set up. Are you a new patient, or have you been with us before?"

[If new:] "Welcome! We're excited to meet you. Can I get your name and the best phone number for you? And is this for yourself or a family member?"

[Gather: name, phone, insurance, preferred dates/times, reason for visit]

"Great. I have [appointment options]. Does [Day] at [Time] or [Day] at [Time] work for you?"

[After confirming:] "Perfect. You'll receive a confirmation text and email shortly, along with a link to complete your new patient forms online. It takes about 5 minutes and saves time when you arrive. Is there anything else I can help you with before we go?"

---

### PHONE-02 — Outbound Recall Call

---

**You:** "Hi, may I speak with [First Name]? ... Hi [First Name]! This is [Name] calling from [Practice Name]. How are you? ... I'm calling because our records show your last visit with us was about [X months] ago, and I wanted to check in and see if you'd like to schedule your next cleaning. Do you have a couple minutes? ... [If yes] Great. We have availability [date range]. Would [Day] at [Time] work for you?"

**If they say they've been meaning to call:**  
"Perfect timing then! Let me get you on the schedule right now."

**If they say they're not ready:**  
"No problem at all — completely understand. Can I send you a reminder in a few weeks, or would you prefer to call us when you're ready? ... Great. We'll be here whenever you are. Take care, [First Name]!"

**If they've switched dentists:**  
"Oh, I'm sorry to hear that — I hope you're being well taken care of! Just in case you ever need us, we're still here at [Phone]. Take care!"

---

### PHONE-03 — Handling "How Much Does It Cost?" Calls

---

**Context:** Patients call asking for prices before they've been seen. This is common and requires a careful response — you want to be helpful without quoting inaccurate costs.

**You:** "Great question — and I want to make sure I give you accurate information. The cost for your visit will depend on a few factors, like what your exam finds and your insurance coverage. What I can tell you is that a routine cleaning and exam is typically [price range without insurance], and with most PPO insurance plans, that's covered at 80–100%."

**If they want implant / cosmetic pricing:**  
"For something like [implants/veneers/Invisalign], we really need to evaluate your specific situation first — costs vary quite a bit based on the individual case. What I can offer is a free [consultation / estimate appointment] where Dr. [Name] can give you exact pricing for YOUR situation. Would that be helpful?"
