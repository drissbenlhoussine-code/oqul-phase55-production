# Patient Review Request System for Dental Practices
## Scripts, Templates, and Response Guides for Getting More 5-Star Reviews

---

### WHY REVIEWS ARE YOUR #1 MARKETING ASSET

When a potential patient searches "[City] dentist," the first thing they see is your star rating and review count. Practices with 50+ reviews and 4.5+ stars get 3–5x more calls than practices with fewer or older reviews.

Reviews are not optional anymore. This system makes getting them easy and systematic.

---

## SECTION 1: HOW TO ASK (The Right Moment + The Right Way)

---

### THE GOLDEN MOMENT: When to Ask

The best time to ask for a review is immediately after a positive moment — when the patient's experience is fresh and their emotional engagement is highest.

**Highest-success triggers:**
1. Patient mentions they feel much better / are happy with their results
2. After a successful cosmetic case (whitening, veneers, Invisalign completion)
3. When a patient says "I was so nervous but you made it so easy"
4. After a pain-free procedure a patient expected to be uncomfortable
5. When a patient has been with you 1+ years and mentions they love the practice
6. When a new patient says "I'm so glad I found you"

**Lower-success moments (avoid):**
- Immediately after extractions or painful procedures
- When the patient seems rushed or distracted
- At the front desk in front of other waiting patients
- During a complex billing or insurance conversation

---

### VERBAL SCRIPTS (In-Office Ask)

---

**SCRIPT V-01 — The Hygienist / Treatment Room Ask (Most Effective)**

*Use at end of appointment, before the patient gets up*

"[Name], thank you so much for coming in today. I really enjoyed working with you. Can I ask a quick favor? We rely almost entirely on Google reviews to help other people in [City] find us. If you had a good experience today and have 60 seconds, a review means the world to us. I can even text you the direct link right now if that makes it easier."

[If they say yes → immediately send the text from Script T-01]  
[If they seem hesitant → "No pressure at all! Just something to keep in mind if you think of it."]

---

**SCRIPT V-02 — The Front Desk Checkout Ask**

*Use as a softer backup when the hygienist didn't have a chance to ask*

"[Name], we hope you had a great visit today! Dr. [Name] asked me to mention — if you have a moment to leave us a Google review, it really helps other families find us. I can text you the direct link if you'd like."

---

**SCRIPT V-03 — The Doctor Ask (For Cosmetic or Major Cases)**

*Use immediately after delivering cosmetic results*

"[Name], I am really happy with how this turned out — and I hope you are too. Honestly, cases like yours are why I love what I do. If you're open to it, I'd love for you to share your experience in a Google review. People searching for [whitening / veneers / implants / Invisalign] need to hear from patients, not just us. Would that be okay?"

## SECTION 4: REVIEW PLATFORM GUIDE

---

### WHERE TO SEND PATIENTS (Priority Order)

| Platform | Priority | Why |
|---------|---------|-----|
| Google Business Profile | ★★★★★ | Shows in search results, Maps; highest impact |
| Healthgrades | ★★★★ | Primary healthcare review platform |
| Yelp | ★★★ | Important in some markets, less in others |
| Facebook | ★★★ | Good for local community reach |
| Zocdoc | ★★ | If you use their booking platform |
| RateMDs | ★★ | Older platform but still indexed |

**Default: Send everyone to Google.** That's where the search-result impact happens. Add other platforms only if you have capacity to manage them.

---

### HOW TO FIND YOUR GOOGLE REVIEW LINK

1. Search your practice name on Google
2. Find your Google Business Profile listing
3. Click "Write a review" (you'll need to be signed into Google)
4. Copy the URL from your browser
5. Shorten it with [bit.ly or similar] for use in texts and emails

**Or:** Go to your Google Business Profile dashboard → Get more reviews → Share review form

## SECTION 5: RESPONDING TO REVIEWS

---

### RESPONSE GUIDE

**Rule 1:** Respond to every single review — positive and negative.

**Rule 2:** Never include patient names or treatment details in your response (HIPAA).

**Rule 3:** Keep positive responses warm but brief. Keep negative responses calm, professional, and solution-oriented.

**Rule 4:** Respond within 24–48 hours.

---

### POSITIVE REVIEW RESPONSES (Rotate These)

---

**Response P-01 (General):**

Thank you so much for taking the time to share your experience, [First Name]! It means so much to our whole team. We're so glad your visit went well and we look forward to seeing you again. — Dr. [Name] and the [Practice Name] team

---

**Response P-02 (After Mentioning a Team Member):**

Thank you, [First Name]! We'll be sure to pass along your kind words to [Team Member Name] — they'll be so happy to hear this. It's always our goal to make every visit as comfortable as possible. See you at your next appointment!

---

**Response P-03 (After Mentioning Anxiety or Fear):**

Thank you so much for sharing this, [First Name]. We understand dental anxiety is very real, and our entire team is committed to making our patients feel safe and comfortable. We're so glad we could be a place you trust. See you soon!

---

**Response P-04 (After Cosmetic Treatment Mention):**

Thank you for this wonderful review! We're thrilled you're happy with your results — seeing the confidence our patients gain from their smile transformations is truly the best part of our work. Can't wait to see you again!

---

**Response P-05 (New Patient):**

Welcome to the [Practice Name] family, [First Name]! We're so happy you found us. It's always a little nerve-wracking choosing a new dentist, and we're grateful you gave us the chance. We look forward to a long relationship with you!

---

### NEGATIVE REVIEW RESPONSES

---

**Response N-01 — Wait Experience / Long Wait Time**

Thank you for bringing this to our attention. We sincerely apologize for the wait — that's not the experience we want for our patients. We're actively working to improve our scheduling process. We'd love the opportunity to make it right. Please contact us directly at [Phone] so we can connect with you personally.

— [Practice Name]

---

**Response N-02 — Billing / Insurance Issue**

We're sorry to hear about your billing experience. We always aim to be transparent about costs and coverage, and we're sorry if we fell short of that here. Billing and insurance can be genuinely complex, and we want to help resolve any confusion. Please reach out to our office manager directly at [Phone] and we'll review your account personally.

— [Practice Name]

---

**Response N-03 — Treatment Dissatisfaction**

We're truly sorry to hear you weren't satisfied with your experience. Patient satisfaction is our top priority, and we take all feedback seriously. We'd very much like the opportunity to speak with you directly about this. Please call us at [Phone] and ask for [Doctor Name / Office Manager] — we want to make this right.

— [Practice Name]

---

**Response N-04 — Rudeness / Communication Issue**

Thank you for taking the time to share your concern. We hold our team to a high standard of communication and care, and we're sorry if we didn't meet that standard for you. This is something we will look into directly. Please feel free to call us at [Phone] — we'd welcome the chance to speak with you.

— [Practice Name]

---

**Response N-05 — Inaccurate Review / Possible Wrong Practice**

Thank you for your review. We want to make sure we address your concern properly — we don't have any record of a visit matching this description. It's possible this review may have been intended for another practice. We'd encourage you to reach out to us directly at [Phone] to verify, and we're happy to clarify any outstanding questions.

— [Practice Name]
