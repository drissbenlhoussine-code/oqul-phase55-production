# Patient Reactivation Scripts for Dental Practices
## Re-Engage Lapsed Patients with Email, Text, and Phone

---

### WHY REACTIVATION MATTERS

Acquiring a new patient costs 5–7x more than reactivating a lapsed one. Your past patients already know your team, trust your practice, and just need the right nudge to return.

A lapsed patient is typically defined as someone who hasn't visited in 18+ months.

**Realistic results:** A well-executed reactivation campaign typically converts 8–15% of lapsed patients back into active patients. For a practice with 200 lapsed patients, that's 16–30 new appointments from people who already like you.

---

## SECTION 1: SEGMENTING YOUR LAPSED PATIENT LIST

Before reaching out, segment by time since last visit:

| Segment | Time Since Last Visit | Tone | Offer |
|--------|----------------------|------|-------|
| A — Warm | 12–18 months | Friendly reminder | Standard recall |
| B — Cooling | 18–36 months | Warmer, personal touch | Small incentive |
| C — Cold | 3–5 years | Re-introduction | Clear incentive |
| D — Very Cold | 5+ years | Start fresh | "We've grown" + offer |

## SECTION 4: PHONE SCRIPTS

---

### PHONE-01 — Warm Reactivation Call (Segment A)

---

**You:** "Hi, may I speak with [First Name]?  
[After greeting] Great! This is [Name] calling from [Practice Name]. I'm just reaching out because our records show it's been about [X months] since your last visit and we wanted to make sure you're still taking care of your smile! How have you been?"

[Let them respond]

"Glad to hear it! We'd love to get you back on the schedule. We have some openings coming up — does [Day] or [Day] work for you?"

[If they hesitate:] "No rush at all! Can I check back with you in a few weeks, or would you prefer we just send you a reminder?"

---

### PHONE-02 — Longer Absence Reactivation (Segment B/C)

---

**You:** "Hi [First Name]! This is [Name] from [Practice Name]. I hope I'm not catching you at a bad time — I'll be quick!

I'm calling because it's been [X years] since your last visit with us and I wanted to personally reach out. We truly care about the patients we've had the privilege of seeing, and I just wanted to make sure you're doing okay and offer to help if there's anything that's kept you from scheduling.

We're also running a special offer for patients we haven't seen in a while — [brief offer description]. No obligation, just a way to help you get back to taking care of yourself.

Would any time in the next few weeks work for a visit?"

[If they're hesitant or mention a concern:]  
"I completely understand. Can I ask — is there anything specific that's made it harder to come in? We might be able to help with that."

[Common objections and responses:]

**"I've been busy":**  
"Totally get it. We're actually open [evenings / Saturdays] now, which a lot of patients find easier. Would [evening time / Saturday] work?"

**"I'm anxious about the dentist":**  
"You're not alone at all — we hear this more than you'd think. Dr. [Name] is actually known for being really gentle and patient. Would you be open to a quick 15-minute get-acquainted visit with no treatment — just to get comfortable with the office again?"

**"I'm not sure about cost":**  
"I hear you. We do offer payment plans and work with [CareCredit / financing options]. We can also start with just a cleaning and exam so you know exactly what's needed before committing to anything. Would that work?"

**"I found another dentist":**  
"Oh, I hope you've been in great hands! If anything ever changes, please don't hesitate to call us. Take care, [First Name]."

---

### PHONE-03 — Post No-Response to Campaign

## SECTION 6: REACTIVATION CAMPAIGN TIMELINE

### Recommended Sequence (90 Days)

| Week | Action | Segment |
|------|--------|---------|
| Week 1 | Email campaign launch (Email A1/B1/C1/D1) | All lapsed |
| Week 2 | Text message follow-up | Non-responders |
| Week 3 | Second email (A2/B2) | Non-responders |
| Week 4 | Phone call campaign | High-value patients only |
| Week 6 | Direct mail (for C/D segments) | Very cold only |
| Week 8 | Final text | Non-responders |
| Week 10 | Phone voicemail (Script PHONE-03) | Final attempt |
| Week 12 | Archive non-responders / remove from active marketing | — |

**Track at month end:** How many appointments booked / response rate / ROI per patient reactivated.
