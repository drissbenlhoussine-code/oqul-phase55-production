# Seasonal Promotion Templates for Dental Practices
## 12 Months of Campaign Ideas, Copy, and Social Posts

---

### HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE

Each month has a theme, a promotion concept, ready-to-use social captions, email copy, and a text blast template. Use these to maintain a consistent marketing presence throughout the year without starting from scratch every month.

**Rule of thumb:** Run one focused promotion per month, promote it consistently across all channels (social + email + in-office), and keep it simple.
