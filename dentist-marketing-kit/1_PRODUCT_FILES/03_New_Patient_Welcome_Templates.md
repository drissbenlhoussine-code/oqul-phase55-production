# New Patient Welcome Templates for Dental Practices
## First Impressions That Build Loyal Patients

---

### WHY THE NEW PATIENT EXPERIENCE MATTERS

The first 30 days of a patient relationship determine whether they become a long-term patient or a one-visit patient. Practices that have a structured new patient welcome process see 40–60% higher retention rates.

These templates cover every touchpoint: from booking confirmation to post-first-visit follow-up.

---

## What to Expect at Your First Visit

**Step 1: Arrival (5 minutes early is great)**  
Check in at the front desk. We'll confirm your information and insurance.

**Step 2: Health History Review**  
Our team will review your completed new patient forms and ask about any current concerns, medications, or previous dental experiences that were challenging.

**Step 3: X-Rays**  
We take digital X-rays to see what's happening beneath the surface. Our digital X-ray technology uses up to 90% less radiation than traditional film X-rays.

**Step 4: Comprehensive Exam**  
Dr. [Name] will review your X-rays and perform a thorough exam of your teeth, gums, jaw, and soft tissues — including an oral cancer screening.

**Step 5: Treatment Discussion**  
We'll walk you through everything we found — clearly, without rushing, and with honest recommendations. You're always in control of your treatment decisions.

**Step 6: Cleaning (if scheduled)**  
Your hygienist will perform a professional cleaning, polishing, and provide personalized home care recommendations.
