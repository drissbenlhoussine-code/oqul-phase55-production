## SECTION 9: GENERAL AD GUIDELINES

---

### AD PERFORMANCE TIPS

**For Facebook / Instagram:**
- Video ads outperform static images (even a simple 15-second talking-head video from the doctor)
- Before-and-after images (whitening, Invisalign) perform well — always get patient consent and follow platform rules
- Testimonial quote graphics build trust
- Carousel ads work well for "What we offer" style ads

**For Google Search Ads:**
- Target high-intent keywords: "dentist near me," "[City] dentist accepting new patients," "emergency dentist [City]"
- Use location extensions so your address shows in the ad
- Call extensions add your phone number directly to the ad
- Match ad copy tightly to the keyword being searched

**Headlines to A/B test:**
- Question format: "Looking for a Dentist in [City] You Can Actually Trust?"
- Urgency: "Same-Day Appointments Available — Call Now"
- Social proof: "50+ Five-Star Reviews — See Why [City] Chooses [Practice Name]"
- Benefit: "[X] Shades Whiter in 60 Minutes — Professional Whitening in [City]"
