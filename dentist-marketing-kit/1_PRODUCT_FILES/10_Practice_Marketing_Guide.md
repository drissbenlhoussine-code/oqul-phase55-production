# Practice Marketing Guide for Dental Offices
## Strategy, Voice, Patient Personas, and 90-Day Action Plan

---

## SECTION 1: YOUR PRACTICE VOICE

The most common mistake in dental marketing is sounding like every other dental office.

Generic: "We provide exceptional dental care in a welcoming environment."  
Better: "We're the practice patients refer their most anxious friends to — because we treat fear like a real thing, not an inconvenience."

Your voice is what makes a potential patient choose you over the other dentist in town. It's built from two things: **who you actually are** and **who your ideal patient is**.

## SECTION 2: PATIENT PERSONAS

Understanding who you're talking to makes your marketing dramatically more effective. Most practices have 3–4 core patient types.

---

### PERSONA 1: THE ANXIOUS AVOIDER

**Who they are:** Adults who haven't been to the dentist in 2–5+ years. They know they should go. They're embarrassed about the gap. They're afraid of judgment, pain, or what they'll find.

**What they need to hear:**
- "No judgment. We see this every week."
- "We go at your pace. You're in control."
- "Let's figure out where you are and where we can go from there."

**Content that works for them:**
- "If you've been putting it off..." posts
- Patient stories about overcoming anxiety
- Behind-the-scenes showing the environment
- Content about sedation options
- Direct, empathetic CTAs: "We're here when you're ready."

**Where they find you:** Google ("anxiety friendly dentist [city]"), Instagram stories, word of mouth from friends

---

### PERSONA 2: THE INFORMED SHOPPER

**Who they are:** Adults actively researching dental options. They're comparing practices on Google, reading reviews, visiting websites. They want specific information and they're skeptical of generic claims.

**What they need to hear:**
- Specific credentials and experience
- Exact pricing and process information
- Real patient reviews (with details, not just stars)
- Clear differentiators — what makes you better

**Content that works for them:**
- "Before and after" case studies
- Detailed procedure explanations
- Review spotlights with specific quotes
- Comparison content (e.g., implants vs. bridges vs. dentures)
- Transparent pricing content

**Where they find you:** Google search, Healthgrades, Yelp, website

---

### PERSONA 3: THE LOYAL FAMILY

**Who they are:** Established households who want one dentist for the whole family. They value consistency, relationships, and convenience. They're loyal once they find somewhere they trust.

**What they need to hear:**
- "We see patients from 1 to 91 years old."
- "We know your family by name — not by chart number."
- "One stop for everyone's dental needs."

**Content that works for them:**
- Family-focused posts (kids' first visit, back to school)
- "We love seeing three generations" type stories
- Convenient scheduling content
- Fun, warm team content

**Where they find you:** Referral from another local family, Nextdoor, Google Maps

---

### PERSONA 4: THE APPEARANCE-MOTIVATED PATIENT

**Who they are:** Adults who care deeply about their smile's aesthetics. They're interested in whitening, Invisalign, veneers, and overall smile improvement. They're often willing to pay premium rates for quality results.

**What they need to hear:**
- "We design smiles that look natural to YOUR face."
- "Here's what's actually possible in your specific situation."
- Visual evidence of outcomes

**Content that works for them:**
- Transformation results (with patient consent)
- Service spotlights: whitening, veneers, bonding, Invisalign
- "What I'd do if I could change one thing about my smile" engagement posts
- Free consultation CTAs

**Where they find you:** Instagram, Facebook ads, word of mouth, Google Images

## SECTION 3: CONTENT STRATEGY BY CHANNEL

---

### INSTAGRAM

**Best for:** Persona 1 (Anxious Avoider) and Persona 4 (Appearance-Motivated)  
**Content mix:** 40% Education, 25% Promotional, 20% Behind the Scenes, 15% Engagement  
**Format:** Mix of Reels (highest reach), Carousels (highest saves), Stories (highest relationship-building)  
**Frequency:** 4–5 feed posts/week + daily Stories

**What works on Instagram:**
- Before/after content (always highest engagement)
- Educational carousels that people save
- Relatable/funny content about teeth habits
- Team personality content in Stories
- Patient testimonials (with permission)

---

### FACEBOOK

**Best for:** Persona 3 (Loyal Family) and local community  
**Content mix:** 30% Promotional, 30% Community/Seasonal, 25% Educational, 15% Reviews  
**Format:** Static images and short videos. Long-form posts work here; they don't work on Instagram.  
**Frequency:** 3–4 posts/week

**What works on Facebook:**
- Local event participation
- Community partnerships
- Holiday and seasonal content
- Review sharing
- Before/after with detailed caption

---

### GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE

**Best for:** Everyone — this is where patients find you in search  
**Updates:** Post weekly (events, promotions, team news, tips)  
**Photos:** Update regularly (team, office, before/after with consent)  
**Reviews:** Respond to 100% within 48 hours

---

### EMAIL

**Best for:** Persona 2 (Informed Shopper) and existing patient retention  
**Send frequency:** 1–2x per month (monthly market update + occasional promotion)  
**List building:** Collect emails at checkout, on your website, in new patient intake  
**What to send:** Monthly newsletter, insurance reminders, seasonal promotions, recall campaigns

## SECTION 4: CONTENT BATCHING SYSTEM

Stop creating content day-by-day. Batch it.

---

### MONTHLY WORKFLOW (2.5 Hours Total)

**1 Hour — Content Planning Session (Start of Month)**
- Pull your content calendar (File 09) for the month
- Choose the promotion focus
- Pull any relevant data (reviews received, seasonal hooks)
- Write 12–15 captions from the caption bank + customize with local details
- Schedule via Meta Business Suite, Later, or Buffer

**30 Minutes — Photo/Video Session (Mid-Month)**
- Take 10–15 behind-the-scenes photos (team, office, equipment)
- Record 1–2 short talking-head Reels (tip of the week, team introduction)
- Capture any patient photos (only with written consent)

**30 Minutes — Story Content (Weekly)**
- Morning market update or dental tip
- Behind-the-scenes moment from that day
- Poll or engagement prompt
- End-of-day reflection or team moment

**30 Minutes — Review Management (Weekly)**
- Respond to any new reviews
- Share standout reviews as social content
- Send review request texts to this week's patients

## SECTION 5: CONVERSION OPTIMIZATION

Getting followers is not the goal. Getting appointments is.

---

### TURNING CONTENT INTO BOOKINGS

**Instagram Bio Setup:**
Line 1: Who you are + where (e.g., "Family & Cosmetic Dentistry in [City]")  
Line 2: Your top differentiator ("Anxiety-friendly • Same-day appointments")  
Line 3: CTA + link ("↓ Book your appointment or call [Phone]")  
Link: Your online booking page or a link tree

**Story CTA System:**
End every educational Story with: "Questions? DM me — I answer personally."  
End every promotional Story with: "DM us [KEYWORD] to [action]"  
Use the countdown sticker for time-sensitive offers

**DM Response Template:**
When someone DMs you about booking:

"Hi [Name]! Thanks for reaching out! We'd love to see you. Are you a new patient or have you visited us before? And is there a particular concern you'd like us to look at, or just a routine cleaning?"

[After they respond, give them 2 specific appointment options and a direct booking link.]

## SECTION 6: METRICS THAT ACTUALLY MATTER

Don't obsess over follower count. Track these instead:

| Metric | Why It Matters | Goal |
|--------|---------------|------|
| New patient appointments from social | Direct revenue metric | Track monthly |
| Google review count and rating | Search visibility impact | +5 reviews/month minimum |
| Instagram saves per post | Content quality indicator | High saves = educational content working |
| Email open rate | List engagement health | 25–35% is healthy for healthcare |
| Profile visits from posts | Content driving discovery | Track which posts drive the most visits |
| DM conversations per week | Pipeline metric | More DMs = more opportunities |
| Appointment no-show rate | Communication effectiveness | Under 10% is target |

## SECTION 7: 90-DAY ACTION PLAN

---

### MONTH 1 — FOUNDATION

**Social/Digital:**
- [ ] Optimize Google Business Profile (photos, hours, description, services)
- [ ] Update Instagram bio (bio + link)
- [ ] Set up 5 Story Highlights (Services, Team, Reviews, Tips, About)
- [ ] Start posting 3x/week feed + daily Stories using content calendar
- [ ] Begin weekly review request texts to all patients

**In-Office:**
- [ ] Train team on review ask scripts (File 02)
- [ ] Implement new patient welcome sequence (File 03)
- [ ] Set up post-appointment follow-up texts

**Email:**
- [ ] Set up email capture on website and intake form
- [ ] Send first monthly newsletter using market update template

---

### MONTH 2 — CONSISTENCY + LEAD GENERATION

**Social:**
- [ ] Run one Story "Ask Me Anything" session
- [ ] Offer first free resource in Stories (buyer guide, dental health checklist)
- [ ] Start responding to every comment and DM within 24 hours
- [ ] Try first short-form Reel (talking to camera — 30–60 seconds)

**Campaigns:**
- [ ] Launch recall campaign to overdue patients (File 05)
- [ ] Plan and run one seasonal promotion from File 07
- [ ] Request 3–5 patient testimonials (with written consent)

**Tracking:**
- [ ] Set up a simple tracking sheet for new-patient-source at booking

---

### MONTH 3 — OPTIMIZATION + GROWTH

**Review:**
- [ ] Review which post types drove the most DMs / profile visits
- [ ] Double down on the content format that's generating the most response
- [ ] Review and refresh Google Business Profile photos
- [ ] Check email open rates — adjust subject line approach if below 25%

**Growth:**
- [ ] Begin consistent Reels posting if not already
- [ ] Consider running first paid Facebook/Instagram ad (File 06)
- [ ] Reach out to one local business for a cross-promotion or community post

**Team:**
- [ ] Celebrate review count growth with the team
- [ ] Recognize team member named most in positive reviews

---

## SECTION 8: COMPLIANCE REMINDER

**Always:**
- Get signed written consent before posting any patient photos or testimonials
- Never post before/after without consent forms that specifically cover social media use
- Do not include Protected Health Information (PHI) in any public post or review response
- Consult your state dental board regulations for advertising rules specific to your state
- Ensure all claims are truthful and substantiable (no "best dentist in [city]" without a source)
