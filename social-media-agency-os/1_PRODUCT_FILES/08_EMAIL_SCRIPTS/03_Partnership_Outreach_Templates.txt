Cold Email:
Subject: Referral partnership? Our clients probably overlap

Hi [NAME],

I came across your work through [HOW — referral, Instagram, LinkedIn, Google] — your portfolio is genuinely impressive. The [specific project or client you noticed] stood out particularly.

I'm [YOUR NAME], founder of [AGENCY NAME] — we handle social media management for [TARGET NICHE] businesses. I've noticed a lot of overlap between our typical clients: businesses that need a new website often also need help with their social media, and vice versa.

I'd love to explore a mutual referral relationship — where we send each other clients when the fit is right. I currently offer a 10% commission for any client you refer who signs up with us.

Would you be open to a 20-minute virtual coffee this week to see if it makes sense?

Best,
[YOUR NAME] | [AGENCY NAME]
[EMAIL] | [INSTAGRAM]

Follow-up (Day 10 if no response):
Subject: Re: Referral partnership?

Hi [NAME],

Just following up on my email from [DATE]. I know inboxes get busy!

If a referral partnership isn't the right fit, no worries at all — I'd still love to connect. If it IS something worth exploring, I'm happy to keep it super simple.

Let me know either way — even a quick "not right now" is totally fine.

[YOUR NAME]

Formal Partnership Email (for serious prospects):
Subject: [YOUR NAME] x [THEIR NAME] — Referral partnership details

Hi [NAME],

Following our call, I wanted to put the partnership details in writing so it's clear for both of us.

HOW IT WORKS:
• You refer a client to [AGENCY NAME] → they sign up → you receive 10% of their first 3 months' retainer
• I refer a client to you → they sign up → you pay me [same or agreed amount]

WHAT I OFFER CLIENTS:
[1-2 sentences on your service and typical client profile]

TYPICAL CLIENT VALUE:
Referrals to me are usually worth $3,000-$9,000 to you over 3 months (10% of $1,000-$3,000/month × 3 months)

NEXT STEP: Would you be willing to try this for 90 days and see how many natural referrals come from each side?

[YOUR NAME]

I work with [type of clients] and you seem to work with [type of clients]. There may be real value in:

• Potentially creating a joint service package that covers both

Would you have 20 minutes for a call this week or next? I'd love to learn more about your work and see if there's a natural fit.

"Hi, I'm [YOUR NAME] and I run [AGENCY NAME] — we help [NICHE] businesses look professional and grow their audience on social media, without the owner having to spend hours every week creating content.

The typical client I'm looking for is a business owner who's doing well — they have customers, a solid reputation — but their social media is inconsistent, they're not sure what to post, and they feel like they should be doing more with it.

If that sounds like someone you know, I'd love a five-minute conversation after the meeting — or an intro over email. And of course, I'm happy to return the favor — if I can ever refer business your way, I will."
