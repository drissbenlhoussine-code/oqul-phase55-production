Subject: Re: [Previous conversation]

Hi [NAME],

I want to address the tone of the recent exchange. I understand you're frustrated, and I want to resolve this — but I'm not able to work productively in a communication environment that includes [name the behavior: personal insults / shouting / threats].

I'm committed to delivering excellent work and resolving whatever issue is at the root of your frustration. I'm also committed to maintaining a professional environment.

Can we reset? I'd like to hop on a call at [TIME] to address the underlying concerns directly and professionally. If that doesn't work, I'm happy to schedule an alternative time.

[YOUR NAME]

[If the behavior continues after this: escalate to a formal warning or contract termination]

"Hey [NAME] — just following up on my email from [DATE] about your social media content. Can you give it a quick look when you get a chance? Content is scheduled to go live [DATE] and I want to make sure you're happy with it first. 🙂"

SITUATION 4 — CHRONIC DISAPPROVER (Hates every piece of content)
Channel: Call first, then email to confirm
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

On the call:
"[NAME], I want to make sure we get the content right for you — and I want to be honest: we've gone through several rounds now and I'm not landing where you want to be. That tells me one of two things: either my interpretation of your brand needs to shift, or our brief-to-execution process has a gap.

Can we do something different? I'd like to schedule a 30-minute visual direction call where I share some reference content and you tell me in real time what resonates and what doesn't. That way I can recalibrate exactly to your taste before creating anything new.

Would [DATE/TIME] work for that?"

Follow-up email:
Subject: Next steps — aligning on creative direction

Hi [NAME],

Following our conversation, I'll be putting together a creative direction document based on our call to serve as our north star for all future content. I'll send that to you by [DATE] for your review.

Going forward, I'll also send visual concept mockups before creating full posts — this way we're aligned before production begins.

I'm committed to getting this right. Thank you for your patience.

[YOUR NAME]

SITUATION 5 — THREATENS TO LEAVE IF YOU DON'T LOWER YOUR PRICE
Channel: Call (urgent) + follow-up email
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

On the call:
"I hear you — and I appreciate you being direct with me.

Let me ask: is this primarily a budget concern, or is there a specific value gap you feel exists between what you're paying and what you're getting?

[Listen to the answer]

If it's budget: I genuinely want to find a solution that works. I could offer [scaled-down version at lower price]. The trade-off would be [reduced deliverables]. Would that work?

If it's value: Tell me specifically what you wish you were getting. I'd rather adjust the service than lose a client I enjoy working with.

What I can't do is reduce the price without reducing the scope — that would be unfair to both of us."

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
