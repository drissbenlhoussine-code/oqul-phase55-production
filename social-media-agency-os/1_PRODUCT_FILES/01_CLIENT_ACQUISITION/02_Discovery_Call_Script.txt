PRE-CALL CHECKLIST (30 minutes before)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
□ Research their Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok for 15 minutes
□ Note their current posting frequency, engagement rate, content style
□ Google them — read about the business, find any press
□ Check if they're running ads (use Facebook Ad Library)
□ Write 2-3 specific observations you can mention early
□ Have your proposal pricing sheet open and ready
□ Test your video/audio — camera on, professional background

OPENING (Minutes 0-2)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

"Hi [NAME]! So great to meet you — I've been looking forward to this.
Before we dive in, I just want to set expectations: I have 45 minutes
blocked for us, and I want to make sure this is genuinely valuable for
you whether we work together or not. Sound good?

Quick note — I actually spent some time on your [Instagram/website/LinkedIn]
before the call. I noticed [SPECIFIC OBSERVATION]. I'm curious about the
story behind that."

[Let them talk. People love being seen.]

RAPPORT & CONTEXT (Minutes 2-7)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

"Before we get into the details, help me understand the bigger picture.
Tell me — how did [BUSINESS NAME] get started? What's the origin story?"

[Listen actively. Reference what they say later.]

"And where are you at right now — how big is the team? What does a
typical week look like for you?"

PAIN DISCOVERY — THE CORE (Minutes 7-22)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

QUESTION 1: "What made you decide to look into social media management
right now? Why now, not 6 months ago?"
[Looking for urgency — the triggering event]

QUESTION 2: "Walk me through what your social media looks like today.
How often are you posting? Who's doing it?"
[Let them expose the chaos. Don't judge.]

QUESTION 3: "When you think about your social media, what frustrates
you most? What keeps you up at night about it?"
[Emotional pain = buying trigger]

QUESTION 4: "What have you already tried? Did you hire someone before,
use an agency, try doing it yourself?"
[Understand past failures — never repeat them]

QUESTION 5: "What results are you expecting from social media? If we
worked together for 6 months, what would success look like to you?"
[Anchor their expectations early]

QUESTION 6: "Why is that important to you? If you hit those goals,
what does that mean for your business?"
[Go deeper — the real 'why' behind the goal]

QUESTION 7: "Have you looked at what competitors are doing on social?
How does yours compare right now?"
[Create a gap between where they are and where they want to be]

QUESTION 8: "What's the cost of NOT solving this? If social media stays
the same for another year, what happens?"
[Make inaction painful — this closes deals]

QUESTION 9: "What's your timeline? Are you looking to get started soon,
or is this more exploratory right now?"
[Qualify urgency and decision speed]

QUESTION 10: "Who else is involved in this decision? Is it just you, or
do you have a business partner / CFO / founder who also needs to be looped in?"
[Identify decision makers — don't waste time with non-buyers]

QUESTION 11: "Do you have a rough budget in mind for marketing? I want
to make sure I'm showing you options that are realistic for where you're at."
[Budget qualification — do this gently but clearly]

QUESTION 12: "What would make you say yes to working with someone today?
What's most important — speed, quality, communication, price?"
[Understand their buying criteria before presenting]

YOUR SOLUTION — BRIDGE (Minutes 22-32)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

"Based on everything you've shared, I want to summarize what I'm hearing.
You're dealing with [PAIN 1], [PAIN 2], and [PAIN 3]. You need [GOAL],
and you need it by [TIMELINE]. Does that feel accurate?"

[Wait for yes. This is critical — you're mirroring their words back.]

"Here's how I work with businesses like yours..."
[Walk through your process in 3-4 simple steps]

"Most of my clients in [THEIR NICHE] start with [PACKAGE NAME] because
[SPECIFIC REASON TIED TO THEIR PAIN]. It includes [TOP 3 DELIVERABLES]."

PRICING PRESENTATION (Minutes 32-37)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

"Investment-wise, I work with clients on a monthly retainer basis.
Most businesses in your situation start between $1,500 and $2,500/month,
depending on platforms and scope. Does that range feel workable for where
you're at?"

[PAUSE. Silence. Let them react. Do not fill the silence.]

If yes → "Great. Let me put together a proposal by [DATE] that shows
exactly what that looks like for your business."

If hesitation → [See Objection Handling document]

CLOSE & NEXT STEPS (Minutes 37-42)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

"So here's what I'd like to do: I'll put together a custom proposal for
you by [DATE — within 48 hours]. It'll include a few options at different
levels so you can choose what makes the most sense.

Once you have that, can we schedule a 20-minute follow-up call to walk
through it together? I find that works better than email for these conversations."

[Book the follow-up call NOW — do not leave it open-ended]

"Last question before I let you go — is there anything I didn't ask that
you think I should know to make the best proposal possible?"

POST-CALL ACTION (within 2 hours)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

□ Send thank-you email referencing 2-3 specific things they shared
□ Update lead tracker with notes, stage, and follow-up date
□ Begin proposal — send within 48 hours maximum
□ Connect on LinkedIn with a personalized note
