MASTER RULE: An objection is not a no. It's a request for more information.
Your job is to acknowledge, empathize, reframe, and ask a clarifying question.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
OBJECTION 1: "It's too expensive."
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
"I hear you — and I want to make sure the investment makes sense.
Can I ask: is it that the number is more than you budgeted, or that
you're not sure the ROI is there?

[If budget]: 'I have a Starter option at $997/month that could be a
great starting point. The goal would be to show you ROI in 90 days,
then we can scale from there. Would that feel more accessible?'

[If ROI concern]: 'Totally fair. Let me share what this looked like for
[CLIENT]. They were at the same point — and within 60 days they were
getting [X leads/month] from Instagram alone. The math was clear pretty quickly.
What would ROI look like for your business to make this a clear yes?'"

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
OBJECTION 2: "I need to think about it."
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
"Of course — this is a real decision. I want to make sure you have
everything you need to think clearly about it.

What specifically do you need to think through? Is it the price,
whether the timing is right, or something else I might be able
to help clarify right now?"

[Listen and address the real concern underneath]

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
OBJECTION 3: "I can do it myself."
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
"Absolutely — and honestly, if you have the time and enjoy it,
that could be the right call. Can I ask — how much time are you
spending on social media right now each week?

[They answer]

What's that time worth to you at your hourly rate? If it's more
than the cost of outsourcing it, the math usually works out pretty clearly.
Plus — what's the opportunity cost of the client calls, product development,
or revenue work you could be doing instead?"

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
OBJECTION 4: "I had a bad experience with an agency before."
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
"I'm really sorry to hear that — and honestly, that's one of the
most common things I hear. Can you tell me what happened? I want to
understand what went wrong.

[Listen]

That makes complete sense. Here's what's different about how I work:
[1-2 specific things that address their exact pain from the bad experience].

And I'm happy to do a trial month so you can see the difference
before committing longer-term. Would that help?"

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
OBJECTION 5: "I don't see the ROI in social media."
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
"That's a really honest and fair point. Social media ROI can be
hard to see if you're not tracking it right.

Let me ask: when a potential customer finds you online — whether
Google, referral, or word of mouth — do they check your social
media before deciding to contact you? [Almost always yes]

That's ROI. Not always a direct click → buy, but the trust that
convinces them to pick up the phone or send an email.

That said — I build tracking into every account so we know exactly
what's attributable to social. What would you need to see in month 1
to feel like this is working?"

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
OBJECTION 6: "My budget is lower than that."
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
"No problem — let me ask what you're comfortable with, and I'll
tell you honestly if I can make something work within that.

[They share number]

At $[their number], here's what I could offer: [scaled-down version
of your service]. The key is starting with one platform, proving ROI,
and building from there. Sound like something worth exploring?"

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
OBJECTION 7: "We already have someone."
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
"That's great — good to know you're already investing in this.
Can I ask how it's going? Are you happy with the results you're seeing,
or is there something you feel is missing?

[If happy]: 'Good to hear. If that ever changes or you want a second
opinion, feel free to reach out. Would it be okay if I followed up
in 3 months?'

[If not completely happy]: 'Tell me more about what's missing.
I might be able to offer a different perspective, or even just
audit what's happening and give you some direction.'"

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
OBJECTION 8: "I need to ask my business partner."
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
"Of course — this is a team decision. Would it be helpful to have
a call with both of you together so I can answer any questions
your partner has directly? It usually saves time compared to
you relaying the information.

If a joint call doesn't work, I'll make sure the proposal has
everything they need to evaluate it on their own. What's the
best email to include them on the proposal?"

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
OBJECTION 9: "Not the right time."
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
"I completely understand. When do you think the timing would be better?

[They give a timeframe]

That makes sense. Can I follow up with you on [specific date]?
And in the meantime, is there anything I can send you that might
be useful — like a quick audit of your Instagram or some
industry benchmarks for your niche?"

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
OBJECTION 10: "Can I try just one month?"
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
"I understand wanting to test first — I'd feel the same way.
The challenge with one month is that social media results compound
over time. One month shows potential; three months shows trajectory.

What if we did a 3-month commitment with a 30-day checkpoint review?
If by day 30 you're not seeing the direction you hoped, we can
discuss adjusting. Would that feel safer?"

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
OBJECTIONS 11-15 (condensed)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

11. "What if I want to cancel?"
"After the initial 3-month term, it's month-to-month with 30 days notice.
No hard feelings — my goal is that you'd never want to leave."

12. "Are you experienced enough?"
"Here's what I'd say: [X case studies]. Let the results speak. I'm also
happy to do a small paid project first so you can experience the work
before committing to monthly management."

13. "Why should I choose you over others?"
"Honestly? You should choose whoever is the best fit for your business.
What I can tell you is: [3 specific differentiators]. The best way to
know is to experience working with me — and that starts with the proposal."

14. "I don't need social media."
"I'd love to hear more about what's driving new business for you right now.
[Let them answer]. Interesting. Does social media ever come up in how
people find or validate you before buying? [They almost always say yes]"

15. "Let me compare others first."
"Totally fair — you should. Can I ask what you'll be comparing on?
If it's just price, I may not be the cheapest — but if it's results and
systems, I'd love the chance to show you why we're worth the difference."
