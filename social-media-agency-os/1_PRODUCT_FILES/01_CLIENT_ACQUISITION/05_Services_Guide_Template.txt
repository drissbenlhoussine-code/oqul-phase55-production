FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q1: How long are your contracts?
A: We work on a 3-month minimum commitment, then month-to-month with 30-day notice.

A: We design graphics using stock photography. If you want custom brand photography,
   we'll provide a brief and direction for your photographer.

Q3: How do you get access to our accounts?
A: We use scheduling tools like Later or Buffer — you never need to share passwords.

Q4: How many revisions do I get?
A: Starter: 1 round. Growth: 2 rounds. Agency: Unlimited.
   Revisions must be requested within 48 hours of content preview.

Q5: What platforms do you manage?
A: Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, Pinterest, Twitter/X. We recommend
   starting with 1-2 platforms and expanding based on your audience.

Q6: Will you respond to my followers' comments and DMs?
A: Yes. Community management is included in all packages (Mon-Fri standard,
   7-days on Agency plan). We use your brand voice for all responses.

Q7: Do you run paid ads?
A: We manage ad strategy and copy. Ad spend (budget paid to Meta/Google) is
   handled directly by you. We recommend starting with $300-$500/month.

Q8: Can I cancel anytime?
A: After the initial 3-month period, you may cancel with 30 days' written notice.

Q9: What results can I expect?
A: Results vary by industry and goals. Typical clients see 15-40% follower
   growth in the first 3 months, and 2-5x engagement rate improvement.

Q10: What if I'm not happy with the content?
A: We address any concerns immediately. We stand behind our work — if the
   content doesn't align with your brand, we revise until it does.
