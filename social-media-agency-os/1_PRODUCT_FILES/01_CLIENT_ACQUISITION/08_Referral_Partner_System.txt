WHO TO TARGET AS REFERRAL PARTNERS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Best referral partners serve the same clients but don't compete with you:
• Web designers / developers
• Brand photographers
• Business coaches / consultants
• PR agencies
• Copywriters
• Bookkeepers / accountants
• Marketing strategists (non-social)
• Business coaches for your target niche

COMMISSION STRUCTURE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Standard: 10% of first 3 months' retainer
Premium Partner: 15% of first 3 months (for high-volume referrers)
One-Time: $200-$500 flat fee per closed referral

Example: You close a $2,000/month client from a referral
→ 10% × $2,000 × 3 months = $600 paid to partner
