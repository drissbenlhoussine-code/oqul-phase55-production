OVERVIEW
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Your lead tracker is the heartbeat of your agency's sales pipeline. Without it,
leads fall through the cracks and revenue stays unpredictable. This system turns
your pipeline into something you can review in 10 minutes every Monday morning.

SPREADSHEET COLUMN SETUP (copy these headers exactly)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Column A  │ Lead Name / Business Name
Column B  │ Contact Name
Column C  │ Email Address
Column D  │ Phone / WhatsApp
Column E  │ Platform Found (Instagram / LinkedIn / Referral / Cold Email / DM)
Column F  │ Date Added
Column G  │ Stage (see Stage System below)
Column H  │ Deal Value / Monthly Retainer Estimate
Column I  │ Discovery Call Date
Column J  │ Proposal Sent Date
Column K  │ Follow-Up Date (next action)
Column L  │ Notes (pain points, budget mentioned, objections)
Column M  │ Outcome (Won / Lost / Ghosted / Not Ready Yet)
Column N  │ Why Lost (Too Expensive / Chose Competitor / DIY / Timing)

STAGE SYSTEM (Color-Code Each Row)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Stage 1 — NEW LEAD          (Gray)    Just discovered, no contact yet
Stage 2 — CONTACTED         (Yellow)  Initial DM/email sent, awaiting reply
Stage 3 — RESPONDED         (Blue)    They replied, building rapport
Stage 4 — CALL BOOKED       (Orange)  Discovery call scheduled
Stage 5 — PROPOSAL SENT     (Purple)  Proposal delivered, awaiting decision
Stage 6 — NEGOTIATING       (Pink)    Active back-and-forth on terms/price
Stage 7 — WON ✅             (Green)   Contract signed, onboarding in progress
Stage 8 — LOST ✗             (Red)     Deal did not close — log reason

LEAD QUALITY SCORING (Score 1-5 on each criterion = max 25)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. Budget Fit       — Can they clearly afford your minimum retainer?
2. Decision Speed   — Do they sound ready to move within 30 days?
3. Pain Urgency     — How badly do they need social media help RIGHT NOW?
4. Communication    — Are they responsive and professional in messages?
5. Niche Fit        — Do you have experience or case studies in their niche?

Score 20-25 = Priority A (pursue aggressively)
Score 14-19 = Priority B (nurture with follow-ups)
Score below 14 = Priority C (low effort until they warm up)

WEEKLY REVIEW PROCESS (Monday, 15 minutes)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

• Review all Stage 4-6 leads — what's the next action?
• Archive any lead inactive for 60+ days into "Cold Leads" tab
• Identify top 3 Priority A leads to focus on this week
• Update Follow-Up Date column for all open leads
• Calculate your pipeline value (sum of all Stage 1-6 Deal Values)
• Set a revenue goal for the week based on pipeline

FOLLOW-UP CADENCE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

After initial contact:       Follow up Day 3, Day 7, Day 14, Day 30
After proposal sent:         Follow up Day 2, Day 5, Day 10
After "I need to think":     Follow up Day 5, Day 14, Day 30
After going cold:            Re-engage at 60 days and 90 days

RULE: The fortune is in the follow-up. 80% of deals close after 5+ touchpoints.
Most SMMs give up after 1-2 follow-ups and leave money on the table.
