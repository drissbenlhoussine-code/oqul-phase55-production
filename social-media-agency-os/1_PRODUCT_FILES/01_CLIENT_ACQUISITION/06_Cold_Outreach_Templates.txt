RULE BEFORE USING ANY TEMPLATE: Personalize the [BRACKETS] with specific,
researched details. Generic outreach = zero responses. Specific = replies.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
TEMPLATE 1 — INSTAGRAM DM (Warm Observation)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

"Hey [NAME]! I've been following [BUSINESS NAME] for a while —
love [SPECIFIC POST or SOMETHING GENUINE].

Quick question: I noticed you're posting [describe their current posting habit —
e.g., "about 2-3x per week"]. Is growing on Instagram something you're actively
working on right now, or is it more of a side thing?

I'm a social media strategist and was curious — always nice to connect with
people building something cool in the [NICHE] space."

[Wait for reply. DO NOT pitch in the first message.]

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
TEMPLATE 2 — INSTAGRAM DM (Result Lead)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

"Hi [NAME] — I just helped a [similar business in their niche] go from
[X followers] to [Y followers] in 90 days and start generating [X leads/month]
from Instagram.

I was looking at your profile and I have 2-3 specific ideas that I think
could do the same for [BUSINESS NAME]. Would you be open to a quick
15-minute chat this week to share them? No sales pitch — just ideas."

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
TEMPLATE 3 — LINKEDIN CONNECTION REQUEST
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Connection note (max 300 characters):
"Hi [NAME] — I read your post about [TOPIC] and it resonated. I work
with [NICHE] businesses on social media strategy. Would love to connect
and learn more about [BUSINESS NAME]."

Follow-up message after they accept (send 24-48 hours later):
"Thanks for connecting, [NAME]! I noticed [SPECIFIC OBSERVATION about
their company's LinkedIn presence]. I actually put together a quick
5-minute audit of [COMPANY NAME]'s LinkedIn — would it be helpful
if I shared it? No catch, just think there are some quick wins available."

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
TEMPLATE 4 — COLD EMAIL (Problem-Led)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Subject: [BUSINESS NAME] — quick question about social media

Hi [NAME],

I was researching [NICHE] businesses in [CITY/REGION] and came across
[BUSINESS NAME]. Really impressive [something specific — product, mission, story].

I noticed your [Instagram / Facebook / LinkedIn] is posting [observation —
e.g., "occasionally but without a consistent strategy"]. I work with
[similar business type] to fix exactly this — and the results are usually
pretty dramatic (one of my clients, [BUSINESS], went from 800 to 4,200
followers in 4 months with a 340% increase in profile visits).

Would it be worth a 15-minute call this week to see if we could do
something similar for [BUSINESS NAME]?

[YOUR NAME]
[TITLE], [AGENCY NAME]

P.S. I put together a quick 3-point audit of your [PLATFORM] that I
can share on the call — no obligation.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
TEMPLATE 5, 6, 7 — FOLLOW-UP SEQUENCE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Follow-Up 1 (Day 3 after no reply):
"Hi [NAME] — just following up on my message below. Totally understand
if the timing isn't right. I did put together those audit notes for
[BUSINESS NAME] — happy to share regardless of whether we work together.
Worth 5 minutes?"

Follow-Up 2 (Day 7):
"[NAME] — last follow-up from me, I promise.
Two options:
A) Quick 15-min call this week
B) I send you the audit notes and you look when you have time
Which works better?"

Follow-Up 3 (Day 30 — Re-engage):
"Hey [NAME] — it's been a month since I reached out.
Wanted to check back in because I just finished a project with
[SIMILAR BUSINESS TYPE] and thought of you. We achieved [RESULT] for them.
If social media is something you're thinking about again, I'd love to reconnect."

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
TEMPLATE 8 — REFERRAL ASK (from existing client)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

"Hey [CLIENT NAME] — quick one. I'm looking to take on 2 new clients
this month in the [NICHE] space. Do you know anyone in your network who
might benefit from what we do together?

If you refer someone and they sign up, I'll [give you a free month /
add a bonus deliverable / send you a gift card] — happy to return the favor.

No pressure at all — just thought I'd ask since you've seen the results firsthand."
