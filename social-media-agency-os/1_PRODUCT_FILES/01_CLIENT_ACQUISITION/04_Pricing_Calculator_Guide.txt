STEP 3 — VALUE-BASED PRICING
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

For premium positioning, price based on VALUE delivered, not hours worked.

Ask yourself:
• If social media generates 2 new clients/month for this business,
  and each client is worth $2,000 to them, that's $4,000 in new revenue.
• Charging $1,500/month means you're delivering 2.7x ROI.
• Charging $2,500/month still delivers 1.6x ROI — easy to justify.

Value-based pricing formula:
Expected monthly value to client:  $________
Your fee as % of that value:         ___% (aim for 20-40%)
= Value-Based Price:                $________

STEP 4 — MARKET POSITIONING
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Budget Range ($300-$800/mo):
  • One-person show, 1-2 platforms
  • Basic graphics, no strategy
  • Target: micro-businesses, solopreneurs
  • Risk: high churn, low-quality clients

Mid-Range ($1,000-$2,500/mo):
  • Professional service, 1-3 platforms
  • Strategy included, full content creation
  • Target: established small businesses
  • This is where most agencies should position

Premium ($3,000-$6,000+/mo):
  • Full-service agency, 3+ platforms
  • Team of specialists, paid ad management
  • Target: funded businesses, larger SMBs
  • Requires strong case studies and authority

RECOMMENDATION: Start at mid-range and move up as you build case studies.

STEP 5 — WHEN TO RAISE YOUR RATES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Raise rates when ANY of these are true:
• You have a waitlist of 2+ potential clients
• You've had the same rates for 12+ months
• You have 3+ strong case studies / testimonials
• Your time is 90%+ booked
• You're attracting difficult clients (symptom of undercharging)
• Your expenses have increased significantly
• You've added certifications or expertise

Rate increase notice: Give current clients 30-60 days' notice.
Script: "As of [DATE], my rates will increase to $[NEW RATE]/month.
Your current service will remain the same, and this reflects the
growing results and investment I'm making in your account."
