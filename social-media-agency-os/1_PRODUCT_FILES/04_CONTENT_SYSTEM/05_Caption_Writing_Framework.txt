THE AIDA FRAMEWORK FOR SOCIAL CAPTIONS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
A — ATTENTION:  The hook. First line/sentence that stops the scroll.
I — INTEREST:   Give them a reason to keep reading. Tease the value.
D — DESIRE:     Make them want what you're offering/teaching/showing.
A — ACTION:     Clear CTA — what you want them to DO right now.

Example AIDA Caption:
A: "Most business owners waste their first $500 on social media. Here's why."
I: "They boost posts with no strategy, hoping someone will click. I've seen it hundreds of times — and the result is always the same: $0 ROI and a frustrating experience."
D: "The businesses that win at social media build an organic foundation first. A great content calendar, consistent posting, and real community engagement. THEN paid ads amplify what's already working."
A: "If you want to stop wasting money and start seeing real results, comment 'STRATEGY' below and I'll send you our free starter guide."

8 HOOK FORMULAS + 3 EXAMPLES EACH
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

HOOK 1 — THE BOLD CLAIM
Formula: "The [most/only/biggest] thing I've learned about [topic] is..."
Examples:
• "The most important thing I've learned about growing on Instagram is that consistency beats virality every single time."
• "The only metric that actually matters in the first 90 days of managing a new account is engagement rate — not followers."
• "The biggest mistake I see businesses make with social media is paying for followers before fixing their content."

HOOK 2 — THE QUESTION HOOK
Formula: "What if you could [desired outcome] without [pain/obstacle]?"
Examples:
• "What if your social media ran smoothly every month — without you lifting a finger?"
• "What if you never had to guess what to post again?"
• "What if your Instagram could generate 10 qualified leads per month on autopilot?"

HOOK 3 — THE CONTROVERSIAL OPINION
Formula: "Hot take: [widely accepted belief] is [wrong/overrated/outdated]."
Examples:
• "Hot take: posting every day is the WORST advice for most small businesses."
• "Hot take: your number of followers is a vanity metric. Here's what actually matters."
• "Hot take: most social media 'coaches' have never managed a real client account."

HOOK 4 — THE NUMBER HOOK
Formula: "[Number] things [audience] do that [result]:"
Examples:
• "5 things every successful social media manager does on Monday mornings:"
• "3 reasons your Instagram engagement dropped this month (and how to fix each one):"
• "7 signs it's time to raise your social media management rates:"

HOOK 5 — THE STORY OPENING
Formula: "Last [week/month/year], [specific situation happened]..."
Examples:
• "Last month, a client came to me completely burned out from posting every day with zero results. Here's what we changed."
• "Last year, I charged $300/month for social media management. Here's what shifted."
• "Last week, I audited 12 Instagram accounts in one day. Here's the #1 problem they all shared."

HOOK 6 — THE MYTH BUSTER
Formula: "Stop [believing/doing] this about [topic]. Here's the truth:"
Examples:
• "Stop believing that more hashtags = more reach. The research says otherwise."
• "Stop posting at '9am on Tuesday' because someone told you it's optimal. Your specific audience data matters more."
• "Stop thinking you need a professional camera for social media content. Your phone is fine. Your strategy isn't."

HOOK 7 — THE URGENCY/PATTERN INTERRUPT
Formula: "If you're [common situation], stop. Right now."
Examples:
• "If you're still posting without a content strategy, stop. You're wasting your effort."
• "If you're responding to DMs once a day, stop. The algorithm rewards fast responses."
• "If you haven't optimized your Instagram bio this year, stop scrolling and go fix it."

HOOK 8 — THE CURIOSITY GAP
Formula: "I [did something specific]. The results were [surprising outcome]. Here's what happened:"
Examples:
• "I tested posting at 6am vs. 9am for 30 days on the same client account. The results surprised me."
• "I stopped using hashtags for 2 weeks on a client's Instagram. Here's what actually happened to their reach."
• "I charged three times my old rate to a new client last week. They said yes immediately. Here's what that taught me."

CAPTION LENGTH GUIDE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

SHORT (1-3 lines): Use for strong visuals that speak for themselves, memes, aesthetic content, product photos. Let the image do the work.

MEDIUM (4-10 lines): Use for tips, announcements, behind-the-scenes. Enough to deliver value, short enough to hold attention.

LONG (10+ lines): Use for storytelling, case studies, thought leadership, vulnerable/personal posts. These drive the most saves and shares when done right.

PLATFORM DEFAULTS:
Instagram: Medium works for most. Long captions hide behind "more" button — use when the story is worth the scroll.
LinkedIn: Long captions perform BEST. LinkedIn rewards dwell time and the feed shows more text.
TikTok: Very short — 1-3 lines max. The video is the content, not the caption.
Facebook: Medium. Longer than Twitter/X but don't bury people in text.

EMOJI USAGE GUIDE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

HOW MANY: 2-5 emojis per caption is the sweet spot. More looks spammy.
WHERE: Start of lines to add visual breaks. End of sentences for tone.
AVOID: Avoid using emojis that feel off-brand. A luxury brand uses ✨ not 🤪.
IN LINE BREAKS: Emojis at the start of a new line help readability on mobile.

Professional/Luxury brands: ✨ 🖤 ✦ ○ → ● — Keep it minimal and intentional.
Friendly/Consumer brands: 🔥 💪 🎉 ❤️ 👇 — More expressive is fine.
