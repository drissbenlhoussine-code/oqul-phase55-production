THE WORKFLOW AT A GLANCE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

[BRIEF] → [CREATE] → [INTERNAL REVIEW] → [CLIENT PREVIEW] → [REVISIONS] → [FINAL APPROVAL] → [SCHEDULE] → [POST] → [ARCHIVE]

STAGE-BY-STAGE BREAKDOWN
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

STAGE 1 — BRIEF (Day 1-2 of each month)
Who: Account Manager
What: Fill out Content Brief for each post. Add to content calendar.
Trigger to move on: Brief is complete and assigned to creator.
Time: 30-60 mins per client per month for full batch briefing

STAGE 2 — CREATE (Day 3-8)
Who: Content Creator (graphics) + Copywriter (captions)
What: Produce all visuals and captions per brief. Save to shared folder.
Naming convention: CLIENT-IG-POST-2025-01-15-v1
Trigger to move on: All files saved and marked "Ready for Internal Review"
Time: 2-4 hours per client per batch (10-12 posts)

STAGE 3 — INTERNAL REVIEW (Day 8-9)
Who: Account Manager or Agency Owner
What: Review every post against the 15-point checklist below.
Trigger to move on: All posts pass internal review OR revisions requested from creator.
Time: 45-90 mins per client batch

INTERNAL REVIEW CHECKLIST (15 items):
□ No spelling or grammar errors
□ Brand voice is consistent throughout
□ Caption hook is strong (would YOU stop scrolling?)
□ Key message is clear in the first 2 lines
□ CTA is present and specific
□ Hashtags are appropriate (not banned, count is correct)
□ Image is high resolution (no pixelation or blur)
□ Brand colors are correct (check HEX codes)
□ Logo is present where required
□ No incorrect claims, exaggerations, or brand inconsistencies
□ Dimensions are correct for the platform and format
□ Any links in caption/bio are working
□ Content aligns with brand strategy and content pillars
□ No competitor mentions or comparisons
□ Content feels appropriate for platform (LinkedIn ≠ TikTok tone)

STAGE 4 — CLIENT PREVIEW (Day 9-10)
Who: Account Manager sends to client
What: Package up all posts in a clean document (PDF or Google Slides)
Format per post: Image/graphic + Caption draft + Hashtags + Publish date + Format
Delivery: Email or shared Google Drive folder
Subject line: "[CLIENT NAME] — Content Preview — [MONTH] — Please review by [DATE]"
Trigger to move on: Client responds with feedback OR deadline passes

STAGE 5 — REVISIONS (Day 10-12)
Who: Creator or Copywriter
What: Make approved changes only. Do not redesign without new brief.
Max rounds: Per contract (Starter: 1, Growth: 2, Agency: Unlimited)
Revision request policy: Changes must be specific ("Change headline color to navy" ✓ vs "it doesn't feel right" ✗)
For vague feedback: "Could you help me get more specific? For example, is it the color, the font, or the overall layout that doesn't feel right?"
Trigger to move on: Revisions complete, files updated to v2, sent back to client

STAGE 6 — FINAL APPROVAL (Day 12-13)
Who: Client
What: Final sign-off. "Approved" reply or checkmark per post.
If no response within 48 hours: Send reminder. If still no response 24 hours before post: post as-is per contract clause.
Trigger to move on: Written approval received

STAGE 7 — SCHEDULE (Day 13-14)
Who: Account Manager or Scheduler
What: Upload all approved content to scheduling tool (Later, Hootsuite, Buffer)
Pre-schedule checklist:
□ Image uploaded at correct dimensions
□ Caption pasted exactly as approved
□ Hashtags included
□ Posting time set for optimal time (check analytics)
□ Link in bio updated if required
□ Tagged accounts added if applicable
Trigger to move on: All posts scheduled, confirmed in tool

STAGE 8 — POST & MONITOR (Publish Day)
Who: Community Manager or Account Manager
What: Confirm post went live (sometimes scheduling tools fail). Monitor first 60 minutes.
First 60 Minutes Protocol:
• 0-5 min: Check post is live. Reply to first comment immediately if any.
• 5-15 min: Share to Stories (if Instagram). Add poll or question sticker if relevant.
• 15-30 min: Engage on 5-10 posts from similar accounts in the same niche.
• 30-60 min: Reply to ALL comments. Respond to DMs triggered by post. Check early insights.

STAGE 9 — ARCHIVE (End of month)
Who: Account Manager
What: Move all posted content to the client's archive folder.
Folder structure: /[CLIENT]/[YEAR]/[MONTH]/[PLATFORM]/Posted
Keep: Final approved versions. Delete: Drafts and rejected versions after 30 days.

EMERGENCY POST PROCESS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

When client needs a post live in under 24 hours:
1. Client submits emergency request with full brief via [preferred channel]
2. Agency confirms receipt and capacity within 2 hours
3. Emergency fee applied: $75-$150 depending on complexity (in contract)
4. Turnaround: 6-12 hours for simple post, 12-18 hours for Reel
5. Approval window: 1 hour for emergency posts (client must be available)
6. No guarantee of production quality equal to standard workflow

VERSION CONTROL NAMING SYSTEM:
CLIENT-PLATFORM-TYPE-DATE-VERSION
Example: ABC_IG_POST_2025-01-15_v1
                                   _v2 (after revisions)
                                   _FINAL (after approval)
                                   _POSTED (after going live)
