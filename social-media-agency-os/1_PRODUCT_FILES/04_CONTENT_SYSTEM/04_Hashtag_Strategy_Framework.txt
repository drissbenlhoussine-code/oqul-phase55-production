STRATEGY GUIDE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

HOW TO MIX HASHTAG SIZES (per post, use 15-20 on Instagram):
• 5 MEGA hashtags (1M+ posts) — broad reach, competitive
• 5 MID-RANGE hashtags (100K-1M posts) — good discovery
• 5-10 NICHE hashtags (under 100K) — high engagement, less competition

ROTATION RULE: Never use the same set twice in a row. Rotate through 3 sets.
REFRESH: Research new hashtags monthly — trends change.

PLATFORM DIFFERENCES:
• Instagram: 15-20 hashtags. Put in caption or first comment. Both work.
• TikTok: 3-5 hashtags only. Mix trending + niche. Caption space is limited.
• LinkedIn: 3-5 hashtags at end of post. Professional tone only.
• Pinterest: 2-5 in description, but keywords matter more than hashtags here.

BANNED/SHADOW-BAN RISK HASHTAGS TO AVOID (check regularly):
#beautyblogger, #sunbathing, #loseweight — Instagram bans these periodically.
Before using any hashtag, search it on Instagram first and check if posts appear.
