THE DELEGATION DECISION MATRIX
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

KEEP (Owner/Founder handles):
• Client strategy and direction
• Proposal writing and pricing
• Discovery calls and sales
• Client onboarding (first call)
• Final approval on all content before it goes to client
• Hiring and performance management
• Billing and financial decisions

DELEGATE FIRST (your first hire):
• Caption writing / copywriting
• Graphic design / template execution
• Scheduling and publishing
• Community management (responses)
• Hashtag research
• Analytics data collection (the pulling, not the analysis)

DELEGATE LATER (as you scale):
• Content strategy development (Account Manager)
• Analytics analysis and report writing (Account Manager)
• Client check-in calls (Account Manager)
• Contractor management (Senior team member)

ROLE DEFINITIONS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

CONTENT CREATOR / GRAPHIC DESIGNER:
Does: Creates graphics, visual assets, Canva posts, image selection
Doesn't do: Write captions, make strategy decisions, communicate with clients
Pay range: $15-$35/hour or $20-$50/post depending on complexity

COPYWRITER / CAPTION WRITER:
Does: Writes captions, scripts for Reels, content calendar copy, email drafts
Doesn't do: Design graphics, post content, communicate with clients
Pay range: $20-$50/hour or $25-$75/caption depending on depth

VIDEO EDITOR:
Does: Edits Reels, TikToks, short-form video content
Doesn't do: Film content, write scripts, make strategy decisions
Pay range: $25-$75/video depending on complexity and length

COMMUNITY MANAGER:
Does: Responds to comments and DMs, engages with similar accounts, reports issues
Doesn't do: Create content, make decisions about content direction
Pay range: $15-$30/hour; or per-client flat rate ($150-$400/month per account)

ACCOUNT MANAGER (higher level hire):
Does: Manages client relationships, writes reports, runs strategy calls, oversees content team
Doesn't do: Replace owner in sales, make major client decisions alone
Pay range: $25-$60/hour or salaried at $3,000-$6,000/month depending on market

HOW TO WRITE A TASK BRIEF FOR A CONTRACTOR
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Every task you delegate needs a written brief. Verbal briefs = wrong output.

BRIEF TEMPLATE:
─────────────────────────────────
Task: [Clear one-sentence description]
Client: [Client name]
Due: [Date and time]
Deliverable: [Exactly what you expect to receive — file format, length, etc.]
Context: [Why this post exists — campaign, promotion, content pillar]
Brand voice: [Link to brand guide OR 2-3 sentences describing tone]
Reference examples: [Link to 2-3 approved posts that hit the mark]
Questions?: [Your Slack handle or email for questions before deadline]
─────────────────────────────────

CONTRACTOR MANAGEMENT SYSTEM
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Track each contractor in a spreadsheet:
Columns: Name | Role | Hourly/Flat Rate | Active Clients | Total Hours (month) | Invoice Due | Quality Score (1-5)

Quality scoring system:
5 — Consistently excellent. No revisions needed.
4 — Good. One revision round typically needed.
3 — Average. Needs improvement in [specific area].
2 — Below standard. On performance improvement or being replaced.
1 — Do not hire again.

QUALITY CONTROL PROCESS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Step 1: Contractor submits work to you (NOT to client directly)
Step 2: You review against the internal checklist (see Approval Workflow doc)
Step 3: If approved → forward to client with your name on it
Step 4: If revision needed → send written notes to contractor with specific changes
Step 5: Log quality score for the month

SCALING TIMELINE — WHEN TO HIRE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Hire #1 (Copywriter or Designer):
Trigger: You're spending 30+ hours/week on client work; at 4+ clients
Goal: Reduce your time per client by 30-40%
Cost: $500-$1,500/month depending on volume

Hire #2 (Second specialist or add capacity):
Trigger: 6+ clients; consistently hitting 40+ hours/week on delivery
Goal: Create bandwidth for sales; take on 2-3 more clients
Cost: Another $500-$1,500/month

Hire #3 (Account Manager or Project Manager):
Trigger: 10+ clients; quality control becoming a bottleneck
Goal: Remove yourself from day-to-day delivery; focus on growth and strategy
Cost: $2,000-$4,000/month for part-time; $4,000-$6,000 for full-time

Copywriter: $30-$60/hour

Per Client/Month (best for consistent workload):
Community Manager: $200-$500/client/month
Caption Writer: $300-$800/client/month depending on volume
Designer: $300-$700/client/month depending on volume
