PRE-HIRE CHECKLIST (Before Day 1)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
□ Contractor Agreement signed (see Freelancer Hire Contract)
□ NDA signed
□ Invoice/payment terms confirmed
□ Role description and first 30-day expectations shared in writing
□ Access granted to tools (see tool list below)
□ Scheduling tool access set up with their email
□ Added to Slack/Discord with correct channels
□ Added to Notion workspace (view-only until confirmed competent)
□ Welcome email sent with Day 1 agenda

WEEK 1 — DAY BY DAY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

DAY 1: WELCOME & ORIENTATION (2-3 hours together)
• Introduction call: who you are, how the agency works, team structure
• Agency values and client service standards explained
• Walk through the tools stack (screen share): Notion, scheduling tool, Canva, Slack
• Assign first test task (low stakes — see below)
• Share the Client Management SOP and ask them to read it by Day 3
• Answer any questions — make them feel genuinely welcomed

DAY 2-3: SHADOW PHASE
• They shadow you on an existing client's content workflow
• They review 2-3 recently published posts and analyze why they performed
• They read the Content Creation SOP completely
• Small assignment: write 3 caption drafts for an existing client (you review)

DAY 4-5: FIRST SUPERVISED TASK
• Assign their first real task: content brief for one client post
• They complete it, you review and give detailed feedback
• If quality is acceptable: assign Week 2 tasks
• If not: give clear written feedback and one revision round

FIRST 30 DAYS — MILESTONES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

By Day 7:   Can fill out a Content Brief independently for any client
By Day 14:  Can write on-brand captions for 2+ clients with minimal edits
By Day 21:  Can manage a full content batch (brief → draft → submit) independently
By Day 30:  Client work reviewed directly; passes internal review first try 80%+ of the time

BRAND VOICE TRAINING
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
For each new client they'll work on:
1. Share the completed Brand Strategy Worksheet
2. Share 5 examples of approved posts they wrote well
3. Share 3 examples of content that was rejected and WHY
4. Have them write 5 caption drafts before going live
5. Review together — give specific feedback on voice, not just grammar

PERFORMANCE EXPECTATIONS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Communication: Respond to Slack messages within 2 hours during business hours
Deadlines:     All work delivered by deadline or proactively communicated 24 hrs early
Quality:       First drafts should require no more than 1 round of revisions after 30 days
Professionalism: All client information is strictly confidential at all times

COMMON ONBOARDING MISTAKES (and how to avoid them):
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Rushing to give them client access before they've proven quality — SLOW DOWN
• Not giving written feedback — verbal feedback is forgotten; write it
• Not having a test assignment — always give a low-stakes first task
• Assuming they understand your brand voice instinctively — always train explicitly
• Skipping the NDA — non-negotiable before sharing any client info
