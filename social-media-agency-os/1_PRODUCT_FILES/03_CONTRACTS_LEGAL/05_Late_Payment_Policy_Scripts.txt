OFFICIAL LATE PAYMENT POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Payment Terms:    Net 14 (14 days from invoice date)
Grace Period:     3 days past due date (no action taken)
Late Fee:         1.5% per month of outstanding balance, activated Day 14
Service Pause:    Day 30 — all services paused until account is current
Collections:      Day 60 — formal collections or legal action initiated
Contract Clause:  All clients acknowledge this policy in their signed contract

PREVENTION SYSTEM:
• Require a deposit (25-50%) for new clients before work begins
• Offer autopay via Stripe with card on file
• Send invoice on the 1st, due the 15th (mid-month vs. start-of-month)
• Use accounting software (QuickBooks, FreshBooks, Wave) for automated reminders

PAYMENT PLAN GUIDE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

WHEN TO OFFER: Only to clients with good payment history who are facing
a temporary hardship. Not for clients who are habitually late.

HOW TO STRUCTURE: Maximum 3 instalments over 45 days.
Example: $3,000 debt → $1,000 now, $1,000 in 15 days, $1,000 in 30 days.

REQUIRE WRITTEN AGREEMENT: Always get the payment plan agreed in writing
via email before resuming services.

WHEN TO LET IT GO: If a client owes less than $500 and the cost of pursuing
(time, stress, legal) outweighs recovery, write it off and do not work with
them again. Update your CRM with a "DO NOT REHIRE" note.
