HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
These benchmarks represent averages across thousands of accounts. Your client
may perform above or below these depending on their niche, audience size,
content quality, and consistency. Use these as context, not absolute targets.

Always compare your client to THEIR OWN previous performance first, then
industry averages. A 40% improvement over their baseline is meaningful even
if they're below industry average — they're trending in the right direction.
