INTRODUCTION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Not all metrics matter equally. Most clients fixate on follower count because
it's visible. Your job is to shift their focus to metrics that actually
indicate whether social media is working as a business tool.

This guide covers the 20 KPIs that matter most, what they tell you,
and what "good" looks like across industries.

THE 20 KPIS — DEFINED WITH FORMULAS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. ENGAGEMENT RATE
Formula: (Likes + Comments + Shares + Saves) ÷ Followers × 100
What it tells you: How interested your audience is in your content.
Good benchmark: 1-5% for Instagram, 0.5-1% for Facebook, 2-5% for LinkedIn
Pro tip: Track by content type — your Reels may have 8% while posts have 1.5%

2. REACH RATE
Formula: Reach ÷ Followers × 100
What it tells you: What % of your followers actually saw the content.
Good benchmark: 10-25% for Instagram organic content
Pro tip: If reach rate drops below 10%, your content strategy needs a reset.

3. FOLLOWER GROWTH RATE
Formula: (New Followers ÷ Total Followers at Start of Period) × 100
What it tells you: How quickly your audience is growing.
Good benchmark: 3-8% monthly for active accounts
Pro tip: Sudden drops in growth rate often coincide with algorithm changes — document them.

4. SAVE RATE
Formula: Saves ÷ Reach × 100
What it tells you: How valuable your content is (saves = "I want to come back to this")
Good benchmark: 1-3% save rate is strong for Instagram
Pro tip: High save rate = evergreen educational content that works for months.

5. STORY COMPLETION RATE
Formula: (Views on last frame ÷ Views on first frame) × 100
What it tells you: Whether viewers are staying through your Stories
Good benchmark: 70%+ is excellent; below 50% means Stories need improvement
Pro tip: Completion drops at every swipe. Keep Stories to 5-7 frames max.

6. CLICK-THROUGH RATE (CTR)
Formula: Link Clicks ÷ Impressions × 100
What it tells you: How effective your CTA is at driving action
Good benchmark: 0.5-1% is average; 2%+ is strong for organic
Pro tip: A/B test CTAs ("Link in bio" vs "Comment READY and I'll DM you") to find what converts.

7. AMPLIFICATION RATE
Formula: Shares ÷ Followers × 100
What it tells you: How often your audience shares content with their network
Good benchmark: 0.1-0.5% is typical; viral posts hit 2%+
Pro tip: Shares signal algorithm that content is worth distributing — prioritize shareable content formats.

8. RESPONSE RATE
Formula: (Comments/DMs you replied to ÷ Total comments/DMs received) × 100
What it tells you: How actively you're engaging the community
Good benchmark: 100% — every comment and DM should get a response
Pro tip: Response time matters too. Within 1 hour of posting = strong algorithm signal.

9. PROFILE VISIT RATE
Formula: Profile Visits ÷ Reach × 100
What it tells you: Whether content is compelling enough to make people want to know more
Good benchmark: 2-5% profile visit rate from reach
Pro tip: Low profile visits + high reach = content is seen but not compelling enough to investigate further.

10. VIDEO VIEW RATE
Formula: (Views ÷ Impressions) × 100 for Facebook/LinkedIn; Watch Time % for TikTok
What it tells you: How engaging your video content is
Good benchmark: 10-20% view rate; 50%+ watch time on TikTok is strong
Pro tip: If watch time is low, the problem is almost always the first 3 seconds.

11. NET FOLLOWER CHANGE
Formula: New Followers Gained - Followers Lost
What it tells you: Real net growth after unfollows
Good benchmark: Positive. Any negative number needs investigation.
Pro tip: Sudden spikes in unfollows often happen after promotional content. Monitor and balance.

12. POST FREQUENCY
Formula: Total posts ÷ Days in period
What it tells you: How consistent you are
Good benchmark: Instagram 3-5x/week; LinkedIn 3-4x/week; TikTok daily
Pro tip: Consistency > quantity. 3 great posts/week beats 7 mediocre ones.

13. SHARE OF VOICE
Formula: Brand Mentions ÷ (Brand + Competitor Mentions) × 100
What it tells you: What % of your niche's online conversation your brand owns
Good benchmark: Depends on industry; tracking trend over time matters more than absolute number
Pro tip: Use Brand24 or Mention to track this automatically.

14. AUDIENCE SENTIMENT SCORE
Formula: Positive Mentions ÷ (Positive + Negative Mentions) × 100
What it tells you: How people FEEL about the brand in comments/DMs
Good benchmark: 80%+ positive
Pro tip: Monitor spikes in negative sentiment — they're early warning signs of a PR issue.

15. WEBSITE TRAFFIC FROM SOCIAL
Where to track: Google Analytics > Acquisition > Social
What it tells you: How many website visits social media generates
Good benchmark: 10-20% of total traffic from social for most SMBs
Pro tip: Set up UTM parameters on all links to track which posts drive traffic.

16. LEAD CONVERSION FROM SOCIAL
Formula: Leads from social ÷ Total website visitors from social × 100
What it tells you: Whether social traffic actually converts
Good benchmark: 1-3% conversion rate from social traffic
Pro tip: Use a dedicated landing page for social traffic to track this accurately.

17. COST PER LEAD (PAID CAMPAIGNS)
Formula: Total Ad Spend ÷ Leads Generated
What it tells you: Efficiency of paid social
Good benchmark: Varies wildly by industry; track trend over time
Pro tip: Your organic cost per lead should be near $0 (just your time).

18. SOCIAL MEDIA ROI
Formula: (Revenue from Social - Cost of Social) ÷ Cost × 100
What it tells you: The business return on social media investment
Good benchmark: 2:1 ROI minimum ($2 returned per $1 spent); 5:1 is excellent
Pro tip: Requires proper attribution — see ROI Calculator document.

19. AUDIENCE GROWTH BY DEMOGRAPHICS
Where to track: Platform insights (audience tab)
What it tells you: Whether you're attracting the right people
Good benchmark: 70%+ of audience should match your ideal customer profile
Pro tip: If your audience skews outside your ICA, content strategy needs adjustment.

20. CONTENT SHELF LIFE
Formula: How long after posting does a piece of content continue getting engagement?
What it tells you: Whether you're creating evergreen vs. time-sensitive content
Good benchmark: Carousels and infographics get engagement for 7-14 days; Reels can resurface for weeks
Pro tip: High shelf-life content = better ROI on production effort.
