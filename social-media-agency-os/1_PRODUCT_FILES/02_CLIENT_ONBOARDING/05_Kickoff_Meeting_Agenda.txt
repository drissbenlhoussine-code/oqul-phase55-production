AGENDA MINUTE BY MINUTE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

MINUTES 0-5 — WELCOME & INTRODUCTIONS
• Warm intro — agency team introduces themselves
• Client introduces themselves (if new faces)
• Thank them for trusting you with their brand

MINUTES 5-20 — DEEP DIVE ON THEIR BUSINESS
Questions to ask (even if answered in questionnaire):
• "Tell me about the business from YOUR perspective — where it's been, where it's going."
• "What's your biggest opportunity you feel you're not capitalizing on right now?"
• "What season of business are you in — building, growing, stabilizing, or scaling?"
• "When you think about social media, what's your gut feeling about what's missing?"

[Listen and take notes. The client should talk 70% of the time here.]

MINUTES 20-35 — REVIEWING YOUR AUDIT FINDINGS
• "Based on our questionnaire and a quick audit, here's what we noticed..."
• Present findings: current posting frequency, engagement rate, profile completeness
• Share 2-3 competitor insights ("Your competitors are doing X — here's the gap")
• Highlight what's working and what's not (honest but positive framing)

MINUTES 35-55 — CONTENT STRATEGY OVERVIEW
• Present your proposed content pillars (5 pillars) and confirm alignment
• Show 3-5 sample content direction concepts ("This is one style we could go with...")
• Discuss platform priority: "For your goals, we recommend starting with X"
• Walk through the content mix (educational/promotional/personal split)
• Confirm posting frequency and platforms

Questions to confirm:
• "Do these content pillars feel aligned with what you want to communicate?"
• "Is there anything here that doesn't feel right for your brand?"

MINUTES 55-65 — WORKFLOW & APPROVALS
• Walk through the approval process step by step
• "Here's how the monthly workflow looks: [walk through calendar]"
• Confirm the primary approver and backup
• Set expectation: 48-hour approval window
• Discuss revision rounds: "You get X rounds per batch"
• Confirm preferred communication channel

MINUTES 65-75 — TOOLS & ACCESS
• Confirm all access items are received or set a deadline for outstanding items
• Walk them through how scheduling tools work (2-minute overview)
• Share any shared folders / Notion workspaces
• Set up communication channel (Slack, WhatsApp, email thread)

MINUTES 75-82 — 30-60-90 DAY PLAN
• "Here's what the next 90 days look like..."
  Month 1: Strategy finalization, content launch, baseline metrics
  Month 2: Optimization based on data, test new formats
  Month 3: Growth acceleration, expand what's working
• Set 3 specific goals for Month 1 (measurable)
• Confirm next touchpoint: when they'll receive first content draft

MINUTES 82-88 — Q&A
• "Before we wrap up — any questions about how we work or what to expect?"
• Address any concerns directly and confidently

MINUTES 88-90 — CLOSE & NEXT STEPS
• Recap action items:
  Agency: Content calendar by [DATE], first batch by [DATE]
  Client: Send outstanding assets by [DATE]
• "Your first content will be in your inbox by [DATE]. We're really excited
  to get started on this — thank you for choosing us."
