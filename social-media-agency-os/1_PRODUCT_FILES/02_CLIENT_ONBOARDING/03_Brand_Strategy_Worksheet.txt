STEP 5 — CONTENT PILLARS (5 Core Themes)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Content pillars are the 5 themes every post fits into. They ensure variety,
consistency, and strategic alignment.

PILLAR 1 — EDUCATE (30% of content)
  Topic area: ________________
  Examples: Tips, how-tos, industry insights, myth-busting

PILLAR 2 — INSPIRE (20% of content)
  Topic area: ________________
  Examples: Transformation stories, motivational, vision, values

PILLAR 3 — CONNECT (20% of content)
  Topic area: ________________
  Examples: Behind-the-scenes, personal brand, team, story

PILLAR 4 — SHOWCASE (20% of content)
  Topic area: ________________
  Examples: Products, services, case studies, testimonials

PILLAR 5 — CONVERT (10% of content)
  Topic area: ________________
  Examples: Offers, CTAs, limited-time promotions, inquiries

Content Mix Summary:
  Educate 30% / Inspire 20% / Connect 20% / Showcase 20% / Convert 10%
