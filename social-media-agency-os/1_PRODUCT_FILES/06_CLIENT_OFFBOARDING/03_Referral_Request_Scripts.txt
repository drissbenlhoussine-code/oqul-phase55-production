SCRIPT 1 — THE HAPPY ENDING (Client leaving voluntarily to go in-house)
Channel: Farewell call + follow-up email
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

During the call:
"This has been such a great run together — I'm genuinely proud of what we built.
Now that you're taking things in-house with a dedicated person, the foundation
is completely solid. You're in a great position.

One quick thing before I let you go — do you know anyone in your network who
might be at the stage where you were when we first started? Someone who's juggling
social media on top of everything else and could really use some support?

I'd genuinely love a warm intro if anyone comes to mind. You've seen exactly how
we work, so your word means a lot."

Follow-up email (send 48 hours after final call):
Subject: Thank you [NAME] — one small ask

Hi [NAME],

Thank you again for [specific thing you appreciated about working together].

One last ask — if you think of any business owner friends who are where you were
when we first met (overwhelmed with social media, wanting more professional results)
— an introduction would mean the world to me. A simple "you should talk to [YOUR NAME]"
is enough — I'll take it from there.

And if you'd be willing to leave a quick Google review, here's the direct link: [LINK]
It takes 2 minutes and genuinely helps other business owners find us.

Cheering for [BUSINESS NAME] from the sidelines. 🙌

[YOUR NAME]

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SCRIPT 2 — THE BUDGET EXIT (Client leaving due to financial constraints)
Channel: Email (call if the relationship was close)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Subject: Thank you — and leaving the door wide open

Hi [NAME],

I completely understand that budgets shift — and I want you to know there are
no hard feelings at all. The work we did together was real, and those results
don't disappear when we stop working together.

When things pick up (and I have no doubt they will) — please come back.
You'll be a priority.

In the meantime, I have a small ask: if you know any business owner who's
in a more stable position and needs what we offer, I'd really appreciate a
referral. Given what you've seen firsthand, your recommendation carries a lot of weight.

And if it's easier — a quick Google review would help enormously: [LINK]

You're in good hands going forward. I'll be rooting for you.

[YOUR NAME]

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SCRIPT 3 — THE PAUSE (Client wants to pause, intends to return)
Channel: Email after confirming the pause
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Subject: Your account is paused — we'll be here when you're ready

Hi [NAME],

All good on our end — your account is officially paused as of [DATE].
When you're ready to restart, just send me a message and we'll pick up
exactly where we left off. No re-onboarding needed.

While we're on pause — if you happen to know someone who needs social media
help, I'd love a referral. Consider it a thank-you gift for being such an easy
client to work with. 😊

Take care, and talk soon.

[YOUR NAME]

P.S. We'll still be following [BUSINESS INSTAGRAM HANDLE] — feel free to
reach out if you have any quick questions in the meantime.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SCRIPT 4 — MID-CONTRACT REFERRAL ASK (Still working together, asking now)
Channel: Voice note, WhatsApp, or casual email
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

"Hey [NAME]! Quick one — I'm looking to bring on 1-2 new clients this month
in [niche]. Do you know anyone in your network who might be at the stage where
you were when we started?

If someone signs on from your referral, I'll [give you a free extra month /
credit your next invoice / send you a small gift] as a thank-you.

No pressure at all — just figured I'd ask since you've seen the results
firsthand. You'd literally be doing a friend a favor."

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SCRIPT 5 — THE REVIEW ASK (Asking specifically for a Google or social review)
Channel: Text, WhatsApp, or email
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

"Hey [NAME]! As we wrap up our work together, I have one small ask.
Would you be willing to leave us a quick Google review?

I'd love if you mentioned: what you were struggling with before we started,
what the experience was like working with us, and the results you saw.
But honestly — even a simple 5-star review with a sentence or two helps massively.

Here's the direct link so it takes less than 2 minutes: [GOOGLE REVIEW LINK]

No pressure, and thank you either way. Working with you has been a genuinely great experience."
