SECTION 1: DAILY PLATFORM TASKS
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

MORNING CHECK-IN (15–20 minutes, per client)
  Time: 8:30–9:00am (before your work day starts)

  □ Check notifications on all active platforms
  □ Respond to any comments on posts from last 24 hours
  □ Respond to DMs (if DM management is in scope)
  □ Like/acknowledge meaningful comments (not all, prioritize)
  □ Check if scheduled posts published correctly
  □ Note any unusual engagement spikes (screenshot for report)
  □ Quick scan of competitors/industry accounts

AFTERNOON CHECK-IN (15 minutes)
  Time: 1:00–2:00pm

  □ Second round of comment responses
  □ Monitor for any negative comments or reviews
  □ Check if Stories are live (24-hour lifespan — replace if expired)
  □ Engage with 5–10 ideal client accounts (like, comment genuinely)
  □ Share any relevant industry content to Stories (with commentary)

EVENING CHECK-IN (10 minutes — optional based on client package)
  Time: 6:00–7:00pm

  □ Final comment sweep
  □ DM responses for any afternoon messages
  □ Note if any post is trending above normal → screenshot for report

DAILY ENGAGEMENT QUOTA (per platform, per client):
  Instagram:  Reply to 100% of comments + DMs within scope
              Engage with 10–20 accounts in target audience
  LinkedIn:   Reply to all comments + DMs
              Engage with 5–10 relevant posts in industry
  TikTok:     Reply to all comments (algorithm rewards this heavily)
              Duet or Stitch 1 relevant piece per week (if applicable)
  Facebook:   Reply to all comments and messages
              Engage in 1–2 relevant Facebook Groups (if in scope)

SECTION 2: INSTAGRAM MANAGEMENT SOP
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

INSTAGRAM ALGORITHM FACTORS (2025)
  Top signals Instagram rewards:
  1. Sends (shares to DMs) — #1 signal for Reels and feed
  2. Saves — indicates high-value content
  3. Comments (especially conversations, not one-word)
  4. Watch time (Reels) — complete views most important
  5. Profile visits from discovery
  6. Story replies and reactions
  7. Consistent posting (gaps hurt reach)

FEED POST BEST PRACTICES:
  □ Post 3–5x per week (4x is the sweet spot)
  □ Mix format: 60% Carousels + 25% Reels + 15% static
  □ First slide of carousel must be a hook (not the title)
  □ Always include a save-worthy component in carousels
  □ Use 5–10 targeted hashtags (not 30 generic ones)
  □ Reply to every comment within 60 minutes of posting (boosts reach)
  □ Pin top 3 posts that best represent the brand

REELS BEST PRACTICES (2025):
  □ 15–30 seconds outperforms longer (unless educational)
  □ Hook in first 0.5–1 second (visual, not verbal)
  □ Native vertical video preferred over repurposed TikTok
  □ Add captions (accessibility + mute viewing)
  □ Use trending audio with < 10K Reels on it (sweet spot)
  □ Do NOT watermark with TikTok logo
  □ Share to Stories immediately after posting
  □ Post Reels 3–5x per week for algorithm acceleration

STORIES BEST PRACTICES:
  □ Post 5–10 Stories per day (maintain "active" status)
  □ Mix types: Polls, Quizzes, Sliders, Behind-scenes, Reposts
  □ First Story of day: high-engagement (poll or quiz)
  □ Use the "Close Friends" list for VIP/exclusive content
  □ Add location tag when relevant (local discovery)
  □ Cross-post feed content to Stories immediately (first 3 hours matter)
  □ Add CTA Stories pointing to link in bio

HASHTAG STRATEGY (2025 update):
  Best practice: 5–8 highly targeted hashtags in caption
  Mix: 2 large (500K–1M posts), 3 medium (50K–500K), 2–3 niche (under 50K)
  Do NOT: Use banned hashtags (check regularly)
  Do NOT: Use the same 30 hashtags on every post (shadowban risk)
  Rotate: Keep 3–5 hashtag sets per content category

SECTION 3: LINKEDIN MANAGEMENT SOP
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

LINKEDIN ALGORITHM FACTORS (2025)
  Top signals:
  1. Early comments (first 60–90 minutes are critical)
  2. Profile-to-profile sharing (direct messages > shares)
  3. Reactions AND comments weighted equally
  4. Dwell time (how long people read the post)
  5. Connection depth (1st degree comments >> 3rd degree)
  6. Creator Mode enabled (expands reach significantly)

POSTING BEST PRACTICES:
  □ Post 3–5x per week for growth (3x for maintenance)
  □ Long-form text (600–1,200 words) outperforms short posts for authority
  □ Line breaks every 1–2 sentences (makes mobile reading easy)
  □ Don't include external links IN post (kills reach) — put in comments
  □ End every post with a question to drive comments
  □ First comment = link to resource or additional context
  □ Best formats: Text-only, Document/PDF carousels, native video

ENGAGEMENT STRATEGY:
  □ Comment on 5–10 posts in target industry daily
  □ Comments must be substantive (3+ sentences adds value)
  □ Never post generic "Great insight!" comments
  □ Reply to everyone who comments on your posts (conversation signals)
  □ Connect with people who engage with your content
  □ Send personalized connection requests (not generic)

CREATOR MODE CHECKLIST:
  □ Enable Creator Mode in LinkedIn settings
  □ Add 5 topic hashtags to profile
  □ Newsletter enabled (if content strategy includes long-form)
  □ Featured section: top 3 content pieces or portfolio pieces

SECTION 4: TIKTOK MANAGEMENT SOP
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

TIKTOK ALGORITHM FACTORS (2025)
  Top signals:
  1. Video completion rate (watch entire video = massive boost)
  2. Rewatches (second view signals = algorithm push)
  3. Shares (sent to DMs = strongest signal)
  4. Comments (especially early comments)
  5. Follows from video (conversion from content)
  6. Saves (bookmarks = "watch later" intent)

CONTENT STRATEGY:
  □ Post 1–3x daily (TikTok rewards volume more than other platforms)
  □ Keep videos 15–30 seconds for max completion rate
  □ Use trending sounds EARLY (first 2–3 days they trend)
  □ Engage with comments within first hour (algorithm watches this)
  □ Post during off-peak hours (7–9am, 7–9pm local time)
  □ Never delete videos (hurts account standing)
  □ Reply to comments with video replies (shows up as fresh content)

HOOKS THAT WORK ON TIKTOK:
  "POV: You're a [ICA] who..."
  "The [topic] hack nobody talks about:"
  "Wait for it..." (pattern interrupt + curiosity)
  "I can't believe this worked:"
  "[Number] secrets [authority] don't want you to know:"
  Direct camera: "[Statement]. Let me explain."

SECTION 5: FACEBOOK MANAGEMENT SOP
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

FACEBOOK ALGORITHM FACTORS (2025)
  Facebook now deprioritizes public posts in favor of:
  1. Facebook Groups content (massive organic reach)
  2. Reels (still heavily promoted)
  3. Stories (high visibility in feed)
  4. Personal profiles (more reach than business pages)
  5. Paid content (reality: organic reach is ~2–5% for pages)

STRATEGY FOR BUSINESS PAGES:
  □ Cross-post Instagram Reels to Facebook Reels
  □ Post 3–5x per week but focus heavily on Facebook Groups
  □ Use Facebook Stories daily (high position in feed)
  □ Go Live 1x per month minimum (massive reach boost)
  □ Respond to all Messenger inquiries within 1 hour (Page rating)
  □ Set up auto-reply for Messenger (first response template)

FACEBOOK GROUPS STRATEGY (if applicable):
  □ Post valuable content in 3–5 relevant groups weekly
  □ Answer questions genuinely — never spam with links
  □ Build relationships before promoting
  □ If client has own Group: post daily, moderate actively

SECTION 6: PINTEREST MANAGEMENT SOP
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PINTEREST ALGORITHM FACTORS (2025)
  Pinterest is a SEARCH ENGINE, not a social network.
  Top signals:
  1. Keyword-rich pin titles and descriptions
  2. Fresh pins (new images, not repins of your own content)
  3. Saves to relevant boards
  4. Click-through rate (sending to website)
  5. Board authority and organization
  6. Consistent pinning (daily preferred)

PINTEREST BEST PRACTICES:
  □ Pin 5–15 fresh pins per day (use Tailwind for scheduling)
  □ Create 10–15 boards organized by keyword topics
  □ Board descriptions must include target keywords
  □ Pin title: lead with keyword (first 40 characters most important)
  □ Pin description: 100–200 words, natural keyword use
  □ 2:3 ratio (1000 × 1500px) — taller pins take more real estate
  □ Link every pin to a relevant website page
  □ Seasonal content: pin 30–45 days BEFORE the season/holiday

KEYWORD RESEARCH FOR PINTEREST:
  1. Open Pinterest search bar
  2. Type core niche keyword (e.g., "meal prep")
  3. Note the bubble suggestions that appear — these are searched terms
  4. Include 5–10 of these in pin descriptions and board names
