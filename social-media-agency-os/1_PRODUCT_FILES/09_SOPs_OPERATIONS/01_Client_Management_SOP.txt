PURPOSE
───────
This SOP ensures every client receives consistent, professional management
from day one through renewal. Follow this procedure for every active client
to eliminate miscommunication, missed deadlines, and scope creep.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 1: MONTHLY WORKFLOW (DAY-BY-DAY)
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WEEK 1 — PLANNING PHASE (Days 1–7)
────────────────────────────────────

DAY 1 (1st of month):
  □ Review client's previous month analytics report
  □ Note top 3 performing content pieces
  □ Note 3 content types that underperformed
  □ Pull any notes from last month's check-in call
  □ Review competitor activity from past 30 days
  □ Check industry news / trends for current month
  □ Update content pillars if needed based on data

DAY 2:
  □ Draft Month [X] content plan in Google Sheets/Notion
  □ Map 4 content pillars to specific days/formats
  □ Create 30-day content calendar with platform notes
  □ Identify 3–5 promotional moments or events this month
  □ Flag any campaign content needed (launches, promos)
  □ Add client approval deadline to shared calendar

DAY 3:
  □ Write all long-form content: blog excerpts, newsletters
  □ Draft caption copy for first 2 weeks of posts
  □ Build 3 carousel or educational slide decks (outlines)
  □ Create 5–10 Reel hooks/scripts if video is in scope
  □ Send first batch for client review (Week 1–2 content)

DAYS 4–5:
  □ Design graphics batch #1 (if design is in scope)
  □ Source licensed UGC, stock images, product photos
  □ Schedule Week 1 posts after client approves
  □ Set up UTM links for any promotional content
  □ Respond to pending DMs and comments (if in scope)

DAY 7:
  □ Confirm Week 1 posts are scheduled and live
  □ Send client brief update message: "Week 1 live ✓"
  □ Pull engagement data from any prior live posts
  □ Update analytics tracker spreadsheet

WEEK 2 — EXECUTION PHASE (Days 8–14)
───────────────────────────────────────

DAY 8:
  □ Monitor Week 1 post performance
  □ Boost top post if paid ads are in scope
  □ Respond to all comments within 2 business hours
  □ Screenshot any client tags/mentions for reporting
  □ DM outreach to engaged followers (if community management in scope)

DAY 10:
  □ Check content approval status for Week 3 batch
  □ Chase client if content approval is overdue (use Email Template #3)
  □ Draft Week 3–4 caption copy
  □ Begin designing graphics batch #2

DAY 14:
  □ Mid-month performance check-in (2-line internal note):
      - Follower growth on track? Y/N
      - Engagement rate above benchmark? Y/N
  □ Adjust Week 3–4 content strategy based on data
  □ Send client a mid-month "pulse update" message if engagement is notably high or low

WEEK 3 — MOMENTUM PHASE (Days 15–21)
───────────────────────────────────────

DAY 15:
  □ Send Week 3–4 content for client approval
  □ Schedule approved content
  □ Continue community management (2x/day checks)

DAY 18:
  □ Pull mid-month analytics from each platform
  □ Update internal tracker
  □ Note any viral posts, tag client, celebrate wins

DAY 21:
  □ Begin monthly report data collection
  □ Export platform analytics (native + third-party tool)
  □ Pull engagement, reach, follower change numbers

WEEK 4 — WRAP-UP PHASE (Days 22–End)
───────────────────────────────────────

DAY 22:
  □ Schedule final week of content
  □ Confirm no gaps in content calendar for month-end
  □ Check upcoming month: any holidays, awareness days, launches?

DAY 25:
  □ Draft monthly analytics report
  □ Write insights narrative (what worked, why, what's next)
  □ Prepare next month content plan draft

DAY 28:
  □ Finalize and send monthly report to client
  □ Send month-end check-in message
  □ Schedule or confirm next monthly call

DAY 30/31:
  □ Log all performance data in master tracker
  □ Archive month's content copies to client folder
  □ Confirm next month retainer invoice sent (if applicable)
  □ Review: Is this client at risk of churning? If yes, flag for retention action.

SECTION 2: TOOL STACK
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CLIENT COMMUNICATION
  Primary:      Slack (workspace per client or agency workspace)
  Backup:       Email (professional address, same domain)
  Video Calls:  Zoom or Google Meet (record all calls)
  Call Notes:   Otter.ai or Read.ai for auto-transcripts

CONTENT CREATION
  Copywriting:  Claude AI / ChatGPT (drafts) → human editing
  Design:       Canva Pro or Adobe Express (templates per client brand)
  Video:        CapCut (Reels) / DaVinci Resolve (advanced)
  Stock:        Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay (free) / Storyblocks (paid)

CONTENT SCHEDULING
  Multi-platform: Later, Publer, Buffer, or Metricool
  Facebook/IG:    Meta Business Suite (free, backup scheduler)
  LinkedIn:       Native scheduler (best performance)
  Pinterest:      Tailwind (best for SEO + scheduling)

ANALYTICS & REPORTING
  Primary:      Metricool (all platforms in one dashboard)
  Social:       Native analytics (always pull natively as backup)
  Link tracking: Bitly Pro or UTM Builder + Google Analytics
  Reports:      Google Slides or Notion (client-facing)

PROJECT MANAGEMENT
  Internal:     Notion, ClickUp, or Asana

INVOICING & FINANCE

SECTION 3: CLIENT COMMUNICATION PROTOCOLS
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

RESPONSE TIME STANDARDS
  Slack messages:      Within 4 business hours
  Email:               Within 8 business hours (same business day)
  Urgent issues:       Within 1 hour (define "urgent" in contract)
  Weekend/holiday:     Next business day (set auto-reply)

CONTENT APPROVAL PROTOCOL
  Step 1: Send content batch via agreed method (Slack, email, or portal)
  Step 2: Allow 48 hours for client review
  Step 3: If no response in 48h → send one reminder
  Step 4: If no response in 72h → auto-approve and schedule
           (This policy must be in your contract. See File 03.01)
  Step 5: Never hold up posting for unapproved content — protect your schedule

MONTHLY CALL AGENDA (30 minutes)
  0:00–0:05   Wins & highlights from past month
  0:05–0:15   Analytics review (top 3 metrics)
  0:15–0:20   Next month strategy preview
  0:20–0:25   Any feedback, questions, changes
  0:25–0:30   Action items recap + next call scheduled

ESCALATION PROTOCOL
  Level 1 — Minor issue (typo, wrong color):
    Fix within 4 hours, message client, no meeting needed
  Level 2 — Strategic concern (campaign not performing):
    Schedule 20-min call within 48 hours, bring data + solution
  Level 3 — Crisis (negative PR, major complaint):
    Use Crisis Protocol SOP (File 09.05), respond within 1 hour

SECTION 4: SCOPE CREEP PREVENTION
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

SCOPE CREEP RED FLAGS
  □ "Can you also quickly do X?" outside of agreed deliverables
  □ Requests for platforms not in the contract
  □ Asking you to manage paid ads when contract is organic-only
  □ "I know it's not in the scope but..." (it IS out of scope)
  □ Increasing revision rounds beyond what's agreed

RESPONSE SCRIPT FOR SCOPE CREEP:
  "That sounds like a great idea! That falls outside our current
   agreement, but I'd love to add it. I can put together a quick
   add-on proposal for [X] — would that work for you?"

ADD-ON PRICING EXAMPLES
  Extra platform:      +$300–$500/month
  Paid ads management: +$400–$800/month + 10% of ad spend
  Extra revision round: $75–$150 per round
  Rush content (<48h):  25–50% surcharge
  Story management add-on: +$200/month

SECTION 5: CLIENT HEALTH MONITORING
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

MONTHLY CLIENT HEALTH SCORE (rate 1–5 each)
  [ ] Communication responsiveness: ___/5
  [ ] Content approval timeliness:   ___/5
  [ ] Asset provision (photos, info): ___/5
  [ ] Invoice payment timeliness:    ___/5
  [ ] Overall satisfaction signals:  ___/5
  
  TOTAL: ___/25

  20–25: Excellent — consider asking for referral or case study
  15–19: Good — maintain current approach
  10–14: Concern — schedule a strategy call
  Below 10: At-risk — initiate retention conversation immediately

CHURN PREVENTION TRIGGERS (act immediately if any apply):
  □ Client hasn't responded to messages in 5+ business days
  □ Client questioned the value of your service
  □ Client mentioned "budget cuts" or "reviewing expenses"
  □ Client asked what would happen if they paused
  □ Invoice was late 2+ months in a row

RETENTION SCRIPT:
  "I wanted to check in — I've noticed [specific observation].
   I want to make sure you're getting maximum value from our work
   together. Could we schedule 20 minutes to align on your goals
   for the next quarter?"

SECTION 6: CLIENT OFFBOARDING TRIGGER CHECKLIST
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  □ Client gave notice to cancel — acknowledge within 24h
  □ Review contract for notice period requirements
  □ Calculate final invoice amount and send immediately
  □ Begin offboarding process (see File 06.01 — Goodbye Packet)
  □ Complete 40-point offboarding checklist (File 06.02)
  □ Transfer all assets: passwords, content, analytics data
  □ Send final wrap-up report (File 06.04)
  □ Request referral/testimonial (File 06.03)
  □ Archive client folder in Google Drive
  □ Remove from active client tracker
  □ Send to former client email sequence (every 6 months)
