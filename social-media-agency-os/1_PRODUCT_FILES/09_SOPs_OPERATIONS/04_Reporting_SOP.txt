SECTION 1: DATA COLLECTION SCHEDULE
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WEEKLY MICRO-TRACKING (every Friday — 20 minutes)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────
Record these in your internal analytics tracker spreadsheet:

  □ Total followers (each platform)
  □ Net new followers this week (+/-)
  □ Total post reach this week
  □ Average engagement rate this week
  □ Top performing post (platform, format, reach, engagement)
  □ Lowest performing post (note format for pattern recognition)
  □ Story views (if applicable)
  □ Link clicks (if applicable)
  □ DMs received this week
  □ Any viral or unusually high-performing content — screenshot

DATA SOURCES BY PLATFORM:
  Instagram:    Instagram Insights (Creator/Business account)
  Facebook:     Facebook Page Insights + Meta Business Suite
  LinkedIn:     LinkedIn Analytics (Creator Mode or Company Page)
  TikTok:       TikTok Analytics (Pro Account)
  Pinterest:    Pinterest Analytics
  All:          Metricool / Later / Sprout Social (if using third-party)
  Website:      Google Analytics 4 (if driving traffic)

MONTHLY DATA PULL (Day 21–25 of each month)
─────────────────────────────────────────────

  □ Export native analytics from each platform (CSV or screenshot)
  □ Pull from third-party scheduling tool (if applicable)
  □ Export Google Analytics data (sessions, traffic sources)
  □ Pull UTM/Bitly link data for tracked campaigns
  □ Note any paid ad data separately (if running ads)
  □ Screenshot top 5 performing posts for visual inclusion in report
  □ Note the date range: 1st to last day of previous month

METRICS TO PULL (per platform):
  Instagram:
    → Follower count (start vs. end of month)
    → Total reach (all posts combined)
    → Total impressions
    → Total engagement (likes + comments + saves + shares)
    → Average engagement rate
    → Reel views and completion rate
    → Story views and tap-forward rate
    → Profile visits
    → Link-in-bio clicks
    → Best performing post (reach, engagement rate)

  LinkedIn:
    → Follower count (start vs. end)
    → Total impressions
    → Total engagement
    → Engagement rate
    → Profile views
    → Post clicks
    → Best performing content

  TikTok:
    → Follower count (start vs. end)
    → Video views total
    → Average watch time
    → Profile views
    → Likes, comments, shares total
    → Best performing video (views, completion rate)

  Facebook:
    → Page likes / followers
    → Total reach
    → Total engagement
    → Page views
    → Best performing post

SECTION 3: WRITING THE INSIGHTS NARRATIVE
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The narrative section turns numbers into meaning. This is what separates
a great SMM from someone who just sends a screenshot of analytics.

NARRATIVE FRAMEWORK (use this structure every month):

SECTION A — WHAT HAPPENED
  Write 2–3 sentences summarizing overall performance.
  
  Template:
  "In [Month], [Client Name]'s social media presence [grew/maintained/
  experienced some challenges with] [key metric]. Overall, the accounts
  reached [X] people across [platforms], generating [X] engagements —
  [comparison to last month: up/down X%]."

SECTION B — WHAT WORKED (and why)
  Identify 2–3 top-performing pieces of content and explain WHY.
  
  Template:
  "[Content piece] was your top performer this month, reaching [X] people
  with an engagement rate of [X%]. This worked because [reason — format,
  topic, timing, hook, trend leverage, etc.]. We'll replicate this
  [format/approach] in [next month]."

SECTION C — WHAT WE LEARNED (what to improve)
  Be honest but solution-focused. Don't just report problems.
  
  Template:
  "[Content type/topic] underperformed relative to benchmarks this month.
  Based on the data, [reason — audience not resonating with X, wrong
  time, oversaturated topic, etc.]. Next month we'll [specific action
  to address it]."

SECTION D — NEXT MONTH FOCUS
  End every report looking forward. Clients feel reassured when
  you have a clear plan.
  
  Template:
  "In [Next Month], our focus will be:
  1. [Specific strategy or content initiative]
  2. [Platform or format to double down on]
  3. [Any campaigns, promotions, or seasonal content]
  We're aiming to [specific goal with number, e.g., reach 20K accounts]."

TONE GUIDELINES FOR REPORTS:
  ✓ Confident, not apologetic
  ✓ Solution-focused (every problem → action)
  ✓ Plain language (no jargon the client doesn't understand)
  ✓ Brief narrative (clients want insights, not essays)
  ✓ Celebrate wins (even small ones — client morale matters)
  ✗ Never blame the client ("If you gave us better photos...")
  ✗ Never over-explain failure without a clear fix
  ✗ Never send a report without narrative — numbers alone are useless

SECTION 4: QUARTERLY REPORTING (90-DAY REVIEW)
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

At the end of every quarter, deliver a strategic 90-day review.
Full template in File 05.04. Summary of what to include:

  □ 90-day performance summary (all 3 months side-by-side)
  □ Goal vs. actual comparison
  □ Top 5 content pieces of the quarter
  □ Audience growth trend line (chart if possible)
  □ Content mix performance analysis
  □ Q+1 strategy recommendation
  □ Revised goals for next quarter
  □ ROI calculation (if e-commerce or lead gen in scope)

QUARTERLY REVIEW MEETING AGENDA (60 minutes):
  0:00–0:10   Relationship check-in (how are they feeling overall?)
  0:10–0:20   90-day highlights and wins
  0:20–0:35   Performance deep-dive (data review together)
  0:35–0:50   Q+1 strategy presentation
  0:50–0:60   Questions, scope review, retainer renewal discussion

PRO TIP: Use the 90-day review as your retention and upsell meeting.
If results are strong → ask for referral or testimonial.
If growth has plateaued → propose an upgrade (new platform, paid ads add-on).
If the client seems hesitant → address concerns head-on and offer a guarantee.

SECTION 6: REPORTING RED FLAGS
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Investigate immediately if you see:
  □ Follower drop of 5%+ in one week (check for spam/bot purge)
  □ Engagement rate drops below 1% on Instagram (algorithm issue)
  □ Reach drop of 30%+ with no content change (possible shadowban)
  □ Traffic from social to website drops to near zero
  □ Sudden increase in negative comments or DMs
  □ Post flagged or removed by platform
  □ Ad account restricted or suspended (if running paid ads)

WHEN TO COMMUNICATE BAD NEWS TO CLIENTS:
  → Proactively — never let them find it first
  → With context — explain what happened
  → With a plan — what you're doing to fix it
  → Quickly — the same day you notice it

SCRIPT FOR BAD NEWS:
  "Hi [Name], I wanted to give you a heads-up: [platform/metric] dipped
  this week, and here's why I think it happened: [reason]. I'm already
  [action you're taking]. Expect [timeframe] for recovery. Let me know
  if you have questions — happy to chat."
