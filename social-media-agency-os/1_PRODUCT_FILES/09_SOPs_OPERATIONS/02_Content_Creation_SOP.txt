PURPOSE
───────
A repeatable, quality-controlled content creation process that ensures
every piece of content is strategic, on-brand, and approved before
publishing — without chaos or missed deadlines.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 1: THE 6-STAGE CONTENT CREATION PROCESS
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

STAGE 1: STRATEGY BRIEF (Day 1 of content batch)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────

Before writing a single word, complete the content brief:

  CLIENT: _______________________________________________
  MONTH/BATCH: __________________________________________
  CONTENT PILLARS THIS MONTH:
    Pillar 1 (Education):   ____________________________
    Pillar 2 (Inspiration): ____________________________
    Pillar 3 (Promotion):   ____________________________
    Pillar 4 (Community):   ____________________________
  
  TOP PERFORMING POST LAST MONTH: _______________________
  FORMAT TO REPLICATE: __________________________________
  UPCOMING PROMOTIONS/EVENTS: ___________________________
  NEW PRODUCTS/SERVICES TO FEATURE: _____________________
  CONTENT CONSTRAINTS (topics to avoid): ________________

  PLATFORM FOCUS THIS MONTH:
    □ Instagram (Feed + Stories + Reels)
    □ Facebook (Feed + Stories + Groups)
    □ TikTok (short-form video)
    □ LinkedIn (thought leadership)
    □ Pinterest (long-tail SEO)
    □ Twitter/X (engagement + news)

STAGE 2: RESEARCH (30–60 minutes per batch)
─────────────────────────────────────────────

  □ Review 5 competitor accounts for content inspiration
  □ Check industry trending topics (Google Trends, Exploding Topics)
  □ Pull platform-specific trending audio (Reels/TikTok)
  □ Research 3 industry hashtags for new additions
  □ Note any viral formats in the niche right now
  □ Check client's DMs/comments for audience questions (content gold)
  □ Review saved posts from inspiration boards

RESEARCH SOURCES BY PLATFORM:
  Instagram:  Instagram Reels Trending tab, Explore page for niche
  TikTok:     TikTok Creative Center → Trending Songs + Hashtags
  LinkedIn:   LinkedIn News, industry newsletters
  Pinterest:  Pinterest Trends tool (trends.pinterest.com)
  General:    BuzzSumo, SparkToro, Feedly (industry RSS)

STAGE 3: COPYWRITING
─────────────────────

CAPTION WRITING PROCESS (per post):
  Step 1: Choose hook type (see Hook Library below)
  Step 2: Write hook line — stop the scroll
  Step 3: Body (2–4 sentences) — deliver the value
  Step 4: CTA — one clear action
  Step 5: Hashtags (if applicable) — see hashtag framework File 04.04
  Step 6: Read aloud — does it sound human?
  Step 7: Edit for clarity — remove filler words

HOOK LIBRARY (rotate through these):
  Question hooks:
    → "Did you know that [counterintuitive fact]?"
    → "What if I told you [unexpected truth about niche]?"
    → "Are you making this [topic] mistake?"

  Statement hooks:
    → "The truth about [topic] nobody talks about:"
    → "I made $[X] doing [Y]. Here's exactly how:"
    → "Stop doing [X]. Here's what actually works:"

  Number hooks:
    → "[#] reasons your [problem] isn't getting fixed:"
    → "I tested [X] strategies. [#] actually work."
    → "[#] things every [ICA] should know about [topic]:"

  Story hooks:
    → "Two years ago I [situation]. Today [result]."
    → "My client went from [before] to [after] in [timeframe]:"
    → "This one mistake cost me [consequence]."

CAPTION QUALITY CHECKLIST:
  □ Hook stops the scroll (first 1–2 lines before "more")
  □ Body delivers real value or emotional resonance
  □ CTA is clear and singular (one action, not three)
  □ Length matches platform best practice:
      Instagram: 150–300 words (value) or 1–3 lines (aesthetic)
      Facebook: 80–120 words
      LinkedIn: 600–1,200 words (long-form performs well)
      TikTok: 100–150 words
  □ No grammar errors (run through Grammarly)
  □ Brand voice matches (see brand strategy worksheet)
  □ No generic filler phrases ("In today's digital landscape...")

STAGE 4: DESIGN / VISUALS
──────────────────────────

DESIGN PROCESS:
  Step 1: Choose from brand template library in Canva
  Step 2: Resize for each platform needed
  Step 3: Apply brand colors, fonts, and logo
  Step 4: Match visual to the content type:
            Education → Clean, readable, slide-style
            Inspiration → Bold typography or quote overlay
            Promotion → Product-forward, clear offer
            Behind the scenes → Raw/authentic feel
  Step 5: Review at mobile size (90% of users are on mobile)
  Step 6: Export at platform-recommended specs (see below)

EXPORT SPECS BY PLATFORM:
  Instagram Feed (Square):    1080 × 1080 px
  Instagram Feed (Portrait):  1080 × 1350 px
  Instagram Story:            1080 × 1920 px
  Instagram Reel:             1080 × 1920 px (vertical)
  Facebook Feed:              1200 × 630 px
  LinkedIn:                   1200 × 627 px (landscape)
  Pinterest:                  1000 × 1500 px (2:3 ratio)
  TikTok:                     1080 × 1920 px (vertical)
  Twitter/X:                  1200 × 675 px

FILE NAMING CONVENTION:
  [CLIENT]_[PLATFORM]_[DATE]_[POST#]_[TYPE]
  Example: ACME_IG_2025-03-15_P01_CAROUSEL.jpg

STAGE 5: CONTENT APPROVAL
───────────────────────────

  Step 1: Compile batch into approval document
           (Google Slides, Notion page, or PDF)
  Step 2: Include for each post:
           - Visual (mockup or actual graphic)
           - Caption copy
           - Platform(s)
           - Scheduled date/time
           - Notes/context for client

  Step 3: Send to client via agreed method with subject:
           "[CLIENT NAME] — Content Batch [DATE RANGE] for Approval"

  Step 4: Set 48-hour approval deadline
  Step 5: Follow up once if no response at 24 hours
  Step 6: Auto-approve at 72 hours (must be in contract)

REVISION POLICY:
  Round 1:  Included in all packages
  Round 2:  Included in Growth and Agency packages
  Round 3+: Billed at $75–$150 per round (or package add-on)

STAGE 6: SCHEDULING & PUBLISHING
───────────────────────────────────

OPTIMAL POSTING TIMES (2025 benchmarks):
  Instagram:
    Best days:    Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday
    Best times:   9–10am, 12–1pm, 7–9pm (client's local time)
  Facebook:
    Best days:    Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
    Best times:   9am, 1pm, 3pm
  LinkedIn:
    Best days:    Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
    Best times:   8–9am, 12pm, 5–6pm
  TikTok:
    Best days:    Tuesday, Thursday, Friday
    Best times:   7–9am, 12–3pm, 7–11pm
  Pinterest:
    Best days:    Saturday, Sunday (evergreen — less time-sensitive)
    Best times:   8–11pm (users planning mode)

SCHEDULING CHECKLIST (per post):
  □ Caption copy pasted (no formatting errors)
  □ Visual attached at correct specs
  □ Hashtags added in correct field
  □ Geotag added (local businesses)
  □ Tagged accounts added (collaborations/features)
  □ Cross-platform scheduling confirmed (where applicable)
  □ UTM link added if post contains a link
  □ First comment pre-written (if hashtags go in comments on IG)
  □ Date/time confirmed in client's timezone

SECTION 2: BATCH CREATION EFFICIENCY SYSTEM
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

BATCH BY TYPE, NOT BY CLIENT
  Instead of: Client A post 1 → Client A post 2 → Client B post 1
  Do this:    All captions for all clients → All graphics → Schedule all

TIME BLOCKING FOR CONTENT CREATION:
  Monday:     Research + strategy briefs for all clients
  Tuesday:    All copywriting (captions, scripts, copy)
  Wednesday:  All design/graphics
  Thursday:   Review, revisions, approval submissions
  Friday:     Scheduling + community management setup

AI ACCELERATION WORKFLOW:
  Step 1: Input content brief into Claude/ChatGPT
  Step 2: Generate 3 variations per post
  Step 3: Select best version as base
  Step 4: Edit for client voice (30% rewrite minimum)
  Step 5: Run through brand voice checklist
  Step 6: Human review before submitting to client

  IMPORTANT: Never submit raw AI output without editing.
  It's a starting point, not a finished product.

CONTENT REPURPOSING MATRIX:
  One piece of content → Multiple formats:
  
  Long-form LinkedIn post → IG carousel (key points)
                          → 3 tweets/X posts
                          → Reel script
                          → Newsletter section
                          → Pinterest pin (if visual)
  
  Podcast/video → Quote graphics
               → Transcript blog post
               → Short clips for Reels/TikTok
               → LinkedIn article

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 3: QUALITY CONTROL STANDARDS
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PRE-PUBLISHING FINAL CHECKS:
  □ Spelling and grammar (Grammarly Pass)
  □ Brand voice consistency (read 2 previous posts for comparison)
  □ No competitor mentions (unintentional)
  □ No copyrighted music (if video)
  □ Links work and go to correct destination
  □ Client hasn't announced anything that makes post outdated
  □ Visual is pixel-perfect at mobile view
  □ No sensitive topics in context of current news cycle
  □ All tagged accounts/handles spelled correctly
  □ Post has been approved by client (or auto-approval timer passed)

COMMON MISTAKES TO PREVENT:
  ✗ Scheduling for wrong timezone (always double-check)
  ✗ Using client A's brand colors in client B's content
  ✗ Forgetting to switch scheduling tool to correct client profile
  ✗ Posting sales content on a holiday (check awareness calendar)
  ✗ Typos in CTA links
  ✗ Captions that end without a CTA
  ✗ Wrong file dimensions causing blurry visuals

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 4: VIDEO CONTENT SOP (REELS/TIKTOK)
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

SCRIPT STRUCTURE (30–60 second Reel):
  :00–:03   Hook — visual or verbal pattern interrupt
  :03–:08   Problem/context setup
  :08–:45   Value delivery (main content)
  :45–:55   CTA (follow, comment, save)
  :55–:60   Brand sign-off or visual logo

PRE-PRODUCTION CHECKLIST:
  □ Script approved or outlined
  □ Location/backdrop is clean and professional
  □ Lighting is adequate (ring light or natural light)
  □ Audio check (quiet environment or mic connected)
  □ Trending audio identified (if applicable)
  □ Outfit/brand colors considered for on-camera content

POST-PRODUCTION CHECKLIST:
  □ Captions added (85% of video watched on mute)
  □ Correct aspect ratio (9:16)
  □ Max 30MB file size for smooth upload
  □ No third-party watermarks (TikTok watermark = IG reach penalty)
  □ B-roll or text overlays keep viewer engaged
  □ Hook is clear in first 1–3 seconds
  □ Sound/music licensed for commercial use
