Choose the tone that fits your hosting style.

---

## HOW AIRBNB REVIEWS WORK (Quick Host Reference)

- Both host and guest have **14 days** to leave a review after check-out
- Reviews are only visible after **both parties** have submitted, or after the 14-day window closes
- You CANNOT edit a review after submission — write thoughtfully
- **Leave the guest review first** — it encourages them to reciprocate
- A 5-star average dramatically improves search ranking and booking conversion

**The formula for 5-star requests:**
1. Thank them genuinely
2. Mention something specific and positive about their stay
3. Ask directly (not vaguely) for a 5-star review
4. Make it easy (remind them where to leave it)
5. Keep it brief

## TEMPLATE 1 — WARM & PERSONAL

**When to use:** Guests who seemed to genuinely enjoy themselves.

---

Hi [Guest Name],

Thank you again for staying at [Property Name]. It was such a pleasure having you — [mention something genuine if possible: "the place looked spotless when you left" / "I loved hearing you enjoyed the neighborhood"].

If your stay was wonderful, I'd be really grateful for a 5-star review on Airbnb. Reviews from guests like you are what help independent hosts like me compete with hotels and big rental companies. It makes a real difference.

You can leave a review in the Airbnb app under Trips → [Property Name] → Write a Review.

Thank you from the heart. I hope to see you in [City] again someday.

[Host Name]

## QUICK REFERENCE — REVIEW REQUEST TIMING

| Action | When |
|---|---|
| Leave guest a 5-star review | 1–2 hours after check-out |
| Send Template 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5 | Same day as check-out message |
| Send Template 8 (follow-up) | 5 days after check-out (once only) |
| Review window closes | 14 days after check-out |

**Key principle:** Never send more than 2 review request messages. One in the post-stay thank you, one follow-up if no review after 5 days. More than that feels pushy and can backfire.
