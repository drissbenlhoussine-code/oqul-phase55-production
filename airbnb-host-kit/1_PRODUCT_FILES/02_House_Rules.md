### 12. VIOLATIONS

Violation of these house rules may result in:
- A written warning via Airbnb message
- Additional charges through Airbnb's Resolution Center
- Immediate termination of the reservation without refund

We want every stay to be positive for both parties. These rules exist to protect the property, the neighborhood, and future guests — not to restrict your enjoyment. Thank you for your understanding.
