## MESSAGE 1 — BOOKING CONFIRMATION

---

Thank you for booking [Property Name] — I'm thrilled to host you!

I'm looking forward to your stay from [arrival date] to [departure date].

**A few quick details:**
- Check-in: [Time]
- Check-out: [Time]
- Address: [Full address]
- Max guests: [Number]

I'll send you full check-in instructions (access code, parking, WiFi, etc.) about 48 hours before you arrive. In the meantime, if you have any questions at all, feel free to reach out here.

Looking forward to it!

## MESSAGE 5 — MID-STAY CHECK-IN

---

Just checking in to make sure everything is going smoothly with your stay. Is the property comfortable? Anything you need?

If you've found something that needs attention — whether it's a light bulb, a noisy appliance, anything — please let me know now so I can take care of it before it impacts your stay.

## MESSAGE 6 — CHECK-OUT REMINDER (EVENING BEFORE)
*(Send between 6–8 PM the evening before departure)*

---

Hi [Guest Name]!

Your stay at [Property Name] is wrapping up tomorrow — I hope it's been a great [number of nights].

**Check-out time: [TIME]**

A few things before you leave:
- Leave dirty linens on the [bed / floor]
- Run the dishwasher or leave dishes in the sink
- Take trash to the outdoor bin at [location]
- Lock the front door: [instructions]
- [Key: return to lockbox / Smart lock auto-locks]

It's been a pleasure hosting you. If your stay was wonderful, I'd be really grateful for a 5-star review on Airbnb — it makes a big difference for independent hosts like me.

Safe travels tomorrow!
[Host Name]

## MESSAGE 7 — POST-STAY THANK YOU + REVIEW REQUEST
*(Send 1–2 hours after check-out time)*

---

Thank you so much for staying at [Property Name] — I hope [City] treated you well!

I've left you a [5-star] review on Airbnb. If you have a moment, I'd love to hear your thoughts on your stay. Your review helps future guests make confident decisions and helps us continue improving as hosts.

Reviews can be left directly through your Airbnb app or at airbnb.com/trips.

Thank you again — it was a genuine pleasure hosting you. I'd love to welcome you back anytime.

With warm regards,
[Host Name]

## MESSAGE 9 — SPECIAL REQUEST RESPONSE (EARLY CHECK-IN)
*(When a guest requests early check-in)*

---

Hi [Guest Name]!

Thanks for reaching out! Let me check on the property schedule.

**[If you can accommodate:]**
Great news — early check-in should work for you! The property will be ready by [earlier time]. Please still message me on the morning of arrival to confirm, and I'll let you know if anything changes with the cleaner's timeline.

**[If you can't accommodate:]**
Unfortunately the property won't be ready before [standard time] on your arrival day — we have a back-to-back turnover and our cleaner needs the full time to get everything right for you.

Here are some options in the meantime:
- [Nearby coffee shop] at [address] is great for working or relaxing
- [Luggage storage option if available]
- I'll message you as soon as the property is ready — sometimes it's earlier than expected

Thank you for understanding!
[Host Name]

## MESSAGE 11 — DAMAGE ACKNOWLEDGMENT
*(When damage has occurred and the guest has reported it)*

---

Hi [Guest Name],

Thank you for letting me know about [description of damage]. I really appreciate your honesty — it makes such a difference.

[Choose response based on severity:]

**Minor damage:**
I completely understand — accidents happen. We'll take care of it. No further action is needed on your end. Enjoy the rest of your stay!

**Damage requiring a claim:**
I'll need to assess the full cost of [repair / replacement] before I know the final amount. I may follow up through Airbnb's Resolution Center. I'll always try to handle this as fairly as possible, and I'll show you the receipts.

Thank you again for reaching out directly. Honest guests are the reason this works.
[Host Name]
