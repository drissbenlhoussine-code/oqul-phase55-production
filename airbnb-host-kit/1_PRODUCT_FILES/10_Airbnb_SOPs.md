## SOP 06 — GUEST SCREENING PROCESS

**Purpose:** Filter out problem guests before they become problem stays.

**Step 1 — Review their profile:**
- Photo present? (Not required but positive signal)
- Bio written? ("I'm a professional who travels for work" vs. blank)
- Verified ID? (Required per your settings — verify)
- Review history: read all reviews, not just the star average

**Step 2 — Evaluate the booking details:**
- How many guests? Does it match your max?
- How long is the stay? Longer stays = more exposure, more scrutiny
- What's the stated purpose? Does it fit your property?
- Any special requests? Note them

**Step 3 — Message them before accepting (optional but recommended):**

*Use this message for new or unclear guests:*

"Hi [Name], thanks for your interest in [Property Name]! Could you tell me a little about your trip — what brings you to [City], and who will be staying with you? We love hosting great guests and just want to make sure we're a good fit. Looking forward to hearing from you!"

**Step 4 — Trust your instincts:**
If something feels off after reviewing, it probably is. Decline professionally:

"Hi [Name], thank you for your interest in [Property Name]. Unfortunately, based on the details of this request, I don't think we're the right fit. I hope you find a great place for your stay!"

## SOP 07 — DISPUTE & CLAIM HANDLING

**Purpose:** Handle damage claims, disputes, and bad reviews professionally.

**If damage is discovered after check-out:**
1. Document everything: photograph all damage before cleaning or repairing
2. Get a repair/replacement quote from a professional within 48 hours
3. File an AirCover claim through Airbnb within **14 days** of check-out
4. Contact the guest first, politely — many guests prefer to settle directly
5. If no response within 24 hours, escalate through Airbnb Resolution Center

**When contacting a guest about damage:**
"Hi [Name], I hope you made it home safely. I noticed some damage to [item] after your departure: [description]. I've attached photos. The replacement/repair cost is estimated at $[amount]. Could we discuss this? I'd prefer to resolve it directly before involving Airbnb's Resolution Center."

**For negative reviews:**
- Wait 24 hours before responding — don't respond while emotional
- Keep the response short (3–5 sentences max) — public response is for future guests
- Acknowledge any valid point without being defensive
- Explain what you've done or will do to improve
- Do not attack or argue with the guest

**For false reviews:**
Contact Airbnb support and request removal if the review violates Airbnb's review policy (extortion, irrelevant content, or factually incorrect statements about your listing).

## SOP 10 — REVIEW MANAGEMENT PROCESS

**Purpose:** Maximize positive reviews and respond to all reviews professionally.

**After every check-out:**
1. Leave the guest a review within 24 hours — be honest but professional
2. Send post-stay thank you with review request (Template 5, Message 7)
3. If no review in 5 days: send one follow-up (Template 5, Message 8)

**Reviewing guests — what to write:**
*For 5-star guests:*
"[Name] was an excellent guest — [Property Name] was left in perfect condition. Communication was easy and respectful of our house rules throughout. We'd welcome [Name] back anytime. Highly recommend!"

*For good guests with minor issues:*
"[Name] was polite and responsive. Check-out went smoothly. I'd recommend [Name] to other hosts."

*For guests you wouldn't host again:*
Be factual and brief. Only note things that affected the property or future guests. Example: "Communication was difficult and the property required additional cleaning at departure. Would not host again."

**Key principle:** Your guest review appears on their profile — other hosts read it. Be honest so the community functions well.
