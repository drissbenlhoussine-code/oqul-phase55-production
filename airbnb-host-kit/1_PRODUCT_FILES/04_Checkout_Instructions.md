# CHECK-OUT INSTRUCTIONS TEMPLATE
## Airbnb Host Starter Kit — Template 4 of 10

**HOW TO USE:**
Two versions below:
- Version A: Short checklist (print and leave in property)
- Version B: Full message (send via Airbnb the night before departure)

---

## VERSION A — POSTED CHECKOUT CHECKLIST
*(Print and laminate — leave on kitchen counter or fridge)*

---

# CHECK-OUT BY [TIME]

Thank you so much for staying with us at [PROPERTY NAME].

Before you leave, please take 5 minutes to complete this checklist:

---

### KITCHEN
- [ ] Dishes washed and put away — OR — dishwasher running
- [ ] Food cleared from fridge (leave sealed, unopened items if you like)
- [ ] Stove wiped down — no food residue
- [ ] Coffee maker emptied / pod removed
- [ ] Trash bag tied and taken to outdoor bin at [location]

### BEDROOMS
- [ ] Dirty linens left on the [bed / bathroom floor] — our cleaner will handle washing
- [ ] Personal items packed (check under pillows, in nightstand drawers)
- [ ] Windows closed

### BATHROOMS
- [ ] Used towels left on [floor / towel rack] — no need to fold
- [ ] Check for personal items (chargers, toiletries, razors, medication)
- [ ] Shower/tub tap off

### LIVING AREAS
- [ ] Remote controls left on [TV stand / coffee table]
- [ ] Couch cushions replaced if rearranged
- [ ] Check for chargers, items under sofa cushions

### DOORS & SECURITY
- [ ] All windows closed and locked
- [ ] Back door / patio door locked
- [ ] Front door locked
- [ ] [Key returned to lockbox / Smart lock will auto-lock]

### FINAL
- [ ] A/C or heat turned [off / to standby temp: ___°F]
- [ ] All lights off
- [ ] Garage closed (if applicable)

---

**Key return:** [Leave in lockbox / Leave on kitchen counter / Return to host]

**Lockbox:** Scramble the code after returning the key.

Questions before you leave? Call or text [Host Name] at [Phone].

*We'd love to host you again. If you enjoyed your stay, a review means the world to us!*

---

## VERSION B — CHECK-OUT MESSAGE
*(Send via Airbnb the evening before departure)*

---

Hi [Guest Name]!

Your stay is almost up — I hope it's been a wonderful [number] nights. Just a quick note with check-out details for tomorrow.

**Check-out time: [TIME]**

---

**Before you leave (takes about 5 minutes):**

**Kitchen:**
- Run the dishwasher or leave dishes in the sink — our cleaner will handle it either way, but running the dishwasher is always appreciated
- Bag up any trash and drop it in the outdoor bin [location]
- Empty the fridge of your food (feel free to leave anything sealed and unused)

**Bedrooms & Bathrooms:**
- Strip the beds and leave linens on the floor — no need to remake them
- Leave used towels in the bathroom
- Double-check for personal items: chargers, toiletries, medication, anything under beds or in drawers

**Doors & Security:**
- Close and lock all windows
- Lock the back/patio door
- Front door: [lock instructions — pull shut / use key + lockbox / keypad auto-locks]
- [If lockbox:] Return the key to the lockbox and scramble the code

**Temperature:**
Please set the thermostat to [temperature]°F before leaving so the property maintains a comfortable base temperature.

---

**A note on your belongings:**
Guests occasionally leave items behind. If this happens, message me ASAP and I'll retrieve the item before the next guest arrives. Items left for more than [X days] without contact will be donated.

---

**Your review:**
If your stay was everything you hoped for, I'd be truly grateful for a 5-star review. Reviews are what make Airbnb hosting sustainable for independent hosts like me — it takes 60 seconds and means everything.

---

It's been a pleasure hosting you, [Guest First Name]. Safe travels, and I hope to welcome you back to [City] someday soon.

Warmly,
[Host Name]
[Phone]

---

## HOST NOTES (not sent to guests)

**Send this message:** The evening before check-out (around 6–8 PM)
**If guest hasn't responded:** No action needed — just a courtesy note
**If late check-out requested:** Confirm or deny before sending this template
**Cleaner notification:** Alert your cleaner after you send this, confirming check-out time
