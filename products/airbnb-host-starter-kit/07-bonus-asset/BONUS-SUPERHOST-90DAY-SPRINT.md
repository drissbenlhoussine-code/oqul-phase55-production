# Superhost 90-Day Sprint
## Week-by-Week Execution Plan · 0 Reviews to Superhost Eligibility

---

## WHAT THIS IS

This is a 13-week, week-by-week execution plan to achieve Superhost eligibility as fast as legally and operationally possible. It maps every required action to a specific week, with the specific outcome that action produces. Follow it in sequence.

**Superhost is assessed on April 1 and October 1.** The fastest path from zero to Superhost is to launch within 3 months of an assessment date with enough lead time to accumulate the minimum 10 stays.

**Minimum criteria to qualify:**
- 10 completed stays OR 100 booked nights
- 4.8+ overall rating
- 90%+ response rate
- 0 host-initiated cancellations

---

## PRE-SPRINT: DECIDE YOUR LAUNCH DATE

Before Week 1, calculate backward from the assessment date:

**Targeting October 1 assessment:**
- Launch no later than: July 1 (gives you 13 weeks + buffer for booking gaps)
- Week 1 of this sprint: July 1

**Targeting April 1 assessment:**
- Launch no later than: January 1 (13 weeks to accumulate minimum stays)
- Week 1 of this sprint: January 1

If you are launching in May (too late for April 1, not enough time for October 1), use the sprint to maximise review count and prepare — target October 1 as your first assessment.

## WEEK 1 — FOUNDATION (Days 1–7)

**Goal:** Listing live, all systems operational.

| Day | Action | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Complete `03-PROPERTY-SETUP-CHECKLIST.md` Sections 1–7 | Property ready for guests |
| Day 1 | Professional photos booked (SOP-01 Step 1) | Photo shoot confirmed |
| Day 2–3 | Load all 14 message templates into Airbnb Saved Messages | Communication system ready |
| Day 3 | Set pricing per `02-PRICING-STRATEGY-PLAYBOOK.md` — base rate, seasonal, disable Smart Pricing | Pricing configured |
| Day 4 | Connect PriceLabs or Wheelhouse | Dynamic pricing active |
| Day 4 | Write all 6 listing description sections (SOP-01 Steps 2–3) | Description complete |
| Day 5 | Photo shoot day | 20–30 images delivered |
| Day 6 | Upload photos, order them, write captions | Visual complete |
| Day 7 | Publish listing | Listing live on Airbnb |

**Week 1 metrics to track:**
- Listing views (check Airbnb Performance tab after 48 hours)
- First booking received: yes/no

**If no booking in 7 days:** Drop base rate by 10% for weeks 2–3. New listings get a visibility boost in their first 14 days — this window is your highest-traffic period. Do not miss it with pricing that is too high.

## WEEK 2 — FIRST BOOKING (Days 8–14)

**Goal:** First confirmed booking. First guest communication perfect.

| Action | Standard |
|---|---|
| Monitor Airbnb inbox: respond to every inquiry within 30 minutes | Response rate: 100% |
| When booking confirmed: send MSG-01 within 2 hours | Communication: first contact done |
| Confirm cleaning team scheduled for check-in day | Operations ready |
| Confirm smart lock setup and first-booking test | Access ready |

**If still no booking by Day 14:**
1. Lower base rate by another 10% (now 20% below your target — temporary)
2. Re-examine your primary photo: compare to the first row of results in your neighbourhood search
3. Check that Instant Book is enabled
4. Check that your calendar is open at least 30 days in advance

**Week 2 mindset:** Your first booking is the hardest to get because you have no reviews. The market is pricing you against competitors with reviews. Accept the temporary discount — the first 5 reviews are worth more than the €20–€30 per stay you are discounting.

## WEEK 3 — FIRST STAY (Days 15–21)

**Assumption:** First guest arriving this week.

| Timing | Action | Template |
|---|---|---|
| 48h before | Send pre-arrival info | MSG-02 |
| Morning of check-in | Send check-in instructions | MSG-03 |
| 2h after check-in time | Confirm arrival (noise monitor or smart lock check) | — |
| Day 2 | Mid-stay check-in | MSG-04 |
| Evening before checkout | Checkout reminder | MSG-05 |
| Within 3h of checkout | Post-checkout thanks | MSG-06 |
| Within 6h of checkout | Review request | MSG-07 |
| Within 24h of checkout | Leave your review | SOP-09 |

**Critical for first stay:** Do every step from the above sequence, on time, without exception. Your first review sets the tone. A 4-star first review makes the path to Superhost harder. A 5-star first review builds immediate trust in the listing.

---

## WEEK 4 — REVIEW ANALYSIS AND ITERATION

**Goal:** First review received. Analyse and improve.

**If first review is 5 stars across all subcategories:**
Continue as planned. Maintain pricing discount until 5 reviews.

**If first review is 4 stars overall or any subcategory below 5:**
Immediately identify the cause:
1. Read the review carefully. What specific language did the guest use?
2. Cross-reference with `04-GUEST-EXPERIENCE-STANDARDS.md` Section 2 (root cause table)
3. Make the change before the next guest arrives
4. Do not accept "4 stars is fine" — at this stage every point matters

**Example causes and fixes:**

| 4-Star Cause | Fix Required | Time to Fix |
|---|---|---|
| "Check-in was confusing" | Rewrite MSG-03, test with third party | 1 hour |
| "Place was clean but..." | Identify specific cleanliness issue, brief cleaner | 30 minutes |
| "WiFi was slow" | Run speed test, upgrade plan if needed | 1–3 days |
| "Listing didn't match" | Audit listing accuracy for overstatements | 2 hours |
| "Host slow to respond" | Set push notifications on all devices | 10 minutes |

## WEEKS 5–7 — ACCUMULATION PHASE

**Goal:** Reach 5 reviews at 4.8+ average. Raise pricing to market rate.

**Pricing adjustment at 5 reviews:**
Move from -15% to market rate. You have earned it — social proof now supports the price.

**Checklist for Weeks 5–7:**

- [ ] All message templates sent on time, every booking
- [ ] Mid-stay check-in sent on Day 2 of every stay
- [ ] Review left within 24 hours of every checkout
- [ ] Airbnb Performance tab checked weekly: views, conversion, response rate
- [ ] Any maintenance issue resolved within 4 hours of being reported
- [ ] Competitor pricing checked: are you still at or below market?
- [ ] Review score tracked in TAB-06 after every review received

**At 5 reviews:**
- Apply for any "New Host" incentive programs Airbnb currently offers (these change annually — check your Airbnb dashboard)
- Review your listing description: quotes from early positive reviews can be incorporated naturally
- Check if any positive review mentions a specific feature prominently — add that feature to your listing title or description if it differentiates you

## WEEK 8 — HALFWAY MARK REVIEW

**Goal:** Self-assessment against Superhost criteria at midpoint.

Run through this checklist at the start of Week 8:

| Criterion | Current Status | Gap | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total stays completed | [X] of 10 | [10-X remaining] | |
| Overall rating (trailing) | [X]/5 | Need 4.8+ | |
| Response rate | [X]% | Need 90%+ | |
| Host cancellations | [X] | Need 0 | |

**Projection:** At your current booking pace, how many stays will you have by the assessment date?

If 10 stays is not achievable at current pace:
- Consider lowering minimum stay to 1 night for a 3-week period (increases turnover count)
- Consider offering weekend discounts to boost short-notice bookings
- Consider switching to 1-night minimum for January or low-demand weeks (these are otherwise going to be empty anyway)

**Warning:** 100 booked nights is the alternative qualifier. If you are running at 80%+ occupancy, you may qualify via nights rather than stay count.

## WEEKS 9–11 — OPTIMISATION PHASE

**Goal:** Reach 8+ stays, 4.8+ average, 100% response rate. Add second platform.

**Week 9 — Second Platform:**

Connect Booking.com or VRBO via a channel manager (Lodgify, Hostaway, or Hostify at a basic tier):
1. Connect Airbnb calendar to channel manager
2. Connect Booking.com or VRBO calendar via the same channel manager
3. Confirm that any booking on one platform instantly blocks the same dates on all others
4. Set your Booking.com or VRBO rates 3–5% below Airbnb (these platforms typically attract slightly more price-sensitive guests)

**Week 10 — PriceLabs Review:**

By Week 10 you have 60+ days of pricing data. Review:
- Were any events in your area underpriced? (Look at your ADR on event dates vs. surrounding dates)
- Were any vacancy periods left empty that a 20% discount would have filled?
- Adjust your minimum price, event multipliers, and lead-time discounts based on actual outcomes

**Week 11 — Listing Photo Review:**

After 60+ days live, compare your listing click-through rate (views to booking requests) to Week 1. If conversion has improved: your photos are working. If it is the same or lower: reshoot the primary photo.

Check whether any guest has mentioned a feature in a positive review that is not prominent in your photos. Add a photo of that feature if missing.

## WEEKS 12–13 — SUPERHOST FINAL PUSH

**Goal:** Reach 10 confirmed stays before assessment date. Confirm all criteria met.

**Pre-assessment checklist (complete by Week 12):**

| Criterion | Status | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| 10+ stays completed | | |
| Overall rating ≥ 4.8 | | |
| Response rate ≥ 90% (check Airbnb Performance tab — use Airbnb's own metric, not your estimate) | | |
| Zero host cancellations | | |
| Assessment date is within next 14 days | | |

**If you are 1–2 stays short of 10:**
Consider accepting a 1-night booking at a moderate discount (your goal is stay count, not maximum revenue in this final window). The Superhost badge, once earned, pays for itself in higher conversion rates within 30–60 days.

**If response rate is below 90%:**
Check Airbnb's Performance tab → Response Rate. Airbnb calculates this from the time of first message — not from every reply. If you have any unanswered inquiries in the past 12 months, they are dragging the rate. Set up Airbnb's automated response for inquiries that arrive when you are genuinely unreachable.

## POST-ASSESSMENT — MAINTAINING SUPERHOST

Superhost is assessed every 6 months (April 1 and October 1). Once earned, it is not permanent — you re-qualify each period.

**Quarterly Superhost check (run in February, May, August, November):**

| Criterion | Current | Threshold | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stays (trailing 12 months) | | 10 | |
| Rating | | 4.8 | |
| Response rate | | 90% | |
| Host cancellations | | 0 | |

The goal is to arrive at every assessment date with comfortable headroom — not exactly 4.80 rating and exactly 10 stays, but 4.87 and 14 stays. Headroom absorbs the occasional 4-star review or response slip.

## THE SUPERHOST REVENUE EFFECT

Based on patterns across European mid-tier markets:

| Before Superhost | After Superhost (same property, same market) |
|---|---|
| Market-rate ADR | +10–15% ADR premium maintained |
| Standard conversion | +15–20% conversion improvement |
| Standard visibility | Appears in "Superhost" filter results |
| Annual revenue at €30,000 base | €33,000–€34,500 projected after badge |

The Superhost badge earns approximately €3,000–€4,500 in additional annual revenue for a €30,000/year property, holding all other variables constant. The 90-day investment to reach it generates a >20× return per year it is maintained.
