# SOP-09: Review Collection
## Standard Operating Procedure — 5-Star Review System

**Run time:** 5–10 minutes per booking  
**Trigger:** Guest checkout  
**Owner:** Host  
**Goal:** Consistent 4.9+ rating; every eligible guest leaves a review

---

## THE REVIEW PSYCHOLOGY

Guests leave reviews when: (1) they had an exceptional experience, (2) they had a terrible experience, or (3) they were asked at the right moment. The "silent majority" of satisfied guests need the ask. Your job is to time it perfectly.

**The optimal review window:** 1–4 hours after checkout. At this point, the guest is in transit (airport, train, coffee), thinking about their trip, and has not yet been absorbed by their next destination. This is the window.

---

## STEP 1: Set Your Review Reminder (During Check-Out Day Setup)

1.1 On the morning of each checkout day, set a phone reminder for 2 hours after checkout time:  
   - Example: "Checkout 11am → Reminder at 1pm: Send review request to [Guest Name]"  
1.2 This is the single most important timing decision in the review process

---

## STEP 2: Send the Review Request Message (2 Hours Post-Checkout)

2.1 Open the Airbnb booking  
2.2 Send MSG-07-REVIEW-REQUEST.md (saved as "⭐ Review Request")  
2.3 The message must feel personal, not automated:  
   - Reference something specific about their stay  
   - Be brief (3 sentences maximum)  
   - Include a warm thank-you before the request  
2.4 Never use "If you have time..." — it gives an exit. Use "We'd really love to hear from you."  
2.5 Log review request sent in your Review Scorecard (Tab-06)

---

## STEP 3: Leave Your Guest Review First (Within 24 Hours of Checkout)

3.1 Navigate to: Airbnb → Trips → Past Trips → Leave Review  
3.2 Write a genuine, specific review of the guest (see SOP-07 for templates)  
3.3 Submit within 24 hours of checkout  
3.4 Why this matters: when you submit your review, Airbnb notifies the guest that "your host has left you a review." This prompts them to submit theirs to see what you wrote.  
3.5 Airbnb shows both reviews only after both are submitted OR after 14 days — whichever comes first

---

## STEP 4: If No Review After 5 Days

4.1 Send one follow-up message (only one — do not spam):
```
Hi [Name], hope you've settled back in! I just left you a review — I'd love to know what 
your experience was like at [Property Name] if you have a moment to share. It genuinely 
makes a difference for future guests. Thank you! [Name]
```
4.2 This is the final outreach. Do not chase again.  
4.3 The 14-day review window closes automatically — after that, the opportunity is gone.

---

## STEP 5: Review Received — Respond Within 24 Hours

When a review is posted, respond publicly. Your response is visible to future guests and is part of your listing's story.

**5-Star Review Response:**
→ Use MSG-13-REVIEW-RESPONSE-5STAR.md  
→ Acknowledge what specifically they praised  
→ 2–3 sentences. Do not write an essay.

**4-Star Review Response:**
→ Thank them warmly  
→ If they mentioned an improvement area: "We've already acted on [feedback]"  
→ Do not become defensive or justify the 4-star rating

**3-Star or Below:**
→ Use MSG-14-REVIEW-RESPONSE-NEGATIVE.md  
→ This is your only chance to present your side to future guests  
→ Be professional, factual, and solution-focused  
→ Do NOT argue, do NOT call the guest out publicly  
→ Future guests will read your response to a bad review more carefully than the review itself

---

## STEP 6: Review Analysis (Monthly)

6.1 Each month, export or note all reviews from the previous 30 days in your Review Scorecard  
6.2 Categorise feedback into themes:

| Theme | Examples | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Cleanliness mentions | "Spotless," "could be cleaner" | Flag to cleaning team |
| Check-in mentions | "Seamless," "confusing instructions" | Update SOP-05 |
| Communication mentions | "Responsive," "slow to reply" | Review response time data |
| Value mentions | "Great value," "pricey for what it is" | Review pricing vs. amenity level |
| Location mentions | "Perfect location," "further than expected" | Review listing description accuracy |
| Comfort mentions | "So comfortable," "bed could be better" | Assess mattress/pillow quality |

6.3 Identify the top 2 recurring themes — fix them before the next month  
6.4 Update the Guest Experience Standards document if a structural issue is found

---

## THE 5-STAR FORMULA

Earning 5 stars is systematic, not accidental. Here is the complete chain:

```
Accurate listing → Right guests arrive → 
Clean property → Smooth check-in → 
Responsive host during stay → 
Problem resolved fast (if any) → 
Review request at the right moment → 
5 stars
```

Every broken link in this chain costs you a star. Track where your 4-star reviews lose their fifth star — that's your optimisation target.

---

## REVIEW BENCHMARKS

| Rating | Action |
|---|---|
| ≥4.9 | Maintain current standards |
| 4.8–4.89 | Identify your most common 4-star category; fix it this month |
| 4.7–4.79 | Conduct a full property audit; rebuild one system |
| Below 4.7 | This is urgent — read every recent review, identify patterns, major overhaul needed |
| Below 4.5 | Airbnb may penalise listing visibility; contact Airbnb support proactively |
