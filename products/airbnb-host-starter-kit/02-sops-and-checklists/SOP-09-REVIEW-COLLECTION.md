# SOP-09: Review Collection
## 5-Star Review System · Review Request Timing · Response Protocol

---

**When to use:** After every checkout.
**Time required:** 10 minutes per booking.
**Goal:** 4.9+ average across Cleanliness, Accuracy, Check-in, Communication, Location, Value.

---

## SECTION 1 — THE REVIEW FUNNEL

Understanding why guests do or do not leave reviews:

| Guest Outcome | % Who Leave Reviews (Industry Average) |
|---|---|
| Stay had 0 problems, no host contact during stay | ~35% |
| Stay had 0 problems, received mid-stay check-in message | ~52% |
| Stay had a problem that was resolved well | ~68% |
| Stay had a problem that was NOT resolved | ~85% (and it will be negative) |

**The lesson:** The mid-stay check-in message (SOP-06) and post-checkout outreach (this SOP) are the two highest-leverage review collection actions. Guests do not review out of obligation — they review when prompted at the right moment or when they feel strongly (positively or negatively).

---

## SECTION 2 — REVIEW TIMING SEQUENCE

The window between checkout and review submission is narrow. Review response rates drop significantly after 48 hours.

| Time After Checkout | Action | Message |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 hours | Post-checkout thank-you | MSG-06 |
| 3–6 hours | Review request | MSG-07 (primary) |
| 24–48 hours (if no review) | Leave your own review first (triggers Airbnb notification to guest) | — |
| 48–72 hours (if still no review) | Follow-up message | MSG-07 follow-up variant |
| 14 days | Review window closes | No further action |

**Leave your review within 24 hours.** When you submit a review, Airbnb notifies the guest that you have reviewed them. This notification is the most effective review prompt available to you. Guests who see this notification are 3–4× more likely to leave a review than guests who received only your message.

## SECTION 3 — THE MSG-07 REVIEW REQUEST

See `04-communication-templates/MSG-07-REVIEW-REQUEST.md` for the full template.

**Key principles:**
1. Ask once, directly: "I'd love it if you had a chance to share your experience in a review."
2. Do not instruct them to leave a 5-star review: Airbnb's terms prohibit incentivising specific review content. Asking for a "5-star review" or "perfect score" can result in a listing suspension.
3. Do not imply you will change your review of them based on their review of you: this is manipulation and detectable.
4. Make it easy: "It only takes 2 minutes and really helps other travellers find the right place."

**What makes guests leave reviews:**
- They feel the host cared about their experience (mid-stay message)
- They were pleasantly surprised by something (welcome basket, local tip, quick problem resolution)
- They are asked at the right moment (in transit, when they have 5 idle minutes)
- They want to help other travellers

**What prevents guests from leaving reviews:**
- They felt anonymous (no host contact during stay)
- Too much time has passed and the stay feels distant
- The request feels transactional or pressuring

## SECTION 4 — HOW TO RESPOND TO REVIEWS

### 5-Star Reviews (MSG-13)

Respond to every 5-star review within 48 hours. Your response is public and visible to potential future guests. It demonstrates engagement and professionalism.

**Four response variations (see MSG-13 for full text):**
1. Standard: thank the guest, mention one specific detail from their review
2. Review-specific: reference something the guest mentioned that was positive
3. Repeat booking invitation: for guests who mentioned they want to return
4. Seasonal: when the review mentions season or specific local event

**Response length:** 2–4 sentences. Short responses read as authentic. Long responses read as marketing.

### Sub-5-Star Reviews (4 stars or below) (MSG-14)

Respond to every review below 5 stars within 24 hours (faster than 5-star responses — the damage mitigation is time-sensitive).

**The structure of a negative review response:**
1. Thank the guest for their stay (not for the review)
2. Acknowledge the specific issue they raised (do not dispute facts, even if you believe they are inaccurate)
3. State what you have done or will do to address it
4. Close professionally — do not end with bitterness

**What NOT to do in a negative review response:**
- Do not argue with the guest's experience
- Do not explain why the problem was not your fault
- Do not mention other positive reviews as counterevidence
- Do not apologise excessively — one clear acknowledgement is enough
- Do not name the guest or repeat their complaint in full (future guests will read this)

**Four scenarios (see MSG-14 for full text):**
- Version A: Cleanliness complaint
- Version B: Accuracy complaint (description mismatch)
- Version C: Communication complaint (response time or issue handling)
- Version D: General 3-star with no specific complaint

## SECTION 5 — PREVENTING NEGATIVE REVIEWS

Most negative reviews are preventable. They come from:

| Root Cause | % of Negative Reviews | Prevention |
|---|---|---|
| Listing does not match property | 28% | Accuracy audit every 90 days (`05-PLATFORM-OPTIMIZATION-GUIDE.md`) |
| Cleanliness below expectation | 24% | SOP-04 enforced on every turnover, random walkthroughs |
| Access or check-in problem | 19% | SOP-05 check-in instruction audit every 90 days |
| Unresolved issue during stay | 16% | SOP-06 mid-stay check-in + 30-minute issue response |
| Host communication | 8% | Templates + 60-minute response standard |
| Pricing / value perception | 5% | Welcome basket + managing expectations in listing |

Addressing the top 3 causes (accuracy, cleanliness, check-in) eliminates 71% of negative review risk.

## SECTION 6 — REVIEW TRACKING

Track in TAB-06 (Review Scorecard). After every review, record:
- Overall score
- Each subcategory score (Cleanliness, Accuracy, Check-in, Communication, Location, Value)
- Any specific issue mentioned
- Your response sent (yes/no, within 48h?)

**Monthly review audit (part of SOP-10):**
- Calculate 30-day rolling average for each subcategory
- Identify any subcategory trending below 4.7
- Assign a root cause from Section 5 above
- Set one action item to address it before the next monthly review

---

## SECTION 7 — PRIVATE FEEDBACK

Airbnb allows you to send a private note to the guest alongside the public review. Use this to:
- Thank a guest who left a particularly helpful review
- Ask for clarification on a specific issue mentioned (do not argue — gather information)
- Acknowledge something they reported privately that did not appear in the review

Private notes are not visible to other guests or future hosts reviewing the same guest.
