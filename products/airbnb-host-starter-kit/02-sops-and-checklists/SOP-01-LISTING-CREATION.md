# SOP-01: Listing Creation
## 13-Step Listing SOP · New Property Launch Protocol

---

**When to use:** Creating a new Airbnb listing from scratch, or rebuilding an existing listing that is underperforming.
**Time required:** 3–4 hours for the full process. Do not rush it.
**Prerequisite:** Sections 1–7 of `03-PROPERTY-SETUP-CHECKLIST.md` complete.

---

## STEP 1 — PROFESSIONAL PHOTOS (Book 2 weeks before listing launch)

Photos determine click-through rate from search results. This step cannot be shortened.

**Photographer brief:**
- Format: landscape orientation for all interior shots (Airbnb crops portrait photos)
- Lighting: all interior lights on, natural daylight through windows, no flash shadows
- Equipment: wide-angle lens (minimum 24mm equivalent)
- Shot list (minimum 20, target 25–30):
  - Hero: main living area or bedroom (whichever photographs best)
  - Bedroom: full room from corner, bed fully made
  - Bedroom: detail of bedside setup (lamp, charger, nightstand)
  - Bathroom: full room, all items stowed
  - Kitchen: full room, surfaces clear, appliances visible
  - Kitchen: any distinctive feature (espresso machine, view out the window)
  - Living area: sofa, TV, natural light
  - Workspace (if present): desk, chair, visible power outlets
  - Outdoor space: balcony, terrace, garden
  - Building entrance (so guests can identify it)
  - View from windows (any view, including street)
  - Welcome basket or distinctive décor detail
  - 3–5 neighbourhood shots: nearest café, market, landmark, street

**After receiving photos:**
- Order them in Airbnb: hero photo first, then the sequence a guest would walk through the property
- Write a caption for each photo (Airbnb allows photo captions — 90% of hosts skip this; captions appear in enlarged view)

## STEP 2 — CHOOSE YOUR LISTING TITLE

**Formula:** [Neighbourhood] [Property Type] · [Most Distinctive Feature] · [Key Practical Amenity]

**Constraints:**
- Maximum 50 characters (Airbnb truncates in search results)
- No emojis in the title itself (they display inconsistently across devices)
- No superlatives ("amazing", "stunning", "beautiful") — Airbnb's algorithm does not weight these and guests filter them out

**Good examples:**
- `Alfama Studio · Rooftop Terrace · 10 min to Tram 28`
- `Mitte 1BR · Exposed Brick · Fibre WiFi + Workspace`
- `Eixample Flat · Private Courtyard · 5 min to Passeig de Gràcia`

**Test your title:** Show it to someone unfamiliar with the property. They should be able to identify the type of property, where it is, and one reason to choose it over a generic alternative.

---

## STEP 3 — WRITE THE LISTING DESCRIPTION

Airbnb gives you six sections. Fill every one. Incomplete sections reduce listing completeness score and leave conversion opportunities on the table.

**Section 1 — About this space (150–250 words)**

Open with the property's one most distinctive feature. Then describe the layout, the quality of the main rooms, and the guest experience.

Template structure:
- Sentence 1: The one thing that makes this property different
- Sentences 2–4: Layout (room count, size, main living area)
- Sentences 5–8: Specific amenities (name the brands if they are quality signals: "Nespresso", "Sonos", "Dyson")
- Sentences 9–12: The neighbourhood in one sentence + who this suits (couples, solo travellers, remote workers)

**Section 2 — The space (detailed room-by-room)**

One paragraph per room. Specific, factual, no adjectives without supporting facts.

*Write:* "The bedroom has a 160cm queen bed with a blackout blind that blocks light completely. There is a 32-inch TV on the wall with Netflix access. The wardrobe fits two weeks of luggage."

*Do not write:* "The cosy bedroom offers a comfortable sleeping space with ample storage."

**Section 3 — Guest access (what they can use)**

List exactly what is included and excluded. "You have full access to the entire apartment, including the outdoor terrace on the 3rd floor. The storage room in the basement is not accessible."

**Section 4 — Other things to note (genuine quirks)**

This section converts browsers into bookers if used honestly. Guests book properties that tell them what to expect.

Include:
- Entry quirks ("The front door requires a firm push after unlocking")
- Any noise sources ("The street has café noise until midnight on weekends")
- Any limitations ("The nearest supermarket is 12 minutes on foot")
- Any positives that are easy to miss ("The upstairs neighbour is quiet — she works early mornings")

**Section 5 — Getting around**

Specific transport, not generic:
- "Bus 28 stops 80m from the front door, runs every 8 minutes until midnight, €1.50 cash or NFC card. The historic centre is 4 stops, 12 minutes."
- Nearest metro/tram station + walking time
- Nearest taxi stand or ride-share availability
- Walking distance to key destinations

**Section 6 — Neighbourhood overview (3–5 sentences)**

Describe the specific street, not the city. What does a guest see when they walk out the front door? What is the atmosphere? What is nearby within 5 minutes?

## STEP 4 — SET AMENITIES ACCURATELY

Go through every amenity checkbox in the Airbnb listing interface. Apply this rule: tick only what is present AND fully functioning on the day a guest checks in.

**Common mistakes:**
- Ticking "TV" when it is not capable of streaming (only local channels)
- Ticking "parking" when it is street parking only and not guaranteed
- Ticking "AC" when the unit only has heating, not cooling
- Ticking "bathtub" when there is only a shower
- Ticking "workspace" when there is no dedicated desk with a chair

**High-value amenities to enable if applicable:**
- Self check-in (unlocks "Self check-in" badge on listing)
- Laptop-friendly workspace (adds to remote worker filter)
- Dedicated workspace (separate from this — both if applicable)
- Long-term stays allowed (adds to monthly filter)
- Crib available (family filter)
- Pet-friendly (pet filter)

---

## STEP 5 — SET HOUSE RULES

Be specific. Vague house rules are unenforceable and provide no protection in disputes.

| Rule Category | Your Specific Rule |
|---|---|
| Check-in time | After [TIME] — no early check-in without prior arrangement |
| Checkout time | By [TIME] — late checkout available for €[AMOUNT], arranged minimum 24h in advance |
| Quiet hours | 10pm to 8am. Noise complaints from neighbours result in a warning, then termination of stay without refund. |
| Smoking | No smoking anywhere inside the property or on the balcony. €150 deep-cleaning charge if evidence found. |
| Pets | [No pets / Pets on request — max 1 pet under 15kg, €[AMOUNT] non-refundable cleaning fee] |
| Parties | No parties or events. Maximum [NUMBER] guests = maximum registered guests only. |
| Additional guests | Not permitted. The booking is for the number of guests declared at reservation only. |

---

## STEP 6 — SET PRICING

Do not use Airbnb Smart Pricing. Follow `02-PRICING-STRATEGY-PLAYBOOK.md` Section 1 to set your base rate. Then:

1. Set base rate (Monday–Thursday, non-peak)
2. Set weekend pricing rules (+30% as a starting point)
3. Set cleaning fee (match your actual cost of one cleaning service — typically €55–€80)
4. Set the calendar for seasonal adjustments for the next 6 months
5. Set minimum stay (2 nights standard, 1 night for January and gap nights)

---

## STEP 7 — SET CANCELLATION POLICY

Select "Firm." This gives you:
- 50% refund to guest if they cancel more than 30 days before check-in
- No refund if they cancel within 30 days

"Flexible" gives you nothing. "Strict" deters some bookings. "Firm" is the correct balance for mid-tier European markets.

---

## STEP 8 — COMPLETE THE HOST PROFILE

Before publishing, complete your host profile:
- Profile photo: photo of you, not a logo or location photo
- Host bio: explain why you host, what you care about as a host, your response style
- Languages spoken: list all languages — it appears in search filters
- Host verification: complete ID verification if not already done

---

## STEP 9 — ENABLE INSTANT BOOK

Enable Instant Book if you have a smart lock. Add guest requirements if you want additional screening:
- Government ID required
- Positive reviews required (guests with 0 reviews must Request to Book)
- Phone number confirmed required

These filters apply automatically to all Instant Book guests and do not reduce your booking rate significantly.

---

## STEP 10 — LOAD MESSAGE TEMPLATES

Before the listing is live, add all 14 message templates to Airbnb Saved Messages:

Go to: Airbnb.com → Inbox → Saved Messages → Create New

Create each template from `04-communication-templates/`. Replace every [PERSONALISATION VARIABLE] with your actual data. Send a test message to yourself to confirm formatting.

Templates must be loaded before your first booking is confirmed.

---

## STEP 11 — PRICING TOOL SETUP

Connect PriceLabs or Wheelhouse to your Airbnb account before going live. A new listing without dynamic pricing will underprice during its first high-demand period and miss its best early revenue window.

Setup takes 60–90 minutes the first time. The tool will pull your calendar and begin suggesting prices immediately. Review the suggestions for the first 30 days manually before setting to automatic.

---

## STEP 12 — SOFT LAUNCH REVIEW

Before clicking "Publish," do a final check:
- [ ] View the listing as a potential guest (Airbnb has a Preview mode)
- [ ] Confirm your hero photo is the right image (it determines click-through rate from search)
- [ ] Read the description aloud — any sentence that sounds unnatural is a sentence to rewrite
- [ ] Confirm the check-in instructions are fully written and tested
- [ ] Confirm your response alert is active on your phone
- [ ] Confirm the cleaning team is briefed and SOP-04 reviewed with them

---

## STEP 13 — POST-LAUNCH (First 14 Days)

**Day 1–3:** Monitor for any initial inquiries. Respond within 30 minutes to maximise the new-listing ranking boost.

**Day 7:** If no bookings yet, review pricing — new listings sometimes need a temporary -10% discount for the first booking to generate the first review.

**Day 14:** Confirm your first booking has a confirmed check-in date. If 0 bookings in 14 days:
1. Check competitor pricing for the same check-in window
2. Review your primary photo (most common cause)
3. Apply a 15% first-booking discount for the next 7 days
4. Run `05-PLATFORM-OPTIMIZATION-GUIDE.md` Section 2 to diagnose
