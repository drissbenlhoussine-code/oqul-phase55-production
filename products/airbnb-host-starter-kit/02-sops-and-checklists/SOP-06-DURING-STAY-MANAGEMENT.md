# SOP-06: During-Stay Management
## Standard Operating Procedure — Active Stay Monitoring & Communication

**Run time:** 5–10 minutes per day  
**Trigger:** Day 2 of every guest stay (and ongoing for longer stays)  
**Owner:** Host  
**Frequency:** Day 2 check-in; every 3 days for stays of 5+ nights

---

## STEP 1: Day 2 Mid-Stay Check-In (Day After Arrival)

1.1 At 10:00–11:00am on Day 2 of the guest's stay, open the Airbnb booking  
1.2 Send MSG-04-MIDSTAY-CHECKIN.md (saved as "👋 Mid-Stay Check-In")  
1.3 The message is brief and invites issues to surface before they affect the review — this is its purpose  
1.4 If the guest responds positively ("All great, thanks!") → no action required; note it in guest log  
1.5 If the guest raises an issue → follow Step 2 immediately

---

## STEP 2: Issue Response Protocol

If a guest reports a problem during their stay, this is the highest-priority task until resolved.

**Response time targets:**
- Functional problem (broken appliance, leak, WiFi down): respond within 30 minutes
- Comfort issue (temperature, noise): respond within 45 minutes
- Information request ("where is the...?"): respond within 60 minutes

**Resolution approach:**
2.1 Acknowledge the issue immediately: "I'm really sorry to hear that — let me sort this for you right now."  
2.2 Diagnose: Ask one clarifying question if needed (e.g., "Is the heating not coming on at all, or is it not reaching temperature?")  
2.3 Triage using the issue table from GUEST-EXPERIENCE-STANDARDS.md Section 5  
2.4 Provide a specific resolution, not a vague one:  
   - Bad: "I'll try to get someone to look at it"  
   - Good: "My plumber is available at 2:30pm — is that a good time? I'll send you a message to confirm when he's confirmed."  
2.5 Follow up after the issue is resolved: "Just checking — is the hot water back on? All sorted?"  
2.6 Log the issue in your Maintenance Log (Tab-05) — include: date, issue description, resolution, time to resolve

---

## STEP 3: Noise Monitoring

3.1 Check your noise monitor app (Minut or NoiseAware) daily — takes 30 seconds  
3.2 Review: has any alert threshold been exceeded?  
3.3 Noise alert thresholds to monitor:  
   - Daytime (08:00–22:00): alerts above 75dB (equivalent to loud conversation)  
   - Quiet hours (22:00–08:00): alerts above 65dB  
3.4 If an alert was triggered:  
   - First: review the duration and time of the alert  
   - Short spike (< 2 minutes, daytime): likely music, laughter, door slam — no action needed  
   - Sustained elevated noise (> 5 minutes, quiet hours): send MSG-09-NOISE-COMPLAINT.md  
3.5 Note: Never mention in your message that you have a noise monitor — it's disclosed in the listing. Just reference "I've received a noise concern" if needed.  
3.6 Log any noise events in the guest record

---

## STEP 4: Longer Stay Check-Ins (5+ Night Stays)

For stays of 5 nights or longer, add mid-stay touchpoints:

**Stay of 5–7 nights:** Day 2 check-in only (standard)

**Stay of 8–14 nights:**
- Day 2: Standard mid-stay check-in
- Day 7: Brief mid-stay welfare check ("Hope the second week is going well! Is there anything you need?")

**Stay of 15–30 nights:**
- Day 2: Standard mid-stay check-in
- Day 7: Mid-stay welfare check
- Day 14: Optional restocking check-in ("Are you running low on any supplies? Happy to arrange a restock of [coffee pods, toilet paper, etc.] — just let me know what you need")
- Monthly stays: Arrange a mid-month mini-clean (3 hours) — include linen change, bathroom deep clean, kitchen reset. This prevents review issues and protects your property.

---

## STEP 5: Monitoring for Policy Violations

5.1 Review noise monitor data each morning — 30 seconds  
5.2 If you have a smart lock: review entry/exit log weekly for long stays  
5.3 Signs of potential policy violation:  
   - Multiple different entry codes used (suggests additional guests)  
   - Entry log shows unusual late-night/early-morning activity patterns  
   - Noise spikes consistent with a party  
   - Guest messaging you asking for more car park spaces or "my friends are joining"  

5.4 If a party or unauthorised guests are suspected:  
   - Do NOT assume or accuse — communicate: "Hi [Name], I wanted to check in — we've had a note about some activity at the property. Could you confirm everything is going well and in line with the booking terms? Happy to chat if anything has come up."  
   - If confirmed violation: contact Airbnb and activate your insurance documentation  
   - If no response within 30 minutes to a clear concern: escalate to Airbnb's trust & safety team

---

## STEP 6: Proactive Service Touches (Optional but High Impact)

For stays of 3+ nights, consider one unexpected service touch:

**Option A: Local tip** (lowest effort, high impact)
Send a short message on Day 3: "If you haven't been to [specific local place] yet — it's 5 minutes walk and genuinely worth it. [One specific thing about it]. Hope you're having a brilliant time."

**Option B: Restocking offer** (for stays of 7+ nights)
"I'd be happy to drop by a few essentials mid-stay — coffee pods, fresh milk, anything else running low. Just let me know and I can leave them at the door."

**Option C: Early check-out offer** (if you have a gap after this booking)
"In case you have an early start, there's no pressure to be out by 11am — 11:30 would be absolutely fine. Enjoy your last morning."

These gestures cost very little and are cited frequently in 5-star reviews by name.
