# SOP-06: During-Stay Management
## Active Stay Monitoring · Mid-Stay Check-In · Noise Protocol · Issue Response

---

**When to use:** From the moment a guest checks in to the moment they check out.
**Tools used:** Airbnb messaging, Minut or NoiseAware noise monitor, smart lock activity log.
**Goal:** Catch and resolve problems while the guest is still in the property — a problem fixed during the stay becomes a footnote; a problem left until checkout becomes a review.

---

## SECTION 1 — CHECK-IN CONFIRMATION (Within 2 Hours of Check-In Time)

Within 2 hours of the standard check-in time, confirm the guest has arrived:

**Method 1 — Noise monitor:** Check your Minut or NoiseAware app. If motion has been detected inside the property, the guest has arrived.

**Method 2 — Smart lock log:** Check your smart lock app for entry activity.

**If no activity 2 hours after check-in time:**
Send: "Hi [GUEST NAME], just checking in — did you arrive okay? Everything should be ready, but please let me know if anything needs attention."

In most cases, the guest has arrived but the monitoring tools are showing a delay. A brief check-in message resolves any lingering access issue before it becomes an evening emergency.

---

## SECTION 2 — DAY 2 MID-STAY MESSAGE

Send MSG-04 (Mid-Stay Check-In) on the second full day of every stay. For 1-night stays, send it at check-in + 3 hours (not the next morning — they will be checking out).

**Why Day 2:** This is when minor issues surface. A guest who found the shower lukewarm on arrival will mention it if asked on Day 2. If they leave without mentioning it, they mention it in the review.

**What the message does:**
1. Gives the guest permission to raise a problem without feeling like they are complaining
2. Demonstrates active hosting, which directly affects Communication subcategory scores
3. Gives you 24–48 hours to resolve anything before checkout

**Response protocol after the mid-stay message:**
- "Everything is great!": Reply with one warm sentence. No further action.
- Guest raises a specific issue: Execute MSG-08 (Issue Response) protocol and activate SOP-08 (Maintenance Response) if needed.
- No response: No action required. Guest is fine.

---

## SECTION 3 — NOISE MONITORING PROTOCOL

A Minut or NoiseAware sensor monitors decibel levels without recording audio. It is legal in most European jurisdictions when disclosed in your Airbnb listing (disclose it — required by Airbnb policy and in most jurisdictions legally).

**Disclosure:** Add to your Airbnb listing under "Other things to note": "This property uses a Minut noise monitor to protect neighbours. It measures decibel levels only — no audio is recorded."

**Noise alert thresholds (set these in the Minut app):**

| Time | Threshold | Escalation |
|---|---|---|
| 8am–10pm | Notify at 75dB sustained for 5 minutes | Send MSG-09 Tier 1 |
| 10pm–midnight | Notify at 65dB sustained for 3 minutes | Send MSG-09 Tier 1 |
| Midnight–8am | Notify at 55dB sustained for 2 minutes | Send MSG-09 Tier 2 |

**Three-tier noise warning system (see MSG-09 for full message text):**

**Tier 1 — First occurrence, reasonable hours:**
"Hi [GUEST NAME], I've been alerted to higher-than-usual sound levels at the property. This is likely nothing — but just a reminder that we have quiet hours from 10pm to protect the neighbours. Enjoy your stay."

**Tier 2 — Second occurrence or during quiet hours:**
"Hi [GUEST NAME], I've received a second noise alert tonight. Under our house rules, I need to ask that noise levels be reduced immediately. If I receive a third alert tonight, I will need to ask you to vacate the property."

**Tier 3 — Third occurrence (enforcement trigger):**
"Hi [GUEST NAME], I've now received multiple noise alerts. Per our house rules and Airbnb's policies, I am initiating an early termination of this booking. Please be prepared to vacate the property by [TIME, minimum 30 minutes from now]. A partial refund for unused nights will be processed. If you need assistance finding alternative accommodation, I can help."

**Before sending Tier 3:** Call Airbnb Support (+353 1 800 812 816 in Ireland, +34 91 123 45 67 in Spain, or the number for your country) to document the escalation. Airbnb Support will advise on the eviction protocol in your jurisdiction. Do not physically confront guests.

## SECTION 4 — DURING-STAY ISSUE TRIAGE

When a guest reports an issue during their stay, respond within 30 minutes. Use this triage framework:

| Issue | Severity | Response Time | Resolution Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| WiFi not working | High | 30 min | 2 hours |
| No hot water | High | 30 min | 4 hours or partial refund |
| Heating failure in winter | Critical | 15 min | 2 hours (space heater as bridge) |
| Air conditioning failure in summer | High | 30 min | 4 hours (fan as bridge) |
| Broken appliance (oven, microwave) | Medium | 60 min | 24 hours |
| Noise from construction or neighbours | Medium | 60 min | Within 24 hours or offer to relocate |
| Cleanliness issue on arrival | High | 30 min | Re-clean within 2 hours |
| Insect infestation | Critical | 15 min | Same day — this is a deal-ending issue |
| Property not matching listing | High | 30 min | Resolve or process refund within 4 hours |
| Guest locked out | Critical | 5 min | Call immediately, resolve within 15 min |

**For Critical and High severity issues:** Call the guest, do not only message. Text is too slow.

**Contractor list (maintain this in your phone):**
- Plumber: [NAME] [PHONE]
- Electrician: [NAME] [PHONE]
- Locksmith (for physical lock issues): [NAME] [PHONE]
- Appliance repair: [NAME] [PHONE]
- Emergency property management cover: [NAME] [PHONE]

## SECTION 5 — EXTENDED STAY PROTOCOL (7+ Nights)

Long stays (7+ nights) require additional contact points and a different dynamic:

**Day 3 or 4 of long stay:** Brief check-in message (MSG-04 variant). Ask if they need anything replenished.

**Day 7 of long stay:** Offer a mid-stay linen service. "Would you like fresh towels and bed linen for the second week? I can arrange for the cleaning team to drop by at a time that works for you." (Charge €18–€25 for this service or include it as a gesture for stays above 14 nights.)

**Day 14+ stays:** Confirm utilities are not being abused (extremely high electricity or water bills in Europe can create disputes). Not every host monitors this, but be aware.

**Long-stay guest experience priority:** Long-stay guests are worth significantly more per booking and tend to leave more detailed reviews. Invest slightly more time in their experience.

---

## SECTION 6 — EARLY CHECKOUT MANAGEMENT

If a guest requests early checkout (leaving before their scheduled date):

1. Ask why. A guest who wants to leave early because of a problem with the property is different from a guest who got an opportunity to leave early for personal reasons.
2. If property-related: investigate the issue. If valid, offer a refund for unused nights proactively.
3. If personal: process the departure normally. Under a Firm cancellation policy, unused nights are not refunded if the guest chooses to leave early. Confirm this politely: "That's no problem at all — just to let you know, under our booking policy, I'm not able to refund the unused nights, but I'll make sure everything is sorted on our end for your departure."
4. After early checkout: enter the property and assess condition. Begin turnover process.
