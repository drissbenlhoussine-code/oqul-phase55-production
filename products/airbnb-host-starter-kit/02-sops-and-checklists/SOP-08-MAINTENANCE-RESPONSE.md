# SOP-08: Maintenance Response
## Issue Triage Runbook · Contractor Templates · Preventive Schedule

---

**When to use:** Any maintenance issue reported by a guest, identified during a turnover, or found during a routine inspection.
**Response time target:** Acknowledgement within 30 minutes. Repair within 24 hours for High severity, same day for Critical.

---

## SECTION 1 — MAINTENANCE SEVERITY MATRIX

| Severity | Definition | Response Time | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| Critical | Renders property uninhabitable or creates immediate safety risk | 15 minutes to phone call; 2 hours to on-site resolution | No hot water in winter, burst pipe, gas leak, total heating failure, security breach, insect infestation |
| High | Significantly impairs guest experience; cannot wait until checkout | 30 minutes to acknowledgement; 4–8 hours to resolution | WiFi down, cold shower, oven not working, lock malfunction, large insect (not infestation) |
| Medium | Reduces guest comfort but property is usable | 60 minutes to acknowledgement; 24 hours to resolution | TV remote missing, microwave not working, one hob ring out, bathroom extractor noisy |
| Low | Cosmetic or minor; can be resolved at next turnover | Next turnover | Scuff on wall, loose shelf, light bulb out in secondary location, minor dripping tap |

---

## SECTION 2 — CRITICAL ISSUE PROTOCOLS

### No Hot Water

1. Ask guest: "Is the boiler switched on? The switch is at [location]. Can you check if any pilot lights are visible?"
2. If boiler is on and no hot water: call your plumber immediately.
3. While waiting for plumber: message guest with realistic ETA.
4. If resolution will take more than 4 hours: offer partial refund (15% per day without hot water) or offer to assist with rebooking.
5. After resolution: confirm with guest that hot water is working.

**Partial refund script:**
> "I'm really sorry for this — I've arranged for a plumber to arrive by [TIME]. In the meantime, I've applied a 15% partial refund for today to your booking as an apology for the inconvenience. I'll keep you updated."

### Gas Leak (Smell of Gas)

1. Tell the guest immediately by phone: "Leave the apartment now. Do not turn any switches on or off. Do not use your phone inside the apartment. Get outside and call the gas emergency number."
2. Gas emergency numbers: Portugal: 800 112 712 · Spain: 900 750 750 · France: 0800 47 33 33 · UK: 0800 111 999 · Germany: local network provider number
3. Call your plumber/gas engineer immediately after the guest is safe.
4. Do not allow the guest back in until the property is confirmed safe by a professional.
5. Issue a full refund for any affected nights.

### Locked Out (Smart Lock Failure at Entry)

1. Call the guest immediately. Do not message — they are standing at the door.
2. Guide them to the lockbox: specific location and code.
3. If lockbox also fails: you need to be on-site or send someone with a physical key within 30 minutes. This is the only scenario that requires physical host presence.
4. If you cannot resolve in 30 minutes: offer to assist with finding nearby alternative accommodation. Airbnb's AirCover includes guest rebooking assistance in genuine lock-out scenarios.

### Pest Infestation

This is a deal-ending issue. Do not attempt to manage this during a stay.

1. Call the guest immediately and acknowledge the problem.
2. Offer to arrange alternative accommodation through Airbnb's rebooking assistance.
3. Process a full refund for all remaining nights.
4. Do not attempt to treat the infestation while guests are present.
5. After checkout: professional pest control company, 24-hour treatment, do not rebook for minimum 72 hours.

## SECTION 3 — CONTRACTOR OUTREACH TEMPLATE

Use this when contacting a contractor for the first time about a guest-stay issue. The template communicates urgency without sounding chaotic.

**Email/WhatsApp template:**

> Subject: Urgent maintenance — STR property, guest in residence
>
> Hi [NAME],
>
> I need [TYPE OF WORK] at [PROPERTY ADDRESS]. This is a short-term rental property with a guest currently in residence. The issue is: [SPECIFIC DESCRIPTION OF PROBLEM — e.g., "The combination boiler is not producing hot water. The pilot light is on, pressure gauge reads 1.2 bar, no error codes displayed on the unit."]
>
> I need someone on-site by [TIME/DATE]. Can you confirm availability?
>
> If you cannot attend today, please advise whether you can recommend someone who can.
>
> Thank you,
> [YOUR NAME]
> [YOUR PHONE]

## SECTION 4 — PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE SCHEDULE

Running preventive maintenance reduces critical maintenance events by 60–70%. Schedule these in your task system annually.

### Every Turnover (Cleaning Team Checks)

- Smart lock battery level (check app)
- Light bulbs in all rooms
- Shower pressure and hot water test
- Fridge and freezer temperature
- Any appliance that was reported as unusual by previous guest

### Monthly

- [ ] Boiler pressure check (reading should be between 1.0 and 1.5 bar — top up if below 1.0)
- [ ] Smoke and CO detector test (press the test button)
- [ ] WiFi speed test at the property (fast.com — should be ≥100 Mbps)
- [ ] Extractor fan operation (kitchen and bathroom)
- [ ] Drains (run hot water and check flow rate — clear hair from shower drain)

### Quarterly

- [ ] Fire extinguisher check (pressure gauge in green zone)
- [ ] Smart lock firmware update (via app)
- [ ] Hob ring check (all burners ignite fully)
- [ ] Washing machine and dishwasher (if applicable): run a hot empty cycle with cleaning tablet
- [ ] Check for signs of damp or mould in bathroom grout, around windows, and on exterior walls
- [ ] Boiler service scheduled? (Annual boiler service is required in most countries for rental properties)

### Annually

- [ ] Boiler annual service by a certified engineer
- [ ] Electrical inspection (not always legally required, but advisable every 3 years for rental properties)
- [ ] Deep clean: behind appliances, under furniture, inside oven, inside washing machine
- [ ] Replace any mattress protectors that have yellowed or lost waterproofing
- [ ] Replace any towels or linens that are worn, pilled, or stained beyond laundry restoration
