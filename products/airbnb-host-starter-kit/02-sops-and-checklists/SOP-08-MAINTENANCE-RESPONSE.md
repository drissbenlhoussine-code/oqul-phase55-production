# SOP-08: Maintenance Response
## Standard Operating Procedure — Issue Triage, Repair & Prevention

**Run time:** 5 minutes triage; repair time varies by issue  
**Trigger:** Maintenance issue reported by guest OR discovered during inspection  
**Owner:** Host  
**Prerequisite:** Contractor contact list populated (see Section 3)

---

## STEP 1: Triage (within 15–30 minutes of issue discovery)

1.1 Classify the issue by urgency:

| Priority | Definition | Examples | Target Response |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 — Emergency | Safety risk or property uninhabitable | Gas leak, flooding, no heating in winter, electrical fault | 15 minutes response; same-day resolution |
| P2 — Urgent | Significantly impacts guest comfort | No hot water, broken lock, key appliance failure | 30 minutes response; same-day resolution |
| P3 — Standard | Noticeable but manageable | Slow drain, washing machine fault, minor leak | 60 minutes response; within 24 hours |
| P4 — Cosmetic | No comfort impact | Light bulb out, dripping tap, scuff on wall | End of stay; next turnover |

1.2 Assess: Can you fix this yourself in under 30 minutes? If yes, go. If no, call a contractor.  
1.3 Do NOT tell a guest "I'll try to get someone to look at it" — give them a specific time and person.

---

## STEP 2: Immediate Guest Communication

2.1 Message the guest within your response target time  
2.2 Use this structure:
```
Hi [Name] — I've got your message and I'm on this right now.

[WHAT]: The [issue] is something I can fix.
[WHEN]: My [plumber/electrician/I] will be there at [specific time].
[WHAT NEXT]: I'll message you 30 minutes before they arrive to confirm.

Really sorry for the inconvenience — I'll make sure this is sorted properly. [Name]
```
2.3 Never promise a resolution you can't guarantee — only commit to a time when someone will be there  
2.4 Never leave a guest without a confirmed next action

---

## STEP 3: Contractor Dispatch

3.1 Call your relevant contractor from your pre-qualified list (see Section 4)  
3.2 Confirm they can attend within your target resolution time  
3.3 Give them the property address, access code (share temporarily if needed), and a clear description of the problem  
3.4 If your primary contractor is unavailable: call backup contractor immediately — do not wait  
3.5 Notify guest of the confirmed time once contractor confirms

---

## STEP 4: During the Repair

4.1 If guest is present: ask their permission before sending a contractor in  
4.2 Always give the contractor a specific time window (e.g., "The guest will be home between 2pm and 4pm")  
4.3 Never send a contractor without guest knowledge or permission  
4.4 If the repair will take more than 60 minutes: offer the guest something (coffee voucher, late checkout, partial refund for the inconvenience)  
4.5 For P1/P2 issues: remain contactable throughout — check in every 30 minutes

---

## STEP 5: Post-Repair Confirmation

5.1 Message the guest once you've confirmed the repair is complete:
```
Hi [Name] — just checking in. Has [the issue] been fully resolved? 
I want to make sure you're comfortable for the rest of your stay. 
Really sorry again for the disruption. [Name]
```
5.2 If not fully resolved: escalate until it is. Do not close the issue until the guest confirms it's sorted.  
5.3 Log the issue in your Maintenance Log (Tab-05): date, description, contractor, cost, resolution time

---

## STEP 6: Post-Stay Follow-Up

6.1 After the guest checks out: inspect the repair — confirm it's permanent, not a temporary fix  
6.2 Update your maintenance log with final resolution status  
6.3 Schedule any follow-up work (e.g., "plumber said the boiler is old — get quote for replacement")  
6.4 Assess whether the issue should trigger a P4 maintenance audit of similar items in the property

---

## COMPENSATION FRAMEWORK

When a maintenance issue significantly impacts a guest's stay, offer compensation proactively (do not wait for the guest to ask). Guests who receive a proactive apology and gesture are significantly less likely to leave a negative review.

| Issue | Duration Affecting Stay | Appropriate Gesture |
|---|---|---|
| No hot water | < 4 hours | Sincere apology; note in review response |
| No hot water | > 4 hours | 20% nightly rate refund |
| Heating failure (winter) | < 4 hours | Blankets provided + sincere apology |
| Heating failure (winter) | > 4 hours | 25% nightly rate refund |
| WiFi failure | < 2 hours | Apology |
| WiFi failure | > 2 hours | €10 mobile data contribution / one night refund |
| Key appliance failure (oven, washing machine) | Entire stay | 15% total booking refund |
| Noise disruption (neighbours, construction) | Recurring throughout stay | 20–30% refund |
| Property not clean on arrival | — | Send cleaner back same day; 10–15% refund |

**How to process a refund:**
- Airbnb → Reservations → [Booking] → Send or Request Money → Send [amount] → Select "Refund"
- Always do this within 24 hours of agreeing to it
- Document the refund reason in your maintenance log and financial records

## SECTION 3: ISSUE-SPECIFIC QUICK FIXES (Runbook)

**WiFi Not Working**
1. Ask guest to forget the network and reconnect
2. If that fails: ask guest to power off their device and reconnect
3. If that fails: ask them to restart the router (instructions in guest guide: unplug, wait 30s, replug)
4. If that fails: log into your router admin panel remotely to diagnose
5. Last resort: provide mobile hotspot instructions or give them a temporary hotspot device

**Heating Not Working**
1. Check if thermostat is set correctly (most guests don't know how to use them)
2. Instructions for your exact thermostat model should be posted near it
3. Check if circuit breaker for heating has tripped
4. If boiler: check pressure gauge (should be 1.0–1.5 bar); instructions in guest guide
5. If none of the above: call emergency plumber immediately

**Smart Lock Not Responding**
1. Check smart lock app — is the device online?
2. If offline: lock has likely lost WiFi — restart your router remotely
3. Ask guest to try again with the code
4. If still failing: give physical lockbox code immediately; do not leave guest waiting
5. After guest is inside: contact smart lock manufacturer support; do not wait for them to call you

**Drains Blocked**
1. Provide guest with a plunger location (should be in utility cupboard)
2. For bathroom: hair typically the cause; provide drain unblocking instructions
3. For kitchen: food debris; provide drain cleaner or a plunger
4. If above ground level (drain outside): call plumber

**Hot Water Not Working (Combi Boiler)**
1. Check boiler display for error code — send photo of display to plumber
2. Common fix: boiler has lost pressure — instructions posted near boiler
3. Common fix: pilot light out — instructions in manual near boiler
4. If unable to fix remotely within 30 minutes: P1 — call emergency plumber
