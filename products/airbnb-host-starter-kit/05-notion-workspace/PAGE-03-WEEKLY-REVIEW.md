## NOTES ON USING THIS TEMPLATE

**Time:** 20 minutes maximum. If it takes longer, you are reviewing too much. The goal is not a comprehensive report — it is 5 specific checks that keep operations on track.

**Archive:** After each week, move the completed review page to a "Weekly Reviews" sub-page. At quarter-end, these reviews give you a narrative of what happened operationally. Useful for identifying improvement patterns and for your SOP-10 monthly review.

**Minimum viable version:** If a week is too busy for a full review, do just Section 2 (upcoming activity). At least confirm the cleaning schedule and smart lock codes are ready for the week's check-ins.
