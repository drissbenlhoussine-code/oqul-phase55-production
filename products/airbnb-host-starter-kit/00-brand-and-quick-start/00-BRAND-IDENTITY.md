# Brand Identity

## PRODUCT TAGLINE

**Run your property like a professional. From the first booking.**

---

## BRAND VOICE

**Three adjectives:** Direct. Specific. Earned.

**What that means in practice:**

| Principle | What it sounds like | What it does NOT sound like |
|---|---|---|
| Direct | "Check-out is 11am. Here is why that matters and how to enforce it." | "We suggest considering your checkout time based on your preferences." |
| Specific | "Send the review request 2 hours after checkout — Airbnb data shows this window has the highest response rate." | "Follow up with guests at a good time." |
| Earned | "This works. We know because it's the same system hosts use to reach 4.9+ at 75% occupancy." | "This revolutionary system will transform your hosting journey!" |

**Voice not to use:**
- Cheerleading ("You've got this!")
- Hedging ("You may want to consider...")
- Tech-product hollowness ("Seamless. Powerful. Game-changing.")
- Coaching-speak ("Unlock your potential as a host")

**Voice to use:**
- Senior property manager briefing a capable new hire
- Clear instruction followed by the reason it matters
- Numbers over adjectives: "4.87 average" not "great rating"

## POSITIONING

**Market position:** The only Airbnb host kit built backwards from the Superhost algorithm — every SOP, template, and checklist maps directly to a specific Airbnb review subcategory outcome.

**Competitive differentiation:** Competing products sell collections of documents. This is an operating system. The difference: you can run a property on this kit without making a single decision about what to do next. The next step is always in the next document.

**Price justification at €149:**
A single booking saved from a 3-star review — which suppresses Airbnb ranking for 30+ days — is worth €200–€400 in lost revenue. This kit prevents that outcome on every booking. It pays for itself in the first month.

**Customer transformation:**
- Before: Reactive host. Rewriting messages from scratch. Forgetting steps. Getting 4-star reviews and not knowing why.
- After: Proactive professional. Every booking runs on a system. Reviews trend above 4.85. Superhost within 6–12 months.

## NAMING CONVENTIONS

**Folders:** Numbered prefix + descriptive name, all lowercase with hyphens.
`00-brand-and-quick-start` `01-core-operating-documents`

**Files:** Prefix code + descriptor in ALL-CAPS with hyphens.
`SOP-04-TURNOVER-CLEANING.md` `MSG-07-REVIEW-REQUEST.md` `TAB-01-REVENUE-TRACKER.md`

**Personalisation variables in templates:** [ALL-CAPS in square brackets]
`[PROPERTY NAME]` `[GUEST NAME]` `[ACCESS CODE]`

**Do not use:** CamelCase, spaces in filenames, generic names like "template1.md"

## TARGET BUYER PROFILE

**Emotional state at purchase:** Overwhelmed. They have 2–3 bookings coming and no system. Or they just got a 4-star review and don't know why.

**What they want to stop doing:** Writing the same check-in message for the seventh time. Wondering if they forgot something. Reacting to problems at 11pm.

**What they want to achieve:** Superhost status. Reviews above 4.9. A hosting operation that runs without them spending 3 hours per booking managing it.

**What makes them feel professional the moment they open this:** A pre-filled, numbered, specific system that assumes they are serious. No inspirational quotes. No "beginner's tips." Just the operating procedures a 200-stay Superhost uses, formatted to hand off to anyone.
