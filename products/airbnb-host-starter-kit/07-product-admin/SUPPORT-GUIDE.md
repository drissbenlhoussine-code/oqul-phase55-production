## Q1: "Where do I start? There are 56 files and I don't know which to open first."

**Start with `00-quick-start/QUICK-START-GUIDE.md`. Read nothing else first.**

The Quick Start Guide is a single page with five numbered steps. It takes 10 minutes. It routes you to the right documents based on your situation. Everything else in the kit is designed to be accessed in context — when you need it — not read cover to cover.

The routing table at the bottom of the Quick Start Guide tells you exactly what to open first based on your specific situation (new listing, existing listing, upcoming guest, pricing review, etc.).

## Q2: "Do I need Notion? What if I don't use it?"

**No. Notion is optional and recommended, but not required.**

Every document in this kit is a Markdown file. You can open it in any text editor, copy and paste it into Word, Google Docs, or any notes app. The content works independently of Notion.

The Notion workspace section (Section 05) gives you a centralised operational hub that's significantly more powerful than a folder of documents — but if you're comfortable using Google Sheets for tracking and a notes app for your SOPs, skip Section 05 entirely and use the rest of the kit without it.

If you want to use Notion and have never built a database before, the `NOTION-WORKSPACE-OVERVIEW.md` file walks you through it from zero. It takes 45–90 minutes to build everything from scratch, and you only do it once.

## Q3: "The Pricing Strategy Playbook has formulas. Do I have to use them?"

**No — but they'll save you money.**

The formulas in the Pricing Playbook are straightforward arithmetic. You don't need a spreadsheet. You can do the Base Rate calculation on your phone calculator in about 2 minutes.

The reason they matter: most hosts price by feel or by copying the host next door. Pricing by feel leaves money on the table when demand is high and kills occupancy when demand is low. The formulas give you a floor (below which you lose money) and a framework for adjusting above that floor based on season and demand.

If you're overwhelmed: start with just the Base Rate calculation (Section 1, Layer 1) to establish your minimum price floor, then set your weekday and weekend rates by hand. That alone puts you ahead of 70% of hosts in most markets.

## Q5: "The SOP for turnover cleaning is very detailed. My cleaner won't read all of it."

**You're right. Don't give your cleaner a 12-page document.**

Here's how to use SOP-04 with a cleaning team:

1. Read SOP-04 yourself — it defines the standard you want your cleaner to achieve
2. Extract the key inspection points for each room (about 5 per room)
3. Create a 1-page physical checklist (or laminated A4 sheet) for your cleaner with just those key points
4. The full SOP is your reference document, not their working document

Alternatively: walk through the property once with your cleaner using the SOP as a guide. A 45-minute in-person walkthrough is worth more than 5 emails. Once they understand the standard, give them the condensed checklist.

The room-by-room photo documentation step (Step B1 and Step 9) is the one non-negotiable — ask your cleaner to always send before-and-after photos. This protects you, them, and gives you a daily record of property condition.

## Q6: "I'm just starting out and haven't got my first booking yet. Which documents matter most right now?"

**Focus on these five in this order:**

1. **PROPERTY-SETUP-MASTER-CHECKLIST.md** — get your property to live-listing standard. Don't launch before completing Phase 1–4 of this checklist.

2. **SOP-01-LISTING-CREATION.md** — follow this step by step to create your Airbnb listing properly the first time. Getting the listing right at launch matters more than at any other time because Airbnb gives new listings a 2-week visibility boost.

3. **PRICING-STRATEGY-PLAYBOOK.md** — set your prices correctly before you go live. Specifically, complete the Base Rate calculation and the Competitor Analysis table. Then price 10% below the market average for your first 8 bookings to accelerate your first reviews.

4. **MSG-01 through MSG-07** — load these 7 messages into Airbnb Saved Messages immediately. These are the messages you'll use for every booking in your first 90 days.

5. **GUEST-EXPERIENCE-STANDARDS.md** — read Section 1 (the 5 review categories decoded). Understanding what guests are actually rating gives you a framework for every decision you make about the property.

The financial model, Notion workspace, and monthly review SOP can wait until you have your first 3 bookings. Nail the first-impression documents first.

If a specific Airbnb platform step is unclear, contact the seller via the purchase platform and we'll clarify. If it's a platform change, we'll update the document.

## Q8: "I already have an Airbnb listing. Is there anything in this kit that applies to me right now?"

**Yes — there are three things that will have an immediate impact:**

1. **Communication templates (Section 04):** Load these into Airbnb Saved Messages today. Even if your listing is already performing well, having all 14 templates ready means you'll never write a message from scratch again, and your response consistency will improve immediately.

2. **Pricing Strategy Playbook — Section 2 (Competitive Benchmarking):** Run this analysis now against your current market. Most established hosts are either overpriced relative to their review count or underpriced relative to their Superhost status. The benchmarking table takes 20 minutes and often identifies an immediate 5–15% pricing adjustment opportunity.

3. **SOP-10 Monthly Host Review:** Run this SOP at the start of next month. Most hosts who've been operating without a structured monthly review discover 2–3 things they've been missing: a pricing opportunity, a recurring maintenance issue, or a messaging gap. The SOP makes this review take 60 minutes instead of half a day.

## Q9: "The Local Area Guide template is mostly [BRACKETED] fields. How much do I need to fill in?"

**Fill in everything — but it takes less time than you think.**

The template structure exists to prompt you through every category. Here's the efficient approach:

1. For each category (coffee, lunch, dinner, things to do): pick your 1–2 actual favourites from memory. Don't overthink it.
2. For addresses: pull them from Google Maps and paste in the links. 10 minutes.
3. For the "Personal Tips" section: write 5 things you've learned about your city/neighbourhood that aren't on TripAdvisor. These are the most-read and most-appreciated part of the guide.
4. For the "host personal note": write it in 5 minutes as if you're texting a friend. Don't edit it — imperfect and personal beats perfect and generic.

A completed Local Area Guide takes about 90 minutes to fill in from scratch. It is cited in guest reviews more than any other single thing in the guest portal. The welcome basket and the local guide together are the two most-mentioned "above and beyond" items in 5-star reviews.

## Q10: "Can I add my own branding to these documents when sharing them with guests?"

**Yes — in fact, you should.**

The guest-facing documents (Guest Welcome Guide, House Rules, Local Area Guide, Emergency Contacts) are designed to be personalised and branded as your property documents, not credited to this kit. Add your property name, your logo or a photo, your host name, and your property's visual style.

The host-facing documents (SOPs, business plan, pricing playbook, tracking systems) can also be branded if you're building a hosting business with a team — rename them as "[Your Company] Hosting SOPs" if that serves you better.

What you may not do: rebrand the entire kit and sell it as your own product. The license covers personal use and team sharing, not commercial redistribution.
