### SCOPE OF v1.0.0

This version is designed for:
- Individual hosts managing 1–3 properties
- Entire-property listings (whole apartment, house, or villa)
- Primarily Airbnb-listed properties
- Mid-term rentals (2+ nights) — not optimised for single-night budget hosting

---

### KNOWN LIMITATIONS IN v1.0.0

1. **Multi-platform management:** This kit is designed for Airbnb as the primary platform. Channel management across Booking.com, VRBO, and direct booking websites is referenced but not covered in detail. A multi-platform addendum is planned for v1.1.

2. **Property management company use:** The Kit is built for owner-operators. Property management companies running portfolios for multiple clients will find the Notion workspace needs adaptation. A PM-specific workspace template is planned for v2.0.

3. **Private room listings:** The kit is primarily designed for entire-property listings. Hosts offering private rooms in their own home will find most content applicable but some sections (e.g., guest access, house rules) will need significant customisation.

4. **Jurisdiction-specific legal content:** Tax, licensing, and insurance sections use examples and frameworks. These must be verified against your specific local jurisdiction — we do not provide legal or tax advice.

5. **Pricing tool integrations:** The Pricing Playbook references PriceLabs, Wheelhouse, and Beyond. Integration specifics are current as of the release date. These platforms update their interfaces regularly — steps may vary.

6. **Local Area Guide:** The template structure is complete and production-ready, but the content must be populated by the host with their specific local knowledge. This is intentional — a generic city guide is not useful.
