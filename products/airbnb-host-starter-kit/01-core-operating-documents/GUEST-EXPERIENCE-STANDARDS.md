# Guest Experience Standards
## The 5-Star Service Framework for Airbnb Hosts

---

## PURPOSE

This document defines what "5 stars" actually means in operational terms. Every standard here maps to one or more Airbnb review categories: Cleanliness, Accuracy, Check-in, Communication, Location, and Value. Follow these standards consistently and 5-star reviews become a predictable outcome, not a lucky one.

---

## SECTION 1 — THE FIVE REVIEW CATEGORIES DECODED

### 1.1 Cleanliness (most-cited reason for 4-star reviews)

Guests rate cleanliness against hotels, not against your home. The standard is:

**5-star cleanliness means:**
- No visible dust on any surface, including tops of wardrobes, door frames, skirting boards
- Bathroom grout clean and free of mould/discolouration
- Kitchen appliances clean inside (microwave, oven, fridge) not just wiped
- No hair anywhere (floor, plughole, soap dish, toilet area)
- Floors mopped, not just swept
- Bed linen crisp, unironed-looking linen gets 4 stars
- Smell: neutral. Any scent (perfume, cleaning products, previous guests) → 4 stars

**Your standard:** If a guest could photograph any surface for TripAdvisor, it is 5-star clean.

### 1.2 Accuracy (listing photos and description must match reality)

**5-star accuracy means:**
- Every photo is current (within last 12 months if property has changed)
- Listing says "mountain view" → guest can see mountains from the listed room
- Bedroom count is accurate (a sofa bed does not count as a bedroom)
- WiFi speed claimed matches actual — if you state "fast WiFi," test it: minimum 50Mbps
- All amenities in the listing are present and working on arrival

**Your standard:** A guest who reads your listing and arrives should feel nothing was oversold. Ideally, the property exceeds expectations on at least one dimension.

### 1.3 Check-in (first impression sets the entire review tone)

**5-star check-in means:**
- Check-in instructions arrive 24–48 hours before arrival, not on the morning
- Guest does not need to call or message to find the entrance
- Smart lock or key access works on first attempt
- Property is at the correct temperature (not stone cold in January, not sweltering in August)
- Welcome message or card is visible immediately on entry
- WiFi password is immediately visible (not buried in the guest book)

**Your standard:** Guest is inside, WiFi connected, and oriented within 8 minutes of arriving.

### 1.4 Communication (response speed is non-negotiable for Superhost)

**5-star communication means:**
- All messages answered within 1 hour (Airbnb measures your response rate precisely)
- Messages are specific and helpful — "I don't know" is not an answer
- Guest is never left wondering what happens next
- Proactive outreach: guests receive messages before they need to ask questions
- Tone is warm and professional — not casual, not corporate

**Your standard:** Guest should feel they have a knowledgeable, available host without being overwhelmed by messages.

### 1.5 Value (perception, not just price)

**5-star value means:**
- Amenities match or exceed what a hotel at the same price point offers
- Property includes thoughtful extras that justify the nightly rate
- Cleaning fee is proportional to stay length (a €65 fee on a 1-night stay feels extortionate)
- Upsell items (early check-in, late check-out) are priced sensibly

**Your standard:** Guest should think "I'd book this over a hotel at the same price."

## SECTION 2 — PHYSICAL EXPERIENCE STANDARDS

### 2.1 The First 30 Seconds Standard
A guest walks in. In the first 30 seconds they form a lasting impression. Engineer it.

| Sense | Standard | Specific Action |
|---|---|---|
| Sight | Uncluttered, light, inviting | Clear all surfaces except décor items |
| Smell | Neutral (no cleaning products) | Air property 30 min before; use minimal scent |
| Touch | Door handle/light switch is clean | Wipe all touch surfaces after cleaning |
| Temperature | 20°C in winter, 22°C in summer | Pre-set thermostat 2 hours before check-in |
| Sound | Quiet | Ensure no appliance hum, beeping, or drip |

### 2.2 The Bed Standard
Sleep quality is the most-cited factor in 5-star reviews after cleanliness.

- Mattress: Hotel-quality, no visible sag or worn areas
- Pillows: Minimum 2 per person, plump (replace annually)
- Duvet: 10.5 tog standard; summer guests will kick it off, which is fine
- Linen: White, crisp, ironed or smooth. No bobbling, no stains, no thin patches
- Hospital corners: Required on the foot end; top sheet folded back 15cm over duvet
- Decorative pillows: Arranged symmetrically, removed at turndown

### 2.3 The Bathroom Standard
**Pre-arrival check:**
- Mirror: streak-free
- Toilet: inside of bowl checked (guests look)
- Shower: glass screen/curtain clean — no limescale on glass
- Floor: mopped, no hair
- Towels: folded hotel-style (tri-fold or rolled), not draped over rail
- Toiletries: full bottles or new hotel-size; not half-used
- Toilet paper: new roll on holder + 2 spares visible

### 2.4 The Kitchen Standard
- Hob: no previous food residue
- Microwave: interior clean (wipe with damp cloth after every stay)
- Fridge: empty, clean, door seals clean, no smell
- Sink: clean, plughole clear
- All dishes clean and put away (none air-drying on rack)
- Bin: empty with fresh bag fitted

## SECTION 3 — WELCOME EXTRAS (The 5-to-4.9 Difference)

These items cost under €12 per stay and are cited disproportionately in 5-star reviews.

### Welcome Basket (cost: €8–€12)
- 1 small bottle of local wine or quality soft drink
- 2–4 locally-sourced chocolates or biscuits
- 1 handwritten welcome card (see template below)
- 1 small water bottle per guest or a glass jug filled

### Welcome Card Template
```
Welcome to [Property Name]!

We're so glad you're here. The WiFi password is: [PASSWORD]

A few things to get you started:
→ Coffee and tea are in the top-left kitchen cupboard
→ Extra blankets are in the wardrobe
→ Check-out is at 11:00am (late check-out available for €15 — just message me)

Our favourite local restaurant is [NAME] on [STREET] — tell them you're staying nearby.

If anything at all needs attention, message me directly and I'll sort it within the hour.

Enjoy every minute,
[Host Name]
```

### Optional Upgrades (per stay, in order of guest impact)
1. Fresh flowers in living area (€5) — highest visual impact per euro
2. Local guidebook or personalised restaurant list (see Local Area Guide)
3. Coffee grinder + fresh local beans (one-time purchase, ongoing impact)
4. Streaming service login (Netflix) visible on TV startup
5. Charging cables in bedside drawer (USB-C, Lightning, Micro-USB)

## SECTION 4 — COMMUNICATION STANDARDS

### Response Time Rules

| Situation | Maximum Response Time | Action if Unavailable |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-booking inquiry | 60 minutes | Auto-reply active (set in Airbnb) |
| Booking confirmation | 2 hours | Trigger MSG-01 immediately |
| Guest message during stay | 45 minutes | Airbnb measures this; prioritise |
| Emergency (lock failure, leak) | 15 minutes | Have backup contact available |
| Post-checkout message | 24 hours | Review exchange window |

### Message Tone Standard
- Start messages with the guest's first name
- One question per message (multiple questions confuse and delay replies)
- End every message with a clear next action ("I'll send the check-in code by 2pm on [date]")
- Never send a message that requires the guest to ask a follow-up for basic information
- No txt speak, excessive emoji, or overly casual language
- Proofread before sending — typos in check-in instructions cause real problems

### What Not To Do (common host errors that cost stars)
- Sending check-in instructions the morning of arrival (send 48 hours before)
- Asking guests to leave a review the moment they arrive (wait until they check out)
- Over-messaging (more than 4 messages per booking is excessive unless guest initiates)
- Responding defensively to any question ("I already sent that information" → never say this)
- Blaming previous guests ("the microwave usually works") → fix it, don't explain it

## SECTION 5 — ISSUE RESOLUTION STANDARD

Every issue has a resolution standard. Meet or beat these times.

| Issue Type | Response Time | Resolution Time | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| WiFi not working | 15 minutes | 2 hours | Have router restart instructions in guest guide |
| Hot water not working | 15 minutes | Same day | Plumber on speed dial |
| Heating failure (Oct–Apr) | 15 minutes | Same day | Emergency electrician + backup heater |
| Lock failure | 10 minutes | 1 hour | Physical lockbox backup always available |
| Kitchen appliance failure | 30 minutes | Same day or compensation | |
| Cleaning complaint on arrival | 15 minutes | 2 hours or refund | Send cleaner back immediately |
| Noise from neighbours | 30 minutes | Communicate to neighbour directly | |
| Guest locked out after hours | 10 minutes | 30 minutes | Backup physical key at local neighbour/contact |

**Resolution escalation rule:** If you cannot resolve within the target time, message the guest proactively with an update and an offer (partial refund, alternative solution). Guests give 5 stars for how problems are handled, not for the absence of problems.

## SECTION 6 — SUPERHOST STANDARDS TRACKER

Airbnb Superhost requires:
- ≥10 stays completed OR ≥100 nights across 3+ reservations in a calendar year
- ≥4.8 overall rating
- ≥90% response rate (within 24 hours)
- 0% cancellation rate (host-initiated cancellations)
