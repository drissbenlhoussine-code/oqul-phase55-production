## SECTION 6 — QUARTERLY MILESTONES

### Q1 — Foundation (Months 1–3)
- [ ] Listing live with professional photos
- [ ] All 10 SOPs added to task system
- [ ] All 14 message templates saved in Airbnb
- [ ] Pricing set per Playbook: base rate, weekend premium, seasonal adjustments
- [ ] First 5 reviews received; average ≥4.8

### Q2 — Optimisation (Months 4–6)
- [ ] Occupancy at or above monthly targets
- [ ] PriceLabs or Wheelhouse connected
- [ ] Superhost eligibility window open (10+ stays, 4.8+ rating, 0 cancellations, ≥90% response rate)
- [ ] Cleaning team fully trained on SOP-04
- [ ] Monthly host review meetings running per SOP-10

### Q3 — Acceleration (Months 7–9)
- [ ] Superhost badge earned (assessed April 1 or October 1)
- [ ] Second platform active (Booking.com or VRBO via channel manager)
- [ ] 20+ reviews at 4.9+ average
- [ ] Late checkout and early check-in upsell active (target €15–€25 additional per booking)

### Q4 — Review and Invest (Months 10–12)
- [ ] Annual financial review against projection
- [ ] Décor refresh and maintenance audit scheduled
- [ ] Pricing recalibrated for next year
- [ ] Decision documented: optimise current property or add second property

## SECTION 7 — RISK REGISTER

| Risk | Likelihood | Revenue Impact | Response |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vacancy >2 weeks | Medium | -€500/month | Day 7 of vacancy: apply 20% flash discount to remaining open dates |
| Property damage by guest | Low | -€200–€2,000 | AirCover claim within 14 days + STR insurance + timestamped photo documentation |
| Cleaning cancellation last minute | Medium | Check-in delayed or cancelled | Two backup cleaners on speed dial; never operate with one cleaner only |
| Platform algorithm demotion | Medium | -20–35% visibility | 30+ reviews is the protection; cross-list on VRBO by Month 6 as insurance |
| Negative review (below 4 stars) | Low | 30-day ranking suppression | SOP-09 review system prevents most; MSG-14 response protocol for damage control |
| Local STR regulation change | Low | Potentially total | Monitor local council meetings quarterly; join local host association |
| Smart lock failure during check-in | Low | Cancellation risk | Physical key lockbox always installed and tested |
