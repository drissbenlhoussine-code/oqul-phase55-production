# Guest Experience Standards
## 5-Star Framework Mapped to Airbnb Review Subcategories

---

## HOW TO USE THIS DOCUMENT

Airbnb's star rating is not a single score. It breaks down into 6 subcategories, each weighted in the search algorithm. A 4-star overall rating with a 3-star Cleanliness score suppresses your listing more than you think. This document maps specific host actions to specific subcategory outcomes. Read Section 1 once. Reference Sections 2–7 before each booking until the standards are memorised.

---

## SECTION 1 — THE SIX REVIEW SUBCATEGORIES

| Subcategory | What Guests Actually Judge | Common 4-Star Cause |
|---|---|---|
| Cleanliness | Smell, surfaces, bathroom, bedding | Hair in drain, dusty corners, stained grout |
| Accuracy | Does the listing match reality? | Photo angle hides a small room; amenity listed but not working |
| Check-in | Ease of entry, instructions clarity | Door code didn't work, found out at 10pm |
| Communication | Response speed, problem resolution | Waited 3+ hours for an answer during stay |
| Location | Honestly described proximity | "10 min walk to centre" that takes 22 min |
| Value | Price relative to quality delivered | Anything that feels missing given what they paid |

**The algorithm logic:** Airbnb weights Cleanliness and Communication most heavily in ranking. A single 3-star Cleanliness score can suppress your listing for 21–30 days. Accuracy affects rebooking rate and conversion from searches. Value is almost always solved by managing expectations upward, not by dropping your price.

## SECTION 2 — CLEANLINESS STANDARDS (4.9-Star Level)

A 4.9-star cleanliness rating is not achieved by cleaning well — it is achieved by cleaning to a photographable standard before every check-in.

### The Four Critical Checks

**Check 1 — The Guest Eye Test**
Walk in through the front door, close your eyes, open them. The first impression is what a guest sees. If the first thing visible is a bin, a charging cable on the floor, or an overhead light with a dead bulb: fix it.

**Check 2 — The Smell Check**
No smell = the correct result. Cleaning product smell is acceptable. Any food smell, damp smell, or stale air is a problem. Solution: open all windows 30 minutes before check-in, close them 10 minutes before.

**Check 3 — The Detail Check**
These are the items guests photograph when they leave a 3-star Cleanliness review:
- Hair (any) in shower drain, sink, or on bathroom floor
- Toothpaste residue on bathroom mirror
- Grease on hob rings or oven interior
- Dust on visible surfaces (headboard, TV unit, window sills)
- Crumbs inside kitchen drawers
- Stains on upholstery or carpet
- Limescale on taps, showerhead, kettle

**Check 4 — The Bed Check**
- Pillowcases and duvet cover fresh (never re-used between guests, even for short stays)
- Mattress protector clean and flat under the sheet
- No visible indentation in mattress
- Bed made to hotel standard: sheets tucked, pillows straight, no wrinkles on the duvet

### Cleaning Frequency Rules

| Item | Frequency |
|---|---|
| Bedding (sheets, pillowcases, duvet covers) | Every single turnover |
| Towels | Every single turnover |
| Bathroom deep clean | Every single turnover |
| Kitchen surfaces and hob | Every single turnover |
| Floors (all rooms) | Every single turnover |
| Fridge interior | Check and clean every turnover (remove any items left by guest) |
| Oven interior | Every 3 turnovers or if visibly used |
| Windows (interior) | Monthly, or if fingerprints visible |
| Behind appliances | Monthly |
| Mattress (vacuum or turn) | Every 6 turnovers |

## SECTION 3 — ACCURACY STANDARDS (4.9-Star Level)

Accuracy reviews fail when a guest arrives and finds a difference between the listing and reality. The fix is not better photography — it is honest descriptions.

### What to Never Exaggerate

| Common Exaggeration | Accurate Alternative |
|---|---|
| "Spacious" | Give the square metres: "38 m² one-bedroom apartment" |
| "5 minutes to the city centre" | Time it yourself by foot, not Google Maps driving time |
| "City view" | "Side view of the street; the main city skyline is visible from the kitchen window" |
| "Fully equipped kitchen" | List what is there: "2-ring hob, oven, fridge, microwave, kettle, cafetière. No dishwasher." |
| "Super fast WiFi" | State the speed: "100 Mbps WiFi (tested; screenshot available on request)" |
| "Quiet neighbourhood" | If it's near a main road: "The street has moderate traffic until 11pm" |

### The Accuracy Audit (Run Before Publishing and After Every Listing Update)

Read your listing as if you have never seen the property. For every claim, ask: "Is this literally true?" If there is any qualifying condition that a guest would notice, add it.

Specific checks:
- Every amenity box ticked: confirm the item is present and functioning
- Every photo: confirm it shows the property as it looks today, not after a renovation 3 years ago
- Room sizes in description: confirm they match the square metre spec
- "Parking available": confirm it is always available or state that it is subject to availability
- "Pets allowed" or "No pets": confirm your policy is reflected

## SECTION 4 — CHECK-IN STANDARDS (4.9-Star Level)

Check-in should require zero assistance from you. If a guest has to call you to get inside, your check-in has failed regardless of outcome.

### The Check-In Instruction Test

Every 90 days, test your check-in instructions using this protocol:
1. Give the written check-in instructions (MSG-03) to someone who has never visited
2. Send them to the property at check-in time
3. They must reach the door and enter without contacting you
4. Note every moment of hesitation or question

Any hesitation point = rewrite that instruction.

### Check-In Instruction Structure

Your check-in message (MSG-03) must contain, in order:
1. Confirmation of check-in time and what to do if arriving before/after
2. Full address with nearest landmark (not just the postcode)
3. Building entry sequence (buzzer code or intercom name)
4. Apartment floor and number
5. Smart lock code and step-by-step operation (assume the guest has never used a smart lock)
6. What they will see when they open the door
7. WiFi name and password (large, bold, cannot be missed)
8. Your phone number for genuine emergencies (frame it: "In case of a genuine emergency — fire, flood, structural issue — call me on [number]")

**Do not include:** Checkout instructions in the check-in message. Directions to restaurants or local tips. Anything the guest doesn't need to get inside.

### Smart Lock Failure Protocol

If your smart lock fails:
1. Guest calls you (they will, within 10 minutes of arrival)
2. You have the physical key lockbox code memorised (not in your phone only — you need it at 11pm with a flat battery)
3. Direct them to the lockbox: "Go back to the front door. On the right-hand side, approximately 1.2m from the ground, there is a small black box. The code is [code]. Turn the dial to each number and press the release button."
4. Send a maintenance request to smart lock provider immediately after the call
5. Offer the guest a complimentary late checkout (no cost to you, absorbs the frustration)

## SECTION 5 — COMMUNICATION STANDARDS (4.9-Star Level)

Communication is the one subcategory entirely in your control. It requires no physical property work — only discipline.

### Response Time Rules

| Trigger | Response Required By | Standard |
|---|---|---|
| New inquiry | 60 minutes | Use MSG-10 or respond personally |
| Booking confirmed | Within 2 hours | Send MSG-01 |
| Any guest message during stay | 60 minutes | 24/7 standard; set phone alert |
| Maintenance issue reported | 30 minutes acknowledgement | Send MSG-08; resolution time separate |
| Checkout message | Within 3 hours of checkout | Send MSG-06 |
| Review received | Within 48 hours | Send MSG-13 or MSG-14 |

**90% response rate** is the Superhost threshold — it means responding to 90% of new conversations within 24 hours. In practice, 60-minute responses on new inquiries keep you above 98%.

### Communication Tone Rules

- Short is better. Guests scan messages, not read them.
- Lead with what they need to know (access code) not with pleasantries.
- Offer one action when there is a problem: "I'll have a plumber there by noon" not "I'm looking into this."
- Never argue with a guest in writing. Acknowledge, apologise for inconvenience (without admitting fault), and resolve.

### During-Stay Protocol

Day 2 of every stay (or Day 1 for 1-night stays): send MSG-04 (Mid-Stay Check-In). The purpose is to catch problems while you can still fix them, before they become review complaints. A guest who reports a cold shower on Day 2 and has it fixed by Day 3 leaves a 5-star review. A guest who deals with a cold shower for 4 days and says nothing leaves a 3-star review you cannot recover from.

## SECTION 6 — LOCATION STANDARDS (4.9-Star Level)

You cannot change the location. You can control how you describe it and how you help guests navigate it.

### Location Description Rules

Write your neighbourhood description using:
- Walking time (not driving time) to the 3 most important destinations for your guest type
- Name of the specific street, not only the neighbourhood
- Honest characterisation: "lively area with bars and restaurants on the main street; quieter on [your specific street]"
- 3 practical references: nearest supermarket (name + walking time), nearest pharmacy, nearest public transport stop

### The Guest-Facing Local Area Guide

`GUEST-03-LOCAL-AREA-GUIDE.md` is the deliverable for location experience. It must include:
- 5 restaurant recommendations with cuisine type and price range
- 3 things to do within walking distance
- 2 day trip options reachable by train or bus
- Supermarket, pharmacy, ATM locations
- Transport options: specific bus numbers, metro line names, taxi app names used locally

Guests who feel well-oriented in the neighbourhood leave higher Location scores even if the property is 20 minutes from the centre.

## SECTION 7 — VALUE STANDARDS (4.9-Star Level)

A 4-star Value score does not mean your price is too high. It means the guest felt something was missing.

### The Value Gap Analysis

After any review below 4.5 stars for Value, ask:
1. Was the property clean and as described? (If no, the Value complaint is actually an Accuracy or Cleanliness complaint)
2. Was any amenity broken or missing during their stay?
3. Was any item in the listing not available (parking spot, WiFi, TV)?

A guest who paid €120/night and found a cold shower, no WiFi, and a broken hob on arrival will leave a 2-star Value review. The price is not the issue.

### The Value Additions That Cost Under €15 Per Stay

These add perceived value above what the price signals:

| Item | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome basket (wine or local snack + card) | €8–€12 | Mentioned in 34% of reviews that include it |
| Coffee pods or quality ground coffee (not instant) | €2–€4 | "Great coffee" appears in reviews disproportionately |
| Local restaurant card (physical card from a nearby restaurant) | €0 | Feels like insider knowledge |
| Charging cables (Lightning + USB-C) in a visible spot | €6 one-time | Solves a common guest problem |
| Printed local transport map | €0 | Reduces "how do I get to X" messages by 60% |

### What Does Not Improve Value Scores

- Candles
- Decorative throws
- Plants
- "Instagram-worthy" staging that doesn't serve the guest

Value is functional. Guests leave high value scores when nothing was missing and something was unexpectedly thoughtful. They leave low value scores when something that should have been there wasn't.

## SECTION 8 — ESCALATION MATRIX

When a guest raises a problem, use this matrix. Do not improvise.

| Problem | First Action | Resolution Time | What to Offer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wifi not working | Restart router (instruct guest) → if still not working: provide mobile hotspot details | 2 hours | Partial refund: 1 night rate if WiFi out >4 hours |
| No hot water | Call plumber immediately (have number in phone) | 4 hours | Partial refund: 15% per day |
| Heating failure in winter | Space heater from hardware store, delivered to guest | 2 hours | Full refund for affected night(s) |
| Noise from neighbours | Acknowledge; if persistent, offer early checkout with full refund | Immediate ack, 24hr resolution window | Refund proportional to disruption |
| Cleanliness issue on arrival | Offer to re-clean within 2 hours or offer rebooking assistance | 2 hours | Full refund if guest cannot stay |
| Property as described complaint | Acknowledge; cross-check with listing; if accurate: apologise for disconnect; if inaccurate: offer partial refund | Immediate | 10–30% refund depending on severity |

**Refund principle:** Offer refunds before guests ask for them. A proactive €20 partial refund prevents a €200 loss in suppressed bookings from a 3-star review.
