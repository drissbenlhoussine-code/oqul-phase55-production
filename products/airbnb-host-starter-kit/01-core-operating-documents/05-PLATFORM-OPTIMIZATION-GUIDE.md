# Platform Optimization Guide
## Airbnb Algorithm · Listing Rank System · Search Visibility

---

## HOW TO USE THIS DOCUMENT

Airbnb's search algorithm has five measurable signals. Each is in your control. This document tells you what the signals are, how Airbnb weights them, and what specific actions move each signal. Run the Listing Audit in Section 6 every 90 days.

---

## SECTION 1 — THE FIVE ALGORITHM SIGNALS

Airbnb's ranking system is not public, but 7 years of host testing, published Airbnb research papers, and patterns across Superhost communities have established these five signals as the primary ranking drivers:

| Signal | What It Measures | Approximate Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Booking Popularity | Booking rate, wish-list saves, view-to-booking conversion | High |
| Guest Satisfaction | Overall rating + subcategory averages (esp. Cleanliness, Communication) | High |
| Response Rate and Speed | % of inquiries replied to within 24h; median response time | High |
| Listing Completeness | % of profile fields filled; photo count and quality; description length | Medium |
| Pricing Competitiveness | Your rate vs. comparable properties in the same neighbourhood | Medium |

**What this means in practice:** Ranking is earned through conversion (guests who see your listing book it) and reputation (guests who stayed loved it). Everything else — photos, description, pricing — supports those two outcomes.

## SECTION 2 — BOOKING POPULARITY SIGNAL

### How to Improve Booking Rate

**Instant Book:** Listings with Instant Book enabled convert at 30–40% higher rates in most European markets. Guests who have already decided to book do not want to wait for approval. If you have a smart lock, enable Instant Book.

**First-photo conversion:** The primary photo determines whether a guest clicks on your listing in search results. Test yours: search for your listing from a logged-out private browser. If your hero photo is not the best-looking in the first row of results, reshoot it.

Primary photo standards:
- Wide-angle interior shot or natural-light exterior
- Main living area or bedroom (whichever photographs better)
- Shot in landscape orientation (portrait photos appear cropped)
- Bright, no motion blur, no clutter visible
- Not a bathroom. Bathrooms as hero photos consistently underperform.

**Wishlist saves:** Each wishlist save is a small positive signal. Saves increase when your listing appears in multiple search filters simultaneously. Maximise filter eligibility:
- Set "Pets allowed" if you accept pets (adds you to the Pets Allowed filter)
- Enable "Workspace" designation if you have a dedicated desk and fast WiFi (remote worker filter)
- Enable "Long stays welcome" if you accept 28+ night bookings (Monthly filter)
- Add a "Self check-in" highlight (it appears as a filter in many markets)

### How to Recover from Low Booking Rate

If your booking rate has dropped (you can see this through Airbnb's Performance tab), the most likely causes in order:
1. Photo quality has not kept pace with competing listings (reshoot)
2. Seasonal demand has dropped and you have not adjusted pricing (apply vacancy protocol from `02-PRICING-STRATEGY-PLAYBOOK.md`)
3. A new competitor has opened nearby with better photos or lower prices
4. Your response time to inquiries has slipped (check your Performance tab response metrics)

## SECTION 3 — GUEST SATISFACTION SIGNAL

### Rating Targets by Signal Milestone

| Milestone | Overall Rating | Reviews Needed | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Maintain positive ranking | 4.7+ | Any | Below 4.7 triggers listing suppression warning |
| Superhost eligibility | 4.8+ | 10 minimum | Assessed April 1 and October 1 |
| Top 10% listing tier | 4.9+ | 20+ | Shows in "Highly rated" filter results |
| Airbnb Guest Favourite | 4.9+ avg subcategories | 50+ | Earns "Guest Favourite" badge; major conversion boost |

### The Review Subcategory Priority Order

If your overall rating is below 4.8, identify which subcategory is dragging it:

1. **Cleanliness below 4.7:** Fix cleaning standards before next booking (see `04-GUEST-EXPERIENCE-STANDARDS.md` Section 2). This is the most common cause of suppressed new listings.
2. **Accuracy below 4.7:** Audit your listing for any overstated claim and correct it.
3. **Communication below 4.7:** Set push notifications on your phone. A 3-hour response to a problem during a stay typically triggers this.
4. **Check-in below 4.7:** Rewrite your check-in instructions. Test them with a third party.
5. **Value below 4.7:** Run the Value Gap Analysis in `04-GUEST-EXPERIENCE-STANDARDS.md` Section 7.
6. **Location below 4.7:** Review how you describe the neighbourhood. Remove any inflated proximity claims.

## SECTION 4 — RESPONSE RATE AND SPEED SIGNAL

### The Metrics Airbnb Tracks

**Response rate:** Percentage of new guest conversations (not all messages, only first contact from new guests) you reply to within 24 hours. Airbnb shows this on your Performance tab.

**Target:** 100% response rate to new inquiries. Any lapse below 90% affects Superhost eligibility and likely affects search ranking.

**Median response time:** Airbnb calculates your typical response time and shows it on your listing profile. "Responds within an hour" appears prominently for fast responders. "Responds within a few days" is a conversion killer.

### How to Maintain 100% Response Rate

1. Enable Airbnb push notifications on your phone — not just email. Email is too slow.
2. Turn on the Airbnb app notification for "New message" and "New inquiry." Test that they work.
3. Pre-saved message templates in Airbnb Saved Messages (14 templates in `04-communication-templates/`) allow 90-second responses to any standard inquiry.
4. If you will be unreachable for more than 4 hours (flight, surgery, remote area): use Airbnb's automated response feature to reply immediately with an estimated response time. Any reply resets the response clock.
5. Set a co-host on your account who can respond if you are unreachable for more than 24 hours.

## SECTION 5 — LISTING COMPLETENESS SIGNAL

Airbnb rewards complete profiles. Incomplete listings are ranked below comparable complete listings in all else being equal scenarios.

### Completeness Checklist

**Profile (Host) completeness:**
- [ ] Profile photo: clear photo of you (not your logo, not your pet, not a sunset). Guests book from people, not brands.
- [ ] Host bio: 100+ words. Specific to your hosting style, not generic. Include: how long you've hosted, what you care about as a host, what makes your property distinctive.
- [ ] Host languages: accurately listed
- [ ] ID verification: completed (required for Superhost and affects some booking filters)
- [ ] Host response time and rate: displayed on your profile, updated automatically

**Listing completeness:**
- [ ] All 6 description sections written (see `03-PROPERTY-SETUP-CHECKLIST.md` item 8.3)
- [ ] All applicable amenities ticked accurately
- [ ] House rules: all 5 categories filled (check-in time, checkout time, quiet hours, smoking policy, pets policy)
- [ ] 20+ photos with captions
- [ ] Pricing: base rate, cleaning fee, weekly and monthly discounts (if applicable) all set
- [ ] Availability calendar: open 6+ months in advance

**Photos — minimum standard:**
- Cover photo: wide-angle, best room, bright daylight
- Full bedroom: bed made, windows visible
- Full bathroom: clean, no personal items visible
- Kitchen: clean surfaces, appliances visible
- Living area (if applicable)
- Any outdoor space
- Building exterior or entrance
- One photo of the view (even if modest — it sets expectations)
- One photo of the welcome basket or any distinctive detail

**Photos — Airbnb's algorithm preference:** More photos perform better than fewer up to approximately 30. Beyond 30, diminishing returns. If you have under 15, this is the highest-priority listing improvement available.

## SECTION 6 — 90-DAY LISTING AUDIT

Run this every 90 days. Takes 45 minutes. Compare to the previous audit to identify regression.

### Audit Step 1 — Performance Metrics Check

Log into Airbnb Host dashboard → Performance tab. Record:

| Metric | Current | Previous Quarter | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Overall rating | | | |
| Views (last 30 days) | | | |
| Conversion rate (views → bookings) | | | |
| Response rate | | | |
| Nights booked (last 30 days) | | | |

If conversion rate has dropped but views have held steady: your listing is not compelling enough once guests click on it — this is a photo or description problem.
If views have dropped but conversion rate is stable: your listing is not appearing in searches — this is a pricing, availability, or algorithm signal problem.

### Audit Step 2 — Competitor Check

Search Airbnb as a guest in your neighbourhood, filtering for your property type and capacity, checking in 2 weeks from today for 3 nights.

Record where your listing appears in the results. Note the position and which properties appear above you.

For the 3 properties directly above yours:
- Are their photos better? (If yes: reshoot priority.)
- Is their price lower? (If yes by more than 15%: price audit.)
- Are their review counts higher? (If yes: you will naturally close this gap over time; no action needed.)
- Do they have Superhost badge? (If yes: Superhost eligibility check — use `BONUS-SUPERHOST-90DAY-SPRINT.md`.)

### Audit Step 3 — Review Trend Analysis

Pull last 10 reviews. For each, note what the guest mentioned (positive or negative). If any negative theme appears 3+ times across reviews, it is systemic and requires a fix before the next booking.

Common systemic issues:
- "Hard to find" → rewrite the arrival directions
- "Cold at night" → add extra blankets or adjust thermostat instructions
- "Noisy street" → add this to the listing honestly to reset expectations
- "WiFi slow" → upgrade plan or move router

## SECTION 7 — PRICING COMPETITIVENESS SIGNAL

Airbnb's algorithm assesses whether your price is competitive relative to comparable properties in real-time. If you are consistently priced 20%+ above your comp set without corresponding quality signals (Superhost, 50+ reviews, 4.9+), your listing may be suppressed in price-filtered searches.

### How to Stay Competitive Without Underpricing

1. Use PriceLabs or Wheelhouse (see `02-PRICING-STRATEGY-PLAYBOOK.md`). These tools are calibrated to market, not to Airbnb's Smart Pricing model.
2. Set minimum price: never below 60% of your base rate.
3. During low-demand periods, lower your base rate rather than adding discounts. A lower displayed price converts better than a higher price with a "10% discount" badge.
4. During high-demand events, price at the event multiplier (Section 2 of `02-PRICING-STRATEGY-PLAYBOOK.md`) 90+ days in advance. Do not lower prices in the week before a peak event — demand is highest in the 14 days before.

### The Occupancy-Rate Pricing Principle

Target occupancy: 72–80% for most European mid-tier markets.
If you are running at 90%+ occupancy: your price is too low. Raise base rate by 10%.
If you are running at below 60% for more than 30 consecutive days: apply vacancy protocol. After 60 days of below 60%, conduct a full listing audit (Section 6) — this is not a pricing problem alone.

## SECTION 8 — SUPERHOST ELIGIBILITY TRACKER

Superhost is assessed on April 1 and October 1 of each year. You need to meet all four criteria in the preceding 12 months.

| Criterion | Threshold | Your Current Status |
|---|---|---|
| Total stays completed | ≥10 stays OR ≥100 nights | |
| Overall rating | ≥4.8 average | |
| Response rate | ≥90% to all messages within 24 hours | |
| Host cancellation rate | 0% (zero host-initiated cancellations) | |

**Critical:** One host-initiated cancellation removes Superhost eligibility for the entire assessment period, regardless of all other performance. Never cancel a confirmed booking unless it is physically impossible to host. Airbnb provides Extenuating Circumstances cancellations for genuine emergencies — document them.

**Superhost effect on revenue:** A Superhost badge typically increases booking conversion by 10–18% for equivalent listings. At €30,000 annual gross revenue, that is €3,000–€5,400 in additional bookings from the same traffic. Pursue it actively.
