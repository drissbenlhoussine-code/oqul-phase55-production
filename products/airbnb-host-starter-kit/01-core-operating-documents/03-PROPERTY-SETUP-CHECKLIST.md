# Property Setup Checklist
## 85-Item Setup-to-Live Checklist · Pre-Launch Quality Gate

---

## HOW TO USE THIS DOCUMENT

Work through each section in order. Do not submit your Airbnb listing for review until every item in Sections 1–7 is checked. Section 8 covers items to complete in the first 30 days after going live. Each item has a specific completion standard — "done" means the standard is met, not that you attempted it.

---

## SECTION 1 — LEGAL AND INSURANCE (Complete before anything else)

- [ ] **1.1** — Confirmed short-term rental is legally permitted at this address. Checked local council or municipality website for STR licensing requirements. (Not assumed — confirmed in writing or via phone call.)
- [ ] **1.2** — STR permit or licence obtained if required. Permit number recorded: _______________
- [ ] **1.3** — STR-rated property insurance policy active. Standard home insurance **does not** cover commercial rental claims. Policy reference: _______________
- [ ] **1.4** — Building management company or landlord informed and written permission obtained if applicable. Stored in property documents folder.
- [ ] **1.5** — Tax registration completed for rental income in your jurisdiction. Consult `06-FINANCIAL-MODEL-GUIDE.md` for country-specific notes.
- [ ] **1.6** — Guest liability documented: confirmed whether your STR insurance policy covers guest injury on premises or whether AirCover provides sufficient coverage for your risk tolerance.

---

## SECTION 2 — SAFETY AND COMPLIANCE

- [ ] **2.1** — Smoke detector installed and tested in every room (including kitchen and bedroom). Battery replaced within last 6 months.
- [ ] **2.2** — Carbon monoxide detector installed if property has gas appliances, boiler, or attached garage.
- [ ] **2.3** — Fire extinguisher (minimum 1 kg dry powder or CO₂) mounted visibly in or near the kitchen. Checked within last 12 months.
- [ ] **2.4** — Fire escape route posted at front door or inside the entrance. Plain text: "In case of fire, exit via [route]. Assembly point: [location]."
- [ ] **2.5** — First aid kit stocked and stored in a visible location. Contents: plasters, sterile wipes, bandage, antiseptic cream, paracetamol, ibuprofen.
- [ ] **2.6** — Emergency contact card posted near front door: local emergency number (112 in EU), nearest hospital address and distance, your phone number.
- [ ] **2.7** — All electrical appliances PAT tested or safety compliant per local regulations. No loose wiring, exposed sockets, or overloaded extension leads.
- [ ] **2.8** — Hot water temperature confirmed below 60°C (to prevent scalding). Particularly relevant for older properties.
- [ ] **2.9** — Any balcony or outdoor space checked: railings secure, no trip hazards, no items that could fall.
- [ ] **2.10** — Wifi router not stored inside locked cupboard or behind hard-to-reach furniture (guests need to restart it).

---

## SECTION 3 — ACCESS AND ENTRY SYSTEM

- [ ] **3.1** — Smart lock installed and tested minimum 10 times with different access codes. Brand: _______________
- [ ] **3.2** — Smart lock code generation process confirmed: new unique code generated per booking, code expires at checkout time.
- [ ] **3.3** — Physical key lockbox installed and functional. Mounted at: _______________ Code: _______________ (change after each use as backup only).
- [ ] **3.4** — Check-in instructions written and tested by someone who has never been to the property. They should reach the door from the street using only your written instructions, without asking you anything.
- [ ] **3.5** — Parking instructions confirmed (if applicable). Specific bay number or street, permit details, nearest alternative if full.
- [ ] **3.6** — Building entry code (if applicable) confirmed and included in check-in instructions. Tested by third party.
- [ ] **3.7** — Elevator instructions included if applicable (some older European buildings have non-obvious operation).

---

## SECTION 4 — BEDROOM SETUP

- [ ] **4.1** — Mattress: firm support, no visible sag or indentation, clean and odour-free.
- [ ] **4.2** — Mattress protector installed (waterproof). Minimum 2 sets per bed in rotation.
- [ ] **4.3** — Fitted sheet (hospital corners or deep-pocket fit). Minimum 3 sets per bed.
- [ ] **4.4** — Flat sheet or top sheet (if using European style — confirm single duvet per person for doubles).
- [ ] **4.5** — Duvet: 10.5–13.5 tog for year-round use in temperate climates. Clean cover. Minimum 3 duvet cover sets.
- [ ] **4.6** — Pillows: minimum 2 per person. Mix of firm and soft preferred. Pillow protectors on all pillows.
- [ ] **4.7** — Blackout blind or curtains confirmed to block >90% of light. Tested at 7am in summer.
- [ ] **4.8** — Bedside table with lamp on each side (or per single bed). Lamp bulb functioning.
- [ ] **4.9** — USB charging socket or universal adaptor on each bedside table. Guests charge phones overnight.
- [ ] **4.10** — Wardrobe or clothing rail with minimum 6 hangers. Accessible storage for 2 guests (minimum 2 drawers or shelf space).
- [ ] **4.11** — Luggage rack or bench at end of bed. Prevents suitcase on floor/bed.
- [ ] **4.12** — Mirror: minimum one full-length mirror in bedroom or bathroom.
- [ ] **4.13** — Bedroom door closes fully and latch or lock functions.

---

## SECTION 5 — BATHROOM SETUP

- [ ] **5.1** — Bath mat (non-slip). Minimum 2 in rotation.
- [ ] **5.2** — Hand towels: minimum 2 per guest per stay. White or neutral colour (shows cleanliness, easy to bleach).
- [ ] **5.3** — Bath towels: minimum 2 per guest per stay. Same quality standard as hand towels.
- [ ] **5.4** — Shower/bath cleaned and grouting inspected. Mould treated and removed.
- [ ] **5.5** — Shampoo and conditioner (minimum 100ml each, not travel-size): replenished before every guest arrival.
- [ ] **5.6** — Body wash or bar soap.
- [ ] **5.7** — Hand soap at sink. Pump dispenser, not a bar soap remnant.
- [ ] **5.8** — Toilet paper: minimum 4 rolls stocked at arrival, regardless of stay length.
- [ ] **5.9** — Spare toilet rolls visible to guest (not hidden in cleaning cupboard).
- [ ] **5.10** — Hairdryer: 1,800W minimum. Tested and functional. Not stored inside a locked drawer.
- [ ] **5.11** — Mirror with good lighting. Guests will not leave a 5-star review after a poorly lit mirror.
- [ ] **5.12** — Bin with lid in bathroom.
- [ ] **5.13** — Shower pressure confirmed adequate. European standard: ≥8 litres/minute. Tested during peak-demand hours.
- [ ] **5.14** — Hot water: confirmed hot within 90 seconds of turning on shower.

## SECTION 6 — KITCHEN SETUP

- [ ] **6.1** — Hob (2 rings minimum, 4 preferred) clean and functioning. All burners tested.
- [ ] **6.2** — Oven clean and functioning. Temperature calibrated (€15 oven thermometer test).
- [ ] **6.3** — Fridge clean, odour-free, set to 4°C. Ice maker or ice cube tray.
- [ ] **6.4** — Microwave clean and functioning.
- [ ] **6.5** — Kettle present and descaled.
- [ ] **6.6** — Coffee maker or cafetière + coffee (ground or pods). Minimum 2 pods or 50g ground per stay.
- [ ] **6.7** — Cutlery: minimum 4 of each (knife, fork, dessert spoon, teaspoon).
- [ ] **6.8** — Plates: minimum 4 dinner plates, 4 side plates.
- [ ] **6.9** — Bowls: minimum 4.
- [ ] **6.10** — Glasses: minimum 4 water glasses, 4 wine glasses.
- [ ] **6.11** — Mugs: minimum 4.
- [ ] **6.12** — Cooking pots: minimum 1 large saucepan (3L+), 1 small saucepan, 1 frying pan.
- [ ] **6.13** — Chopping board + 1 chef's knife (sharp).
- [ ] **6.14** — Tin opener, bottle opener, corkscrew.
- [ ] **6.15** — Washing-up liquid, sponge, and dish rack (or dishwasher and tablets).
- [ ] **6.16** — Kitchen bin with lid. Bin bags provided (minimum 5).
- [ ] **6.17** — Cleaning spray and cloth under sink (guests will use it if they spill something).
- [ ] **6.18** — Welcome items stocked: tea bags (4 per stay), sugar (small jar), salt and pepper, olive oil or cooking oil.
- [ ] **6.19** — Recycling system explained: separate bins labelled for general waste, recycling, glass (per local rules).

## SECTION 7 — LIVING SPACE AND CONNECTIVITY

- [ ] **7.1** — Wifi network name and password displayed prominently (printed card or frame at eye level near TV or door).
- [ ] **7.2** — Wifi speed tested at 100Mbps download minimum. Use fast.com. Screenshot saved as evidence for listing.
- [ ] **7.3** — TV with streaming app access (Netflix, YouTube at minimum). Remote controls tested and batteries fresh.
- [ ] **7.4** — Sofa or seating with capacity for maximum guest count.
- [ ] **7.5** — Adequate lighting: no areas that feel dark. Standing lamp or table lamp where ceiling light is insufficient.
- [ ] **7.6** — Temperature control confirmed: thermostat, portable heater, or fan depending on season. Guests must be able to adjust temperature without calling you.
- [ ] **7.7** — House manual printed or displayed: check-in/checkout time, bin day, parking rules, noise rules, how to operate appliances. Printed and laminated preferred. Digital version in `GUEST-01-WELCOME-GUIDE.md`.
- [ ] **7.8** — Iron and ironing board (or steamer) available and accessible.
- [ ] **7.9** — Extra blanket stored visibly (not in a locked cupboard).

## SECTION 8 — AIRBNB LISTING SETUP

- [ ] **8.1** — Professional photos booked and completed. Minimum 20 images. Shot on a camera (not a phone) with wide-angle lens, during daylight, with all lights on.
- [ ] **8.2** — Listing title follows the formula: [Neighbourhood] [Property Type] · [1 key feature] · [1 key amenity]. Example: "Alfama Studio · Rooftop Terrace · 5 min Walk to Fado Museum".
- [ ] **8.3** — All 6 Airbnb listing sections completed fully (no section left at default or blank):
  - [ ] About this space (minimum 150 words)
  - [ ] The space (specific room-by-room description)
  - [ ] Guest access (exactly what guests can use)
  - [ ] Other things to note (actual quirks: "The shower needs 45 seconds to warm up. The front door key needs a firm turn to the left.")
  - [ ] Getting around (public transport + walking to 3 landmarks)
  - [ ] Neighbourhood overview (3–5 sentences specific to the exact street, not the city)
- [ ] **8.4** — All amenities accurately ticked in Airbnb dashboard. Untick anything not available (even "TV" if it's not streaming-capable). False amenity claims are the leading cause of Accuracy review deductions.
- [ ] **8.5** — Pricing set per `02-PRICING-STRATEGY-PLAYBOOK.md`. Smart Pricing disabled.
- [ ] **8.6** — Availability calendar: open at minimum 6 months in advance from your launch date.
- [ ] **8.7** — Instant Book enabled if you have a smart lock. It increases booking velocity by 30–40% in most markets.
- [ ] **8.8** — 14 message templates saved in Airbnb Saved Messages per `00-QUICK-START-GUIDE.md` Step 2.
- [ ] **8.9** — House rules set: checkout time, quiet hours, smoking policy, pet policy, party policy. Use the exact language from `GUEST-02-HOUSE-RULES.md`.
- [ ] **8.10** — Cancellation policy set to "Firm" (not Flexible). Firm gives you 50% if a guest cancels within 30 days. Flexible returns nothing.

---

## SECTION 9 — SOFT LAUNCH CHECKLIST (First 30 days after going live)

Complete these within the first 30 days to protect your new-listing ranking boost.

- [ ] **9.1** — First 3 bookings confirmed. If after 14 days you have 0 bookings, apply -15% discount temporarily and review listing photos (the single most common issue).
- [ ] **9.2** — First 5 reviews received, average 4.8+. If any review comes in below 4.5, read `04-GUEST-EXPERIENCE-STANDARDS.md` before the next guest arrives.
- [ ] **9.3** — Response rate: 100% to all inquiries within 1 hour. Set Airbnb notification to push alerts on your phone.
- [ ] **9.4** — PriceLabs or Wheelhouse connected and 90-day pricing confirmed (Section 4 of `02-PRICING-STRATEGY-PLAYBOOK.md`).
- [ ] **9.5** — Revenue tracker (TAB-01) live and first 3 bookings entered.
- [ ] **9.6** — Cleaning team confirmed and SOP-04 reviewed with them in person.
- [ ] **9.7** — Photo review: request feedback from 2 people who have never seen the property. If they cannot identify what makes it special in under 10 seconds, reshoot the hero photo.
