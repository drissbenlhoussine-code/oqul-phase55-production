# Platform Optimisation Guide
## Airbnb Algorithm, Listing, and Search Rank System

---

## HOW AIRBNB RANKS LISTINGS

Airbnb's search algorithm (called "Smart Ranking") weighs five primary signals. Understanding these is the difference between a listing buried on page 8 and one that appears in the top 10 results.

### The Five Ranking Signals (in approximate order of weight)

| Signal | Weight | What It Means | How to Improve |
|---|---|---|---|
| Booking popularity | Very High | How many bookings your listing generates relative to views | Improve conversion rate (photos + price + reviews) |
| Guest satisfaction | High | Star rating across all categories | Execute Guest Experience Standards document |
| Response rate & speed | High | % of inquiries responded to within 24h (ideally <1h) | Enable mobile notifications; use saved messages |
| Listing completeness | Medium | % of available listing fields filled in | Fill in every field; update Description seasonally |
| Pricing competitiveness | Medium | How your price compares to similar listings nearby | Use PriceLabs; avoid Airbnb Smart Pricing |

### Additional Signals That Amplify Ranking

- **Conversion rate:** Listing views that result in bookings. A higher conversion rate (>3%) signals to Airbnb that your listing is compelling
- **Wishlist saves:** Guests who save your listing signal interest — more saves correlate with higher rank
- **Acceptance rate:** Accepting (or pre-approving) more requests improves standing
- **Superhost badge:** Listed separately in Superhost filter results — effectively a second search pool
- **New listings boost:** Fresh listings get 2–4 weeks of elevated visibility. Optimise before you launch

## SECTION 1 — LISTING TITLE OPTIMISATION

### The Title Formula
Airbnb titles are capped at 50 characters. Every character counts.

**Formula:**
```
[USP] + [Property Type] + [Neighbourhood/View] w/ [Key Feature]
```

**Examples:**
| Draft Title | Score | Better Version |
|---|---|---|
| "My apartment near city centre" | 3/10 | "Bright Studio in Alfama w/ Sunset Views" |
| "Cosy flat, great location" | 2/10 | "Design Flat | Old Town | Private Terrace" |
| "Entire 2BR apartment" | 3/10 | "Architect Loft · 2BR · 5-min Walk to Beach" |
| "Nice house with garden" | 2/10 | "Detached Cottage w/ Private Garden + Hot Tub" |

**Title rules:**
- Lead with the most distinctive feature (view, unique amenity, design style)
- Include neighbourhood only if it's recognisable to travellers (Shoreditch, Marais, Alfama)
- Use the pipe character `|` or middle dot `·` to separate elements economically
- Never: "cosy," "nice," "great," "lovely" — these signal nothing
- Always: specific, concrete nouns and adjectives

### Title A/B Testing
Change your title every 4 weeks if occupancy is below 65%. Track bookings in the 2 weeks before and after each change. Keep the version that performs better.

## SECTION 2 — LISTING DESCRIPTION ARCHITECTURE

Airbnb's description is not a brochure. It's a conversion tool. Structure it in five specific sections.

### Section 1: The Hook (first 2 sentences — shows in search preview)
The first 150 characters appear in search results before guests click. Sell the experience, not the specs.

**Formula:** Emotion + unique feature + call to action
```
Wake up to [specific view/experience] in this [property type] designed for [guest type].
Everything you need for a [adjective] stay in [neighbourhood/city] is already here.
```

**Example:**
> Wake up to floor-to-ceiling views over Lisbon's rooftops in this architect-renovated studio in Alfama. Everything you need for a design-forward stay in the city's most iconic neighbourhood is already here.

### Section 2: The Space (factual, specific)
One paragraph. Cover: size, layout, standout design or furniture, natural light, outdoor space.

**Example:**
> The apartment is 42m², open-plan, with polished concrete floors, exposed brick, and handmade Portuguese tiles in the kitchen. The bedroom area is separated by a custom oak bookshelf. The south-facing terrace fits two chairs and a small table — and gets direct sun until 4pm in summer.

### Section 3: The Amenities Highlights (top 5–7 amenities, not the full list)
Airbnb lists all amenities separately. In the description, highlight the ones that set you apart.

**Example:**
> Highlights: Nespresso machine with starter pods, 65" Samsung Smart TV, super-fast 200Mbps fibre WiFi, Netflix pre-loaded, monsoon shower, hotel-quality 400-thread-count linen.

### Section 4: The Neighbourhood (position, not directions)
Tell guests why your location is worth choosing, not just where it is.

**Example:**
> You're a 4-minute walk from Alfama's famous viewpoints, 8 minutes from the 28 tram stop, and 12 minutes by Uber from the main Lisbon train station. The bakery two streets away opens at 7am — their pastel de nata is the best in the city, and we'll argue about it.

### Section 5: Guest Fit (who this space works for)
Be clear about who thrives here. Honesty prevents bad-fit bookings and 3-star reviews.

**Example:**
> This space is ideal for couples, solo travellers, or one business traveller. The spiral staircase to the sleeping loft has 9 steps and a rail — unsuitable for guests with significant mobility limitations or young children. We don't allow smoking anywhere on the property.

## SECTION 3 — PHOTOGRAPHY STANDARDS

Photos are your conversion rate. The difference between 1.5% and 4% conversion is usually the photos.

### The Minimum Photo Set (20 images)

| # | Shot | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero exterior or entrance | Best possible first impression; used as thumbnail |
| 2–3 | Living room (2 angles) | Show the full space; natural light; no people |
| 4–5 | Bedroom (2 angles) | Crisp white linen, pillows arranged, curtains open |
| 6–7 | Kitchen (2 angles) | Clean counter, coffee machine prominent |
| 8–9 | Bathroom (2 angles) | Rolled towels, product bottles lined up |
| 10–11 | Outdoor space (if any) | Golden hour preferred |
| 12–13 | Detail shots (2) | Welcomed extras, design features, coffee setup |
| 14–15 | View shots (if available) | From inside looking out, from terrace |
| 16–17 | Natural light shots | Afternoon sun through windows |
| 18–19 | Neighbourhood (2) | Coffee shop, park, local street |
| 20 | Building exterior / entrance | Helps guests identify on arrival |

### Photography Rules
- Hire a professional: $150–300 investment returns 10–20% more revenue annually. Non-negotiable.
- Shoot on a clear day, mid-morning for bedroom/living; afternoon for south-facing rooms
- Remove all personal items: family photos, toiletries, stray cables, fridge magnets
- Style the space before shooting: fresh flowers, open books, arranged cushions
- Never use wide-angle distortion — guests will feel deceived when they arrive
- Minimum resolution: 3000 × 2000 pixels (Airbnb compresses, so start large)

### Thumbnail Strategy
Airbnb's thumbnail is your ad. Test different images as your primary photo (first in sequence) every 3 months. Track your click-through rate improvement.

Best-performing thumbnail types (in order):
1. Outdoor/terrace/view shot with property visible
2. Living room with natural light and distinctive design feature
3. Bedroom with hotel-style bedding and view
4. Exterior of a characterful building

## SECTION 4 — AMENITY STRATEGY

### The 10 Amenities With Highest Search Filter Impact

These amenities appear as guest search filters. Having them means you appear in filtered results; not having them means you're invisible to that search segment.

| Amenity | Search Filter? | Difficulty to Add | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| WiFi (stated speed) | Yes | Low | Essential |
| Kitchen | Yes | Low | Essential |
| Washer | Yes | Low | High |
| Air conditioning | Yes | Medium | Very High (summer bookings) |
| Free parking | Yes | Varies | High (if applicable) |
| Pool | Yes | High | Very High (premium segment) |
| Pet-friendly | Yes | Low (policy change) | Medium (niche demand) |
| TV (streaming) | Yes | Low | Medium |
| Dedicated workspace | Yes | Low | High (remote workers) |
| Self check-in | Yes | Low-Medium | High |

### Workspace Setup (Remote Worker Filter)
Remote workers now represent 22–35% of Airbnb bookings in urban markets. To qualify for the "dedicated workspace" filter:
- Desk with minimum 80cm × 50cm clear working surface
- Chair with back support (not a dining chair)
- Power outlet accessible from desk
- WiFi speed minimum 25Mbps (test and publish speed)

## SECTION 5 — REVIEW VELOCITY STRATEGY

Reviews compound. A listing with 5 reviews and a 4.9 rating outperforms a listing with 80 reviews and a 4.6 rating in many markets.

### Getting Your First 10 Reviews Fast
1. Price 10–15% below market for your first 8 bookings
2. Send MSG-07-REVIEW-REQUEST exactly 2 hours after checkout (highest response window)
3. Leave a review for every guest within 24 hours of their checkout (Airbnb shows yours when they submit theirs — it incentivises them to review)
4. Invite friends/family for paid stays at low rates (this is permitted by Airbnb) to build review count

### Review Quantity Thresholds and Their Effects

| Reviews | Effect |
|---|---|
| 0–4 | Listing in "new" section; some guests filter these out; price down to compensate |
| 5–15 | Graduated into normal search pool; conversion improves significantly |
| 16–30 | Price can move to market rate; Superhost window opens |
| 31–50 | Strong social proof; can price 5–10% above market |
| 51+ | Established listing; algorithm favours for sponsored placement |
