## SECTION 7 — RISK REGISTER

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Extended vacancy (>2 weeks) | Medium | High | Activate 20% flash discount on days 7–14 |
| Problem guest / property damage | Low | High | Airbnb AirCover + own STR insurance policy |
| Cleaning team cancels last minute | Medium | High | Maintain 2 backup cleaners on speed dial |
| Platform algorithm change | Medium | Medium | Cross-list on VRBO/Booking.com by Month 4 |
| Negative review (below 4 stars) | Low | High | SOP-09 + MSG-14 response within 24 hours |
| Regulatory change (STR ban/limits) | Low | Very High | Monitor local council planning meetings quarterly |
| Smart lock failure | Low | High | Physical key lockbox as backup at all times |
