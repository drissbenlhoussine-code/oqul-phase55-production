# Financial Model Guide
## P&L Framework, KPI Definitions, and Monthly Reporting

---

## PURPOSE

This document defines every financial metric you need to track as an Airbnb host, how to calculate it, and what good looks like. Use it to build your monthly reporting habit and to make pricing and investment decisions with data rather than instinct.

---

## SECTION 1 — KPI DEFINITIONS

### Revenue Metrics

**1. Gross Booking Revenue (GBR)**
> Total amount guests paid before any fees are deducted.
> Includes: nightly rate + cleaning fee + any extras (early check-in fees, etc.)
> Formula: `Sum of all guest payments in the period`
> Benchmark: Track month-over-month and year-over-year

**2. Net Host Revenue (NHR)**
> What Airbnb pays you after deducting their host service fee (typically 3%).
> Formula: `Gross Booking Revenue × 0.97`
> This is the number you use for all profitability calculations.

**3. Average Daily Rate (ADR)**
> The average revenue earned per booked night.
> Formula: `Net Host Revenue ÷ Total Booked Nights`
> Example: €3,200 net revenue / 28 booked nights = **€114.29 ADR**
> Benchmark: Should be ≥ market average for your property type. Track monthly.

**4. Revenue Per Available Night (RevPAN)**
> Also called RevPAR in hotel industry. Measures revenue efficiency across ALL nights, not just booked ones.
> Formula: `Net Host Revenue ÷ Total Available Nights`
> Example: €3,200 / 31 available nights = **€103.23 RevPAN**
> This is the single most important revenue metric. A high ADR with low occupancy gives a low RevPAN.

**5. Cleaning Fee Revenue**
> Total cleaning fees collected from guests. This should largely offset cleaning costs.
> Formula: `Number of bookings × cleaning fee charged`
> Target: Cleaning fees collected ≥ 95% of actual cleaning costs

### Occupancy Metrics

**6. Occupancy Rate (OCC)**
> Percentage of available nights that were booked.
> Formula: `Booked Nights ÷ Available Nights × 100`
> Note: "Available" means nights you had open for booking — subtract any blocked dates you chose to block.
> Benchmark targets: Low season ≥50%, Standard ≥65%, Peak ≥85%, Annual ≥70%

**7. Average Length of Stay (ALOS)**
> Average number of nights per booking.
> Formula: `Total Booked Nights ÷ Total Number of Bookings`
> Example: 180 booked nights / 62 bookings = **2.9 nights ALOS**
> Impact: Longer stays = fewer turnovers = lower cleaning costs per night

**8. Booking Lead Time**
> How far in advance guests typically book.
> Formula: `Average days between booking date and check-in date`
> Healthy range: 18–35 days. Under 10 days suggests pricing is too high or listing is weak.

### Expense Metrics

**9. Cost Per Occupied Night (CPON)**
> Total operating costs divided by booked nights. Use this to price correctly.
> Formula: `Total Monthly Operating Costs ÷ Booked Nights`
> Example: €650 costs / 22 nights = **€29.55 CPON**
> Your ADR must exceed CPON by your target profit margin.

**10. Gross Profit Margin (GPM)**
> The percentage of revenue left after all operating costs (before mortgage/rent).
> Formula: `(Net Host Revenue − Total Operating Costs) ÷ Net Host Revenue × 100`
> Target: ≥65% (well-run short-term rental)
> Below 50%: Review pricing, cleaning costs, and consumables

## SECTION 5 — INVESTMENT DECISION FRAMEWORK

Use this when deciding whether to spend money on your property.

### The 12-Month Payback Rule
Only spend on improvements that pay back within 12 months through increased revenue or reduced costs.

**Formula:**
```
Payback Period (months) = Cost of Improvement ÷ Monthly Revenue Increase
```

**Examples:**

| Improvement | Cost | Monthly Revenue Increase | Payback |
|---|---|---|---|
| Professional photography | €300 | +€150 (10% higher occupancy) | 2 months |
| Smart lock installation | €350 | +€50 (self-check-in saves hosting time) | 7 months |
| Hot tub installation | €5,000 | +€400 (rate premium + new searches) | 12.5 months |
| Air conditioning (per unit) | €1,800 | +€300 (summer bookings, higher rate) | 6 months |
| Dedicated workspace setup | €400 | +€100 (remote worker segment) | 4 months |

### Break-Even Calculator

If you're deciding whether to list at all, or evaluating a second property:

```
Monthly Revenue to Break Even = Monthly Fixed Costs ÷ (1 − Variable Cost Rate)

Where Variable Cost Rate = Total Variable Costs ÷ Total Revenue
```

**Example:**
```
Fixed Costs: €600/month (utilities + WiFi + insurance + subscriptions)
Variable Cost Rate: 22% (cleaning, laundry, consumables as % of revenue)

Break-Even Revenue = €600 ÷ (1 − 0.22) = €600 ÷ 0.78 = €769/month

At €95 ADR, that's 8.1 booked nights/month (26% occupancy).
Anything above 26% occupancy = profit.
```
