## PROPERTY EMERGENCY PROCEDURES

### GAS SMELL
1. Do NOT turn any lights or switches on or off
2. Open all windows and doors immediately
3. Leave the property immediately
4. Once outside: call the gas emergency line: [GAS EMERGENCY NUMBER]
5. Call your host: [HOST PHONE]
6. Do NOT re-enter until cleared by emergency services

### FIRE
1. Activate the nearest fire alarm if it hasn't triggered automatically
2. Leave the property immediately — take the stairs, NOT the lift
3. Call 112 / 999 / 911 immediately
4. Assemble at: [ASSEMBLY POINT — e.g., "the pavement opposite the building entrance"]
5. Call your host: [HOST PHONE]
6. Do NOT re-enter for any reason

**Fire extinguisher location:** [e.g., "Kitchen — under the sink / on the wall next to the oven"]  
**Fire blanket location:** [e.g., "Kitchen — in the top drawer"]  
**Exit route:** [e.g., "Front door → stairs on the right → building main entrance → left on [street name]"]

### FLOODING / WATER LEAK
1. Locate the main water stopcock and turn it off: [EXACT LOCATION — e.g., "under the kitchen sink, blue tap, turn clockwise to close"]
2. Call your host immediately: [HOST PHONE]
3. If water is near electrical outlets or appliances: do NOT touch — leave the area and call host

### POWER OUTAGE
1. Check if the outage is building-wide (look out the window — are neighbouring properties also dark?)

   - Property only: the fuse box is at [LOCATION]. The main switch is labelled. Try resetting any tripped breakers (they'll be in the middle position, not fully on or off).
2. Call your host: [HOST PHONE] — we'll guide you through it or send someone
