## [PROPERTY NAME] — HOUSE RULES

**Effective from your check-in date through checkout.**

---

### 1. CHECK-IN AND CHECKOUT

- **Check-in:** From [CHECK-IN TIME]. Earlier entry is not guaranteed — the property may be in use until that time.
- **Checkout:** By [CHECKOUT TIME]. Late checkout must be arranged at least 24 hours in advance and is subject to availability.
- **Late arrivals:** If arriving after [LATE ARRIVAL TIME, e.g., 10pm], please message the host in advance. All access is self-managed via smart lock — no host presence is required.

---

### 2. GUESTS

- Maximum [NUMBER] guests. This is the number declared at booking. Guests not listed on the booking are not permitted in the property.
- Parties, events, and gatherings of any kind are not permitted, including birthday gatherings that exceed the maximum guest count.
- Any undeclared additional guests are grounds for immediate termination of the stay, without refund.

---

### 3. NOISE AND NEIGHBOURS

- **Quiet hours: 10pm to 8am.** During quiet hours, all conversation, music, and TV audio must be at a level that does not disturb adjacent units or neighbours.
- The property uses a noise monitoring device (Minut) that measures decibel levels only — no audio is recorded. It is disclosed in the Airbnb listing.
- A noise warning will be sent via Airbnb message if the monitor triggers an alert. A second warning authorises the host to initiate early termination of the stay.

---

### 4. SMOKING

- No smoking anywhere inside the property, on the balcony, or within 5 metres of open windows.
- Evidence of smoking inside the property (smell, ash, marks) will result in a mandatory deep-cleaning charge of **€150**, applied via Airbnb's Resolution Centre.

---

### 5. PETS

*Select the option that applies to your property:*

**[OPTION A — No Pets:]**
No pets are permitted in the property. This applies to all animals, regardless of size or breed.

**[OPTION B — Pets on Request:]**
One pet (maximum 15kg) is permitted with prior approval. A non-refundable pet cleaning fee of €[AMOUNT] applies and will be added to the booking via the Airbnb Resolution Centre. Pets are not permitted on beds or furniture. Any pet-related damage is the guest's financial responsibility.

---

### 6. PROPERTY CARE

- Please treat the property with the same care you would your own home.
- Report any accidental damage before checkout. Guests who disclose damage are treated with respect — hidden damage discovered after checkout may result in a claim through Airbnb's Resolution Centre.
- Do not rearrange furniture. The property is set up specifically for the photographer's images and for the cleaning team's turnaround efficiency.
- Do not move or unplug the noise monitor (Minut). It is permanently installed.

---
