## THE SHORT VERSION

We trust our guests completely. These rules exist to protect the property for future guests, the neighbours who live nearby, and you (so there are no surprises).

---

## CHECK-IN & CHECK-OUT

- **Check-in:** From 3:00pm. If you arrive before 3pm, message your host — early check-in is often possible if the property is ready.
- **Check-out:** By 11:00am. Late check-out (until 12:30pm or 1pm) is available on request for a small fee — ask the evening before.
- After checkout, you are welcome to leave your luggage in the property for a few hours if you're continuing your day — just ask.

## GUESTS & VISITORS

- A maximum of **[X] guests** may sleep at the property — this is the number on your booking.
- Daytime visitors are welcome, but the total number of people in the property at any time should not exceed [X + 2].
- Please inform your host if you're expecting any visitors — this isn't about surveillance, it's so we can make sure the space is comfortable for everyone.

## SMOKING

- **Smoking is not permitted anywhere inside the property** — including the balcony/terrace if the smoke enters open windows.
- If you smoke, please do so on the street and dispose of cigarette ends responsibly.
- The smoke detector is live and will trigger if smoking occurs inside. The property will be inspected for smoking evidence at checkout, and the security deposit may be used to cover deep cleaning and airing costs.

---

## NOISE & NEIGHBOURS

- **Quiet hours: 10:00pm to 8:00am.** During quiet hours, please keep voices and music at indoor levels.
- The building has [X] other apartments/units. The walls are [description — e.g., "reasonably well insulated but not soundproof"].
- Music, TV, and conversation during the day are all completely fine — enjoy yourselves.
- We have a noise monitor in the [living area] that alerts us to sustained loud noise. This is disclosed in our Airbnb listing.

---

## PARTIES & EVENTS

- **No parties, events, or large gatherings.** This is a residential building and this rule is absolute.
- We define a "party" as any gathering that exceeds the listed maximum occupancy or generates sustained elevated noise.
- If you're planning a birthday dinner or small celebration for the listed guests only, that's completely welcome — just let us know in advance.

## RUBBISH & RECYCLING

- Please empty the kitchen and bathroom bins before checking out. Bin bags are under the kitchen sink.
- The outdoor waste bins are [location and directions].
- Please recycle where possible — recycling bins are [location]. The bins are colour-coded: [blue = paper / yellow = plastic & metal / green = glass].

## PROPERTY CARE

- Please treat the property as you would a friend's home.
- Report any damage immediately — accidents happen and we don't charge for honest accidents that are reported promptly. We do take action when damage is discovered silently at checkout.
- Please don't move furniture between rooms. If you need something rearranged, ask your host.
- Do not use the balcony railings for hanging laundry visible from the street.

---

## CHECK-OUT COURTESY (not rules — just kindness)

These aren't mandatory, but they make the cleaning process smoother and help the next guest arrive to a fresh space:

- Strip the bed and leave linen in a pile on the mattress
- Leave used towels in the bath/shower
- Wash any used dishes or place them in the dishwasher and run a cycle
- Close all windows
- Switch off lights, heating, and air conditioning

---

## SECURITY DEPOSIT

A security deposit of €[AMOUNT] is held by Airbnb for the duration of your stay and released automatically 14 days after checkout if no claim is made.

A claim may be made for:
- Property damage beyond normal wear and tear
- Missing items
- Smoking evidence in a non-smoking property
- Undisclosed pets (cleaning cost)
- Excessive cleaning required beyond the normal turnover standard

We very rarely make claims. But when we do, we document everything thoroughly with time-stamped photos.
