**Please don't suffer in silence.** We'd rather fix something now than have it affect your experience. Here's how:

1. **Message [HOST NAME]** via Airbnb — we aim to respond within 45 minutes, often sooner
2. **Call [HOST PHONE]** if it's urgent — [HOST NAME] is available [hours — e.g., 8am to 10pm]

- **WiFi not connecting:** Restart your device → try again → restart router (unplug 30 seconds)
- **Hot water slow:** Let the tap run for 15 seconds — it warms quickly after that
- **Door not unlocking:** Press the wake button first, then enter code, then confirm button
- **Heating not responding:** Check thermostat is set above current room temperature
