# MSG-03: Check-In Instructions
## Send: Day of check-in, 12:00 noon (or 2 hours before check-in time)

**Save in Airbnb as:** 🔑 Check-In Instructions  
**When to send:** Day of arrival, around noon — gives guests time to plan  
**Variables:** [GUEST NAME], [ACCESS CODE], [WIFI NAME], [WIFI PASSWORD], [HOST PHONE]

---

Hi [GUEST NAME]! Your arrival day is here 🎉

[FULL ADDRESS INCLUDING FLOOR/APARTMENT NUMBER]

**📶 WiFi**

*(The router is located [location in property: e.g., on the shelf in the living room]. If you can't connect: restart your device and try again, or restart the router — just unplug it for 30 seconds and plug it back in.)*

**📞 Need Anything?**

[HOST NAME]

---

## NOTES FOR HOST

- Test this exact sequence yourself with a family member acting as a first-time guest — identify any gaps
- Bold the access code — guests need to see it immediately and type it correctly
- The "restart router" instruction in the WiFi section prevents 80% of WiFi complaint messages
- Send at noon, not at 3pm — guests in transit need time to read it
- If guests arrive after dark, add: "There's a small light on the left side of the door — press it if the landing is dark"
