# MSG-13: 5-Star Review Response
## Post publicly on Airbnb after receiving a 5-star review

**Save in Airbnb as:** 🌟 5-Star Review Response  
**When to post:** Within 24 hours of receiving a 5-star review  
**Where:** Airbnb → Reviews → Respond

---

## BASE TEMPLATE

Thank you so much, [GUEST NAME]! [Specific acknowledgement of something they praised — e.g., "I'm so glad the morning light in the living room made such an impression — it's one of my favourite things about the apartment too."] Guests like you make hosting a genuine pleasure. Hoping [CITY] stays in your memory for a long time, and you're always welcome back. [HOST NAME]

---

## PERSONALISED VARIATIONS

### Variation 1 — Guest praised the cleanliness

Thank you so much, [GUEST NAME] — reviews like this one mean everything to us. Our cleaning team works incredibly hard, and it's wonderful to know it shows. Come back to [PROPERTY NAME] anytime — you'd be very welcome. [HOST NAME]

---

### Variation 2 — Guest praised the location

Thank you, [GUEST NAME]! [NEIGHBOURHOOD] really is something special — I'm so pleased you got to experience it. It's why I fell in love with the area too. We'd love to have you back, and next time I'll have even more local tips for you. [HOST NAME]

---

### Variation 3 — Guest praised the welcome pack / extras

Thank you so much for this lovely review, [GUEST NAME]! I'm really glad the welcome felt warm — I believe the small touches make the biggest difference. You were a brilliant guest and you're always welcome back. [HOST NAME]

---

### Variation 4 — Guest praised communication

Thank you, [GUEST NAME] — this is so kind of you to share. I always try to make sure guests feel genuinely supported throughout their stay, so it's lovely to hear it came through. You were a pleasure to host, and we'd love to welcome you again. [HOST NAME]

---

## NOTES FOR HOST

- Response length: 2–4 sentences maximum. A wall of text in a review response reads as desperate.
- Always acknowledge something specific they wrote — generic responses feel automated
- Always end with an invitation to return — this is your guest acquisition strategy
- Your response is public and visible to future guests browsing your listing — write it knowing they'll read it
- If a 5-star review mentions something you didn't actually provide (confusion with another listing): do not correct them publicly; thank them warmly regardless
- Post within 24 hours: reviews with host responses within 24 hours show better listing engagement rates
