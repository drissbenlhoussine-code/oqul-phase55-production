# MSG-11: Soft Decline Message
## Send: When declining a booking request or redirecting a guest

**Save in Airbnb as:** 🚫 Soft Decline  
**When to send:** When you need to decline a request while preserving goodwill  
**Variables:** [GUEST NAME], [REASON], [HOST NAME]

---

## VERSION A: DATES CONFLICT / PERSONAL USE

Hi [GUEST NAME],

Thank you so much for your interest in [PROPERTY NAME] — I'm sorry that I'm not able to accept this booking.

[DATES] is actually a period I have reserved for personal use, so the property isn't available for guests during that time.

I hope you find a wonderful place for your trip — if you're ever looking for those dates in a different period, I'd genuinely love to host you.

All the best,
[HOST NAME]

---

## VERSION B: GUEST COUNT EXCEEDS MAXIMUM

Hi [GUEST NAME],

Thank you for reaching out! I'd love to welcome you to [PROPERTY NAME].

However, I noticed the booking is for [# guests], and the property is set up for a maximum of [max guests] — it's a matter of comfort and space more than anything else. With more guests than the space is designed for, I couldn't guarantee you the quality stay you deserve.

If you're able to adjust the guest count to [max], I'd be delighted to confirm straight away. If the full group needs to stay together, I'm happy to suggest some alternative local properties that might work better.

All the best,
[HOST NAME]

---

## VERSION C: CONCERNS ABOUT FIT (tactful, without accusations)

Hi [GUEST NAME],

Thank you for your booking request for [PROPERTY NAME].

After reviewing the details, I don't think [PROPERTY NAME] is the right fit for your stay — it's quite a compact space and I want to make sure you have a genuinely great experience rather than feeling cramped.

I hope you find a perfect option for your trip, and I wish you a wonderful time in [CITY].

[HOST NAME]

---

## NOTES FOR HOST

- Always decline within 24 hours — leaving a request pending is worse for your acceptance rate than a prompt, polite decline
- Be genuine: if you don't want to explain the actual reason (e.g., concerning messages), use Version C
- Never say anything that could be read as discriminatory — stick to property suitability and factual limitations
- On Airbnb, when you click "Decline" you must select a reason — your message to the guest can be warmer than the dropdown reason you select
- Declining too frequently (>20% of requests) will affect your search ranking — use wisely
