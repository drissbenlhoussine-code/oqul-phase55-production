# MSG-14: Negative Review Response
## Response to Sub-5-Star Review · 4 Variations · Save as "📝 Negative Review Response"

---

**When to use:** When a guest leaves an overall rating of 4 stars or below, or leaves written criticism in a 5-star review.
**When to send:** Within 24 hours of the review being posted. Faster than your 5-star response — the longer a negative review sits without a host response, the more weight future guests give to it.
**Purpose:** Demonstrate professionalism to future guests (your actual audience), acknowledge the guest's concern, and state what action you have taken.
**Critical rule:** Your response is not for the guest who left the review. It is for every future guest who reads it. Write accordingly.

---

## VARIATION A — CLEANLINESS COMPLAINT

---

Thank you for staying at [PROPERTY NAME] and for sharing this feedback. I am sorry the cleanliness did not meet your expectations — this is the standard I hold most seriously, and this outcome is not one I accept.

I have reviewed the specific issue with the cleaning team and updated the checklist to address it. Every guest who follows you will benefit from this review.

---

## VARIATION B — ACCURACY OR LISTING DESCRIPTION COMPLAINT

---

Thank you for this feedback. I have reviewed the specific point you raised about [THE CLAIM IN QUESTION — e.g., "the proximity to the metro" / "the workspace description"] and updated the listing to reflect a more accurate description. I take accuracy seriously — guests should arrive knowing exactly what to expect.

I am sorry your experience did not match your expectations. The listing now reflects [THE CORRECTED INFORMATION].

---

## VARIATION C — COMMUNICATION OR HOST AVAILABILITY COMPLAINT

---

Thank you for raising this. I am sorry my response time during your stay was not what it should have been — [SPECIFIC REASON WITHOUT EXCUSE, e.g., "I had a gap in monitoring messages on the evening of your arrival"]. This is not a standard I defend.

I have updated my notification settings to ensure this does not affect future guests. Your feedback directly improved the experience for the next person who stays here.

---

## VARIATION D — GENERAL 3-STAR OR MIXED REVIEW (No Specific Complaint)

Use when a guest leaves 3–4 stars with positive and negative elements, or a vague complaint.

---

Thank you for your stay at [PROPERTY NAME] and for taking the time to review. I am glad [THE POSITIVE ELEMENTS THEY MENTIONED] worked well for you.

[If a specific concern was raised:] I have noted [THE SPECIFIC ISSUE] and will address it before our next guests arrive. [If no specific concern:] I take mixed reviews seriously and will use your feedback to review each aspect of the stay against my standards.

I hope to have the opportunity to host you again and show you what this property looks like when everything runs as it should.

---

## PERSONALISATION VARIABLES

- [PROPERTY NAME]
- [THE CLAIM IN QUESTION] — exact phrase from their review
- [THE CORRECTED INFORMATION] — what the listing now says
- [SPECIFIC REASON] — what actually happened
- [THE POSITIVE ELEMENTS THEY MENTIONED] — quote from their review
- [THE SPECIFIC ISSUE] — exact issue they raised

---

## THE RULES OF NEGATIVE REVIEW RESPONSES

**What to always do:**
1. Thank the guest for their stay (not for the review)
2. Name the specific issue (do not be vague)
3. State the action you took in the past tense ("I have updated / I have reviewed / I have addressed")
4. Write for future guests, not to win the argument

**What to never do:**
1. Argue with the guest's experience — "Actually, the WiFi was working" is not a productive response
2. Blame the guest — even when they are partly responsible, the response is not the place to say so
3. Post a defensive wall of text — anything more than 4 sentences reads as agitated
4. Mention the star rating in your response — "I was disappointed with your 3 stars" is irrelevant to future guests and makes you look fragile
5. Compare this guest to better guests — "Most guests love this property" is condescending

**What matters:**
A well-handled negative review response converts future bookings. Guests who read "I've updated the listing to reflect this accurately and addressed it with the cleaning team" feel confidence in the host. They book. Guests who read a host defending themselves and blaming the guest do not.

---

## WHEN A REVIEW IS DISHONEST OR VIOLATES AIRBNB POLICIES

If a review contains factually false statements, discriminatory content, or violates Airbnb's review policy:
1. Do not respond publicly with a dispute
2. Report the review to Airbnb via: Airbnb.com → Profile → Reviews → [REVIEW] → Flag
3. Provide evidence (photos, messages) that contradict the false claim
4. Airbnb reviews flagged reviews within 72 hours

Airbnb rarely removes reviews based on host disagreement alone — they remove reviews for policy violations (personal insults, false factual claims, coerced reviews). If your flag is rejected, write a professional response using Variation D and move on.
