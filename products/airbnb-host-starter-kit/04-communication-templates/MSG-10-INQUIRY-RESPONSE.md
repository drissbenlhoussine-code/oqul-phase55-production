# MSG-10: Inquiry Response
## Pre-Booking Inquiry Reply · Save as "💬 Inquiry Response"

---

**When to send:** Within 60 minutes of receiving an inquiry. Response rate is tracked by Airbnb from the moment the inquiry arrives — long delays affect both your response rate metric and the guest's likelihood of booking elsewhere.
**Purpose:** Answer the guest's specific question, confirm availability, and move toward a booking confirmation.

---

## TEMPLATE — STANDARD INQUIRY

---

Hi [GUEST NAME],

Thanks for your interest in [PROPERTY NAME].

[ANSWER THE SPECIFIC QUESTION(S) FROM THEIR INQUIRY — directly and completely. If they asked about parking: answer about parking. If they asked about the kitchen: describe it specifically. Do not give a generic response.]

To confirm availability for your dates ([CHECK-IN DATE] to [CHECK-OUT DATE]): [YES — the property is available / LET ME CHECK — I'll confirm within the hour].

Is there anything else you need to know before booking? I'm happy to answer any questions.

[HOST NAME]

---

## TEMPLATE — INQUIRY WITH SPECIAL REQUEST

When the guest has asked about something outside your standard terms (early check-in, extra guests, pets):

---

Hi [GUEST NAME],

Thank you for asking in advance — I appreciate the transparency.

[RE: EARLY CHECK-IN]: My standard check-in time is [TIME]. I can offer early check-in from [EARLIER TIME] on [DATE] [FOR €X / at no charge if the property is free from the previous night]. Would that work?

[RE: EXTRA GUESTS]: The property is set up for [MAX GUESTS] guests. If you're looking to host more, I'm afraid I'm not able to accommodate that under the current booking — the maximum is non-negotiable.

[RE: PETS]: My policy is [NO PETS / ONE SMALL DOG UNDER 15kg IS WELCOME — there is a €[AMOUNT] non-refundable cleaning fee which I'll add to the booking].

Happy to confirm the booking once we've aligned on these details.

[HOST NAME]

---

## TEMPLATE — SHORT INQUIRY (Guest Only Asked About Availability)

When the guest asked only "Is this available?" or made a very brief inquiry:

---

Hi [GUEST NAME],

Yes, [PROPERTY NAME] is available for [DATES]. Happy to confirm the booking whenever you're ready.

If you have any questions about the property before booking, just ask — I'm quick to respond.

[HOST NAME]

---

## NOTES ON INQUIRY RESPONSE

**Answer the actual question.** The most common response mistake is sending a generic "thanks for your inquiry, hope to have you stay" without addressing what was asked. If a guest asked about the lift in the building and you reply without mentioning it, they assume there is no lift and may not book.

**Response time:** The Airbnb app shows hosts' typical response time on their listing profile. A 30-minute typical response time converts significantly better than "within a few hours." Aim to respond to all inquiries within 60 minutes during waking hours.

**If the inquiry is from a guest you would not accept (red flags from SOP-02):** Do not ignore — respond with a soft decline (MSG-11) within the hour. Ignoring inquiries damages your response rate.
