# MSG-10: Pre-Booking Inquiry Response
## Send: Within 60 minutes of any guest inquiry

**Save in Airbnb as:** 💬 Inquiry Response  
**When to send:** Immediately upon receiving any pre-booking inquiry (not yet booked)  
**Variables:** [GUEST NAME], [PROPERTY NAME], [DATES], [HOST NAME]

---

## STANDARD INQUIRY RESPONSE

Hi [GUEST NAME],

Thank you for reaching out about [PROPERTY NAME] for [DATES] — I'd love to have you!

[Answer their specific question here — address exactly what they asked before anything else]

A few things that might be helpful to know:
- Check-in is from 3pm; checkout by 11am (late checkout often available on request)
- The property comfortably sleeps [max guests] — it's [brief description: e.g., "perfect for a couple or two close friends"]
- [One specific thing about the location that matches what they mentioned: e.g., "The festival you mentioned is a 15-minute walk away"]

If everything looks good, feel free to book through Airbnb and I'll send you all the details straight away. If you have any other questions, I'm happy to answer them here first.

Looking forward to welcoming you!

[HOST NAME]

---

## NOTES FOR HOST

- Always answer their actual question in the first paragraph — hosts who reply with generic information without addressing the question feel dismissive
- This response should be sent within 60 minutes — Airbnb measures your response rate from inquiry to response
- If you cannot accept the booking (dates unavailable, guest doesn't meet criteria): still respond helpfully within 60 minutes (see MSG-11 for soft decline)
- Your response rate counts even for inquiries you decline — always respond
- If the inquiry is vague ("are your dates available?") — confirm availability AND pre-qualify: "Yes, those dates are available! Could you confirm how many guests will be joining? The property is set up for a maximum of [X]."

---

## WHEN AN INQUIRY ASKS SOMETHING YOU'RE UNSURE ABOUT

Always answer immediately, even if the answer is "Let me check and come back to you within 2 hours."

Hi [GUEST NAME],

Great question — let me confirm that for you. I'll come back to you within the next [2 hours / this evening] with a definitive answer.

[HOST NAME]

*Then follow up within that timeframe — no exceptions. A guest who waits more than 2 hours without an update has likely booked elsewhere.*
