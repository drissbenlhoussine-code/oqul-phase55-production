# MSG-13: Five-Star Review Response
## Response to 5-Star Review · 4 Variations · Save as "🌟 5-Star Review Response"

---

**When to use:** When a guest leaves an overall rating of 5 stars.
**When to send:** Within 48 hours of the review being posted (best within 24 hours).
**Why it matters:** Your response is public. Future guests read it. A warm, specific response signals an engaged host. A generic "thank you for your review!" response signals an absent one.
**Rule:** Reference something specific from their written review if they wrote one. Generic responses are worse than no response.

---

## VARIATION 1 — STANDARD 5-STAR (No Written Review or Generic Written Review)

---

Thank you, [GUEST NAME] — really glad the stay worked well. [PROPERTY NAME] is at its best when guests enjoy it as much as you did. Hope to have you back the next time you're in [CITY].

---

## VARIATION 2 — GUEST MENTIONED A SPECIFIC FEATURE OR EXPERIENCE

Use when the guest praised something specific (the neighbourhood, the coffee, the view, the check-in, etc.).

---

Thank you, [GUEST NAME] — so glad [THE SPECIFIC THING THEY MENTIONED, e.g., "the rooftop" / "the morning light in the bedroom" / "the local recommendations"] stood out. That is exactly what [PROPERTY NAME] is about. Hope to welcome you back soon.

---

## VARIATION 3 — GUEST MENTIONED THEY WANT TO RETURN

---

Thank you, [GUEST NAME] — that means a lot. [PROPERTY NAME] will be here when you're ready to come back. Just message ahead and I'll make sure the timing works.

---

## VARIATION 4 — BUSINESS OR REMOTE WORK GUEST

Use when the review mentions work, remote working, or a business trip.

---

Thank you, [GUEST NAME] — great to know [PROPERTY NAME] worked well as a base for your time in [CITY]. The workspace is something I've invested in specifically for guests like you. Hope the [work / project / trip] went well — you are welcome back anytime.

---

## PERSONALISATION VARIABLES

- [GUEST NAME]
- [PROPERTY NAME]
- [CITY]
- [THE SPECIFIC THING THEY MENTIONED] — pull directly from their written review

---

## RESPONSE LENGTH NOTE

Keep all review responses to 2–4 sentences. Longer responses read as marketing copy and dilute the sincerity. The purpose is to acknowledge the guest, not to sell the property to future readers.

Future guests who read your responses are not looking for enthusiasm — they are checking whether you are present, engaged, and professional. Two thoughtful sentences accomplish this better than a paragraph.

---

## WHAT NOT TO SAY IN REVIEW RESPONSES

- "We" when you are a solo host (unless you genuinely have a team — "we" sounds impersonal when false)
- "Amazing!" "Wonderful!" "Fantastic!" — overuse of superlatives reads as hollow
- Guest's home country or workplace in a way that feels like you are profiling
- Any reference to the score: "Thank you for your 5 stars" sounds transactional
