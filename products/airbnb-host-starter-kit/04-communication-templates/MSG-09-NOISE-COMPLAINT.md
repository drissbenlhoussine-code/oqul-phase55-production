# MSG-09: Noise Complaint / Rule Violation
## Send: When noise alert triggers or rule violation suspected

**Save in Airbnb as:** 🔇 Noise Complaint  
**When to send:** When noise monitor triggers quiet hours alert or when a neighbour reports noise

---

## TIER 1 — GENTLE FIRST MESSAGE (use this first, always)

Hi [GUEST NAME],

Hope you're having a brilliant evening! I just wanted to drop you a quick note — we've had a noise concern from the area, so I wanted to check in and make sure everything's okay with you.

Our quiet hours are 10pm to 8am, so if you're planning a late night, just being mindful of the neighbours would be really appreciated. I know it's easy to lose track of volume when you're having fun!

Enjoy the rest of your evening, and sorry to interrupt.

[HOST NAME]

---

## TIER 2 — CLEAR REQUEST (use if Tier 1 gets no response or issue continues)

Hi [GUEST NAME],

I'm getting another noise concern from the property — I need to ask you directly to please reduce the noise level now. Our quiet hours policy runs from 10pm to 8am and this is a firm house rule.

I appreciate that you may be celebrating something, and I don't want to spoil your evening — but I do need this addressed immediately.

Please confirm you've received this message.

Thank you,
[HOST NAME]

---

## TIER 3 — FINAL WARNING (use only if Tier 2 produces no response or escalation)

Hi [GUEST NAME],

I've sent two messages tonight regarding noise levels at [PROPERTY NAME] that remain unresolved. This is a serious breach of our house rules and I'm required to take the next step.

If the noise does not stop immediately, I will contact Airbnb's Trust & Safety team and ask them to intervene. This could result in the cancellation of your booking without a refund.

Please respond to confirm you've received this message and that the noise has stopped.

[HOST NAME]

---

## NOTES FOR HOST

- Always start with Tier 1, regardless of how severe the alert appears — false positives happen
- Do NOT mention the noise monitor by name in your message — state "we've had a noise concern" instead
- Document all noise events in your Maintenance Log (Tab-05) with timestamp and alert level
- If you suspect a party (many people, sustained noise over 80dB): skip to Tier 2 immediately
- If Tier 3 is required: contact Airbnb Trust & Safety via the Resolution Centre immediately — do not wait
- A host-cancelled reservation for rule violation is not penalised by Airbnb if properly documented
- Retain all noise monitor data (Minut/NoiseAware provide logs) — this is your evidence if a claim is needed

## NEIGHBOUR COMPLAINT HANDLING

If a neighbour complains directly to you (not via Airbnb):  
1. Acknowledge the complaint genuinely  
2. Contact the guest immediately (Tier 1 or 2 depending on severity)  
3. Follow up with the neighbour after resolution: "I've spoken with my guests and it's resolved — thank you for letting me know"  
4. Long-term: consider a note to your neighbours with your contact number so they always come to you before escalating
