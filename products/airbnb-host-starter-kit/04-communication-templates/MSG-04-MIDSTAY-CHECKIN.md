# MSG-04: Mid-Stay Check-In
## Send: Day 2 of guest stay, 10:00–11:00am

**Save in Airbnb as:** 👋 Mid-Stay Check-In  
**When to send:** Morning of Day 2 of every stay  
**Variables:** [GUEST NAME], [HOST NAME]

---

## MESSAGE

Hi [GUEST NAME],

Hope you had a great first night! Just checking in to make sure everything is perfect for you.

Is there anything you need or anything I can help with? If something isn't quite right, please just let me know and I'll sort it out straightaway — I'd much rather fix something now than have it affect your stay.

Enjoy the rest of your trip!

[HOST NAME]

---

## WHY THIS MESSAGE MATTERS

This is the single most important message you will send. It:

1. **Surfaces problems while you can still fix them** — a guest who has a small issue but doesn't tell you will go home and write a 3-star review. A guest who tells you gets it fixed and writes a 5-star review about your responsiveness.

2. **Signals that you're attentive** — guests feel cared for. This translates directly to the Communication rating.

3. **Creates the opening for upsells** — a guest who replies positively might be receptive to: "If you'd like a late checkout, I can offer 12:30pm for €15 — just let me know!"

**The message is deliberately short.** A long message at Day 2 feels performative. Three sentences gets maximum response rates.

---

## RESPONSE HANDLING

If they reply "All great, thank you!" → No further action. Note "positive mid-stay" in booking log.

If they raise any issue → Execute SOP-06 Step 2 immediately.

If no response → Do not follow up. Some guests prefer minimal contact. They still appreciate that you asked.
