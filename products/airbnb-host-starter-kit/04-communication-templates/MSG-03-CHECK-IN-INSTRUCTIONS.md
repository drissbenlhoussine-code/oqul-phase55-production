# MSG-03: Check-In Instructions
## Day-of Check-In Message · Save as "🔑 Check-In Instructions"

---

**When to send:** Morning of check-in (7am–9am). Earlier than 7am goes unread. Later than 11am risks the guest arriving before they have the code.
**Purpose:** Deliver the exact access sequence required to enter the property without contacting you.
**Standard:** A guest who has never been to the property must be inside within 5 minutes using only this message.

---

## TEMPLATE

---

Hi [GUEST NAME],

Your stay at [PROPERTY NAME] starts today — here is your complete check-in guide.

**Check-in time:** From [CHECK-IN TIME]

---

**GETTING THERE**

[PROPERTY NAME] is at: [FULL ADDRESS]

Coming from [MAIN CITY CENTRE / TRAIN STATION]: [SPECIFIC DIRECTIONS — e.g., "Take Bus 28 towards Martim Moniz. Exit at Rua do Século. Walk 50m uphill — the building is on your right, dark green door, number 14."]

---

**ENTERING THE BUILDING**

[IF BUILDING HAS ENTRY CODE: "Press [CODE] on the keypad at the main door, then push immediately — the lock releases for 4 seconds."]
[IF NO BUILDING ENTRY CODE: "The building door is open during the day. Ring buzzer [NUMBER/NAME] if it is closed."]

---

**ENTERING THE APARTMENT**

Floor [FLOOR NUMBER], door [APARTMENT NUMBER/LETTER].

Your door code is: **[ACCESS CODE]**

To open:
1. Enter [ACCESS CODE] on the keypad
2. Press the ✓ (check/confirm) button
3. Wait for one beep and a green light
4. Push the door to open

Note: Do not try the handle before the beep — wait for the green light first.

---

**INSIDE**

WiFi: **[WIFI NETWORK NAME]**
Password: **[WIFI PASSWORD]**

You will find a welcome card on the [kitchen table / entrance shelf] with appliance guides and local recommendations.

Check-out is [CHECKOUT TIME] on [CHECKOUT DATE].

---

**IF ANYTHING DOESN'T WORK**

Call me: [HOST PHONE NUMBER]

If I do not answer within 5 minutes, use the backup key:
There is a small [COLOUR] key box [DESCRIPTION OF LOCATION: e.g., "on the right side of the building entrance, at chest height"]. Code: [LOCKBOX CODE]. Turn each number, press the release. Return the key to the box before you leave.

---

Have a wonderful stay.

[HOST NAME]

## NOTES ON FORMAT

Bold the access code, WiFi network, and WiFi password. These are the three pieces of information the guest needs immediately on arrival — they should be scannable.

The numbered list for door entry is deliberate. Smart locks are unfamiliar to many guests; a list is faster to follow under stress than a paragraph.

**Do not shorten this message.** The length reflects the complexity of the task (entry into a building and apartment the guest has never seen). Cutting information to appear concise creates check-in failures.
