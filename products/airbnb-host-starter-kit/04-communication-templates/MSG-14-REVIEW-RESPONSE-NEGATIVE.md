# MSG-14: Negative Review Response
## Post publicly on Airbnb after receiving a 3-star or below review

**Save in Airbnb as:** 📝 Negative Review Response  
**When to post:** Within 24 hours of receiving a negative review  
**Tone: Professional. Factual. Solution-focused. NEVER defensive, NEVER accusatory.**

---

## THE GOLDEN RULE

Your response to a negative review is NOT for the reviewer. It is for every future guest who reads your listing. Write accordingly.

A well-handled negative response can actually increase bookings. A defensive or emotional response kills them.

---

## TEMPLATE — MAINTENANCE/COMFORT ISSUE

Thank you for your feedback, [GUEST NAME]. I'm genuinely sorry that [specific issue] affected your stay — you're absolutely right that this should have been better, and I've already [specific action taken: e.g., "had the boiler serviced" / "upgraded the mattress" / "added clearer WiFi instructions"]. I hold my properties to a high standard and I take any gap in that seriously. I hope you'll give us another chance in the future. [HOST NAME]

---

## TEMPLATE — CLEANLINESS CONCERN

Thank you for sharing this, [GUEST NAME]. Cleanliness is a top priority for us, and I'm sorry we fell short on this occasion. I've reviewed this with my cleaning team and [specific change: e.g., "added a dedicated drain inspection to the turnover checklist"]. This isn't the standard we set or maintain, and I'm grateful for the feedback that helps me keep improving. [HOST NAME]

---

## TEMPLATE — ACCURACY CONCERN (listing vs. reality)

Thank you for your honest feedback, [GUEST NAME]. I've reviewed your comments and [either: "I've updated the listing description to make [item] clearer for future guests" OR "I'm sorry if [item] felt different in person — [brief factual clarification if genuinely needed]"]. Accuracy is something I take seriously, and I appreciate you raising this. [HOST NAME]

---

## TEMPLATE — GUEST MADE UNREASONABLE CLAIMS (handle very carefully)

Thank you for sharing your experience, [GUEST NAME]. I'm sorry the stay didn't meet your expectations. To give future guests the full picture: [one calm, factual sentence that provides context — e.g., "Our check-in instructions, which are sent 48 hours in advance and on the morning of arrival, include a step-by-step entry guide" OR "The WiFi speed at the property tests at 185Mbps and is included in the listing details"]. We welcome guests of all kinds and always aim to deliver a genuinely great experience. [HOST NAME]

---

## WHAT NOT TO DO (review response errors that damage your listing)

❌ "This guest left the property in a terrible state..."
❌ "I'm shocked by this review given that..."
❌ "This is completely unfair — we went above and beyond..."
❌ "Other guests have never complained about this..."
❌ Making personal observations about the guest's behaviour
❌ Writing more than 100 words in a response

All of the above make future guests nervous. They suggest you handle problems with blame, not solutions.

---

## PROCESS AFTER A NEGATIVE REVIEW

1. Wait 24 hours before writing the response — do not write angry
2. Write the draft, then read it as if you're a future guest seeing it for the first time
3. Ask: "Does this response make me more or less likely to book this property?"
4. Edit until the answer is "more likely"
5. Post within 24 hours
6. Add the issue to your SOP-10 Monthly Review — identify root cause
7. Make the improvement, then reference it in your response if appropriate ("I've already actioned this")
