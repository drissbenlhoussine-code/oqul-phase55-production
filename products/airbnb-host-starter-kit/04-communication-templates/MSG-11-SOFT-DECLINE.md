# MSG-11: Soft Decline
## Decline — 3 Versions · Save as "🚫 Soft Decline"

---

**When to send:** Within 60 minutes of receiving the inquiry or booking request. Never leave a decline request unanswered — guests can leave a review even after a declined booking, and ignoring reads as hostile.
**Purpose:** Decline professionally without creating conflict or implying discrimination.
**Rule:** Decline on booking signals and property fit, not on personal characteristics.

---

## VERSION A — DATES NOT AVAILABLE (Use When Honest)

Use when you have a genuine reason the dates do not work — maintenance, personal use, or legitimate overlap.

---

Hi [GUEST NAME],

Thank you for your interest in [PROPERTY NAME]. Unfortunately I'm not able to accept this booking — I have some maintenance scheduled during that period that I hadn't yet blocked on the calendar. I'm sorry for the inconvenience.

I hope you find a great place for your stay.

[HOST NAME]

---

## VERSION B — GUEST COUNT OR HOUSE RULES MISMATCH

Use when the booking or inquiry requests something you cannot accommodate.

---

Hi [GUEST NAME],

Thank you for reaching out. [PROPERTY NAME] has a maximum of [NUMBER] guests, and [the dates you've requested fall below my minimum stay / your group size exceeds the property capacity / the booking request includes something outside my house rules].

I'm not able to accommodate this booking, but I hope you find the right place for your group.

[HOST NAME]

---

## VERSION C — GENERAL DECLINE (No Specific Reason Required)

Use when a guest scores in the Red zone from SOP-02 but you cannot cite a specific, articulable reason.

---

Hi [GUEST NAME],

Thank you for your interest in [PROPERTY NAME]. After reviewing the booking details, I'm not able to accept this request at this time.

I hope you find a great place for your stay.

[HOST NAME]

---

## WHEN TO USE EACH VERSION

| Situation | Use Version |
|---|---|
| Genuine scheduling conflict | A |
| Group too large | B |
| Below minimum stay | B |
| Party indicators in message | B (cite house rules) |
| No profile, 0 reviews, concerning message | C |
| Gut feeling with no specific articulable reason | C |
| Previously caused damage (visible on profile) | C |

---

## WHAT NOT TO SAY IN A DECLINE

**Never mention:**
- The guest's nationality, age, religion, or any protected characteristic
- Anything that could be read as discriminatory
- A reason that is obviously pretextual (changing your story between declines)

**Decline on:**
- Booking criteria (dates, guest count, minimum stay)
- Conduct signals visible on their profile (prior host complaints)
- Property fit (your maximum capacity, your minimum stay, your house rules)

Airbnb's non-discrimination policy applies to all declines. A decline based on discriminatory grounds can result in listing removal. The three versions above are designed to be enforceable and non-discriminatory.

---

## AFTER DECLINING

Immediately block the dates if you used Version A (maintenance or scheduling). Airbnb tracks cancellation and decline patterns — declines that are followed by immediate bookings of other guests on the same dates may be flagged.

If you use Version C frequently: review your Instant Book settings and screening requirements in SOP-02. If you are declining more than 10% of booking requests, your Instant Book requirements or listing clarity may need adjustment.
