# MSG-09: Noise Warning
## Rule Violation — 3 Tiers · Save as "🔇 Noise Warning"

---

**When to send:** Triggered by a Minut/NoiseAware alert exceeding the threshold set in SOP-06 Section 3.
**Purpose:** Communicate a noise violation clearly without being hostile. Three escalating tiers.
**Response time:** Send within 5 minutes of receiving the alert — delayed noise warnings are ignored.

---

## TIER 1 — FIRST OCCURRENCE (Reasonable Hours)

Use when: first noise alert of the stay, before 10pm.

---

Hi [GUEST NAME],

I've been alerted to higher sound levels at [PROPERTY NAME] tonight. This is likely nothing — but as a heads-up, we have agreed quiet hours from 10pm to protect the neighbours, and the monitor flagged an alert.

If you're hosting guests, please note that the maximum registered guest count for the property is [MAXIMUM GUESTS], and this is reflected in the booking.

Enjoy your evening — thanks for being mindful.

[HOST NAME]

---

## TIER 2 — SECOND OCCURRENCE (Any Time) OR FIRST OCCURRENCE DURING QUIET HOURS (After 10pm)

Use when: second alert during the same stay, OR first alert after quiet hours begin.

---

Hi [GUEST NAME],

I've received a second noise alert from [PROPERTY NAME] tonight. Under the house rules you agreed to at booking, quiet hours begin at 10pm. I need to ask that noise levels are reduced immediately.

If I receive another alert this evening, I will need to take further action under Airbnb's policies.

[HOST NAME]

---

## TIER 3 — THIRD OCCURRENCE (Enforcement)

Use when: third alert during the same stay or severe violation (confirmed reports from neighbours).

Before sending Tier 3: Call Airbnb Support to document the escalation and confirm the procedure for your jurisdiction.

---

Hi [GUEST NAME],

I have now received multiple noise alerts during your stay, following warnings sent earlier this evening.

Under the house rules agreed at booking and Airbnb's policies on property use, I am initiating an early termination of this booking. You will need to vacate the property by [TIME — minimum 30 minutes from now].

Refund: Airbnb will process a refund for unused nights automatically.

If you need assistance finding alternative accommodation tonight, please respond here and I will help.

[HOST NAME]

---

## POST-TIER 3 PROCESS

After sending Tier 3:
1. Call Airbnb Support immediately. Tell them you have sent a Tier 3 noise warning and need to process an early termination. They will guide you through the platform process.
2. Do not physically confront guests. Your role is to communicate; Airbnb's process and (if required) local authorities handle enforcement.
3. Document everything: timestamps of all alerts, timestamps of all messages, any photos.
4. In your review of the guest: note the noise violations factually. "This stay required two noise warnings during quiet hours" is honest and protects future hosts.

---

## NOTES ON TONE

**Tier 1 tone:** Friendly but clear. Not apologetic.
**Tier 2 tone:** Firm. No hedging. One sentence per instruction.
**Tier 3 tone:** Formal and procedural. This is a legal communication. Do not use casual language.

The three tiers are a graduated response — they give the guest a clear chance to correct before escalation. Document that you sent each tier; this record supports you if the guest disputes in a future review.
