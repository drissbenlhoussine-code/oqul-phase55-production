# MSG-04: Mid-Stay Check-In
## Day 2 Welfare Message · Save as "👋 Mid-Stay Check-In"

---

**When to send:** Day 2 of the stay (24–36 hours after check-in). For 1-night stays: 3 hours after check-in.
**Purpose:** Give the guest an easy way to report any issue while you still have time to fix it. This message prevents review complaints about problems you never knew existed.
**Length:** Short. This is a check-in, not a conversation.

---

## TEMPLATE — STANDARD VERSION

---

Hi [GUEST NAME],

Hope you're settling in well at [PROPERTY NAME]. Just checking everything is working as it should — WiFi, hot water, heating, and so on.

If there is anything you need or anything that is not quite right, just reply here and I'll sort it out.

Enjoy [CITY / THE REST OF YOUR STAY].

[HOST NAME]

---

## TEMPLATE — LONG STAY VERSION (7+ nights)

---

Hi [GUEST NAME],

You're a few days into your stay at [PROPERTY NAME] — how is everything going?

If anything has come up — appliances, supplies running low, anything at all — just reply here. I can usually respond within an hour.

I can also arrange a mid-stay linen refresh if you'd like fresh towels and bedding — just let me know a convenient time.

Enjoy the rest of your stay.

[HOST NAME]

---

## TEMPLATE — SPECIAL OCCASION VERSION

Use if the guest noted a birthday, anniversary, or honeymoon at booking:

---

Hi [GUEST NAME],

Hope you're having a wonderful [anniversary / birthday / honeymoon] at [PROPERTY NAME]. Just checking in to make sure everything is working well.

If there is anything I can do to make the stay more special, don't hesitate to ask.

[HOST NAME]

---

## PERSONALISATION VARIABLES TO REPLACE BEFORE SAVING

- [GUEST NAME]
- [PROPERTY NAME]
- [CITY] — e.g., "Lisbon" or "the rest of your stay" if city name feels awkward
- [HOST NAME]

For the long-stay and special occasion versions: these are custom sends, not Airbnb Saved Messages. Personalise at send time.

---

## NOTES ON HANDLING RESPONSES

**"Everything is great!"** or similar: Reply with one sentence — "Great to hear — enjoy the rest of your stay." Nothing more. The conversation is complete.

**Guest raises an issue:** Move immediately to MSG-08 (Issue Response) protocol. Acknowledge within 30 minutes. Resolve within the timeframe from SOP-06 Section 4 triage matrix.

**No response:** Do not follow up. Guest is fine. The message served its purpose (it shows Airbnb's system that you made contact, which is tracked in Communication scores).
