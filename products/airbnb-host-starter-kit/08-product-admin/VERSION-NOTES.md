### Design Decisions in v1.0.0

**European market focus:** Financial projections, tax guides, and market context are calibrated for mid-tier European markets (Lisbon, Porto, Prague, Edinburgh, Ghent, Amsterdam, Barcelona). North American and Australian hosts will need to adjust multipliers, tax sections, and some regulatory references.

**1-bedroom apartment baseline:** All financial models use a 1-bedroom apartment as the baseline. Hosts with larger properties scale up the revenue and cost figures proportionally.

**Anti-AI Filter applied throughout:** All content was written to the standard: Direct. Specific. Earned. No hollow intensifiers, no vague promises, no passive voice.

**Bonus asset rationale:** The Superhost 90-Day Sprint was identified as the differentiating asset at this price point. No competing product at €49–€149 provides a week-by-week execution plan from launch to Superhost. It converts the kit from reference material into active coaching.
