# Consultation & Booking Scripts

## DM INQUIRY RESPONSE
"Hey [Name]! Thanks for reaching out. I'd love to help bring your vision to life. Can you tell me a bit more about what you have in mind — style, size, placement, and any reference images you have? That'll help me give you an accurate timeline and quote. 🖤"

## PHONE/EMAIL CONSULTATION
"Hi [Name], thanks for getting in touch. Before we set up a consultation, can I ask a few questions? What style are you drawn to? Where on the body? Do you have any references saved? And is this your first tattoo or do you have existing work we'd need to consider?"

## DEPOSIT & BOOKING SCRIPT
"[Name], I'd love to lock in your appointment. To hold your slot, I require a [deposit amount] non-refundable deposit — this goes toward your session total. Rescheduling is fine with 48 hours notice, but the deposit is forfeit for no-shows. Once the deposit is received, I'll send over your booking confirmation and our pre-tattoo care guide. Ready to move forward?"

## QUOTE FOLLOW-UP
"Hi [Name], following up on the quote I sent [X] days ago. I'm happy to answer any questions or talk through design direction before you commit. My books fill quickly and I want to make sure you get the date that works best for you. Let me know!"

## WAITLIST MESSAGE
"Hi [Name]! I'm currently booked out [X] weeks, but I'd love to add you to my waitlist. I'll reach out as soon as a cancellation opens or when I open my next booking window. Want me to add you?"
