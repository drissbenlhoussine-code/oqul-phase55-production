# Tattoo Studio Local Marketing Playbook

## BUILDING YOUR REPUTATION IN YOUR CITY

### Instagram as Primary Platform
- Post daily (portfolio, BTS, education, polls)
- Use location tags on every post
- Collaborate with local photographers for high-quality portfolio shots
- Run flash sales and limited availability announcements
- Stories: daily — process, studio life, client reactions

### Google My Business
- Upload portfolio photos regularly
- Post weekly: new work, promotions, booking announcements
- Respond to every review — good and bad
- Optimize for: "[city] tattoo," "[city] tattoo artist," "[style] tattoo [city]"

### Local Collaborations
- Other artists: collaborate on joint flash events
- Barbers: share client bases (often overlap)
- Clothing boutiques: cross-promotions and in-store pop-ups
- Photographers: styled shoots featuring tattooed subjects
- Local brands: sponsored collaborations if brand aligns

### Flash Events & Pop-Ups
- Host quarterly flash events in studio
- Participate in local art markets and tattoo conventions
- Partner with bars or music venues for special events
- Charity flash days (portion to local cause)

### Community Presence
- Sponsor local music or art events
- Donate gift certificates for charity auctions
- Be active in local arts community
