# Tattoo Business Systems Guide

## RUNNING A PROFESSIONAL STUDIO

### Client Flow System
1. Inquiry received → respond within 24 hours
2. Consultation (in person or digital)
3. Design approval → deposit collected
4. Booking confirmed → pre-care instructions sent
5. Day of session → full professional experience
6. Post-session → aftercare guide sent immediately
7. Day 5 check-in text
8. 2-week review request
9. 30-day touch-up offer

### Deposit Policy
- Non-refundable on no-show or cancellation under 48 hours
- Transferable once to a new date with 48+ hours notice
- Applied to final session total
- Amount: $[X] per hour of estimated session time, or flat $[X]

### Booking Management
- Use a dedicated booking system (Vagaro, Square Appointments, Booksy)
- Block out time for breaks, setup, and cleanup
- Build in design time before session start
- Never overbook — one artist, one client per time slot

### Portfolio Strategy
- Photograph every finished piece before client leaves
- Capture healed work at 4–6 week follow-up
- Organize portfolio by style and size
- Feature only your best 20–30% of work publicly

### Financial KPIs
- Revenue per session
- Average hourly rate
- Deposit to appointment conversion rate
- Client return rate (% who book again)
- Referral rate (% of new clients from referrals)
