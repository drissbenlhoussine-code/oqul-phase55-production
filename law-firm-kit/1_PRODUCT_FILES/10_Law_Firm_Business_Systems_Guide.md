# Law Firm Business Systems Guide

## BUILDING A REFERRAL-DRIVEN PRACTICE

### Client Experience Standards
Every client should feel:
- **Heard** — We listen fully before we advise
- **Informed** — We explain in plain language at every step
- **Respected** — We respond promptly and treat every matter as important
- **Guided** — We tell them what to expect, not just what we're doing

### Response Time Standards
- New inquiry: respond within 2 business hours
- Client calls/emails: respond within 1 business day
- Urgent matters: same day

### Matter Management System
1. Intake → conflict check → assign to attorney
2. Initial consultation → engagement letter signed
3. Matter opened in case management system
4. Milestones tracked with client notifications at each
5. Matter closed → final billing → follow-up email → review request
6. Annual outreach for estate/business clients

### Referral Tracking
- Log every referral source in your CRM
- Track revenue generated by referral source
- Send thank you within 24 hours of receiving referral
- Update referral partner on matter conclusion (within ethical bounds)

### Financial KPIs
- New client intake per month
- Consultation-to-retention conversion rate
- Average matter value
- Revenue per attorney
- Realization rate (billed vs. collected)
- Client satisfaction score (post-matter survey)
