Day 1: The most expensive legal mistake is waiting until you need a lawyer to find one. Relationships built before crises are the most valuable.
Day 2: Legal tip: every adult should have four documents: a will, a healthcare directive, a durable power of attorney, and a financial power of attorney. Do you have all four?
Day 3: 5-star review: "[Firm] handled our family matter with professionalism and genuine compassion. We felt guided, not just represented." — [Client]
Day 4: There's a reason lawyers specialize. You wouldn't see a cardiologist for a broken arm. Make sure the attorney you hire actually practices in your area of need.
Day 5: The law is always changing. An estate plan from 10 years ago may not reflect today's laws — or your current life situation.
Day 6: Poll: Do you have an updated will or estate plan? ✅ Yes, current | 📅 Yes, but outdated | 🤷 No, meaning to | ❌ Don't have one
Day 7: Your legal rights don't protect themselves. Knowing them is step one. Having representation to enforce them is step two.
