# Email & Nurture Templates

## WELCOME EMAIL (after retention)
Subject: Welcome to [Firm Name], [Name]

Dear [Name],

We are pleased to welcome you as a client of [Firm Name]. We take our responsibility to represent you seriously and want to ensure our relationship starts on the right foot.

What you can expect from us:
✓ Prompt responses to your inquiries (within [X] business hours)
✓ Regular updates on the status of your matter
✓ Clear explanations in plain language — no jargon without explanation
✓ Honest assessments, even when the news isn't what you hoped to hear

What we ask of you:
✓ Provide complete and accurate information
✓ Respond to our requests for documents or information promptly
✓ Notify us immediately of any new developments
✓ Keep contact information current

Your primary contact at our firm is [Attorney/Paralegal Name] at [direct line/email].

Thank you for trusting us.

[Attorney Name]

## ANNUAL ESTATE PLAN REVIEW

Subject: Annual estate plan review — [Name]

It's been a year since we finalized your estate plan. This is a good time to consider whether your documents still reflect your current wishes.

Changes that typically prompt a review:
□ Marriage, divorce, or death of a beneficiary
□ Birth or adoption of a child or grandchild
□ Significant change in assets
□ Change in state of residence
□ Change in tax law

If any of these apply — or if you simply want peace of mind — please contact us to schedule a review meeting.
