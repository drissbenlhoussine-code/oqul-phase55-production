# Consultation Scripts

## INITIAL CONSULTATION OPENING
"[Name], thank you for coming in. I want to start by saying — this conversation is completely confidential. My job today is to listen to your situation, understand what you're facing, and give you an honest assessment of your legal options. There's no pressure to hire us today. This consultation is about making sure you have the information you need. So tell me — in your own words, what brought you in?"

## DISCOVERY QUESTIONS (by practice area)

**Family Law:**
1. "How long have you been married?"
2. "Do you have children together, and what are their ages?"
3. "Are there any current court orders in place?"
4. "What's your primary concern — property, custody, or support?"
5. "Have you tried mediation or counseling?"

**Estate Planning:**
1. "Do you currently have a will or trust in place?"
2. "What are your main goals — protecting assets, providing for specific people, minimizing taxes?"
3. "Do you have a healthcare directive and power of attorney?"
4. "Are there any family dynamics we should be aware of?"

**Personal Injury:**
1. "When did the incident occur?"
2. "Have you received medical treatment? Do you have records?"
3. "Has anyone from the other party's insurance contacted you?"
4. "Have you signed anything or given a recorded statement?"

## CLOSING THE CONSULTATION
"Based on everything you've shared, here's my honest assessment: [legal situation summary]. Your options are [options]. If you decide to retain our firm, here's what the process looks like: [next steps]. My fee for this matter would be [fee structure]. Do you have any questions before we discuss whether moving forward makes sense?"
