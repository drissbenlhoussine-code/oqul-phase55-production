## PHONE INTAKE SCRIPT

"Thank you for calling [Firm Name], this is [Name]. How can I help you today?"

[After they explain:]
"I understand. Before I can connect you with one of our attorneys or schedule a consultation, I need to gather some basic information. May I ask your name and the nature of your legal matter? And I'll need to do a quick conflict check to make sure we're able to represent you."

[After intake:]
"Thank you. Based on what you've described, this sounds like something [Attorney Name] would handle. I can schedule you for a [free/paid] consultation on [days/times]. Does [day] at [time] work for you?"
