# Review Request System

NOTE: All review requests must comply with your state bar's ethical rules regarding testimonials and endorsements. Have this reviewed by your ethics counsel before implementation.

## POST-MATTER FOLLOW-UP (at case conclusion)
"[Name], it has been our privilege to represent you through this matter. We hope the outcome brings you some peace. If you feel our representation was helpful, a Google review — if permitted — is one of the most meaningful ways to help others in similar situations find trusted counsel. We understand if this isn't something you're comfortable with. Either way, please stay in touch."

## EMAIL REVIEW REQUEST
Subject: Thank you for trusting us, [Name]

Dear [Name],

It has been an honor to represent you in your [matter type] matter. We hope the resolution brings you and your family peace.

If you feel comfortable, we would be grateful if you'd share a brief review of your experience with our firm on Google. Many people in difficult situations seek trusted legal counsel, and your words could help them find it.

[Leave a Google Review →]

We wish you all the best in the chapter ahead.

Warmly,
[Attorney Name], [Firm Name]

## HANDLING NEGATIVE FEEDBACK
1. Never respond defensively in public
2. Acknowledge concerns: "We're sorry your experience didn't meet your expectations."
3. Offer to discuss privately: "Please contact us directly so we can address your concerns."
4. Never discuss case specifics publicly
5. Consult your state bar guidelines on responding to negative reviews
