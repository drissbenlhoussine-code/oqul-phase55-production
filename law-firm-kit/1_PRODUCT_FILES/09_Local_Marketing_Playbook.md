# Law Firm Local Marketing Playbook

NOTE: All marketing activities must comply with your state bar's rules of professional conduct for attorney advertising.

## BUILDING YOUR REFERRAL NETWORK

### Professional Referral Partners (Priority #1)
- **Accountants/CPAs** → estate planning, business law, tax matters
- **Financial advisors** → estate planning, asset protection
- **Real estate agents** → real estate law, landlord/tenant, closings
- **Mortgage brokers** → real estate transactions
- **Doctors/healthcare** → personal injury, medical malpractice referrals
- **Other attorneys** (outside your practice area) → reciprocal referrals

Strategy: Monthly touchpoint with each referral partner. Send value — case updates, legal news relevant to their clients — not just "send me referrals."

### Community Presence
- Speak at local business events, chambers, civic organizations
- Host free legal education seminars (estate planning, business basics)
- Write a local column or blog on legal topics in plain language
- Sponsor local community events

### Google My Business
- Complete profile with practice areas clearly listed
- Upload team photos and office photos
- Post educational content 2x per week
- Request reviews from satisfied clients (per ethical rules)

### Networking Groups
- BNI or similar referral networking groups
- Local bar association committees
- Community leadership organizations (Rotary, Lions)
