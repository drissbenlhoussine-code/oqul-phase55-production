Getting Started

Post in 5 Steps.
Start in 5 Minutes.

You already have everything you need. Open your caption bank, follow these five steps, and you'll have your first post ready before you finish reading this guide.

01

Open Your Caption Bank

Open 365_Captions_Estheticians.md in Google Docs or any text editor. Use Ctrl+F (or Cmd+F on Mac) to search by keyword — type "acne," "SPF," "booking," or any topic to jump directly to relevant captions.

💡 Tip: Save the file to your Google Drive for easy mobile access while posting.
02

Choose Your Caption

Pick a caption that matches your content for today. Use the 30-Day Content Plan if you want guidance — it tells you exactly which caption number to use each day.

💡 Monday = Educational. Thursday = Promotional. Check your content plan.
03

Add Your Business Details

Replace any [BRACKETED PLACEHOLDER] with your real information. This takes 30–60 seconds per caption.

Example: Replace [SERVICE NAME] → "HydraFacial"  |  [YOUR BUSINESS] → "Glow by Sofia"
04

Add Your Local Call to Action

End every promotional caption with a location-specific CTA. Even one word changes conversion significantly.

Example: "Book your facial in [CITY] — link in bio."  or  "Now accepting new clients in [NEIGHBORHOOD]."
05

Select Hashtags & Post

Open Hashtag_Bank_200.txt, pick the Ready-to-Use Set that matches your post type, copy it, paste below your caption, and publish.

Educational post → Set 1  |  Promotional post → Set 2  |  BTS post → Set 3

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to use every caption in order?

No. Jump to any caption that fits your content for the day. Use the 30-Day Content Plan to stay organized, or search by keyword to find exactly what you need.

Can I use the same caption more than once?

Yes. Your audience grows and changes. A caption posted 2–3 months ago is fresh content to someone who just followed you. Rotate freely.

What if a caption doesn't sound like me?

Change one sentence. Add your own opener. Adjust the tone. Every caption is a starting point — not a script. The Customization Guide has before-and-after examples showing exactly how to do this.

How many hashtags should I use?

Instagram's current best practice is 5–10 targeted hashtags per post. Use the pre-grouped sets in your Hashtag Bank — each set is already calibrated for a specific content type.

Can I use these on TikTok or Facebook?

Yes. Every caption works across Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn as-is. For TikTok, trim to the first 2–3 sentences to match shorter attention spans.

What if I need help?

Contact the seller through your Etsy purchase. Most questions are answered within 24–48 hours.
