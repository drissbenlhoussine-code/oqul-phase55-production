Customer Success Guide 03
Hashtag Strategy

How to Rotate Your Hashtags

Using identical hashtags on every post triggers Instagram's spam filters and reduces reach. Rotate between your hashtag sets to maintain visibility.

Week 1

Use Sets 1, 3, 5, 9, 11
Rotate between educational and engagement sets.

Week 2

Use Sets 2, 4, 6, 10, 12
Rotate between promotional and results sets.

Week 3

Use Sets 7, 8, 13, 15, 16
Mix seasonal, tips, and new client sets.

Week 4

Return to Week 1 sets.
Your audience has cycled — old sets feel fresh.

The 5–10 Rule

Use 5–10 hashtags per post, not 30. Smaller, targeted sets consistently outperform large generic stacks. Instagram's algorithm rewards relevance over volume.

Common Mistakes

What to Avoid

  • ✕ Posting without personalizing Generic captions get ignored. The [BRACKETS] are there for a reason — fill them in. One business name changes everything.
  • ✕ Only posting promotions If every post is a booking CTA, followers tune out. Use the promotional pillar once per week — no more.
  • ✕ Identical hashtags every day Rotate your sets weekly. Same hashtags every post = reduced reach. The hashtag bank gives you 20 sets for this reason.
  • ✕ Posting then disappearing Spend 15 minutes after posting replying to every comment. Early engagement signals tell Instagram your post is worth showing to more people.
  • ✕ Skipping the call to action Every promotional caption needs one clear next step. "Link in bio," "DM me," or "Book now" — choose one. Two CTAs in one post reduces action by 50%.

How to Avoid Sounding Generic

Add your location

"Serving clients in [CITY]" turns a generic post into a local discovery post. Essential for booking-focused captions.

Name your treatment

Replace "facial" with your actual service name. "HydraFacial" or "Custom Deep Pore Treatment" is specific. "Facial" is not.

Reference a real moment

Add one sentence from your actual week. "Had a client come in today who..." immediately makes the caption feel live and real.

Match your voice

If you usually say "y'all" or sign off with "xo" — do that here too. Consistent voice builds recognizable brand identity over time.

The One Sentence Rule

Add one sentence that is 100% yours — something only you could write about your specific business, client, or day. That one sentence is the difference between a caption that converts and one that scrolls by.
