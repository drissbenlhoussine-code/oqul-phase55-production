==============================================================
DESIGN NOTES FOR ALL 6 IMAGES
==============================================================

Font Recommendations:
- Headline font: A clean serif (Playfair Display, Cormorant Garamond) for premium feel
- Body/list font: A sans-serif (DM Sans, Lato, Inter) for readability
- Avoid script fonts in small sizes — illegible at thumbnail scale

Image Size: 2000 x 2000 px (square) for Etsy listing images

Consistent Branding Elements Across All 6:
- Same color palette (warm neutrals: cream, sage, blush, terracotta)
- Same font pairing used consistently
- Small logo or business name in corner of each image
- Consistent border or margin treatment

Tools for DIY Creation:
- Canva (free or Pro) — search "Etsy listing" templates and customize
- Adobe Express (free tier available)
- Pixelmator (Mac)

File Export: PNG at 300 DPI for best quality at full zoom

Order of Images in Etsy Listing:
1. Hero (stops the scroll)
2. What's Inside (removes doubt)
3. Pillar Breakdown (shows variety)
4. Sample Caption (proves quality)
5. Before/After Positioning (creates desire)
6. Trust + CTA (closes the sale)
