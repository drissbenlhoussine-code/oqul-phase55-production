==============================================================
DESIGN NOTES FOR ALL 6 IMAGES
==============================================================

Font Suggestions:
  - Headline font: A clean serif (Playfair Display, Cormorant Garamond) or a modern sans-serif (DM Sans, Jost, Libre Franklin)
  - Body/caption font: Clean readable sans-serif at 14–18pt minimum
  - Avoid: Script fonts at small sizes, overly decorative fonts, more than 2 font families per image

Color Consistency:
  Use 2–3 colors across all 6 images for a cohesive listing. Suggested palette:
    Primary: Sage green (#C8D9C0)
    Accent: Dusty rose (#E8C9C0)
    Neutral: Warm cream (#F5F0E8)
    Dark: Deep forest green (#2C4A3E)

Avoid:
  - Clutter or too many design elements
  - Stock photos that look generic
  - Clip art or low-resolution graphics
  - All-caps text in body copy (harder to read and can feel aggressive)
  - Filters that make the listing look overly "Instagram-aesthetic" rather than professional

Design Tools That Work Well:
  - Canva (free/pro) — easiest for Etsy listing images
  - Adobe Express
  - Figma (for more design-forward results)
