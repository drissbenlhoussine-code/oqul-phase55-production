PRICING & LAUNCH STRATEGY NOTES
Product: 365 Instagram Captions for Estheticians
==============================================================

PRICING TIMELINE
- Launch price: $12.00
- After 10 reviews: raise to $15.00
- After 50 reviews: introduce 3-niche bundle at $35.00

Do NOT launch at $7. A low price signals low quality to a professional audience.
Estheticians charge $80–$150/hour. They expect professional-quality tools.
$12 is the sweet spot — low enough to be impulsive, high enough to signal value.

==============================================================
ETSY CATEGORY
Primary: Templates > Social Media Templates
Secondary: Business Tools > Marketing Templates
==============================================================

FIRST 7 DAYS — LAUNCH CHECKLIST

Day 1: Go live. Share in your personal Instagram stories.
Day 2: Post in 2–3 esthetician Facebook Groups. Do NOT pitch.
  Comment value first. Post listing only when a relevant question appears.
  Example groups to search: "Esthetician Network," "Licensed Estheticians,"
  "Beauty Business Owners," "Esty Business Support"

Day 3: Post a TikTok or Instagram Reel: "POV: I just launched my first digital
  product — 365 captions for estheticians." Document the process. Not the product.

Day 4: Reach out to 2 esthetician contacts and ask them to purchase and leave
  an honest review. Offer them the product at no charge if needed for early reviews.

Day 5: Post one sample caption in an esthetician community with the note:
  "I have a full caption bank of 365 for sale — here's a sample of what's inside."
  Let the quality sell itself.

Day 6: Check Etsy stats. Look at impressions vs. clicks. If impressions are
  high but clicks are low, your thumbnail image needs work. If impressions
  are low, your title or tags need adjustment.

Day 7: Reply to every message, every question. Response time affects Etsy ranking.

==============================================================
ETSY ADS
- Do NOT run Etsy Ads until you have at least 3 reviews
- Low review count + paid traffic = wasted budget and reduced conversion
- Start Etsy Ads at $1–2/day after 5+ reviews
- Run ads on this listing first before expanding to new listings

OFFSITE ADS
- Etsy automatically runs Offsite Ads if your shop makes over $10,000/year
- If your shop is under that threshold, you can opt out
- For new shops: opt in only after you have at least 5 reviews
