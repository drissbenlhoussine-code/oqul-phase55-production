--------------------------------------------------------------
RECOMMENDED PRICING LADDER
--------------------------------------------------------------

LAUNCH PRICE: $12.00
  - Competitive entry point for the esthetician and beauty professional market
  - Attracts early buyers quickly, which builds social proof (reviews) faster
  - At $12, the perceived value vs. price ratio is extremely strong — estheticians spend more than this on a single product sample
  - Mentally easy for buyers to justify: "That's less than one coffee per month of content."

RAISE TO $15.00 AFTER: 10 verified reviews
  - At 10 reviews, your listing has social proof that converts browsers into buyers
  - The $3 increase is barely perceptible to buyers who see 4-5 star reviews
  - Ten reviews on Etsy typically takes 4–8 weeks at a $12 price point with consistent traffic

RAISE TO $18.00 AFTER: 25 reviews
  - This is a sustainable price that reflects the volume and quality of content
  - 365 captions at $18 = less than 5 cents per caption — still an obvious value
  - You may start seeing organic repeat traffic by this point

INTRODUCE BUNDLE PRICING AFTER: 50 reviews
  - By 50 reviews, your listing is established enough to upsell
  - Bundle idea: Esthetician Social Media Starter Kit — captions (this product) + a second product (e.g., Email Nurture Templates, Story Prompts, or a Canva template pack)
  - Bundle price: $35–$45 (individual products: ~$24–$36 separate)
  - Bundle buyers have higher lifetime value and are more likely to leave reviews

FIRST 7 DAYS PROMOTION STRATEGY
--------------------------------------------------------------

Day 1 — Launch:
  - Publish the listing at your optimal posting time (typically Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11 AM in your time zone)
  - Share the listing link on your personal Instagram account with a behind-the-scenes story about why you made it
  - Post in any Facebook groups you're already a member of for estheticians or beauty professionals (read group rules first; some prohibit direct sales links, but most allow sharing resources)

Day 2:
  - Post a "What's Inside" Reel or Stories on Instagram showing the product files (screen recording)
  - Use relevant esthetician-specific hashtags in your Instagram post
  - Pin the Instagram post to your profile

Day 3:
  - DM 5–10 esthetician peers (not cold; people you know or have interacted with) letting them know you launched something and would love their feedback or support
  - Ask if they'd be willing to leave a review after purchase — keep it casual, not transactional

Day 4:
  - Share a sample caption from the product in an esthetician Facebook group or Reddit community (r/esthetics, r/SkinCareAddiction) as a "free tip" post — include a soft mention that more are available in your Etsy shop
  - This drives organic traffic without being spammy

Day 5:
  - Post your second Instagram promotional post about the product
  - Focus on a specific pain point: "If you've ever deleted a caption instead of posting it..."

Day 6:
  - Follow up with your first buyers (Etsy allows seller messages) thanking them for their purchase and asking if they have any questions — this often triggers review-leaving behavior

Day 7:
  - Evaluate: How many views, favorites, and sales? What's your conversion rate (sales/views)?
  - Adjust your cover image, title, or first bullet points in the description if conversion rate is below 2%

--------------------------------------------------------------
ETSY ADS — TIMING AND STRATEGY
--------------------------------------------------------------

DO NOT run Etsy Ads until you have at least 3 confirmed reviews.

Here's why: Etsy Ads drive paid traffic to your listing — but if someone clicks your listing and sees zero reviews, conversion rates plummet. You're paying for traffic that doesn't convert. This is a common and expensive mistake for new sellers.

When to activate Etsy Ads:
  - You have at least 3 reviews with an average of 4 stars or higher
  - Your listing has at least 50 views organically (confirming the search terms are working)
  - You've confirmed your cover image and title are driving clicks (check your Etsy Stats click-through rate)

Starting Etsy Ads budget: $1–$3/day
  - This is enough to gather data without overspending
  - Run for 2 weeks, then evaluate which search terms are converting
  - Increase budget to $5/day once you identify converting keywords
  - Etsy will automatically bid for relevant searches — you don't need to manually select keywords in standard Etsy Ads

High-value keywords to ensure appear in your title and description:
  - "instagram captions for estheticians"
  - "esthetician social media"
  - "caption bank"
  - "done for you captions"
  - "beauty professional content"
  - "skincare captions"

FACEBOOK GROUP STRATEGY (Highest-Converting Free Channel)
--------------------------------------------------------------

Esthetician and beauty professional Facebook groups have tens of thousands of members who are exactly your target buyer. This is the highest-ROI free traffic source for this product.

How to use it without being spammy:
  1. Identify 3–5 active groups (search Facebook for "esthetician community," "licensed estheticians," "beauty business owners," etc.)
  2. Join them and participate genuinely for 1–2 weeks before promoting anything
  3. Share a single sample caption from the bank as a free resource post: "Here's a caption I wrote for estheticians who never know what to post — feel free to use it. I made a full bank of 365 if anyone's interested."
  4. Let the engagement happen organically — interested people will ask where to find it
  5. Reply to comments with your Etsy link or direct people to your profile where the link lives
  6. Repeat with a new sample caption 1–2 weeks later

What NOT to do:
  - Do not post "Buy my product!" in groups — it will get removed and you may get banned
  - Do not post the same caption in multiple groups simultaneously — space it out
  - Do not DM every group member unsolicited

  - Pinterest has long content longevity — pins can drive traffic for months or years

TikTok (optional but high-reach):
  - A 30-second screen recording showing the inside of the document, captioned "POV: you never have to write a caption again" can go viral in the beauty business niche
  - Include your Etsy shop link in your TikTok bio

Instagram Stories:
  - Share a swipe-through of 3–5 sample captions in a Stories series
  - Add a "link" sticker to your Etsy listing
  - Pin the Stories highlight to your profile as "Caption Bank" or "Shop"

--------------------------------------------------------------
REVIEW VELOCITY MATTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------

On Etsy, listings with 10+ reviews rank significantly higher in search results than listings with 0–5 reviews. Your first priority after launch is getting those first 10 reviews. Strategies:

  - Send a follow-up message to buyers 7 days after purchase asking how they're enjoying the captions and if they'd be willing to leave a review
  - Offer a free bonus (e.g., a "5 Bonus Captions for Stories" PDF) to buyers who leave a review — note: you cannot require a review for the bonus, but you can offer it as a thank-you after they review
  - Keep your product quality high and your customer service responsive — happy buyers leave reviews unprompted more often than you'd expect
