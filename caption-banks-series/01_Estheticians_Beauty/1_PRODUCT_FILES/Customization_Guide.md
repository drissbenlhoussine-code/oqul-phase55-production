## PART 1: HOW TO PERSONALIZE YOUR CAPTIONS

### Step 1: Replace All Placeholders

Every caption that contains a placeholder looks like this: `[INSERT PRICE]`, `[SERVICE NAME]`, `[CLIENT QUOTE]`, or `[MONTH]`. Do a quick scan and fill in these details with your real information before posting. Never post a caption with brackets still in it.

**Common placeholders you'll encounter:**
- `[INSERT PRICE]` — Your actual service price
- `[SERVICE NAME]` — The specific treatment you're promoting
- `[MONTH]` / `[DATE]` — The current month or a specific date
- `[INSERT CLIENT QUOTE]` — A real quote from a client (with their permission)
- `[NUMBER]` — Your actual number of available openings, years in business, etc.
- `[BRAND NAME]` / `[PRODUCT NAME]` — The actual brands and products you use

---

### Step 2: Add Your Treatment Names

You have a menu. Use it. Instead of "a facial," say "my Signature Glow Facial." Instead of "a chemical peel," say "my [Your Peel Name] Peel." Adding your specific service names:
- Makes the caption feel more real and credible
- Helps with searchability if someone Googles your services
- Distinguishes you from every other esthetician using generic language

---

### Step 3: Adjust the Tone to Match Your Voice

These captions are written in a warm, confident, professional tone — but your voice is unique. As you read through the captions, ask yourself:

- **"Is this too formal for me?"** — If so, loosen it up. Add contractions. Use slang you actually use.
- **"Is this not formal enough for my brand?"** — If your brand is more polished and clinical, elevate the language.
- **"Would I actually say this?"** — If the answer is no, rewrite the sentence until you would.

You should be able to read any caption in this bank out loud and have it feel natural coming from you.

---

### Step 4: Add Local References

Your city, neighborhood, or regional culture can make a caption feel instantly more personal and searchable by local clients.

**Examples:**
- "As an esthetician in [YOUR CITY]..." 
- "If you're dealing with the hard water situation we have here in [REGION]..."
- "Winter here in [CITY] is no joke for your skin barrier..."

Local specificity builds trust because it signals you understand their actual environment.

---

### Step 5: Insert Real Client Results (Carefully)

The Testimonial captions all include `[INSERT CLIENT QUOTE]` placeholders. When filling these in:
- Always get verbal or written permission from the client before sharing their words
- Paraphrase if needed — you don't have to use word-for-word quotes
- Avoid any language that makes specific medical promises ("this cured my acne")
- Focus on how they *feel* and what they *notice*, not clinical outcomes
- First names are fine; full names require explicit consent

## PART 3: BEST POSTING TIMES FOR BEAUTY PROFESSIONALS

Posting times vary by audience, but here are the research-backed windows that tend to perform well for beauty and wellness accounts:

### Instagram Feed Posts
| Day | Best Times |
|---|---|
| Monday | 7–9 AM, 11 AM–1 PM |
| Tuesday | 8–10 AM, 12–2 PM |
| Wednesday | 7–9 AM, 5–7 PM |
| Thursday | 8–10 AM, 6–8 PM |
| Friday | 7–9 AM, 11 AM–1 PM |
| Saturday | 9–11 AM (lower overall engagement day) |
| Sunday | 10 AM–12 PM (great for self-care content) |

### Notes on Timing:
- **Morning posts (7–9 AM)** catch people during their commute or coffee routine
- **Lunch posts (11 AM–1 PM)** catch people scrolling on break
- **Evening posts (5–7 PM)** catch people unwinding after work
- **Avoid posting late at night** — most beauty service audiences are not active after 9 PM

### The Most Important Rule:
Consistency beats optimal timing. Posting at 8 AM every Tuesday outperforms posting at the "perfect" time randomly. Your audience will learn when to expect you.

### Track Your Own Data:
After 30–60 days, check your Instagram Insights (available on business accounts). Look at "Most Active Times" under your Audience tab. That data is more accurate than any general benchmark for *your specific* audience.

## PART 4: CONTENT MIX RECOMMENDATION

### The 5-Post Weekly Framework

A well-balanced week of content for a beauty professional includes:

**Monday** — Educational Post (Pillar 1)
- Build authority and start the week with value
- Great for saves and shares (boosts algorithm)
- Example: ingredient spotlight, skincare myth, routine tip

**Tuesday** — Behind the Scenes (Pillar 4)
- Humanize your brand early in the week
- Build trust through vulnerability and process
- Example: treatment prep, unboxing, studio snapshot

**Wednesday** — Promotional Post (Pillar 2)
- Midweek is ideal for booking-focused content
- Pair with a clear booking CTA
- Example: service spotlight, limited-time offer, new client promo

**Thursday** — Engagement Post (Pillar 3)
- Drive comments and algorithm signals before the weekend
- End with a direct question
- Example: poll, "tell me your..." question, hot take

**Friday** — Testimonial or Seasonal Post (Pillar 5 or 6)
- End the week with social proof or timely content
- Great for inspiration heading into the weekend
- Example: client love, holiday prep, seasonal tip

### Monthly Cadence at a Glance:

| Week | Theme Focus |
|---|---|
| Week 1 | New month energy — education + new client push |
| Week 2 | Community + engagement heavy |
| Week 3 | Promotion + social proof |
| Week 4 | Seasonal/trending + behind the scenes |

Adjust this framework around your booking cycles, seasonal promotions, and any launches or events. The key is variety — your audience should see a mix of content types, not 5 promotional posts in a row.
