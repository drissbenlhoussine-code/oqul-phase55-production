# Caption Customization Guide
## Making These Captions Sound Like You

---

### Step 1: Find Your Placeholder and Replace It

Every caption uses brackets for personal details:
- [YOUR NAME] → Your first name or business name
- [YOUR BUSINESS] → Your salon, studio, or business name
- [SERVICE NAME] → e.g., "HydraFacial," "Deep Cleansing Facial"
- [CITY] → Your location
- [PRICE] → Your actual price
- [DURATION] → How long your treatment takes
- [X YEARS] → Your years of experience
- [TIMEFRAME] → A realistic timeframe for results
- [INSERT CLIENT QUOTE] → A real client quote with permission

Takes about 60 seconds per caption.

---

### Step 2: Add One Personal Detail

The difference between generic and converting is one personal sentence:
- "I've been doing this in [CITY] for [X] years, and this result keeps me going."
- "If you've been following me, you know how much I focus on [YOUR NICHE]."
- "This is the treatment I book for myself before offering it to clients."

One sentence. Your voice. Done.

---

### Step 3: Match Your Tone

**More casual:** Shorten sentences. Add "honestly" or "real talk." Drop formal transitions.

Original: "Double cleansing is not about being extra. It's about being effective."
Casual: "Double cleansing isn't being extra. It's the whole point."

**More clinical:** Lead with the fact. Tighten language. Remove conversational asides.

Clinical: "Double cleansing removes surface residue in pass one and cleanses skin in pass two."

### Before & After Examples

EXAMPLE 1 — Educational

Original:
"Retinol is not a serum you slap on and forget. Start two nights a week. Build from there."

Personalized:
"Retinol is not a serum you slap on and forget. I always start new clients on [PRODUCT] at two nights a week before building up. DM me if you want my specific protocol."

---

EXAMPLE 2 — Promotional

Original:
"Last few spots open this week. If you've been wanting to try [SERVICE NAME], this is your window."

Personalized:
"Last two spots open this Thursday afternoon. If you've been wanting to try my Deep Pore Cleansing Facial, DM me 'THURSDAY' to claim your spot."

---

EXAMPLE 3 — Engagement

Original:
"Tell me your skin type and your #1 current concern below. I'll give you one genuinely helpful tip."

Personalized:
"Tell me your skin type and biggest concern right now. I'll reply to every comment with one specific tip — no generic answers. Drop it below."
