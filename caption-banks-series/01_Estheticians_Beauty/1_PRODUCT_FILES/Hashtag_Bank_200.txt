==============================================================
TIPS FOR USING HASHTAGS ON INSTAGRAM
==============================================================

1. ROTATE YOUR SETS: Don't use the exact same hashtags on every post. Instagram may reduce reach when you use identical hashtag sets repeatedly. Mix and match from the sections above.

2. IDEAL NUMBER: Instagram allows up to 30 hashtags, but 10-15 tends to perform better for most accounts. Quality over quantity.

3. PLACEMENT: You can place hashtags in the caption itself or in the first comment. Both work. Experiment to see which your audience prefers.

4. NICHE FIRST: When in doubt, lean toward niche and mid-range hashtags. Broad hashtags (#skincare, #beauty) move so fast your post is buried in seconds.

5. LOCAL HASHTAGS: Add your city or neighborhood hashtags to reach local clients. Example: #[YOURCITY]esthetician #[YOURCITY]skincare #[YOURCITY]facials

6. RELEVANCE MATTERS: Only use hashtags that are directly relevant to the post you're sharing. Irrelevant hashtags can reduce your content's trust score with the algorithm.
