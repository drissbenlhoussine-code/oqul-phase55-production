## VOICE ADJUSTMENT

**If your brand is scientific/evidence-based:** Keep captions as-is. They're already research-informed.

**If your brand is motivational/energetic:** Add "LET'S GO." at the end of educational posts. Lead with the emotional hook before the education.

**If your brand is premium/exclusive:** Replace "clients" with "members." Replace "program" with "transformation experience." Add words like "curated" and "elite."

**If your brand is accessible/community:** Add "No experience needed" where appropriate. Soften promotional copy with "no pressure" language.
