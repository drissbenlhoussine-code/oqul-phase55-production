Common Questions
Everything You Need
to Know Before You Start
What if a caption doesn't sound exactly like me?
Read the Customization Guide for voice adjustment tips. Most captions need 1–2 word swaps to match your personality. The structure is already done.
Can I use these on Facebook, TikTok, or LinkedIn?
Yes. Optimized for Instagram but work on any text-based platform. On TikTok, use the first 1–2 sentences as your on-screen hook and the rest as the caption.
How often should I post?
4–5 times per week consistently outperforms 7 times one week and 0 the next. Consistency is the strategy. Use the 30-Day Calendar to eliminate the decision.
I'm an online coach. Will these work for me?
Yes. Many captions are written specifically for online trainers. Simply adjust location references and emphasize your DM/booking link in promotional captions.
What are the best times to post for a fitness trainer?
5:30–7 AM (early gym-goers) · 12–1 PM (lunch scrollers) · 5–7 PM (post-work fitness crowd) · 8–9:30 PM (high save rate). Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday perform best overall.
What local hashtags should I add?
Add: #[YOURCITY]personaltrainer, #[YOURCITY]gym, #[YOURCITY]fitness. These are low-competition and searched by people actively looking to hire a trainer in your area.
