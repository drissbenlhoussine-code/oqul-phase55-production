Common Questions
Everything You Need
to Know Before You Start
What if a caption doesn't sound exactly like me?
Read the Customization Guide for voice adjustment tips. Most captions only need 1–2 small word swaps to match your personality. The structure is the hardest part — that's already done for you.
Can I use these on Facebook, TikTok, or LinkedIn too?
Yes. The captions are written for Instagram but work on any text-based social platform. On TikTok, use the first 1–2 sentences as your on-screen text or hook, and the rest as the caption.
How often should I post?
For barber accounts, 4–5 times per week consistently outperforms 7 times one week and 0 the next. Consistency is the strategy. Use the 30-Day Content Calendar to stay on track without thinking about it.
I run a hair salon, not a barbershop. Will these work?
Yes. The educational, promotional, engagement, and BTS pillars are easily adapted for any hair salon. Simply swap "barber" with "stylist" and "fade" with "cut" or "color" where relevant. The structure and strategy are universal for hair professionals.
What photos should I use with these captions?
Your own work is always best. Before/after shots, process videos, and clean studio photos perform well. The 30-Day Content Calendar includes a "Photo/Video Idea" column for every post. Phone quality is fine — good lighting matters more than a professional camera.
What are the best times to post for a barber account?
Monday 7–9 AM (men planning their week) · Thursday 7–9 PM (making weekend plans) · Friday 8–10 AM (booking weekend cuts) · Saturday 8–10 AM (pre-appointment scrolling). Full schedule in the Customization Guide.
What local hashtags should I add every post?
Add at minimum: #[YOURCITY]barber, #[YOURCITY]barbershop, #[YOURCITY]haircut. These are low-competition, high-intent hashtags. A man searching #AtlantaBarber is looking to BOOK — that's your most valuable audience.
