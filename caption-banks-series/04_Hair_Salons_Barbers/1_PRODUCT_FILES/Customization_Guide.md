## ADDING YOUR LOCAL SPIN

The most powerful upgrade you can make to any caption:

**Add your city and neighborhood.**

Instead of: "Book your appointment: link in bio"
Write: "Book your appointment in [NEIGHBORHOOD], [CITY]: link in bio"

Instead of: "Looking for a barber in [CITY]?"
Write: "Looking for the best barber in [SPECIFIC NEIGHBORHOOD]?"

**Local hashtags to always include:**
```
#[YOURCITY]barber
#[YOURCITY]barbershop
#[YOURCITY]hair
#[YOURCITYNEIGHBORHOOD]
```

These local hashtags have low competition and very high intent. A man searching #AltantaBarber is looking to BOOK. That's your best possible audience.

Educational captions → post with a photo of you explaining something (your tools, demonstrating technique, pointing to hair)

Testimonials → screenshot the real message/review (with permission) or post the transformation photo with the quote in the caption

## VOICE PERSONALIZATION TIPS

These captions are written in a professional, direct, confident tone that matches most barber brands.

If your brand voice is different, here's how to adjust:

**More casual / street:** Add "fr" or "lowkey" where appropriate. Shorten sentences. Start lines with "Bro," or "Real talk,"

**More premium / upscale:** Expand sentences. Remove slang. Add words like "precision," "craftsmanship," "curated experience"

**More local / community:** Add neighborhood references, local event mentions, shout-outs to local culture. Use the names of streets, landmarks, or local teams.

The skeleton of each caption is strong. Your voice makes it yours.
