TIPS FOR USING HASHTAGS ON INSTAGRAM
==============================================================

1. LOCAL IS POWERFUL: Add your city or neighborhood hashtags to every post.
   Example: #[CITY]barber #[CITY]barbershop #[CITY]haircut
   This is the single most effective tactic for local service businesses on Instagram.

2. ROTATE YOUR SETS: Don't use identical hashtag sets on every post.
   Instagram may reduce reach for accounts that repeat the same 10 hashtags daily.
   Alternate between sets, or mix and match.

3. IDEAL NUMBER: 10–15 hashtags per post performs well for most barber accounts.
   More is not always better — relevance matters.

4. PLACEMENT: Hashtags in the caption OR first comment both work.
   Test which your audience responds to better.

5. NICHE FIRST: Broad hashtags like #hair and #barber move so fast your post
   is buried in seconds. Lead with niche and mid-range tags.

6. STORY HASHTAGS: Use 3–5 hashtags on Stories as well (not just feed posts).
   Story hashtag stickers can bring local clients to your profile.

7. COMMUNITY HASHTAGS: Following niche hashtags like #barbercommunity and
   #barberconnect helps you engage with other barbers, which builds your following.
