PHOTOGRAPHY BUSINESS KIT — 200 HASHTAG BANK
============================================================

How to Use This Hashtag Bank

Instagram allows up to 30 hashtags per post. For best results, pull 2-3 hashtags from each tier that applies to your specific post — do not dump all high-volume tags every time. Mix size ranges: pair 1-2 mega-volume tags with several mid-volume and a few niche tags. This approach gives you broad exposure while landing in feeds where you can actually compete.

Rotate your sets. Using the exact same 30 hashtags on every post trains Instagram's algorithm to deprioritize your content. Use the 5 ready-to-rotate sets at the end of this file, or build your own combinations by swapping in different tags from each relevant tier.

Replace [CITY], [STATE], and [REGION] placeholders in Tier 8 with your actual location before posting. Local tags have lower volume but far higher booking conversion — a bride in Austin searching #AustinWeddingPhotographer is actively looking to hire.

Save these sets in your phone's notes app or a spreadsheet so you can copy-paste quickly after uploading.
