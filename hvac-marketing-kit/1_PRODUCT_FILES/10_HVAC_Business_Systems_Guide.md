# HVAC Business Systems Guide

## SCALING YOUR HVAC BUSINESS

### Service Call Flow
1. Call comes in → dispatched within 30 min during business hours
2. Tech calls customer 30 min before arrival
3. On arrival: introduce, diagnose, quote before any work
4. On completion: collect payment, request review, offer maintenance agreement
5. 24-hour follow-up text or call

### Maintenance Agreement Program
Goal: Convert 30% of service customers into maintenance agreement holders
- Pitch at every service call
- Email campaign to past customers without agreements
- Incentivize with $[X] off first agreement

### Revenue Streams to Build
1. Emergency service (premium pricing)
2. Maintenance agreements (recurring revenue)
3. Equipment replacement (highest margin)
4. Indoor air quality products (add-on sales)
5. Smart home integration (thermostats, controls)
6. Commercial contracts (steady revenue base)

### Financial KPIs to Track
- Revenue per technician per day
- Average ticket size (service vs install)
- Maintenance agreement renewal rate
- Customer acquisition cost by channel
- Gross margin by job type
- Emergency vs planned service ratio
