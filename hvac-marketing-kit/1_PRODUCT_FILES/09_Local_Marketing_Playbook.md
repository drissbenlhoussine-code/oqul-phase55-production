# HVAC Local Marketing Playbook

## DOMINATING YOUR SERVICE AREA

### Google My Business (Priority #1)
- Complete profile with all services listed
- Upload 25+ job photos (before/after, team, equipment)
- Post 2–3x per week (seasonal tips, promotions, reviews)
- Respond to every review within 24 hours
- Target: 100+ Google reviews with 4.7+ average
- Use Google Local Services Ads for pay-per-lead visibility

### Nextdoor for HVAC
- Create business profile and verify service area
- Answer every HVAC question posted in your area
- Post seasonal tips with your branding
- After every job, ask customer to post a Nextdoor recommendation

### Direct Mail Campaigns
- Spring: mail to homeowners about AC tune-ups in March/April
- Fall: mail about heating prep in September/October
- After installs: mail 30 surrounding homes with "your neighbor just upgraded their HVAC" message

### Strategic Partnerships
- Real estate agents: pre-listing HVAC inspections + buyer inspections
- Property managers: service contracts for rental units
- Home warranty companies: become a preferred vendor
- Building contractors: HVAC for new builds and renovations
- Electricians, plumbers: reciprocal referral relationships

### Neighborhood Completion Marketing
After every install or major job:
"We just installed a new [system] at [nearby address]. If your system is aging or not keeping up, we'd love to give you a free assessment. [Company] [Phone]"
