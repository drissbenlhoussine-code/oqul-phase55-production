# Customer Follow-Up & Service Scripts

## INITIAL CALL INTAKE
"Thank you for calling [Company], this is [Name]. What's going on with your system today?"

[Diagnose:]
"Is it not cooling/heating at all, or just not keeping up? And when did you first notice the problem?"

[Schedule:]
"I can have a technician out [today/tomorrow] between [time window]. We have [Time 1] or [Time 2] — which works better? The diagnostic fee is $[X], which goes toward the repair if you proceed."

## POST-SERVICE FOLLOW-UP (24 hours)
"Hi [Name]! [Technician] just wanted to check in — how's your system running since yesterday's service? If everything is good, we'd really appreciate a quick Google review. [Link] It means a lot to our small business. — [Company]"

## MAINTENANCE AGREEMENT UPSELL SCRIPT
"[Name], based on what we saw today, I want to talk to you about our maintenance agreement. For $[X]/year, we do two tune-ups — spring and fall — priority scheduling, and 10% off all repairs. Most of our maintenance customers save more than the cost of the agreement in one repair. Would you like me to put you on the list?"

## QUOTE FOLLOW-UP SEQUENCE
Day 1: "Hi [Name], [Technician] here from [Company]. Just checking — did you have any questions about today's quote? Happy to walk through anything."
Day 3: "Hi [Name], following up on your repair/replacement quote. We're scheduling for [week] and wanted to see where you were in the decision. No pressure — just here if you need anything."
Day 7: "Hi [Name], last follow-up from [Company]. Our quote is valid through [date]. If you'd like to move forward or have questions, we're here. If the timing isn't right, no worries at all."
