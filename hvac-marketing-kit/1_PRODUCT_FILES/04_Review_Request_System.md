# HVAC Review Request System

## POST-SERVICE TEXT (within 2 hours)
"Hi [Name]! [Technician] just wanted to check in — how's the system running? If everything's good, a quick Google review would really help us out. [Link] Thanks! — [Company]"

## EMAIL FOLLOW-UP (Day 2)
Subject: How's your system running, [Name]?

Hi [Name],

It's been a day since [Technician] serviced your system. We hope everything is running smoothly!

If you're happy with the service, we'd be grateful if you'd leave us a quick Google review. Even one sentence helps other homeowners in [City] find reliable HVAC service.

[Leave a Review on Google →]

Also — if you notice anything at all with your system, don't hesitate to call. We stand behind every service call.

Thank you,
[Company] Team

## IN-PERSON REQUEST SCRIPT
"[Name], thank you for choosing us today. Can I ask one thing before I head out? If you're satisfied with the service, a Google review would really help our small business. I can text you the link right now if you'd like — it only takes about a minute."

## HANDLING NEGATIVE EXPERIENCE
If a customer expresses frustration:
1. Acknowledge: "I completely understand and I'm sorry this wasn't the experience you deserved."
2. Don't defend — listen fully.
3. Offer to resolve: "What would make this right for you?"
4. Follow through within 24 hours.
5. Follow up again 3 days later to ensure satisfaction.
