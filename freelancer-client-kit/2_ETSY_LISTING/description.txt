The document you send the moment a client signs and pays their deposit. Covers: how you work, communication expectations, what you need from them, the revision policy, file delivery, and next steps. Short version for contracts and proposals. Full version for client onboarding. Plus a revision request form for clients to use when submitting feedback. Ends scope creep before it starts.

4-week project timeline, 8-week project timeline, and ongoing retainer schedule. Includes a project status tracker and two email templates: weekly client update and timeline change notice.
