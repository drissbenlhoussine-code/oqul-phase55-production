# CLIENT REVISION POLICY

**[YOUR NAME / BUSINESS NAME]**

---

### WHAT'S INCLUDED

Your project includes **[X] revision round(s)** as part of the agreed scope.

A "revision round" is a single consolidated batch of feedback submitted after I deliver a phase of work. I incorporate all feedback in one round, then deliver the revised version.

**Example of one revision round:**
I deliver the first draft of your logo. You review it and compile feedback: "Make the icon 20% smaller, change the font to be bolder, try the color in dark green instead of teal." I address all of these in one pass and deliver the revision. That is one round.

---

### WHAT COUNTS AS A REVISION

Revisions are adjustments to the approved direction, including:
- Color, font, or typography changes
- Copy edits (word changes, tone adjustments)
- Layout or spacing adjustments
- Size or proportion changes
- Minor additions to existing elements

---

### WHAT IS NOT A REVISION

The following will be scoped and priced as additional work:

**Direction change:** You approved a minimalist, monochrome logo direction in Phase 1. In Phase 2, you decide you want something bold and colorful. This is a direction change — not a revision.

**Scope expansion:** The project included 3 social media templates. You now want 3 more. This is a new deliverable.

**Concept redesign:** If the approved concept needs to be substantially rebuilt rather than adjusted, that is new work.

**New stakeholder input:** If a new decision-maker (partner, CEO, investor) is introduced after Phase 1 approval and requires us to restart from a different direction, additional fees apply. This is why I ask you to identify all stakeholders at the start.

---

### HOW TO SUBMIT REVISION REQUESTS

**Format:** One consolidated email with numbered, specific feedback.
**Not acceptable:** Multiple emails over several days, verbal requests, "I'll know it when I see it."

**Good feedback example:**
1. Change "Book a Call" to "Get Started"
2. Make the hero section background darker — try #1A1A1A
3. The logo feels too small on mobile — can we increase by 15%?
4. Remove the testimonials section from the homepage entirely

**Unclear feedback (will slow things down):**
- "It doesn't feel right"
- "Can you make it more professional?"
- "Something is off with the colors"

If your feedback is unclear, I'll ask clarifying questions before proceeding. This protects your revision rounds.
