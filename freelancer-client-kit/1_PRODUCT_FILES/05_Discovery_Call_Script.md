## THE CALL STRUCTURE

### OPENING (2–3 minutes)
*Goal: Build rapport, set the agenda.*

"Hi [Name], great to meet you. Thanks for taking the time today.

Just to set the stage — I have [30/45/60] minutes blocked for us. My plan is to spend the first [20–30] minutes learning as much as I can about what you're working on, and the last [10–15] minutes answering any questions you have for me. Does that work?

Before we dive in — I had a chance to look at your [website/Instagram/LinkedIn] before the call. [Insert one genuine observation]. I'd love to hear more about what's behind that."

### QUALIFYING QUESTIONS (Ask internally — don't read aloud)

Before proposing, ask yourself:
- Is the budget realistic for the scope they described?
- Is the timeline achievable?
- Does this client seem collaborative or controlling?
- Do I want to work with this person for [X] weeks?
- Is this project within my expertise?

**Red flags to watch for:**
- "I just need something simple" (never simple)
- "I had a bad experience with my last [freelancer type]" (without taking any responsibility)
- No clear decision-maker identified
- Budget significantly below market rate
- Hostile or dismissive tone
- "I just need you to execute — I have the whole vision" (without actually showing you the vision)
