## REVISION POLICY

**Included:** [X] round(s) of revisions on each phase.

**What counts as a revision:**
Adjustments to the approved direction — color tweaks, layout shifts, copy edits, minor changes.

**What is NOT a revision (and will be billed separately):**
- Changing the concept or direction after it's been approved
- Adding deliverables not in the original scope
- Requests that require substantially re-creating completed work

**How to submit revision requests:**
- Compile all feedback into one email with numbered points
- Be specific: "Change the blue to a darker navy (#003366)" not "make it look more professional"
- Reference the document or file name you're commenting on

**Additional revisions:** $[Rate]/hour or $[flat fee] per round — invoiced separately.
