## INVOICE SENDING CHECKLIST

Before sending any invoice, verify:
- [ ] Invoice number is sequential and unique
- [ ] Client name and contact info are correct
- [ ] Project name matches the signed contract
- [ ] All line items are described clearly
- [ ] Math is correct (subtotal, tax, total, balance)
- [ ] Payment methods and due date are included
- [ ] Saved as PDF before sending (not editable)
- [ ] Sent to the right email address

**Recommended tools for professional invoicing:**
- Wave (free) — wave.com
- FreshBooks — freshbooks.com
- HoneyBook — honeybook.com
- PayPal Invoicing (free with PayPal account)
- Bonsai — hellobonsai.com (popular with freelancers)

**Tax note:** If you're required to charge sales tax in your jurisdiction, add a line item. Consult a local accountant for tax obligations in your country/state.
