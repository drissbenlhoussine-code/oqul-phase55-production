## 4. REVISIONS

## 7. CONFIDENTIALITY

Both parties agree to keep confidential any proprietary information shared during the course of this project. Neither party will disclose the other's trade secrets, business plans, pricing, or client data to any third party without written consent.

This obligation survives termination of this agreement.

## 8. CANCELLATION & TERMINATION

**Cancellation by Client:**
- If the Client cancels before work begins: the deposit is forfeited.
- If the Client cancels after work has begun: the Client owes payment for all work completed to date, calculated at $[HOURLY RATE]/hour, plus the non-refundable deposit.
- Completed deliverables are released only upon full payment of outstanding balance.

**Cancellation by Freelancer:**
- The Freelancer may terminate this agreement if the Client breaches payment terms, is abusive or unreasonable, or if the project becomes legally or ethically problematic.
- In this case, the Freelancer will refund any unused portion of payments received, less compensation for work completed.

---

## 9. WARRANTIES & LIABILITY

The Freelancer warrants that the Work will be original and will not knowingly infringe on any third-party intellectual property.

The Freelancer's total liability under this agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid. The Freelancer is not liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.

The Client is responsible for ensuring all content, names, trademarks, and logos provided to the Freelancer do not infringe on third-party rights.

---

## 10. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR

The Freelancer is an independent contractor, not an employee of the Client. The Freelancer is responsible for all taxes, insurance, and business expenses related to their work. Nothing in this agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, or employment relationship.
