## WHAT I NEED FROM YOU

Great work is a collaboration. Here's your role:

**Be specific with feedback.**
"I love the direction" is wonderful but not actionable. "The font feels too formal — can we try something with more personality?" gives me exactly what I need.

**Consolidate your feedback.**
One email with all feedback = one revision round. Multiple emails = confusion and wasted rounds.

**Respond within [X] business days.**
This keeps the project on schedule. If you need more time, just say so — I'd rather know than be in the dark.

**Designate a single decision-maker.**
If multiple people need to approve the work, please compile everyone's feedback before sending it to me.

---

## THE REVISION PROCESS

You have **[X] round(s) of revisions** included.

After I deliver each phase, you review and send feedback. I incorporate it. That's one round.

**What's a revision:** Color changes, font adjustments, copy tweaks, layout shifts.
**What's not a revision:** Direction changes, scope additions, or substantial redesigns.

If you're not sure whether something is a revision or a change, ask me. I'd rather have the conversation early.
