# PROJECT COMPLETION & OFFBOARDING TEMPLATE
## Freelancer Client Kit — Template 10 of 10

**HOW TO USE:**
Use this at the end of every project. It closes the relationship professionally,
maximizes your chance of referrals and repeat business, and gets you the testimonial.

## TESTIMONIAL REQUEST EMAIL

*(Send 2–3 days after the final delivery, after client has had time to explore their files)*

---

Subject: One small ask — [Project Name]

Hi [Client Name],

I hope you've had a chance to explore your files and everything looks great in use.

I have one small ask: would you be willing to leave a short testimonial about working together?

It can be as brief as 2–3 sentences — just an honest take on the experience: what the project was, what you found valuable, and what you'd say to someone considering hiring me.

**Where to share it (choose whatever's easiest):**
- **Google Business:** [Your Google Business link]
- **LinkedIn recommendation:** [Your LinkedIn profile]
- **Email me directly:** [Email] — I'll post it to my website

It takes under 5 minutes and makes an enormous difference for my business. I'll send you the link to your finished work to share with others, as a thank you.

No pressure if the timing isn't right — just know how much it would mean.

Thank you, [Client Name].

[Your Name]

## OFFBOARDING HANDOFF CHECKLIST

*(Use this before closing out every project)*

**Files:**
- [ ] All final files organized and clearly named
- [ ] Delivered in agreed formats
- [ ] Upload link sent and confirmed accessible
- [ ] Project folder organized (client can navigate without guidance)

**Documentation:**
- [ ] Usage guide or notes included (if needed)
- [ ] Font files / license info included (if applicable)
- [ ] Password or credentials document shared securely (if applicable)

**Financials:**
- [ ] Final invoice sent
- [ ] Final payment received and confirmed
- [ ] Receipt / payment confirmation sent to client

**Relationship:**
- [ ] Testimonial requested
- [ ] Referral ask made (or planned)
- [ ] Client left a review on Google (if applicable)
- [ ] Your own review of client written / noted for future reference

**Your own records:**
- [ ] Invoice filed
- [ ] Contract archived
- [ ] Project photos / screenshots saved for portfolio
- [ ] Client contact saved in CRM / contact list
- [ ] Project notes filed (what worked, what didn't, what you'd do differently)
