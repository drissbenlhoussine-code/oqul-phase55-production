## THE PROCESS

Here's how I work, step by step:

**Phase 1 — Discovery & Strategy** *(Week 1)*
We begin with a [90-minute / 60-minute] kickoff call to align on goals, audience, competitive landscape, and creative direction. I'll send you a project questionnaire beforehand to maximize our time.

Deliverable: Creative brief summarizing our agreed direction.

**Phase 2 — Concepts** *(Week 2)*
I'll develop [X] initial concept directions for your review. Each direction will include [description of what's presented].

Deliverable: [X] concept options presented via [PDF / Figma / Google Slides].
Your action: Select a direction and provide written feedback within [X] business days.

**Phase 3 — Refinement** *(Week 3)*
Based on your feedback, I'll refine the selected direction through [X] revision round(s).

Deliverable: Refined [deliverable].
Your action: Final approval.

**Phase 4 — Final Delivery** *(Week 4)*
I'll prepare all final files in the formats you need: [list formats — PNG, PDF, AI, etc.].

Deliverable: Final file package + usage guide.
